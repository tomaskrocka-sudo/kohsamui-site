When is Songkran 2027 on Koh Samui? | Songkran is a public holiday on Tuesday 13, Wednesday 14 and Thursday 15 April 2027. On Samui the street water fights usually start a day early, on Monday 12 April. The 2026 official programme ran 12–15 April in Bo Phut, Maret and Ang Thong subdistricts, and the 2027 programme is normally announced in March.
Where is the biggest water fight on Samui? | Chaweng Beach Road is the biggest, with bars, shops and hotels joining in from late morning until after dark on 12–15 April. Lamai is smaller and more relaxed, and Nathon is more traditional, with temple ceremonies alongside the water. Beach clubs and Central Samui in Chaweng run their own parties.
Is Songkran on Koh Samui family-friendly? | Yes, in daylight and away from the busiest bars. Children love the water guns, and Lamai, Nathon and Fisherman's Village are calmer than central Chaweng. Use goggles for small children, keep them off scooters in water zones, and avoid Chaweng Beach Road in the evening when the crowd is drunker.
Is alcohol sold during Songkran on Samui? | Yes. Songkran is not a Buddhist holy day, so there is no alcohol-sales ban. Since 29 May 2026, shops may sell alcohol from 11:00 to 24:00, and licensed bars and hotels can stay open later. The legal drinking age is 20. Drinking and riding is the main cause of Songkran road deaths, so don't combine them.

How do I protect my phone during Songkran? | Put it in a waterproof pouch with a neck strap, sold on Chaweng Beach Road and in 7-Eleven, and double-bag it in a zip-lock bag because cheap pouches leak. Leave your passport in the hotel safe and carry a copy. Take only the cash you need, in a second pouch.
Is it safe to ride a scooter during Songkran? | It is the riskiest time of year to ride. Over Songkran 2026, Thailand recorded 1,242 road accidents and 242 deaths in seven days, mostly involving motorcycles. On Samui, avoid riding through water-fight zones, never ride after drinking, and use Grab or a songthaew on 12–15 April.
Do hotels on Samui get booked up for Songkran? | Yes, increasingly. Foreign Songkran bookings for Samui rose 65 % in 2025 after The White Lotus aired, and mid-April often overlaps Easter holidays. Book villas, flights and boat trips early, ideally two to three months ahead for Chaweng and Fisherman's Village.
