What personal data do you need for a tour booking? | Only what we need to run the trip: your name, email, WhatsApp number with country code, hotel or villa and area, trip date and the number of adults, children and infants. Card details go straight to our payment processor, Stripe, so we never see your full card number. We do not ask for passport numbers on day trips.
Do you share my details with the boat crew? | Yes, but only what they need to run your trip: your name, number of guests, hotel, pickup time, phone number and any request that matters on the day, such as a mobility need. Crews, drivers and partner operators may use these details only for your trip. We never sell or rent personal data or share it for advertising.
Do you use cookies on this site? | Yes, three kinds. Necessary cookies run the cart, checkout and your cookie choice. Preference settings such as light or dark theme and display currency are stored only on your device. Google Analytics cookies run only if you accept them in the cookie banner, and choosing Reject all is honoured. We use no advertising cookies by default.
How long do you keep my booking data? | Booking and payment records are kept for as long as Thai accounting and tax law requires. Enquiries that do not become bookings and WhatsApp chats are kept for up to 2 years unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Newsletter subscriptions last until you unsubscribe.
How do I ask you to delete my data? | Send your request to the email address on our Contact page or to WhatsApp +66 63 704 0888, with the name and email or phone number you booked with. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we reply within 30 days at no charge. Booking records we must keep for tax law are kept until that period ends, then deleted.
Does GDPR apply if I book from Europe? | Yes. If you are in the European Union, the GDPR gives you rights over your data, alongside Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, which applies to us as a Thai business. You can access, correct, delete or move your data and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
