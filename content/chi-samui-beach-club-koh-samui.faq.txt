What are Chi Samui's opening hours? | Chi Samui is open daily from about 11:00 to midnight, and hours can change on Thai public holidays. Daybeds fill early, so arrive near opening time at weekends and from December to April.
Is there an entry fee at Chi Samui? | No entry fee is charged on normal days. You pay for what you eat and drink, but book a sofa bed or daybed ahead in high season and ask whether a minimum spend applies before you sit down. Special events such as holiday DJ parties may be priced differently.
Can you swim in the sea at Chi Samui? | Not really. Bang Rak's sea is shallow and often murky, so the 25 m saltwater infinity pool with its swim-up bar is the swim. For clear sea, go to Choeng Mon, about 10 minutes east.
Is Chi Samui good for children? | By day, yes. It has a separate beachfront kids' pool, and the crowd is a mix of couples and families. There is no published age limit, but the music gets louder in the late afternoon, so families usually come at lunchtime.
Can you see the sunset from Chi Samui? | You see the sky colour over Koh Phangan, but the sun itself sets behind the Bophut headland, not into the sea. Sunset falls between about 17:56 (mid-November) and 18:45 (mid-July). For the sun dropping into the sea, go to the west coast.
What is on at Chi Samui at Christmas 2026? | Chi Samui has listed a Christmas Day "Slow Beach Life" afternoon on Friday 25 December 2026, from 15:30 to 21:30, with DJ BOSSO. No New Year's Eve plans had been announced by 2 November 2026.
How do I get to Chi Samui from Chaweng? | It is about 6–7 km and 10–15 minutes by car; Grab costs roughly 120–200 THB. From Fisherman's Village it is about 3 km and 5–10 minutes. There is parking on site, and we can arrange a driver for the trip back after dark.
Is Chi Samui close to the Koh Phangan piers? | Yes. The Big Buddha pier, where the Haad Rin Queen ferry leaves for Haad Rin at 10:30 and 17:00, is about an 8-minute walk away, and the other Bang Rak piers are a few minutes' drive. It makes a comfortable place to wait for a boat, but leave plenty of time.
Is Chi Samui part of a hotel? | The beach club is run by Kalara, a local property group with apartments on Bang Rak. A sister hotel, Chi Samui Resort, gives its guests a free shuttle to the beach club and food and drink discounts.
