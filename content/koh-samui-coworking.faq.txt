Can I work remotely from Koh Samui? | Yes, legally, on a Destination Thailand Visa or a Long-Term Resident visa, as long as you work only for employers and clients outside Thailand. Working on a 30-day visa exemption is not what it is for, and working for Thai companies or clients needs a work permit.
What is the DTV visa? | The Destination Thailand Visa is a 5-year multiple-entry visa allowing stays of up to 180 days per entry, for remote workers, freelancers and people on long courses such as Muay Thai. It costs 10,000 THB, needs at least 500,000 THB in funds and, since 31 August 2026, proof of residence where you apply and a criminal record certificate.
Is there coworking on Koh Samui? | Yes, but only a few spaces. Desk + Chair in Chaweng is open 24/7 with day passes of about 600 THB and monthly memberships of about 5,900 THB, and a resort-based space called The Exchange sells memberships from about 1,000 THB a month. Most nomads work from home or cafés.
Is the internet good on Koh Samui? | In the main areas, yes. AIS 3BB Fibre3, True Online and NT sell fibre plans up to 1 Gbps, and AIS and True run 5G in Chaweng, Bophut and other populated areas. Older villas can be slow, so ask for a speed test before you sign, and keep a backup SIM for storms.
What does it cost to live on Koh Samui per month? | Roughly 35,000–60,000 THB for one person in a studio, including rent of 12,000–30,000 THB, a scooter at 2,000–4,500 THB and a mobile plan at about 1,199 THB. A private pool villa starts at around 40,000 THB a month on its own.
Which area is best for digital nomads on Samui? | Bophut and Maenam for a calm base with cafés and fibre, Lamai for value and gyms, and Chaweng if you want to be near the main coworking space and nightlife. The west coast is beautiful but far from everything.
Do I pay Thai tax as a digital nomad? | You may. If you spend 180 days or more in Thailand in a calendar year you are a Thai tax resident, and foreign income earned since 2024 can be taxable when you bring it into Thailand. Treaties can give credits; take advice from a tax adviser.
Can I work for a Thai company on a DTV? | No. The DTV doesn't allow a Thai work permit, a job with a Thai company or freelance work for Thai clients. For that you need a Non-Immigrant B visa and a work permit sponsored by the Thai employer.
Can I apply for the DTV from Laos or Malaysia? | Not since 31 August 2026, unless you are a national or permanent resident there. Thai missions now accept DTV applications only from people who live permanently in the country where they apply.
