How much is an ATV tour on Koh Samui? | In 2026 a driver pays from 1,500 THB for 30 minutes or 1,800 THB for 1 hour, and a passenger from 900 THB. Two-hour rides with a waterfall cost 2,200–2,800 THB per driver, and the 2.5–4 hour routes with lunch cost 4,250–4,500 THB.
Do I need a driving licence for an ATV tour on Samui? | Not for the guided off-road tours, which run on private tracks: operators set a minimum age and give you a riding test on the practice circuit instead. One Maenam operator asks buggy drivers for a car driving licence.
Can children ride as passengers on a Koh Samui quad tour? | Yes. On the quad trail children from 5 ride as passengers with an adult, and on the Lipa Noi ride from 4. Children can drive their own quad from 11–12 on some tours and from 16 on others, so check the age rule on each product.
Is quad biking on Samui safe for beginners? | Yes, if you follow the guide. Every tour starts with a briefing and a practice lap, and the guide rides in front at a pace beginners can manage. Keep a full vehicle length from the rider ahead, and skip it if you are pregnant or have back, neck or heart problems.
What should I wear for an ATV ride on Koh Samui? | Closed shoes, clothes you don't mind getting muddy and sunglasses or the goggles provided. Wear swimwear underneath if the route includes a waterfall, and leave valuables at your hotel.
Do the ATV tours run in the rain? | Yes, they run all year, but heavy rain makes tracks slippery and operators may shorten the route or close trails after a stormy night. The wettest months are October to December; if a ride is cancelled for weather you choose a new date or a refund.
Does the ATV tour include transfers from my hotel? | Most do from the main beach areas: the 1–2 hour quad trail, the Maenam 2-hour tour and the 4-hour mountain tour include return transfer. The 30 and 45-minute Polaris rides and the Lipa Noi rides don't; a shared Lipa Noi transfer costs 200 THB per person.
Can I rent a quad bike for the road on Koh Samui? | We don't recommend it and don't arrange it. Rental vehicles in tourist areas are often unregistered for public roads, quads are unstable on steep wet hills, insurers rarely cover them and damage claims after road rentals are common.
Can I do an ATV ride and the zipline on the same day? | Yes. Take the 09:30 zipline session and the 14:00 quad ride, with lunch in Maenam between. Both include transfer from the main beach areas; message us on WhatsApp and we'll book both for you.
Which ATV tour is best for views on Koh Samui? | The longer hill routes: the 4-hour mountain tour from Maenam goes to the island's highest point, and the 1.5 and 2.5-hour Polaris rides add a 360-degree viewpoint and the Secret Buddha Garden.
