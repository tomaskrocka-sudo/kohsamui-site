Why go: the top of the island, under your own power

Samui's interior rises to about 635 m. This tour drives you as close to the top as the trails allow. From the base in Maenam, the 4-hour mountain route climbs through rubber and coconut plantations and rainforest to the highest viewpoint on the island, where on a clear day you can see Koh Phangan and the Ang Thong islands. The way down stops at a waterfall pool with time to swim, then at a jungle restaurant for a Thai buffet lunch.

The choice of vehicle changes the experience. A single ATV is the most hands-on. Double ATVs suit couples or a parent with a child. The CFMoto buggies have seatbelts and roll cages, and the 4-seat Z950 is the cheapest per person if you can fill it. The 1.5–2-hour beginner ride is for first-timers or anyone short of time; it skips the summit and lunch.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: adventurous couples and groups, families with children who can ride as passengers, and anyone staying in Maenam, Bophut or Bang Rak.
  • Choose something else if: you only want an hour of fun. The 1 or 2-hour quad ride is cheaper. If you'd rather not drive at all, the jeep safari is a gentler way to see the interior.
  • Not suitable: during pregnancy or with back or neck problems.

Good to know

  • Insurance: many policies exclude off-road vehicles. Check yours. Buggy drivers need a licence.
  • Dust and mud: the dry months (February to August) are dusty; October to December is muddy. Wear clothes you can write off.
  • Private tours: the private mountain tour costs about double but lets you set the pace, which is worth it for nervous riders.
