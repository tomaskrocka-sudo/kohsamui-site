Why go: a fun-focused catamaran with a slide and toys

Scarlet is a Privilege 43, a 14 m French-built sailing catamaran with three cabins, a large shaded deck and a water slide off the side. It is set up for an active day: paddle boards, snorkel gear and fishing rods are on board, and the anchor stops are chosen for clear, calm water. Hotel transfers on Samui are included, which is unusual for a private catamaran and saves a taxi to Bang Rak.

Morning trips sail to Koh Som off the north coast or to the small island of Koh Mat Lang near Thong Son Bay. The longer trips add more time at anchor and finish with the sunset.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: families with children, groups of friends and anyone who wants swimming and toys more than sightseeing.
  • Choose something else if you want to reach Ang Thong or Koh Tao: Scarlet stays close to Samui. See yacht charters for longer routes.
  • Large group? Scarlet takes 20, but above about 14 the per-head charge makes a bigger boat better value.

Good to know

  • Slide: used only at anchor and when the crew says the water is clear. Children should wear life jackets.
  • Snorkelling: the bays near Samui are shallow; don't stand on coral.
  • Weather: from October to December the north-east wind makes the north coast choppy.
  • Drinks: soft drinks are included; ask us about bringing your own alcohol.

Choosing a trip

The 2.5 hour sunset does not include the slide, so families should pick a half day or longer. The 6 and 8 hour trips combine the swim stops with the sunset sail home and are the best value per hour.
