Why go: the island behind the series, at your own pace

The third season of The White Lotus was filmed on Koh Samui in 2024, and many visitors now want to see the places from the screen. This private van tour strings the main ones into a relaxed day: temple and cultural grounds in the morning, the beach at Choeng Mon, lunch in Fisherman's Village in Bophut, the lotus pond and meditation salas of a luxury resort seen in the series, and finally the quieter west coast at Taling Ngam for the sunset.

Because it is private, you can stay longer where you like and skip what you don't. The guided option adds a licensed guide who can explain the temples and the filming.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: fans of the series, couples and small groups who want a comfortable sightseeing day in air conditioning.
  • Choose something else if you have not seen the show: a jungle safari covers more of the island's landscape for less money per person.
  • Want more time in Bophut? Spend the evening at the Friday walking street.

Good to know

  • Snake farm: the partner route includes an optional snake farm stop. We do not sell animal shows, so we ask the driver to use that hour elsewhere.
  • Resort access: the luxury resorts are private property. Book a table or afternoon tea ahead if you want more than a look from the public areas.
  • Dress: cover shoulders and knees at temples; resort bars expect smart-casual clothes.
  • Weather: the west coast is usually the calmer side for sunsets from October to December.
