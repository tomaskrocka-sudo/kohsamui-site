Christmas Eve (Thursday 24 December 2026) is also a Full Moon Party night on Koh Phangan, and New Year's Eve (Thursday 31 December) brings the NYE countdown party on Haad Rin, a free countdown with fireworks at Central Samui and the island's biggest beach fireworks in Chaweng, followed by Fisherman's Village, Lamai, Maenam and Choeng Mon. Resort galas cost from about 6,000 THB to 14,500++ THB per person, and galas and boats sell out by mid-December. Flights and ferries off the island are packed on 1–2 January.

December also has a few community events. The Samui Santa Run was held on Saturday 13 December in 2025; the 2026 date had not been announced by early November. Father's Day on 5 December is a public holiday marked with ceremonies at government offices, but it has no alcohol ban. There are no alcohol-ban days in December 2026, and alcohol can be sold from 11:00 to 24:00, with licensed venues and hotels open later.

Christmas on Samui

Christmas is not a Thai public holiday, and ordinary life carries on: markets, shops and tours run as normal. The big resorts decorate their lobbies from early December and run Christmas Eve dinners, Christmas Day brunches and family events, mostly for hotel guests but often open to outside bookings. Two examples already published for 2026 give the price range:

  • Explorar Koh Samui (Maenam): Christmas Day dinner on 25 December, 19:00–22:00, 3,000 THB net per person, a Thai sharing menu.
  • The large international resorts in Bophut, Chaweng and Choeng Mon typically charge more for their Christmas brunches and galas, usually with "++" prices.

"++" means 10 % service charge plus 7 % VAT on top, so a 5,000++ THB dinner costs 5,885 THB. If you want a Western Christmas lunch, book it by early December. If you would rather spend Christmas Day on the water, the sea is usually calming by late December and Ang Thong is open again from 16 December.

Where to see in the New Year

Fireworks go up from almost every beach at midnight. The biggest displays are on the biggest beaches, in roughly this order:

Chaweng and Central Samui

Chaweng has the island's biggest party. Central Samui, the mall opposite Chaweng Lake, held a free public countdown on NYE 2025 with live concerts, cultural shows and midnight fireworks, and we expect the same format in 2026 (the programme is usually announced in December). On Chaweng Beach, bars and beach clubs run their own countdowns, and fireworks are let off along the whole bay. Expect heavy traffic on Chaweng Beach Road all evening. See Chaweng and Koh Samui nightlife.

Fisherman's Village

Bophut's Fisherman's Village is the more upmarket alternative: beach-front restaurants with set dinners, DJs and fire shows, and fireworks over the bay with Koh Phangan on the horizon. NYE 2026 falls on a Thursday, so there is no Friday walking street, but the village is packed. Book dinner tables weeks ahead. See Fisherman's Village.

Lamai

Lamai has a smaller beach-road party, with bars and fireworks along the beach. It is busier than Maenam and calmer than Chaweng. See Lamai.

Maenam and Choeng Mon (family-friendly)

Maenam and Choeng Mon are the most family-friendly places for midnight: resort fireworks on calm beaches, no bar crowds, and easy to get home. Explorar Koh Samui in Maenam has published a 2026 countdown party with live music, DJs, fireworks and a lucky draw, alongside its NYE beachfront buffet (6,000 THB net). See Maenam and Choeng Mon.

Good to know: many Thais welcome the year at a temple, chanting with the monks through midnight (suad mon kham pee). If you pass a temple on 31 December, you may hear it; visitors can watch quietly from the back, dressed modestly.

Beach-club countdowns

The beach clubs publish their NYE plans in November and December. On NYE 2025, ARKbar on Chaweng Beach ran a countdown beach festival from 19:00 until late, with DJs, fire shows and fireworks, free before 21:00 and 500 THB after. Other 2025 events included a free-flow party at 79 Beach Club in Bang Rak and NYE nights at SEEN Beach Club (Avani Chaweng), Nikki Beach (Lipa Noi) and several five-star resorts. Expect similar events in 2026, with prices rising for tables and sunbeds near the fireworks. Tips:

  • Arrive before any free-entry cut-off (21:00 at ARKbar in 2025).
  • Book tables and daybeds in advance; minimum spends are common on NYE.
  • Check whether the ticket includes drinks ("free-flow") or only entry.

Full Moon Party at Christmas and New Year's Eve from Samui

Koh Phangan holds two Full Moon-style parties in the festive week: the Christmas Eve Full Moon Party on Thursday 24 December 2026, and the NYE countdown party on Haad Rin on Thursday 31 December. These are the busiest nights of the year on Haad Rin. Entry is about 200 THB in cash at the beach; we don't sell party entry.

  • Shared Full Moon Party speedboat: 1,400 THB per person, return, for 24 December, with hotel pickup from most areas of Koh Samui. Check the booking page for the 31 December boat.
  • Private Full Moon Party speedboat: up to 10 guests, your own times.

Book both nights by early December; on NYE public ferries and Haad Rin rooms sell out first. Remote areas (Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Bang Kao, Thong Krut) add 400 THB per person, return. See Full Moon Party from Koh Samui and all Full Moon Party dates.

NYE on the water

Watching midnight from a boat is possible but depends on availability, the sea state in late December and safe night navigation. Private charters on 31 December are booked early and priced individually. Message us on WhatsApp with your group size and where you are staying, and we will tell you honestly what is possible. For daytime celebrations, a private boat on 31 December or 1 January to Pig Island or, from 16 December, Ang Thong is easier to arrange. See yacht charters and private speedboats.

Weather at Christmas and New Year

December is the tail end of Samui's wet season: about 263 mm of rain over 12 days on average, against 445 mm in November. The second half of the month is usually drier and the sea calmer, and January averages 127 mm over 8 days. Highs are about 29 °C and the sea about 28 °C. Tropical storms are rare but possible in December and January (Storm Pabuk closed ferries in January 2019), so keep a day spare before your flight home.

Book-ahead checklist

  • Villas and hotels: peak-season rates apply from about mid-December to mid-January; book months ahead and check minimum stays and gala supplements.
  • NYE dinners and galas: popular parties and set dinners sell out by mid-December.
  • Full Moon boats for 24 and 31 December: book by early December.
  • Boat trips: Ang Thong speedboat (1,800 THB, from 16 December) and Pig Island speedboat (1,450 THB) fill up between Christmas and New Year.
  • Flights out on 1–2 January: Bangkok Airways flights were full after NYE 2025; book early or leave on 3 January.
  • Airport transfer: pre-book it for 1–2 January.

Getting home after midnight

Getting home is the hardest part of NYE on Samui. Songthaews mostly stop running by about 22:00. Taxis are negotiated (they don't use meters), are scarce after midnight and charge about 20–30 % more after 22:00, and much more on NYE. Grab works best in Chaweng, Bophut and Lamai, but expect long waits and surge prices around 00:30–02:00. Options:

  • Celebrate within walking distance of your hotel.
  • Pre-book a driver for a fixed pick-up time.
  • Agree a meeting point away from the main beach road, where traffic is stuck.
  • Never ride a scooter after drinking: police checkpoints are common over New Year, and the roads are at their most dangerous.
