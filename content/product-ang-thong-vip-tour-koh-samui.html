Why go: the Ang Thong VIP tour

The VIP tour visits the same places as every Ang Thong day trip, but with only 15 guests on a big speedboat. That one number changes the day. There is room to sit where you like and stretch out on the crossing, no queue for kayaks or snorkel gear, less waiting at each stop, and a guide who has time to answer questions, help nervous snorkellers and point out the dusky langurs on Koh Wua Talab. It is still a shared tour, not a private charter.

The park itself is 42 limestone islands about 30 km west of Koh Samui, around 102 km² in all, with the headquarters on Koh Wua Talab (Wua Ta Lap). You climb 15–20 minutes of steep wooden steps on Koh Mae Ko to look down on Thale Nai, the Emerald Lake, a saltwater lagoon enclosed by cliffs and linked to the sea through underwater tunnels. On Koh Wua Talab you choose between the 500 m viewpoint trail (25–30 minutes up, ropes near the top) and the beach. Kayaking (45–60 minutes when the sea allows) and a snorkelling stop, often around Koh Sam Sao, Koh Wao or Koh Tai Plao, fill the afternoon.

The timing matches the standard speedboat: pickup 07:30–08:30, departure at 09:00, about an hour's crossing, back on Samui at 16:00–16:30. A big speedboat with fewer people rides more comfortably than a full standard boat, but it is still a speedboat and still bounces in swell. You pay 4,500 THB instead of 1,800 THB for space and attention, not for different islands.

Who this tour is for

  • Couples and small groups marking an occasion who want a comfortable, personal day without paying for a whole boat.
  • Families with children, where a small group makes life jackets, snorkel help and toilet stops easier. Children aged 3–11 pay the child price and infants up to 2 travel free.
  • Photographers and slower walkers who don't want to be rushed at the viewpoints.
  • Anyone who dislikes crowded boats.

Who should choose another tour

  • On a budget: the standard speedboat tour (1,800 THB) or the big boat (1,500 THB) see the same sights.
  • Very prone to seasickness: the big boat is the steadiest ride.
  • Group of 10 or fewer who want the boat to themselves: compare the private speedboat at 33,000 THB per boat.
  • Want sunset and an empty park: the overnight bungalow trip.
  • Pregnant or with back problems: we don't advise an open-sea speedboat crossing.

What to bring

  • Cash for the park fee: 300 THB per foreign adult, 150 THB per child aged 3–14.
  • Swimwear, a towel and a change of clothes.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses with a strap.
  • Shoes with grip for the stairs and the viewpoint trail.
  • A dry bag for phone and camera.
  • Seasickness tablets if you need them.
  • Your passport number for the booking (accident insurance).

Good to know

  • Season: Ang Thong closes every year 1 November–15 December and reopens on 16 December. February to April is calmest; May to September is good most days; October is choppier and wetter. With only 15 seats per day and park quotas from December to February, VIP dates sell out first, so book ahead.
  • Weather: rain does not cancel; strong wind and waves do. If the captain cancels for safety you hear the evening before and choose a full refund or a new date. Check windy.com or our sea conditions page.
  • Ride: the smoothest seats on any speedboat are towards the back.
  • Mobility: the Mae Ko steps are steep and the Wua Talab trail has a rope section. Both are optional, and the beach stop needs no climbing.
  • Children and non-swimmers: children's life jackets are always on board and children wear them at all times on the boat. Snorkelling is optional and can be done in a life jacket.
  • Park rules: no swimming in the Emerald Lake, no fires, no feeding wildlife, no touching coral, rubbish back on the boat. Sunscreens with oxybenzone, octinoxate, 4-MBC or butylparaben are banned in Thai marine national parks.
  • Drones: national parks require prior permission from the Department of National Parks, plus CAAT/NBTC registration.
  • Special requests: celebrating something, or need vegetarian or allergy-safe food? Tell us when you book; vegetarian dishes are always available at lunch.

Pickup and meeting point

Hotel transfers are included from the main areas of Koh Samui: Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut, Maenam, Choeng Mon, Bang Rak and Plai Laem. Remote areas (Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Bang Kao, Thong Krut and similar) cost 400 THB per person return, added on the booking card. Pickup is 07:30–08:30, then check-in at the pier at 08:30–08:45 with a toilet stop, life-jacket fitting and safety briefing. Your exact pickup time and the pier location come by email and WhatsApp the day before. The VIP tour departs from Koh Samui only.
