Why go: a fast, comfortable private boat with a toilet

A twin-engine speedboat covers the water between Koh Samui and Koh Phangan in well under an hour, so even a half day leaves real time for swimming and beaches. The toilet and swim platform matter more than they sound: they make the boat a sensible choice for families and mixed groups who would struggle on an open boat all day.

The 2.5-hour sunset cruise is a good way to see the Samui coast from the water at the best time of day without committing to a full charter.

Which option to choose

The sunset cruise leaves at 16:00 and is back by 19:00, which suits couples and families who want a short private trip at the coolest time of day. The half day crosses to Koh Phangan for snorkelling and a beach stop and is back by early afternoon. The full day around Koh Samui lets you combine snorkelling, a beach lunch and quieter bays at your own pace. Two crew handle the boat, so the guide can help children and first-time snorkellers in the water.

Who it suits

  • Groups of 6–10 friends or family.
  • Families with young children who need a toilet on board.
  • Anyone who wants to plan the day around their own beaches.

Who should choose something else

  • Couples or small groups on a budget: a private longtail to Pig Island costs much less.
  • Anyone wanting Ang Thong: our Ang Thong private speedboat runs from 16 December.
  • Pregnant guests or those with back problems: speedboats bounce in chop.

Good to know

  • Sea by month: February to September is calmest. From November to January the north-east monsoon can make the crossing to Koh Phangan rough.
  • Seasickness: sit towards the back of the boat, where the motion is gentlest.
  • Booking: online booking for tomorrow closes at midnight Thai time.
