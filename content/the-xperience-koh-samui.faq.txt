What is The Xperience on Koh Samui? | An immersive art and theme park in the hills above Lipa Noi on the west coast, opened in early 2026. It has 12 themed zones with more than 100 artworks: giant hyper-realistic sculptures, a heart-shaped lagoon with a beach club, infinity pools, a light maze, a wellness village, and projection mapping and fire shows after dark.
How much is a ticket to The Xperience Koh Samui? | The Day Pass costs 1,500 THB per person, the same for adults and children aged 3 and over; children aged 0–2 go free. Permanent Samui residents pay 500 THB with proof of residence. The park also lists a VIP Day Pass at 6,000 THB with access to private areas.
What are The Xperience opening hours? | Wednesday to Sunday from 11:00 to 22:00, and on Fridays until midnight for The Xperience After Dark. The park is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays.
Can children go to The Xperience? | Yes, on Family Days: Thursday, Saturday and Sunday. Wednesdays and Fridays are 16+ only. Children must stay with a parent at all times, children 0–2 enter free, and from age 3 they pay the full Day Pass price.
What is The Xperience After Dark? | The Friday night edition: the park stays open until midnight with live DJs, illuminated sculptures, projection mapping, fire performers and sunset cocktails. It is 16+ only and the normal 1,500 THB Day Pass covers it.
How long do you need at The Xperience? | Plan 5–6 hours. The best plan is to arrive around 15:00, explore the zones and swim while it's light, then stay for sunset and the night light show, which most visitors call the highlight.
Where is The Xperience and how do I get there? | At 11/45 Moo 4, Lipa Noi, in the hills about 5 minutes by car from Nikki Beach and 40–50 minutes from Chaweng. Grab works by day but is scarce on the west coast at night, so a private driver who waits is the easiest way to stay for the night show.
Can I bring food or drinks into The Xperience? | No. Outside food and drink are not allowed. The Playa Beach Club, Mystic Café and the bars serve food and drinks inside the park, and Samui residents with the resident pass get 20% off.
What should I bring to The Xperience? | Swimwear and sunscreen for the pools and lagoon, comfortable shoes for the hillside paths, mosquito repellent for the evening, and your booking confirmation. Pets, drugs and nudity are not allowed.
Is there a dress code at The Xperience? | No. The park encourages guests to express themselves, and people in bold Burning Man-style costumes can ask for a free Costume Pass, which the door judges approve or refuse.
Is The Xperience worth it? | For an afternoon and evening, most visitors say yes: the sculptures, lagoon and night show are unlike anything else on Samui, and early reviews are very positive. The main complaints are the 1,500 THB entry and drink prices, so it is poor value for a quick one-hour visit.
