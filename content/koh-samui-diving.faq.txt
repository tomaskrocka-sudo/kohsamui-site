Is Koh Samui good for diving? | Samui is a good base but not a great dive destination in itself: its own reefs are shallow and modest. The good diving is a boat ride away at Sail Rock, about an hour north, and at the Koh Tao sites, 1.5–2 hours away.
How far is Sail Rock from Koh Samui? | Sail Rock lies in open water roughly halfway between Koh Phangan and Koh Tao, north of Samui. Dive boats from Samui's north-coast piers take about an hour, and a day trip with two dives runs from about 08:00 pickup to 16:00 drop-off.
How much is a Sail Rock dive trip from Koh Samui? | From about 4,650 THB per certified diver in 2026 for two dives, including equipment, dive insurance, lunch, soft drinks and transfers from the main areas. You need an Open Water certification or equivalent.
Should I learn to dive on Koh Samui or Koh Tao? | Koh Tao, if you can spend a few nights there: an Open Water course costs about 11,000–14,000 THB and the sites are minutes away. Starting from Samui with two nights on Koh Tao costs about 18,000 THB.
How long does an Open Water course take? | Usually 3–4 days: theory (which you can do online before you travel), confined-water skills in a pool or shallow bay, and four open-water dives. It certifies you to dive to 18 m with a buddy.
When is the best time to dive around Koh Samui? | February to October is the season, and March and April usually have the clearest water and calmest sea. October to December brings the north-east monsoon, rough crossings and visibility of 5–10 m.
Can you see whale sharks at Sail Rock? | Sometimes. Sail Rock and Chumphon Pinnacle are the best places in the Gulf to see one, but sightings are never guaranteed. Dive centres point to March–May, and the Koh Tao Tourism Association names June–September as the peak.
Is there a recompression chamber on Koh Samui? | A recompression service has been listed in Bophut, but check it with your dive centre on the day; the SSS hyperbaric network currently lists chambers on Koh Tao and in Phuket. In any dive emergency, give oxygen and call 1669 and your dive insurer.
How long after diving can I fly? | The Divers Alert Network recommends at least 12 hours after a single no-decompression dive and at least 18 hours after multiple dives or multiple days of diving. A normal two-dive day therefore means waiting 18–24 hours before your flight.
Can I dive in November near Koh Samui? | Yes, on Koh Tao's sheltered sites, but crossings from Samui are often cancelled and visibility drops to 5–10 m in the monsoon. Stay on Koh Tao rather than commuting, keep spare days, and remember Ang Thong is closed until 15 December.
Do I need dive insurance to dive from Samui? | Yes. Make sure your travel insurance covers scuba diving to the depth you plan, plus evacuation and recompression treatment, or buy specialist dive insurance. Many standard travel policies exclude diving or cap the depth.
