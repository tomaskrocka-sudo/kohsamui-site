Why go: a short, competitive burst of fun

Karting is one of the few things on Koh Samui that works in any weather window and at night. The two floodlit tracks and computer timing make it easy to turn a session into a family race or a contest between friends, and sessions are short enough to fit between a beach afternoon and dinner.

The range of karts is the main draw for families: 100 cc karts for children from 9, faster 160 cc karts for older children, and a two-seater so a parent can take a younger child round the track.

Which session to book

Eight minutes is enough for most first-timers and children: you learn the corners in the first laps and push for a time in the rest. The 16-minute session suits competitive adults and teenagers over 1.5 m who want to beat a lap time; it costs a little more than two short sessions but saves queuing again. For a younger child who can't drive yet, the two-seater kart lets a parent drive with them beside you. The track also has a bar and restaurant with a big screen, so non-drivers have somewhere to wait.

Who it suits

  • Families with children aged 9 and over, or younger children as passengers.
  • Groups who want an evening activity that doesn't involve a bar.
  • Anyone with an hour to fill on a rainy-season afternoon once the track is dry.

Who should choose something else

  • Anyone who wants a half-day outing with transport included: try the island sightseeing tour.
  • Thrill-seekers who want off-road riding: see the ATV and buggy tour.

Good to know

  • Height rules are strict: staff measure children at the desk; book the option that matches their height.
  • Getting there: taxis are expensive on Samui; agree the fare before you set off, or use a ride app.
  • Booking: online booking for tomorrow closes at midnight Thai time.
