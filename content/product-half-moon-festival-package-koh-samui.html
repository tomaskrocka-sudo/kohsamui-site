Why go: the Half Moon Festival from Koh Samui

The Halfmoon Festival is Koh Phangan's long-running electronic music festival, held about every two weeks in a jungle coconut grove at Ban Tai, on Phangan's south coast. The main event is the night in the Halfmoon Magic Forest, which runs from about 21:00 until sunrise on up to three stages: Prime Stage, G Floor and The Cave, with music from progressive and psytrance to house, techno, hip hop and R&B. Unlike the Full Moon Party on Haad Rin beach, it is a ticketed festival with a smaller, music-focused crowd and a purpose-built site.

From Samui, the problem is logistics: a ticket bought online, a boat across at night, a taxi from the pier to the forest and the same in reverse at 04:00. Our package puts all of it in one booking and one payment. We buy your festival ticket for you at the organiser's price and add the hotel pickup, the return speedboat and the taxis between Ban Tai pier and the forest. Packages start from 2,700 THB per person; the organiser's own 2026 Magic Forest ticket costs 2,500 THB online on its own.

There are two packages. Magic Forest Wonder Access (from 2,700 THB) is the standard festival pass with the boat and taxis. Magic Forest VIP Stand (5,700 THB) adds VIP access, the VIP zone and VIP restrooms, plus Halfmoon merchandise. Both are sold for the Magic Forest nights only; the beach-club day parties on the day before are not part of the package.

Who this tour is for

  • Music fans staying on Koh Samui who want a festival night without moving islands.
  • Groups who want everything paid in one go, with no ticket queue or taxi haggling at Ban Tai.
  • Guests aged 20 and over with ID.

Who should choose another tour

  • Want the beach party with no ticket and flexible return times: the Full Moon Party speedboat, with returns from 01:00 to 06:00.
  • Want to leave before 04:00 or stay after sunrise: the package has one return boat only; book a private speedboat to Koh Phangan or a private party boat on Full Moon nights.
  • Under 20: the festival does not admit you.
  • Not sure of your date: tickets can't be refunded once issued, so wait until your plans are fixed.

Stay safe

  • Watch your drinks and never leave them unattended.
  • No drugs or illegal substances: the organiser refuses entry, and Thai penalties are severe. See our drugs and alcohol law guide.
  • Stay with your group, agree a meeting point, and be at the taxi point for 03:45.
  • Carry water; the forest is hot and humid even at night.
  • Wear the life jacket on the boat, and keep your phone charged and in a waterproof pouch.

What to bring

  • Your passport or ID. The name must match the name on your festival ticket; the organiser also accepts a photo of the ID.
  • Cash for drinks and food at the festival.
  • Closed shoes: the forest floor is uneven and dark.
  • Insect repellent and a light layer for the 04:00 boat.
  • A waterproof pouch for your phone and a power bank.

Good to know

  • Tickets are personal and non-refundable once issued: names can't be changed and tickets can't be transferred. Book only when your date is certain. If we cancel the boat because of weather or sea conditions, the whole package, including the ticket, is refunded in full.
  • One return boat: it leaves Ban Tai pier at 04:00 and cannot wait. If you miss it, you arrange and pay for your own way back to Samui.
  • Weather: December falls in the north-east monsoon, and a rough night can cancel the crossing. Check windy.com and our sea conditions page the day before.
  • Entry rules: guests must be 20 or older. Entry can be refused for damaged, fake or unauthorised tickets.
  • Not included: the beach-club day party at Harmony Beach Club the day before (about 15:00–24:00), which is ticketed separately by the organiser.

Pickup and meeting point

Hotel pickup and return drop-off by taxi are included from Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut, Maenam, Bang Rak and Bang Po. Remote areas such as Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam and Laem Sor carry a surcharge per person, shown on the booking card. We can't collect from hotels or villas up on the mountain roads; we send a nearby meeting point instead. The taxi collects you between 21:00 and 21:30 and takes you to Bang Rak pier for the 22:00 speedboat, which reaches Ban Tai pier at about 22:30; a taxi then takes you to the Magic Forest. On the way home, the taxi leaves the festival at 03:45 for the 04:00 boat, and a taxi takes you from the Samui pier back to your hotel. We confirm your exact pickup time and send your ticket on WhatsApp after booking.
