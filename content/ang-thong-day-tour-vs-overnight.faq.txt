Can you stay overnight in Ang Thong Marine Park? | Yes, on Koh Wua Talab, the park headquarters island, in a park bungalow or a tent. Our overnight trips from Koh Samui cost 3,600 THB (tent) or 4,200 THB (bungalow) per adult and include the boats, the day tour and the accommodation.
What is included in the Ang Thong overnight tour? | Hotel transfers from most areas on both days, the speedboat out and back, the full day 1 tour with guide, kayaks and snorkel gear, day 1 Thai buffet lunch, one night in a tent or bungalow, life jackets and basic accident insurance. The park fee and other meals are extra.
Tent or bungalow: which should I choose? | The bungalow (4,200 THB) has beds, a fan, bedding, towels and very clean bathrooms; the tent (3,600 THB) comes with a sleeping pad, sleeping bag, blanket, pillow, mosquito net and torch. Choose the bungalow for comfort and the tent for a cheaper night under canvas.
Is there electricity and a shower on Koh Wua Talab? | Yes, but basic. Electricity runs until 21:00, there is no air-conditioning or hot water, and Wi-Fi is limited. Bring a power bank and a torch for the sunrise climb.
Is the Ang Thong overnight trip suitable for children? | Yes, for children who can manage the viewpoint stairs and sleep without air-conditioning. Infants aged 0–2 travel free, children aged 3–11 pay the child price (3,400 THB tent, 3,900 THB bungalow), and children's life jackets are always provided and worn on the boat.
What is the cancellation policy for the Ang Thong overnight tour? | Full refund if you cancel 48 hours or more before departure; no refund under 48 hours or for a no-show. If we cancel because the sea is unsafe on either day, you choose a full refund or a new date.
When can I do the Ang Thong overnight tour? | Any date from 16 December to 31 October; the park is closed from 1 November to 15 December. February to April has the calmest sea, and December to February is peak season, so book bungalows early.
