Is November a good time to visit Koh Samui? | It depends on your plans. November is the wettest month (about 445 mm over 16 rain days) and Ang Thong is closed, so it is a poor choice for a beach-and-boats holiday. It is good for low prices, quiet roads, green scenery, Pig Island trips and Loy Krathong with the Full Moon Party on 24 November 2026.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in November? | About 445 mm on 1991–2020 averages (one dataset gives 463 mm), over about 16 days with 1 mm or more. That is roughly seven times February's rain; the rain usually comes in heavy bursts, and about half the days stay dry.
~Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in November? | No. Ang Thong closes every year from 1 November to 15 December and reopens on 16 December. In November we recommend the Pig Island speedboat tour (1,450 THB), which runs on any date, or the Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan day trip (2,200 THB) when the sea allows.
What boat trips run in November? | Pig Island and Koh Tan tours from Thong Krut (speedboat 1,450 THB, sunset 1,550 THB, private boats), the Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan speedboat trip (2,200 THB) sea permitting, and the Full Moon Party speedboat (1,400 THB) on 24 November. All run subject to safe seas, with a full refund or new date if we cancel.
When is Loy Krathong on Koh Samui in 2026? | Tuesday 24 November 2026, the full moon of the 12th lunar month, which is also the Full Moon Party night. On Samui, people float krathongs at Chaweng Lake, Big Buddha, beaches and hotel ponds; sky lanterns are banned, so choose a bread or banana-leaf krathong.
Can I go to the Full Moon Party from Samui in November? | Yes, on Tuesday 24 November 2026. Our shared return speedboat costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup and drop-off on Samui and runs subject to safe seas; party entry (about 200 THB) is paid at the gate in Haad Rin.
Is the sea rough in November? | On the east coast, often. The north-east monsoon brings surf, rip currents and frequent red flags to Chaweng and Lamai, and crossings to Koh Phangan and Koh Tao are choppier, with some sailings cancelled in strong wind. If swimming matters, choose a hotel with a pool or a sheltered bay such as Choeng Mon, and never swim when red flags are up.
Is December better than November? | Yes, especially after mid-month. December averages about 263 mm of rain against about 445 mm in November, Ang Thong reopens on 16 December, and the sea improves through the month. Prices rise sharply from about 20 December for Christmas and New Year.
