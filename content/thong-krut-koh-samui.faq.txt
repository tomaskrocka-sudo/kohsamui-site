Where is Thong Krut pier? | Thong Krut pier is on Koh Samui's south-west coast in Taling Ngam subdistrict, around GPS 9.4207, 99.9549, about 13 km from Lamai and 24 km from Chaweng; several operators use separate jetties, so ask for your boat's exact pin.
How do I get to Thong Krut from Chaweng or Lamai? | Drive the ring road south-west past Hua Thanon and turn towards the coast for Thong Krut: about 20–25 minutes from Lamai and 35–40 minutes from Chaweng; a taxi costs roughly 350–500 THB from Lamai and 500–750 THB from Chaweng.
Do your Pig Island tours pick up from my hotel? | Yes, hotel pickup is included from most areas of Koh Samui; from remote areas such as Thong Krut, Bang Kao, Taling Ngam, Lipa Noi and Nathon there is a 400 THB per person return supplement.
Can I hire a longtail at Thong Krut myself? | Yes, boatmen at the pier charter longtails to Koh Madsum and Koh Tan; expect roughly 3,500–6,000 THB per boat for a private half day in 2026, and check life jackets and what is included before you pay.
Is there parking at Thong Krut pier? | Yes, there is free parking by the pier for cars and scooters; don't block boat ramps or village houses, and lock helmets to your bike.
Is Thong Krut worth visiting without a tour? | It is worth an hour for the fishing harbour, a seafood lunch and the late-afternoon view of Koh Tan, combined with Laem Sor Pagoda and Taling Ngam, but the beach itself is tidal and not for swimming.
How long is the boat ride from Thong Krut to Pig Island? | About 15–30 minutes depending on the boat: speedboats take around 15 minutes and wooden longtails up to about 30.
Is there a fee to land on Pig Island? | Yes, Koh Madsum charges a landing fee of about 50 THB per person, paid in cash on the island where it isn't included in your trip.
Do boats leave Thong Krut in November? | Yes, the south-west coast is sheltered from the north-east monsoon and Pig Island trips run all year, but any boat can be cancelled on a rough day, and November is the wettest month.
Can I swim at Thong Krut beach? | Not really: the bay is shallow and tidal, with wide flats at low tide and moored boats near the pier, so swim at Koh Madsum or Koh Tan on a boat trip instead.
Is there an ATM at Thong Krut pier? | No, bring cash for the Koh Madsum fee and drinks; the nearest reliable ATMs are on the ring road towards Lamai or Nathon.
Is the Samui Snake Farm near Thong Krut open? | We could not confirm any current opening hours or prices in 2026, so we don't list it as open and don't recommend snake shows.
