What is the best area to stay in Koh Samui? | For most first-time visitors, Chaweng: the most restaurants, beach clubs and hotels, a good swimming beach from January to September and a 10–15 minute ride from the airport. Choose Choeng Mon or Chaweng Noi for quiet, Bophut for food, Lamai for value and the west coast for sunsets.
Is Chaweng or Lamai better? | Chaweng is busier, with more restaurants and nightlife and a shorter airport transfer (10–15 minutes). Lamai has similar sand, fewer people and lower prices (about 1,500–3,500 THB a night mid-range) but is 25–30 minutes from the airport and quieter at night. Both get red flags in the October–December monsoon.
Where should families with young children stay on Koh Samui? | Choeng Mon, for one of the calmest bays on the island, soft sand and a 5–10 minute airport transfer, or Lipa Noi on the west coast, which is very shallow and calm at high tide. North Chaweng is a good compromise if you want restaurants nearby.
Where should I stay on Koh Samui for a honeymoon? | A resort or pool villa in Choeng Mon or Chaweng Noi for a swimming beach, Bophut for dinners in Fisherman's Village, or a west-coast villa in Lipa Noi or Taling Ngam for privacy and sunsets. Luxury resorts start around 10,000 THB a night.
Which Koh Samui beaches are best for swimming? | North and Central Chaweng, central Lamai, Choeng Mon, Chaweng Noi and Silver Beach, all on the east and north-east coasts, from January to September. North and west-coast beaches such as Maenam and Lipa Noi are tidal, so swim there at high tide.
Where should I stay for the Full Moon Party? | Near Big Buddha and the Bangrak piers in the north-east if you plan to use public boats, because the Haad Rin Queen to Haad Rin leaves from Big Buddha pier. With our Full Moon Party speedboat (1,400 THB return, hotel pickup and drop-off on Samui) you can stay anywhere on the island.
Is Bophut beach good for swimming? | Not really. The sand is narrow and the water is often cloudy, so Bophut is best for dining, the Friday walking street and sunset drinks. For swimming, Choeng Mon and Chaweng are about 15 minutes away.
What is the cheapest area to stay on Koh Samui? | Maenam, with mid-range beachfront rooms from about 1,000–2,500 THB a night and many monthly rentals, followed by Lamai (about 1,500–3,500 THB). Budget guesthouses back from the beach in Chaweng start around 500–950 THB.
How far is each area from the airport? | Approximately: Bang Rak 5 minutes, Choeng Mon 5–10, Chaweng and Bophut 10–15, Maenam 20–30, Lamai 25–30, the south and Nathon 35–45, and the west coast (Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam) 40–50 minutes by car outside rush hour.
