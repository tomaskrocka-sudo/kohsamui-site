Is the water at Bophut Beach clean enough to swim? | We don't recommend swimming there. The water is often cloudy, an outflow is reported near the eastern entrance of Fisherman's Village and jellyfish are reported. Some visitors swim without problems, but Choeng Mon, 15 minutes east, is calmer and clearer.
Why do some guides say Bophut is fine for swimming? | The bay is calm, warm and gently sloping, so it looks ideal, and some guides describe it that way. Our advice is based on water clarity, the reported outflow, jellyfish reports and the shallow, tidal bottom. We'd rather you swim at Choeng Mon or Chaweng and dine at Bophut.
Can you smoke on Bophut Beach? | No. Ao Bo Phut has been one of Thailand's 24 no-smoking beaches since February 2018, with fines of up to 100,000 THB, up to a year in prison or both. Vapes are illegal everywhere in Thailand.
How long is Bophut Beach? | About 3 km, from the headland towards Maenam in the west to the point before Bang Rak in the east, with Fisherman's Village in the middle. The sand is narrow and slightly coarse, with a few pebbles.
Can you see the sunset from Bophut Beach? | Bophut faces north towards Koh Phangan, so you see the sky colour over the island rather than the sun dropping into the sea. Sunset falls between about 17:55 and 18:45 depending on the month; for a sea sunset go west to Bang Por, Lipa Noi or Taling Ngam.
Is Bophut Beach good to stay on without swimming in the sea? | Yes, if food and atmosphere matter more than the beach. Most Bophut hotels have pools, you can walk to dozens of restaurants and the airport is 10–15 minutes away. Mid-range rooms cost about 2,500–5,000 THB a night in the shoulder season.
