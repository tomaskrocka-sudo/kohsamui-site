Why go: a small, friendly catamaran for the southern islands

Nina is a 38 ft sailing catamaran based on Samui's south coast, close to Koh Tan, Koh Madsum and Koh Rap. With a crew of three, paddle boards, a dinghy and a slide, it is a relaxed, family-friendly way to spend a day around the southern islands, and its sunset sail is one of the more affordable private catamaran trips on the island.

What you'll do

The sunset sail anchors off Koh Tan for a swim and sails back as the light fades. Half days and 6-hour trips combine Koh Tan's reef with Pig Island. The 8-hour day adds Koh Rap for swimming and fishing before a sunset sail home. Snacks and soft drinks are on board; bring or order lunch.

Who it suits

  • Families with children, who love the slide and paddle boards.
  • Couples and groups of up to 8 wanting a private sailing day without a big budget.

Who should choose something else

  • Groups larger than 15: see Kia Ora or Serenity.
  • Two people who just want the pigs and the reef: our private longtail or the shared Pig Island speedboat tour cost far less.

Good to know

  • Pigs: watch and photograph them but don't chase or lift them, and don't feed them junk food.
  • Sea: the southern islands are more sheltered in the windy months of October to December than routes to the north.
  • Fees: the Koh Madsum fee is about 50 THB per adult, in cash.

Before you book

Choose a trip and tell us the number of guests and children. If you want lunch on board, we can arrange food or you can bring a picnic. For groups above 8 we add the extra-guest charge before you pay.
