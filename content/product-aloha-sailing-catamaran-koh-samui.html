Why go: a real sailing day for a small group

Aloha is a 9.5 m sailing catamaran: small, simple and very much a sailing boat rather than a floating party. There is a sun deck, shade and a crew of three, but no cabins and no air-conditioning. The upside is the price. It is one of the cheapest private sails on Samui, and with 8 guests or fewer you have the whole deck.

Aloha moors on a river on the south-west coast near Lipa Noi, which puts Koh Tan and Pig Island (Koh Madsum) a short sail away. A typical half day covers snorkelling at Koh Tan, beach time on Koh Madsum and an hour of fishing, with sashimi if anything bites. The afternoon sail turns west for the sunset, and the full day does both at a slower pace.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: couples and small families who want to sail, anglers, and guests staying in Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Nathon or the south.
  • Choose something else if: you need cabins, a toilet with shade or air-conditioning. The Elizabeth has two air-conditioned cabins. If you're staying on the north coast, the Winner sails from Bang Rak.

Good to know

  • Sun: there is limited shade. Pack hats, long sleeves and reef-safe sunscreen.
  • Season: the south-west coast stays sheltered in the north-east monsoon (October to December), so this is one of the more reliable sailing trips at that time of year.
  • Getting there: from Chaweng the mooring is about 45–60 minutes by road. Book the van or a taxi.
  • Fishing: catches aren't guaranteed, but the crew know the spots around Koh Tan. Anything you land can be eaten fresh as sashimi on board.
