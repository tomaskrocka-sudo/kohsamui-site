Is the Big Buddha on Koh Samui free? | Yes. Entry to Big Buddha (Wat Phra Yai) is free; donation boxes on the platform fund the temple, and stalls at the base sell flowers, candles and incense for offerings.
What are the Big Buddha's opening hours? | Big Buddha is open every day from 07:00 to 18:30; arrive by 17:30 if you want time on the platform before it closes.
What should I wear to the Big Buddha? | Cover your shoulders and knees, avoid beachwear and take your shoes off on the upper platform; sarongs are sold at the stalls at the foot of the causeway.
How tall is the Big Buddha on Koh Samui? | Heights of 12 m and 15 m both circulate and we have not found an official measurement, so we don't quote one; it dominates the headland and is easy to spot from the Koh Phangan boats.
How do I get to the Big Buddha from Chaweng? | It is about 7 km and 15–20 minutes north via the airport road and Route 4171; a Grab costs roughly 150–250 THB and a taxi 400–600 THB, agreed before you set off.
Can you visit the Big Buddha at sunset? | Yes, but the temple closes at 18:30, so arrive by about 17:30; the best sunset silhouette is from Bang Rak beach just west of the causeway.
Is Wat Plai Laem near the Big Buddha? | Yes, Wat Plai Laem is about 1 km east, a 3-minute drive; most visitors see both in about two hours.
Where is the Big Buddha pier? | The pier for the Haad Rin Queen boats to Koh Phangan is just west of the causeway; crossings take about 30–50 minutes and timetables change by season, so check the day before.
How many steps are there up to the Big Buddha? | We have not published a step count yet; it is one naga staircase, a steady climb of a few minutes with no lift, and the causeway and base are flat.
Is Big Buddha busy at Loy Krathong? | Yes. On Tuesday 24 November 2026 families float krathongs at the causeway from early evening, parking fills from about 18:00, and sky lanterns are banned on Samui.
Is there parking at the Big Buddha? | There is a car park and roadside space at the foot of the causeway; it fills on weekend evenings when the approach road becomes a small market, so a scooter or Grab is easier then.
