Where is Laem Yai on Koh Samui? | Laem Yai is the far north-west tip of the island, at GPS 9.5696, 99.9163, about 5 km north of Nathon and 22 km west of Chaweng by road.
Can you swim at Laem Yai beach? | Only at high tide: at low tide the sea retreats over rocks and stony flats, while at high tide the water is clear enough for a swim and some snorkelling; wear water shoes.
Is Laem Yai a good sunset spot? | Yes, it faces west over open sea, so on clear evenings the sun sets into the water, between about 17:55 in November and 18:45 in July.
Is Laem Yai where The White Lotus was filmed? | Much of season 3 was filmed at the Four Seasons Resort Koh Samui on Laem Yai; the resort is private, so see the coast from the public shore or from a boat, and contact the resort directly about visiting.
Can I walk along the beach in front of the Four Seasons at Laem Yai? | Much of the Laem Yai frontage is private resort land, so stay on the public shore and lanes, don't enter resort grounds without permission, and see that stretch from the water instead.
Is there parking at Laem Yai? | There is no public car park, only limited roadside space on public lanes; never park in resort driveways, and a scooter is easier than a car.
Are there restaurants at Laem Yai? | Only resort restaurants and a small minimart; Bang Por's seafood restaurants are 3 km east, and Nathon's night food market (16:30–22:00) is 5 km south.
How do I get back from Laem Yai at night? | Grab is weak on the west coast after dark, so book a return taxi in advance or come by scooter, riding slowly on the steep, partly unlit hill road.
Do your tours pick up from Laem Yai? | Yes, we pick up from Laem Yai hotels and villas; as for nearby Nathon, the remote west-coast supplement of 400 THB per person return applies.
Is Laem Yai good for snorkelling? | It is fair at high tide, with clear water and small fish around the rocks and no rentals on the shore; for real reefs take a boat to Koh Tan or Ang Thong.
Is Laem Yai calm in November? | The west side is sheltered from the big north-east monsoon waves that hit Chaweng and Lamai, so the sea is often calmer, but November is still the wettest month with about 445 mm of rain.
Laem Yai or Lipa Noi for a sunset? | Laem Yai is closer to the north coast and quieter but rocky; Lipa Noi has flat sand, wide low-tide reflections and shallow water for children, about 10 minutes further south of Nathon.
