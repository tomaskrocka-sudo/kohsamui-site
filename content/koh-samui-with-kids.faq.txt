Is Koh Samui good for families with young children? | Yes. It has direct flights, private hospitals with paediatric departments, calm bays such as Choeng Mon, and short boat trips like Pig Island that suit toddlers. Go between February and September for the calmest sea, and avoid November if beach time matters most.
What is the best boat trip on Koh Samui for kids? | The Pig Island and Koh Tan speedboat tour: a 15–30 minute crossing from Thong Krut, pigs on the beach, shallow snorkelling and a meal, all in 4–5 hours door to door. It costs 1,450 THB per adult and 1,250 THB per child and runs every day of the year, weather permitting.
Do children pay full price on your tours? | No. On our shared boat trips children aged 3–11 pay the child rate (for example 1,250 THB on the Pig Island speedboat and 1,100 THB on the Ang Thong big boat), and infants aged 0–2 travel free. Private boats are priced per boat, so children cost nothing extra up to the boat's limit.
Are life jackets provided for children? | Yes. Children's life jackets are provided on every one of our boats, in sizes for toddlers and older children, and children must wear them on the boat. Tell us your children's ages when you book, especially for babies and toddlers.
~Is there an ethical elephant sanctuary on Koh Samui? | Yes, several sanctuaries let you feed and watch elephants with no riding, no bathing and no shows, and those are the only elephant visits we sell. Half-day visits cost about 3,000 THB per adult and 1,500–1,700 THB per child in 2026, with hotel transfer from the main areas.
Which attractions on Koh Samui are closed? | We could not confirm that Samui Aquarium and Tiger Zoo, Samui Butterfly Garden, Coco Splash waterpark, Samui Snake Farm or Namuang Safari Park were open in 2026, and Overlap Stone 2 was reported closed in July 2026. Phone before you go and don't prepay a driver for them.
Is there a children's hospital on Koh Samui? | There is no separate children's hospital, but Bangkok Hospital Samui near Chaweng Noi has a paediatric department and a 24-hour emergency room (+66 77 429 500, or 1719 from a Thai phone). The other private hospitals and the government hospital near Nathon also run children's clinics.
Is November OK for a family trip? | Only if you are flexible. November is Samui's wettest month (about 445 mm), the east-coast beaches often fly red flags and Ang Thong is closed until 16 December. Prices are low, and Pig Island trips still run on most days when the sea allows.
Can we take a baby on a speedboat? | Infants travel free and we provide small life jackets, but speedboats bounce in a chop. With a baby, choose the Pig Island speedboat on a calm morning (the crossing is only 15–30 minutes), or a private longtail or catamaran, which move more gently. Ask your doctor before boat trips in pregnancy.
Do taxis on Koh Samui have child seats? | Rarely. Taxis and Grab cars on Samui almost never carry child seats, so bring a lightweight travel seat or booster, or book a private van and ask for a seat in advance. Never ride a scooter with children.
