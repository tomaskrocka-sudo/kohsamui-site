What day is the Fisherman's Village market? | Friday is the main Walking Street night, filling the whole village from about 17:00 to 23:00. Smaller markets run on Monday and Wednesday evenings, the Free Wave Market by the village sign opens daily 16:00–24:00, and the Samui Green Market runs on the last Sunday of the month.
What time does the Friday walking street start? | Stalls open from about 17:00 and run until about 23:00, and the beach road closes to traffic from about 16:30. Crowds peak between 19:00 and 20:30, so arrive by 17:30–18:00 to park and eat in comfort.
Where do I park for the Friday market? | At The Wharf car park at the west end (about 20 THB for a scooter, 40 THB or more for a car) or in the signed paid lots on the lanes from the ring road. On Fridays The Wharf is full after about 18:00, and scooters left on the street are towed (500 THB or more to release).
Can you swim in front of Fisherman's Village? | We don't recommend it. The water is often cloudy, an outflow is reported near the village's eastern entrance and jellyfish are reported. Swim at Choeng Mon or Chaweng and come to Bophut for dinner and the view.
Is Fisherman's Village worth visiting on a non-market night? | Yes. The restaurants, beach bars and Coco Tam's fire shows (about 19:15–19:30 and 21:00) run every night, and it is easier to get a table and park. Many people prefer a quiet Tuesday or Thursday dinner here to the Friday crowds.
How far is Fisherman's Village from Chaweng? | About 9 km, or 15–20 minutes by car outside rush hour. Grab costs roughly 150–250 THB; a songthaew costs about 40–50 THB for locals and 60–80 THB is a fair tourist price, but songthaews mostly stop by 20:00–22:00.
Is Fisherman's Village good for families? | Yes for an evening: the street is walkable, the market has plenty of cheap food and the beach is fine for playing on. On Fridays it is very crowded for pushchairs, so families often come on Monday or Wednesday instead.
Are there boats from Bophut to Koh Phangan? | The scheduled ferries to Koh Phangan and Koh Tao leave from the Bang Rak piers about 10 minutes east, and the Haad Rin Queen from the Big Buddha pier. A private speedboat can collect you from the Bophut area by arrangement.
Is Fisherman's Village busy at New Year? | Very. It has the second-biggest fireworks on Samui after Chaweng, and the beach and street fill up from early evening on 31 December. Book dinner well ahead, and come by Grab or taxi because parking is almost impossible.
