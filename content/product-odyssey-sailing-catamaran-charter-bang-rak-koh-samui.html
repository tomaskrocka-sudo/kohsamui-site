Why go: a roomy sailing catamaran with your own drinks on board

Odyssey is a 44 ft Shuttleworth sailing catamaran with three cabins and a crew of three or four, kept at Bang Rak near the Big Buddha. It suits groups who want space and stability rather than speed: up to 18 guests on day trips, with paddle boards, snorkel gear, fishing rods and a dinghy for going ashore. You can bring your own wine or beer with no corkage charge, which keeps the cost of a celebration down.

Short trips stay along Samui's north coast. The full day sails across to Koh Phangan for lunch, snorkelling and paddling, and the overnights anchor off Koh Phangan or inside Ang Thong Marine Park, with two air-conditioned double cabins.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: families, groups of friends and couples who want a calm, social day with their own drinks.
  • Choose something else if you want to reach Koh Tao or cover several islands in a day: a private speedboat is two or three times faster.
  • Just want a sunset? Compare options in our sunset cruise guide.

Good to know

  • Sailing: catamarans motor when there is too little wind. Expect a mix of sailing and engine.
  • Ang Thong: the park closes from 1 November to 15 December; day trips there carry a surcharge for fuel and crew time.
  • Cabins: two doubles have air conditioning, the single has a fan.
  • Weather: from October to December the boat stays on sheltered routes and may board by dinghy from Bophut.

Choosing the right trip

The sunset trip is a short sail with drinks and no water toys. Half days include snorkelling, paddle boarding and fishing at anchor and suit families. The full day to Koh Phangan is the best value if your group wants a long lunch ashore. The overnights are for up to six and work well for couples or two families sharing.
