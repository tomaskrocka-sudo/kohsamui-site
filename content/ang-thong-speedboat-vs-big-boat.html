Book the speedboat (1,800 THB, about 8 hours) for a shorter day with the same stops and an earlier return. Book the big boat (1,500 THB, about 10 hours) for a slower, steadier ride with shade and room to move, which suits anyone prone to seasickness, pregnant or with back problems. The VIP tour (4,500 THB) takes only 15 guests on a big speedboat, and a private speedboat costs 33,000 THB per boat. Every option adds the park fee of 300 THB per adult (150 THB per child) in cash, and the park is closed from 1 November to 15 December.

The verdict: who should pick which

  • Pick the speedboat if you want the classic day in the least time: back at your hotel by about 17:00, with an hour's crossing each way.
  • Pick the big boat if you get seasick, are pregnant, have back or neck problems, are travelling with grandparents, or want the lowest price. Accept a later finish (hotel 18:00–19:00).
  • Pick the VIP for a couple's day or a special occasion: only 15 guests on a big speedboat, more space, more guide time and quicker landings.
  • Pick a private speedboat for a family or group of six or more, for toddlers who need their own pace, or to choose your own stops and start time.

All four include a guide speaking English and Thai, drinking water and soft drinks, life jackets for every age, and basic accident insurance for ages 3 months to 75 (we need a passport number at booking). Infants aged 0–2 travel free. The big boat crossing time and return times come from our current published schedule and vary with the sea.

The day on each boat

Both shared boats visit the same places. The difference is how long you spend getting there, and so what time you get home. Times are approximate, and the captain may reorder the stops for tide, wind and crowds.

Speedboat timeline

  1. 07:30–08:30 Hotel pickup from most areas of Koh Samui.
  2. 08:30–08:45 Pier check-in, toilets, life jackets and safety briefing.
  3. 09:00 Depart; about 1 hour to the park.
  4. ~10:00 Park entry; pay the park fee in cash.
  5. 10:15–11:15 Koh Mae Ko and the Emerald Lake platform (15–20 minutes up wooden stairs).
  6. 11:30–12:30 Koh Wua Talab: viewpoint climb (25–30 minutes up) or beach.
  7. 12:30–13:30 Thai buffet lunch.
  8. 13:30–14:30 Kayaking, sea permitting.
  9. 14:30–15:15 Snorkelling.
  10. 15:30 Leave the park.
  11. 16:00–16:30 Back at the pier; hotel drop-off 16:30–17:30.

Big boat timeline

  1. 07:30–08:30 Hotel pickup from most areas of Koh Samui.
  2. 08:30–08:45 Pier check-in and safety briefing.
  3. 09:00 Depart; about 1.5 hours to the park.
  4. ~10:30 Park entry; pay the park fee in cash.
  5. 10:45–11:45 Koh Mae Ko and the Emerald Lake platform.
  6. 12:00–13:00 Thai buffet lunch, with vegetarian dishes.
  7. 13:00–14:30 Koh Wua Talab: viewpoint climb or beach.
  8. 14:30–15:30 Kayaking, sea permitting.
  9. 15:30–16:15 Snorkelling and swimming.
  10. 16:30 Leave the park.
  11. ~18:00 Back at the pier; hotel drop-off 18:00–19:00.

The big boat spends about as long in the park as the speedboat. What it adds is an extra hour on the water and a later evening. The VIP follows the speedboat timeline, and a private boat can start earlier to reach the Emerald Lake before the day boats.

Seasickness and sea conditions

February to April is usually calm and both boats are comfortable. October and the weeks after the reopening on 16 December can bring north-east swell, and January has some windy days. On those days the speedboat slams over the waves for most of the crossing, while the big boat rolls more slowly. If you know you get seasick, the big boat is the better choice, even though you are on the water longer.

[kp_sea_now]
  • Take a seasickness tablet 30–60 minutes before departure.
  • On the speedboat, sit towards the back, where the motion is smallest. On the big boat, sit low and near the middle.
  • Watch the horizon and keep your phone in your bag on the crossing.
  • Eat a light breakfast and drink water.

We cancel if the sea is unsafe, and you choose a full refund or a new date. For today's waves and how we decide, see Koh Samui sea conditions.

Kids, pregnancy and mobility

Our age bands are the same on every boat: adults 12+, children 3–11, infants 0–2 free. The park fee counts children up to 14 (150 THB). Children's life jackets are always provided and must be worn at all times on the boat.

  • Toddlers: both shared boats work, but the big boat gives more room to move around; a private boat lets you set naps and meal times.
  • Pregnancy and back or neck problems: we don't advise the speedboat, because it crosses open sea and can slam in a swell. Choose the big boat.
  • Limited mobility: the Emerald Lake walk is 15–20 minutes of wooden stairs, and the Wua Talab viewpoint is a steep 500 m climb with ropes near the top. Both are optional. You step on and off the boat at beaches, sometimes in shallow water.

Family advice for the whole island is in Koh Samui with kids.

What's included and what isn't

Included on the shared speedboat, big boat and VIP

  • Hotel transfers from most areas of Koh Samui
  • Boat, captain and crew
  • Guide speaking English and Thai
  • Thai buffet lunch (the VIP adds fresh fruit)
  • Drinking water and soft drinks
  • Mask and snorkel; kayaks when the sea allows
  • Life jackets for every age, including children's sizes
  • Basic accident insurance, ages 3 months to 75

Not included

  • National park fee: 300 THB per foreign adult, 150 THB per child aged 3–14, cash at the park
  • Transfers from remote areas (Lipa Noi, Nathon, Taling Ngam, Bang Por, Bang Kao, Thong Krut and similar): 400 THB per person return
  • Towels, tips and personal belongings insurance

The private speedboat includes the boat, captain, crew, guide, drinks, snorkel gear, kayaks, life jackets and insurance; lunch (400 THB per person) and transfers (400 THB per person return) are optional, and you can meet the boat at the pier for free.

When to book

Online booking for the next day closes at midnight Thai time, and same-day online booking is not available. December to February is peak season, when park quotas limit numbers, so book a week or more ahead; the VIP's 15 seats go first. Your booking is confirmed by email or WhatsApp after payment, and prices are charged in THB.

Ang Thong tours follow one cancellation rule: a full refund 48 hours or more before departure, no refund under 48 hours or for a no-show. If we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.

The closure and what to do instead

Ang Thong closes every year from 1 November to 15 December, and no boat of any size may visit. The 2026–27 season reopens on Wednesday 16 December 2026, and all four options can be booked for dates from then. Until then, the Pig Island and Koh Tan speedboat (1,450 THB) runs every day from the sheltered south-west coast, and the Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan day trip (2,200 THB) runs when the sea allows. Staying on Koh Phangan? Speedboat and private Ang Thong tours also leave from Thong Sala; see angthongmarinepark.com.
