Why go: an easy half-day with the kids

The capybara café in Lamai is one of the most requested family stops on Koh Samui: a garden where these calm, oversized rodents wander around visitors, with feeding sessions through the day and a café for parents. This booking is simply a private van from your hotel and back, with the driver waiting while you visit, so you don't have to negotiate taxis with tired children.

What you'll do

You choose the time. The driver collects you, takes you to Lamai, waits while you pay entry, watch and feed the animals and have a drink, then takes you back. Because there is no time limit, it works well combined with Lamai beach or the Hin Ta and Hin Yai rocks nearby; ask the driver to stop on the way.

Who it suits

  • Families with young children, especially on a cloudy or rainy day.
  • Guests staying in Chaweng, Bophut, Maenam or further away who don't want to ride a scooter with children.

Who should choose something else

  • If you are staying in Lamai, a short taxi ride is cheaper.
  • Travellers who prefer wildlife in its natural setting: a snorkelling day around the southern islands, such as the Pig Island and Koh Tan speedboat tour, is a better fit.

Good to know

  • Animal welfare: capybaras are gentle but they are not toys. Let them come to you, follow staff instructions and leave if an animal looks stressed.
  • Hygiene: wash hands after feeding; bring wet wipes for small children.
  • Heat: the garden is partly shaded; late afternoon is cooler than midday.

Before you book

Tell us your hotel, the number of adults and children, and roughly when you want to arrive. Late morning, just after feeding starts at 11:00, and the early evening are usually quieter than mid-afternoon. If you want to add a beach stop or the rocks on the way back, mention it so the driver can plan the timing.
