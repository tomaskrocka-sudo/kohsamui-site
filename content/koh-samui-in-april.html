April is one of the best months to visit Koh Samui. It is hot, with highs of 32 °C and lows of 26 °C, and dry, with about 83 mm of rain over 6 rain days. The sea is 30 °C and among the calmest of the year, Ang Thong Marine Park is open, and the water is often at its clearest for snorkelling and diving. Songkran runs from 13 to 15 April 2027, with street water fights from 12 April, and the Full Moon Party is on Wednesday 21 April.

Good to know: Songkran week is one of Samui's busiest weeks. Book rooms, flights and boat trips early for 12–15 April. Ang Thong is open all month: the Ang Thong speedboat tour costs 1,800 THB, plus the 300 THB park fee in cash.

The verdict

April suits almost everyone who can handle heat. It is the right month for a boat-first holiday, and the wrong month if you want cool walks or full waterfalls.

Good for:

  • Ang Thong, Koh Tao and Pig Island in calm water
  • Snorkelling and diving, with visibility often the best of the year
  • Songkran water fights on 12–15 April 2027
  • Beach days on every coast
  • Shoulder-season room rates outside Songkran week

Not for:

  • Long midday walks and sightseeing: April and May are the hardest months in the heat
  • Waterfalls, which thin to a trickle
  • A quiet stay in Chaweng over Songkran
  • Cheap last-minute rooms for 12–15 April

If your dates are flexible, the first ten days and the last ten days of April give you the same weather without the Songkran crowds. Compare the whole year on Koh Samui weather by month.

April has a little more rain than February on paper, but only about one day in five is wet, and the sea is at least as calm. It is the hottest month of the dry season.

What April heat feels like

Samui's heat is steady rather than extreme, but nights stay at around 26 °C and the humidity is high, so there is little relief after dark. April and May are the hardest months for walking and sightseeing. Plan temples, viewpoints and markets for the early morning, spend 12:00–15:00 in the shade, the sea or a pool, and drink more water than you think you need. If you hike, finish by about 10:00.

The sun is strong all day. Use a high-factor, reef-safe sunscreen and wear a rash vest on snorkelling trips, where backs burn fastest. On a speedboat the breeze hides the burn until evening.

Two other things change in April. The waterfalls run at their lowest, so Na Muang and Hin Lad are thin ribbons over dark rock. And the tide turns: low water usually falls in the afternoon, around 16:00, so swim on the north and west coasts in the morning. See Koh Samui tides.

Songkran is a public holiday from Tuesday 13 to Thursday 15 April 2027, and on Samui the street events usually start a day early. Chaweng Beach Road is the main water fight, from late morning until after dark. Lamai's is smaller and more relaxed. Nathon is the most traditional, with temple ceremonies in the morning. Beach clubs and Central Samui run their own parties; in 2025 the mall held a foam party from 12:00 to 24:00 on 13–15 April. The official programme is normally announced in March. Songkran is not an alcohol-ban day.

A few rules: don't splash monks, elderly people, babies or anyone on a motorbike, and don't ride a scooter through the water-fight zones. Put your phone in a waterproof pouch, and double-bag it. Full guide: Songkran on Koh Samui.

The Full Moon Party falls on Wednesday 21 April 2027, the week after Songkran. The date is provisional until the Haad Rin organisers confirm it, and we re-check it about 30 days ahead. Our shared return Full Moon Party speedboat costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup and drop-off from most areas of Samui. We sell the boat only; entry (about 200 THB) is paid in cash at the beach. See Full Moon Party dates.

Where to stay in April

  • Chaweng puts you in the middle of Songkran: you walk out of the hotel into the water fight. The beach is good for swimming all month.
  • Bophut and Fisherman's Village are a short Grab ride from Chaweng and calmer at night. A good choice for families over Songkran.
  • Lamai has a smaller Songkran and good swimming, with lower room rates than Chaweng.
  • Maenam, Choeng Mon and the west coast (Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam) are quiet enough to avoid Songkran, apart from a few splashes on the ring road. The north and west beaches are tidal, so swim in the morning.
  • Avoid Bang Rak for swimming: the water there is murky in April and May.

Kitesurfers should know that April is the tail of the season at the Ban Harn and Hua Thanon lagoon on the south-east coast. The wind fades towards the end of the month, so bring a big kite. See kitesurfing and compare areas in where to stay on Koh Samui.

Diving and snorkelling in April

April is a peak month underwater. The Gulf diving season runs from February to October, and visibility is often the year's best in March and April. That makes it the best month for underwater photography at Sail Rock and around Koh Tao. Whale sharks are possible but never guaranteed: Koh Tao dive centres point to March to May.

For snorkellers, Ang Thong's reefs are at their clearest, Koh Tan on the Pig Island trip is calm and shallow, and Koh Nang Yuan has the clearest water within a day trip. Nang Yuan's 250 THB landing fee is paid in cash, day visits run 10:00–15:00, and fins are not allowed. In Ang Thong, sunscreens containing oxybenzone, octinoxate, 4-MBC or butylparaben are banned. See snorkelling and diving.

Prices in April

April counts as shoulder season, with rooms well below the mid-December to February peak, when rates run 30–80% higher. The exception is Songkran: Christmas, New Year and Songkran sell out first, so book 12–15 April early. In 2027 Easter Sunday falls on 28 March, so the Easter rush comes about two weeks before Songkran rather than in the same week.

Daily costs don't change with the season: street dishes cost 50–120 THB, a one-hour Thai massage about 300–500 THB and a scooter 150–350 THB a day. Ang Thong and Koh Nang Yuan fees are cash only, so carry more on boat days. The full price list is in is Koh Samui expensive?

Getting here and around in April

Bangkok Airways flies from Bangkok about 20–25 times a day; book direct flights early for Songkran. January to April is good weather on both of Thailand's coasts, so April works well for a Samui and Phuket trip; Bangkok Airways flies Phuket–Samui about 7–8 times a day. Ferries to Koh Phangan and Koh Tao run normally. See how to get to Koh Samui.

Over Songkran, expect slow traffic on the ring road through Chaweng and Lamai, and allow extra time for airport runs on 12–15 April. Grab cars are the driest option; in an open songthaew you will get wet. Songkran's "seven dangerous days" are the deadliest week on Thai roads, so leave the scooter parked. If you do ride, wear a helmet (fines up to 2,000 THB) and carry an International Driving Permit that covers motorcycles. See getting around Koh Samui.

What to pack

  • Reef-safe sunscreen (SPF 50), a hat and sunglasses: the UV index is high all day.
  • Light cotton or linen clothes, and a cover-up for temples (shoulders and knees covered).
  • A rash vest for snorkelling days.
  • Water shoes for stony beaches such as north Lamai, Bang Por and Coral Cove.
  • For Songkran: a waterproof phone pouch with a zip-lock bag inside, goggles or wraparound sunglasses, quick-dry clothes and sandals with straps.
  • Seasickness tablets if you are prone to it, even on calm days.
  • A light layer for air-conditioned buses, boats and the airport.
