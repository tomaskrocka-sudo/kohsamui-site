Why go: space, service and a smooth ride

The Lagoon 500 is one of the largest sailing catamarans you can charter from Samui. It is 16 m long and almost half as wide, so even with 20 or more guests on board nobody is squeezed. A butler looks after drinks and food, and the crew handle the sailing. Paddle boards and snorkel gear are ready when you anchor. On routes outside Ang Thong, an optional pool and a water slide off the side keep children busy.

The morning half day goes to Pig Island and Koh Tan. The afternoon version swaps the order and ends with the sun setting behind the islands. Full days go further: a long Koh Tan and Pig Island day, the Koh Phangan coast, or Ang Thong from 16 December, which is the longest day at 10 hours.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: families, villa groups, birthdays and corporate days, and any group of 10–45 who want to stay together on one boat.
  • Choose something else if: you're a couple or a small family. A smaller sailing catamaran such as Aloha or Elizabeth costs a fraction of this. If you want to cover a lot of water fast, take a private speedboat.

Good to know

  • Food: lunch isn't included. Ask us about catering when you book, or eat at a beach restaurant on the full-day routes.
  • Sailing: sails go up when the wind allows. On calm days you motor, which is smoother.
  • Season: February to September is calmest. In the monsoon (October to December), choose a half day close to Samui.
  • Drinks: soft drinks and ice are included. Ask us about bringing your own wine or beer before the day.
