Koh Samui has four main zipline courses. The best known is the treetop course near Tan Rua waterfall in the hills behind Maenam: about 2,000 m of lines and rope bridges in roughly 60 minutes, with sessions at 09:30, 12:30 and 14:30, from about 1,590 THB on the market and 1,900 THB with us including transfer from Chaweng, Choeng Mon, Bophut, Maenam and Lamai. A bigger jungle park runs 14 or 21 ziplines (1,900 or 2,700 THB), a west-coast canopy course costs 1,500–1,800 THB, and short rides at Lamai Viewpoint start at 800 THB. Age and weight limits apply: typically 6–70 years and 100–120 kg.

Which course should you choose?

  • First zipline, staying in Chaweng, Bophut, Maenam or Lamai: the treetop course near Tan Rua. It is the easiest to reach, the session is short, and you get time at the waterfall afterwards.
  • You want the longest day in the trees: the Golden course at the jungle park, with 21 ziplines over about 2 hours.
  • Staying in Lipa Noi, Nathon, Taling Ngam or Bang Por: the west-coast canopy course, with free transfer from those areas and four sky bridges.
  • A quick thrill with a view, or a short budget: Lamai Viewpoint, from 800 THB for two zip rides, if you can get there yourself.
  • Children aged 5–6: the treetop course accepts 6-year-olds and 5-year-olds on request; the jungle park starts at 7.

Treetop zipline near Tan Rua waterfall

This course sits in the forest above the north coast, close to Tan Rua waterfall. Market listings describe 8 platforms and about 2,000 m of line in roughly 60 minutes; our partner's current course has 10 ziplines and rope bridges over the same distance. Sessions start at 09:30, 12:30 and 14:30, and pickup is about an hour before. After the course you get about 30 minutes at the waterfall or the café. Children pay the adult price (1,900 THB with us, transfer included), so for families the jungle park can work out similar per head. Read more about the area in our Maenam guide.

Jungle zipline park: Silver and Golden courses

The largest course on the island, with Petzl harnesses, double-lock lanyards, abseils and suspension bridges. The Silver course has 14 ziplines across 22 platforms and takes about an hour; the Golden course has 21 ziplines across 33 platforms and takes about 2 hours. Pickup is at 08:00, and the day lasts about 5.5 hours (Silver) or 6.5 hours (Golden) door to door, with fruit, snacks and drinks included. Adults and children aged 7 and over pay the same: 1,900 THB (Silver) or 2,700 THB (Golden).

West-coast canopy course

A compact course on the west side of the island, good for guests who are staying away from the busy east coast. Programme B (1,500 THB) has 16 platforms, 5 ziplines and 4 sky bridges; Programme A (1,800 THB) has 18 platforms and 8 ziplines. Both finish with a 15 m abseil, and the longest line runs about 300 m. A 140 m "sky bicycle" costs 200 THB extra. Sessions run at 10:00, 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00. Transfer is free from Lipa Noi, Nathon, Taling Ngam and Bang Por; from elsewhere it costs 200 THB per person or 2,000 THB for a private car for up to 10.

Lamai Viewpoint zipline

The zip lines here run about 80 m above the jungle on the hill behind Lamai, with sea views. Packages start at 800 THB for two rides and a walk up, and go up to 2,400 THB for four rides, the Sky Adventure course, the cable car up and a drone video of your ride. Check-in is from 09:00. Transfer isn't included: ride or take a taxi there, or book a private return van for 2,500 THB for 1–10 people. Entrance to the viewpoint is included in every package.

What to expect on the course

  1. Pickup About 1 hour before your session, by 4WD or minibus. Steep villa roads may get a nearby meeting point.
  2. Briefing 15–20 minutes: harness and helmet fitting, how to hold the line, how to brake, and what to do if you stop short of a platform.
  3. Practice A low, short line first, with a guide on each platform.
  4. The course Guides clip you on and off at every platform; you never unclip yourself. Rope bridges and sky bridges are walked with a safety line.
  5. Finish A drink, the waterfall or café at Tan Rua, then the drive back.

You don't need any fitness beyond climbing a few flights of stairs between platforms. Nervousness about heights usually fades after the practice line. If you are worried, tell the guide at the briefing so they can go first and talk you through each platform.

Safety and limits

  • Age: treetop course 6–70 (5 on request); jungle park 7 and over; west-coast course and Lamai Viewpoint set weight rather than age limits, so ask us about young children.
  • Weight: maximum 120 kg (treetop), 105 kg (west coast) and 100 kg (jungle park and Lamai Viewpoint). The west-coast course also has a 20 kg minimum.
  • Health: not during pregnancy, and not with heart conditions or recent back or shoulder injuries.
  • Equipment: helmet, full harness and double-lock lanyards are included on every course. Don't ride if your gear doesn't feel right; ask for a refit.
  • Insurance: the treetop, jungle park and west-coast courses include accident insurance. Check that your own travel insurance covers adventure activities.

Transfers and remote-area surcharges

For the treetop course, transfer is included from Chaweng, Choeng Mon, Bophut, Maenam and Lamai. Other areas, including Taling Ngam, Nathon, Hua Thanon and Lipa Noi, pay 1,800 THB per group, and hillside villas pay 1,000 THB per group, because the 4WD has to make a special trip. If you are staying on the west coast, the canopy course is the better fit. If you have your own scooter, ask us about meeting at the course; but don't ride the steep hill roads after rain.

Does the zipline run in the rain?

Yes, the courses run daily all year and in light rain, which is common from October to December. Lines may be paused during thunderstorms or strong wind, and the forest floor is muddy, so wear shoes with grip. If a session is cancelled for weather, you choose a new date or a refund. Combine the zipline with an ATV ride

The 09:30 treetop session finishes in time for a 14:00 quad ride in the same hills, both with transfer from the main beach areas. See routes and prices on our ATV and quad bike guide. For a full day inland with waterfalls and temples, the jungle safari is the alternative, and the waterfalls guide covers Tan Rua and the others.

What to wear and bring

  • Closed shoes with grip (trainers are ideal); flip-flops come off on the platforms.
  • Shorts or light trousers that don't ride up under a harness; avoid skirts and dresses.
  • Tie back long hair. Leave dangling jewellery and loose hats behind.
  • A phone strap or zip pocket if you want to film; some courses sell photo and video packages.
  • Insect repellent and water.
  • Swimwear if you plan to swim at Tan Rua waterfall afterwards.
