Why go: a slow, characterful day on a traditional junk

Blue Dragon is a 21 m teak junk with deep red sails, built in the style of the old Chinese trading boats that once worked this coast. It cruises at about 10 knots. That is slower than a speedboat, but the point is the boat itself: wide wooden decks, shade, space to lie out and a ride that doesn't slam. With up to 6 guests at the base price, you have the whole boat to yourselves.

The Koh Tan and Pig Island day is a shorter cruise to the south coast for snorkelling on Koh Tan's reef and a beach landing on Koh Madsum by dinghy. The Ang Thong day, from 16 December, sails past the Five Islands to the caves at Koh Tai Plao, the snorkelling at Koh Wao and the Emerald Lake on Koh Mae Ko, with lunch served on board and an evening cruise home.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: families, older travellers, photographers and small celebrations. Anyone who dislikes speedboats will enjoy the calmer motion.
  • Choose something else if: you want to reach several islands fast. A private speedboat covers more ground. On a per-person budget, our shared Ang Thong big-boat tour runs the same park from 16 December.

Good to know

  • Fees: bring cash for the Ang Thong park fee and the Pig Island fee.
  • Long day: the Ang Thong route ends around sunset. Pack a light layer for the evening crossing.
  • Climbing: the Koh Mae Ko steps up to the Emerald Lake viewpoint are steep. Wear grippy shoes.
