Why go: a flexible twin-engine boat from the north coast

Bang Rak, next to Big Buddha, is one of the closest Samui piers to Koh Phangan, so this boat spends more of your time at the beaches and less on the crossing. The twin engines give a smoother, faster ride than single-engine boats, and the crew speaks English and Thai.

The half day suits families who want a morning in the water and the afternoon at the hotel; the full day suits groups who want to string together Koh Phangan's coves at their own pace.

Half day or full day

The half day gives about four hours: enough for one or two snorkel stops and a beach on Koh Phangan's nearer coast, or a loop of the quieter bays on Samui's north and east coasts. The full day allows a long lunch on the beach and more distant coves on Koh Phangan, and the Koh Phangan version can run until 18:00. The guide speaks English and Thai and can help order lunch at local beach restaurants. Kayaks, paddleboards and fishing gear are not carried, so bring your own snorkel if you prefer it to the boat's.

Who it suits

  • Groups of 6–10, including families with children aged 4–9.
  • Guests staying in Bophut, Bang Rak, Plai Laem or Choeng Mon, close to the pier.
  • Anyone who wants a private boat without paying for a yacht.

Who should choose something else

  • Couples who want sails and quiet: try a catamaran.
  • Guests who need everything included: the Sting includes transfers from Bophut, Choeng Mon and Chaweng.
  • Anyone wanting Pig Island: see our Pig Island private speedboat.

Good to know

  • Bring towels: they are not provided.
  • Sea by month: February to September is calmest; the north-east monsoon brings chop from November to January.
  • Booking: online booking for tomorrow closes at midnight Thai time.
