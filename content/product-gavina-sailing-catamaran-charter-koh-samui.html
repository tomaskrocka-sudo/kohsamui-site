Why go: a sociable sailing day with toys on board

Gavina's air-conditioned saloon and two cabins make it comfortable in the midday heat, and the paddleboards, dinghy and fishing gear keep groups busy at anchor. Koh Som, just off Samui's north-east coast, is a short sail away, so the half days spend most of their time swimming rather than travelling.

The full days either cross to Koh Phangan's south coast or loop around Samui to Pig Island and the reef at Koh Tan, finishing with a sunset sail.

Half day or full day

The morning half day sails the north-east coast to Koh Som, a small island off Samui's north-east tip, for swimming, snorkelling and paddleboarding, with a beach stop and some fishing before you return at 13:00. The afternoon version leaves around 14:30 and comes back with the sunset. The full days start at 10:00 and add either a bay on Koh Phangan's south coast with lunch at anchor, or a loop to Koh Madsum and Koh Tan. Order the BBQ lunch in advance if you want a hot meal.

Who it suits

  • Families and groups of friends up to 8, or up to 24 for parties.
  • Guests who want to fish, paddle and snorkel in one trip.
  • Anyone who prefers a stable catamaran to a speedboat.

Who should choose something else

  • Couples wanting a smaller, cheaper sail: the King 9 starts lower.
  • Anyone wanting lunch included: the Red Baron cruises include meals.

Good to know

  • Towels: not provided; bring your own.
  • Weather: from November to January the north-east coast can be choppy; the crew may choose sheltered anchorages.
  • Booking: online booking for tomorrow closes at midnight Thai time.
