Why go: a long, unhurried day under sail

C'este La Vie is a Grand Voyage 42, a 14 m sailing catamaran built in 2020 with three cabins, three bathrooms and a dinghy for going ashore. The boat is based on Koh Phangan, and this is its one trip that starts from Koh Samui: a full day sailing the Samui coast, from 09:00 to 19:00. That is a long day on the water, which means several swim and snorkel stops, paddle boarding in a quiet bay and a sunset on the sail home, without rushing.

The crew plans the route on the day according to wind and sea. A catamaran's twin hulls keep it level, so it suits guests who don't like the slam of a speedboat.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: families and groups of friends up to 10 who want a calm day with time to swim and relax.
  • Choose something else if you want a shorter outing from Samui: the Odyssey catamaran offers half days and sunsets from Bang Rak.
  • Staying on Koh Phangan? Ask us about the boat's trips from there.

Good to know

  • Speed: the boat cruises at about 7 knots, so it does not try to reach distant islands on this route.
  • Lunch: not included. A picnic order from a Samui restaurant keeps the day flexible.
  • Weather: from October to December the north-east monsoon brings wind and rain; the south and west coasts are the usual shelter.
  • Sun: ten hours on the water is a lot of sun. Reapply sunscreen after every swim.

Planning the day

Tell the crew what matters most when you board: snorkelling, quiet beaches, fishing or simply sailing. With ten hours, there is time for three or four stops. Order a picnic or plan a beach restaurant lunch in advance.
