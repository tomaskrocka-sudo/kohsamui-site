Where is Lad Koh viewpoint? | On Koh Samui's east-coast ring road between Chaweng Noi and Coral Cove, at 9.4990, 100.0600 (Plus Code F3X5+JX5). It is about 5 km south of central Chaweng and 6 km north of Lamai.
Is Lad Koh viewpoint free? | Yes, entry and parking are free and the viewpoint is open 24 hours. The only cost is a drink or coconut ice cream from the van that usually parks there.
Is it safe to climb on the rocks at Lad Koh? | No. A 24-year-old visitor was swept off the rocks below the viewpoint by 2–3 m waves on 29 November 2024, and the municipality then closed access to the rocky area. Stay on the platform and steps, especially from October to December.
When is the best time to visit Lad Koh viewpoint? | At sunrise, about 06:00–06:40 depending on the month, until about 09:00, when the light is soft and the tour vans haven't arrived. February to September has the calmest, clearest sea.
Is there parking at Lad Koh viewpoint? | Yes, a small free roadside area that fills quickly with tour vans. It is on a winding stretch with blind bends, so slow down early, and if it is full drive on and come back rather than stopping on the road.
How long should I spend at Lad Koh viewpoint? | 10–15 minutes is enough for photos and a drink. Combine it with Coral Cove and Silver Beach, 1–2.5 km to the south, for a morning on the east coast.
What is near Lad Koh viewpoint? | Coral Cove (1 km south) for snorkelling, Silver Beach (2.5 km south) for a swim, and Chaweng Noi just to the north. Lamai, Hin Ta and Hin Yai and Overlap Stone are 10–20 minutes further south.
