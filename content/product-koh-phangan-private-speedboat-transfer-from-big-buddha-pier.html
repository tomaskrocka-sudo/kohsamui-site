Why go: skip the ferry and land at your beach

The public ferries run to Thong Sala and Haad Rin on fixed timetables, and from there you still need a taxi over Koh Phangan's hilly roads. A private speedboat leaves when you are ready, carries your luggage and can land at beaches that are slow to reach by road, such as Haad Yuan, Bottle Beach and Thong Nai Pan.

It makes most sense for groups, families with lots of luggage, late flights and villa stays on the north and east coasts.

How the transfer works

A driver meets you at Samui airport or your hotel and takes you to the pier near Big Buddha, about 15 minutes from the airport. The speedboat then runs straight to your chosen pier or beach: Ban Tai, Haad Rin and Thong Sala are the quickest, while Haad Yuan, Haad Tien, Thong Nai Pan, Bottle Beach and Koh Ma take longer. The standard price covers one large suitcase per person. Tell us your flight number, group size, bags and beach, and we confirm the total before you pay.

Who it suits

  • Groups of 4–10 sharing the cost.
  • Families arriving with luggage and tired children.
  • Guests staying on beaches with poor road access.

Who should choose something else

  • Solo travellers and couples with light bags: the ferry is far cheaper; see Koh Samui to Koh Phangan.
  • Party-goers heading to Haad Rin on a Full Moon night: book our Full Moon Party speedboat.

Good to know

  • Night crossings depend on sea conditions and the captain's decision.
  • Beach landings may mean stepping into knee-deep water; pack valuables in a dry bag.
  • Booking: we quote on WhatsApp; confirmation follows by email or WhatsApp after payment.
