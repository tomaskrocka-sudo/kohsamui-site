Where is the best snorkelling near Koh Samui? | Offshore: Koh Nang Yuan by Koh Tao has the clearest water, Ang Thong's sheltered reefs are good from 16 December to October, and Koh Tan off Thong Krut is the easiest reef for beginners, open all year. On Samui itself, Coral Cove and Silver Beach are the best shore spots.
Can you snorkel straight from the beach on Koh Samui? | Yes, at a few rocky coves: Coral Cove, Silver Beach, the Chaweng Noi rocks, Laem Yai and the ends of Choeng Mon. Expect modest coral and fish, wear water shoes and go on calm mornings between February and September.
Is Koh Tan or Koh Madsum better for snorkelling? | Koh Tan. It has a shallow reef along Ao Ok beach that suits beginners, while Koh Madsum (Pig Island) is about the pigs and the beach. Every one of our Pig Island trips stops at both.
Is Ang Thong good for snorkelling? | It is pleasant rather than world-class: shallow fringing reefs such as Koh Wao and Koh Sam Sao, best in March and April. Most people go for the Emerald Lake and the viewpoint and enjoy the snorkel as one stop of four. The park is closed 1 November–15 December.
What is the best month for snorkelling around Samui? | March and April usually give the clearest water, with February and May close behind. October to December brings rain run-off and rough east-coast seas, though Koh Tan off the sheltered south-west coast is still snorkelled on most days.
Do I need to bring my own snorkel gear? | Not on our boat trips: every guest gets a mask and snorkel, and life jackets come in adult and child sizes. Fins are not included, so bring your own if you want them, but fins are banned at Koh Nang Yuan.
Can non-swimmers snorkel on your tours? | Yes. Non-swimmers can float in a life jacket near the boat while the guide keeps an eye on them, and the reef at Koh Tan is shallow and calm on most days. Children's life jackets are always provided and must be worn on the boat.
Are there jellyfish when snorkelling around Samui? | Sometimes. Most are harmless, but box jellyfish occur occasionally and are dangerous. If stung, pour vinegar on the sting for at least 30 seconds, don't rub it or use fresh water, and call 1669; wear a rash vest to reduce exposed skin.
Does the tide matter for snorkelling on Koh Samui? | Yes, at the shallow spots. At very low tide the reef flats at Laem Yai and parts of Koh Tan are too close to the surface to swim over safely, so aim for mid to high tide. Our boat crews choose the reef with the tide in mind.
Is Ang Thong or Koh Tao better for snorkelling? | Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan have clearer water and more reef. Ang Thong has better scenery above the water: the Emerald Lake, the viewpoint and kayaking. From 1 November to 15 December only the Koh Tao trip runs.
