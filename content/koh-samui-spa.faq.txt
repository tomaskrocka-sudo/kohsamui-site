How much is a Thai massage on Koh Samui? | About 300–500 THB for an hour at a walk-in shop in 2026: 400–600 THB in Chaweng, 300–450 THB in Lamai and 300–400 THB in Bophut and Maenam. Oil massage costs 400–700 THB, and resort spas charge about 1,800–3,500 THB plus service and VAT.
Where are the best spas on Koh Samui? | For a setting, Tamarind Springs, a forest spa with herbal steam caves in the hills behind Lamai, and the resort spas on the headlands, such as Kamalaya at Laem Set. For value, the better shops in Lamai, Bophut and Maenam give excellent Thai massages for 300–450 THB.
Is Thai massage painful? | It is firm, with deep pressure and assisted stretches, but it should never be sharply painful. Ask for "bao bao" (softer), say "jep" if something hurts, and choose an oil massage if you want something gentle.
Should I tip after a massage? | Yes at shops and day spas: about 50–100 THB after an hour, or 100–200 THB at mid-range spas, handed directly to the therapist. Resort spas add a 10% service charge, so an extra tip there is optional.
Is beach massage safe? | Generally yes, and prices are similar to shops (about 300–500 THB). Bring your own sarong to lie on, choose Thai massage rather than oil on windy days so sand doesn't stick, avoid the midday sun, and skip it if you are sunburnt.
Can I have a massage when pregnant? | Not a traditional Thai massage. Book a dedicated pregnancy massage with trained therapists, such as the two-hour treatment at Tamarind Springs (5,000 THB++), and check with your doctor first. Avoid herbal steam rooms while pregnant.
Is there a sauna on Koh Samui? | There are herbal steam caves at Tamarind Springs (Steam and Dream, 1,500 THB++) and Kamalaya, plus saunas or steam rooms at some resorts and gyms. We could not confirm a public village herbal sauna operating in 2026.
Do I need to book a spa in advance? | Not for street shops, where you can walk in. Book day spas a few hours ahead and resort or forest spas a day or more ahead in high season (December–April), especially for couples' rooms and evening slots.
How long should a first Thai massage be? | One hour is enough for a first try; 90 minutes or 2 hours lets the therapist work through the whole body without rushing. Many visitors add a 30-minute foot massage after a day in sandals.
Can I get a massage if I'm sunburnt? | Skip Thai and oil massage on burnt skin, as pressure and oil make it worse. Ask for an aloe vera after-sun treatment instead, which most shops offer, and drink plenty of water.
