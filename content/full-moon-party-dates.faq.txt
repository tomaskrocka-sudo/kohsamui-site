Why is the Full Moon Party not always on the full moon? | Thailand bans alcohol sales on Buddhist holy days, which often fall on a full moon. When that happens, the Haad Rin business association moves the party by a day, usually to the next night. The party is also a night event, so when the full moon peaks in the early morning, the party is held the night before.
Which 2027 Full Moon Parties have moved dates? | Four 2027 parties move off Buddhist holy days: Monday 22 February (Makha Bucha is 21 February), Friday 21 May (Visakha Bucha is 20 May), Tuesday 20 July (Asahna Bucha and Khao Phansa) and Saturday 16 October (Ok Phansa, about 15 October). Many sites still list the raw lunar dates, which are ban days with no party.
~Is there a Full Moon Party on New Year's Eve? | Yes. Haad Rin holds a countdown party every 31 December, whatever the moon is doing, so there is one on Thursday 31 December 2026 and Friday 31 December 2027. It is the busiest night of the year, so book boats and rooms early. In 2026 the Christmas Eve party (24 December) falls in the same week.
How much does the Full Moon Party cost? | Entry is about 200 THB per person in cash at the beach in 2026. From Koh Samui, add the boat: our shared return speedboat is 1,400 THB with hotel pickup from most areas. Drinks cost more than in Chaweng, and foreign-card ATMs charge 220–250 THB per withdrawal, so bring cash from Samui.
~Do I need to buy Full Moon Party tickets online? | No. There is no official online entry ticket for the Full Moon Party. You pay about 200 THB in cash at the beach entrances and receive a wristband. Be wary of sites selling "entry tickets". What you should book ahead is transport, because boats from Samui fill up on Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve and Loy Krathong.
What time does the Full Moon Party start and finish? | The beach gets busy from about 21:00, the peak is from midnight to 03:00, and the last bars play past sunrise, usually until 06:00–08:00. From Koh Samui, our shared boats leave in an 18:00–23:00 window, and your return slot is confirmed when you book, so you don't have to stay until morning.
~How do I get there from Koh Samui? | The easiest way is a shared return speedboat with hotel pickup from most areas of Samui, 1,400 THB per person with us. Groups can book a private speedboat for up to 10 guests. The public Haad Rin Queen from Big Buddha pier costs about 200 THB but has no late-night return. Full details are on our Full Moon Party from Koh Samui guide.
Can the Full Moon Party date change at short notice? | It can, but it rarely does. The Haad Rin association confirms each party a few weeks ahead, and changes are almost always linked to Buddhist holidays or official events. We re-check every date about 30 days ahead and again in party week. If a date moves after you book our boat, we contact you straight away to rearrange your booking.
Is the Full Moon Party on in the rainy season? | Yes. The party goes ahead in rain, including in November, Samui's wettest month at about 445 mm. Boats only cross when the sea is safe. If we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date. February to April has the calmest crossings of the year.
