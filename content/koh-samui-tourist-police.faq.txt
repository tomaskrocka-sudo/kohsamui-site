What is the Tourist Police number in Thailand? | 1155. It is the national Tourist Police hotline, staffed 24 hours a day by English-speaking operators, and it works from any Thai phone. For a crime in progress dial 191, and for an ambulance dial 1669.
Where is the Tourist Police office on Koh Samui? | The Tourist Police have a local office on Koh Samui and patrol Chaweng, Lamai and Bophut. The quickest way to find the nearest officer is to call 1155 and say where you are; search "Tourist Police Koh Samui" on Google Maps for the office pin.
How do I get a police report for my insurance? | Report the incident to the Tourist Police (1155) or the local police station within 24 hours, bring your passport, a list of items with values and any evidence, and ask for an English report or summary. Check the details before signing and keep a copy.
What should I do if my passport is stolen on Koh Samui? | Get a police report from the Tourist Police or local police, contact your embassy in Bangkok for an emergency travel document or new passport, then visit Koh Samui Immigration in Maenam or airport immigration so your entry record moves to the new document.
Can the Tourist Police help with a jet-ski damage dispute? | Yes. Call 1155 and ask an officer to attend or speak to the operator. Show your before-and-after videos and contract. Officers usually mediate a settlement, but they can't force a refund. Never leave your passport as a deposit.
Do the Tourist Police speak English? | Yes. The 1155 hotline is answered in English, officers on Samui speak English, and some speak other languages. Volunteers from the foreign community also help with translation in busy areas.
Is 1155 free to call? | The 1155 hotline is a national short-code number for tourists and is generally free from Thai mobile phones. If you have no Thai SIM, ask your hotel, a shop or a bar to call for you.
