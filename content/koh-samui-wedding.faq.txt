Is it legal for foreigners to marry in Thailand? | Yes. Foreigners can marry legally by registering at a Thai district office after getting an affirmation of freedom to marry from their embassy, a certified Thai translation and legalisation by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Bangkok. A beach, villa or boat ceremony on its own is symbolic, not legal.
How much does a Samui wedding cost? | It depends on guests and venue, so price the building blocks: a 3-hour wedding cruise on the Red Baron junk with celebrant for up to 20 guests costs 72,000 THB, a 6–10-bedroom pool villa 30,000–120,000+ THB a night in low season, and a private van for guests from 2,500 THB. Ask suppliers for itemised quotes including "++" charges.
What is the best month for a beach wedding on Koh Samui? | February to April, when rain and swell are lowest; March is the safest bet. May to September is also good and cheaper. Avoid October to December, Samui's rainy season, with November the wettest month.
Can we get married on a boat in Koh Samui? | You can hold a symbolic ceremony on a boat: our Red Baron wedding cruise includes a celebrant, flowers and a sunset toast for up to 20 guests (72,000 THB, 3 hours), and sailing catamarans start at 20,000 THB per boat. A legal marriage still has to be registered at a district office.
Do we need a permit for a beach ceremony? | Resorts arrange ceremonies on their own grounds. For a public beach, ask your planner or venue whether the municipality needs notice, and keep set-ups small and temporary. Drones need registration and cannot fly near Samui airport.
How far ahead should we book a Koh Samui wedding? | For February–April or Christmas and New Year, book the venue as soon as you have a date, then guest rooms, then boats and suppliers. In the May–September low season a few months is usually enough. Allow at least a month for embassy appointments if you are marrying legally in Thailand.
Can guests stay in a villa together? | Yes. Pool villas with 6–10 bedrooms cost about 30,000–120,000+ THB a night in low season for the whole house. Confirm in writing that events are allowed, the guest limit, music curfews and any event deposit before you pay.
What if it rains on our wedding day? | Agree a covered fallback and a decision time with your venue, and keep the start time flexible, because showers outside October–December usually pass within an hour. For boat weddings the captain makes the safety call; if we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.
Can same-sex couples marry in Thailand? | Yes. Thailand's Marriage Equality Act took effect on 23 January 2025, and same-sex couples, including foreigners, can register a marriage at a district office through the same process. Check how your home country recognises the marriage.
Can we have a Buddhist blessing at our wedding? | Yes. A monks' blessing is usually held in the morning, because monks eat before noon, and is often followed by the water-pouring ceremony. It is a blessing, not a legal marriage; dress modestly and remember that women must not touch monks.
