Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in November? | No. Ang Thong National Marine Park closes every year from 1 November to 15 December for the north-east monsoon, and no tourist boats may land. Pig Island and Koh Tao trips from Samui keep running in that window, sea permitting.
When does Ang Thong reopen after the monsoon closure? | The park reopens on 16 December each year; in 2026 that is Wednesday 16 December. You can book any date from 16 December onwards now, and December to February places go fast because of park quotas.
How much is the Ang Thong park fee? | 300 THB per foreign adult and 150 THB per foreign child aged 3–14 (and under 140 cm), paid in cash to the rangers. Children under 3 are free, and Thai nationals pay 40 THB (adult) and 20 THB (child).
Is the Ang Thong park fee included in your tour price? | No. None of our Ang Thong tours include the national park fee. Bring the exact amount in Thai baht cash for everyone in your group; the rangers collect it when the boat enters the park.
~Should I go by speedboat or big boat to Ang Thong? | Take the speedboat (1,800 THB, about 8 hours) for a shorter day with more time at each stop. Take the big boat (1,500 THB, about 10 hours) for a steadier ride with shade, especially if you get seasick, are pregnant or have back problems.
How long is the boat ride from Koh Samui to Ang Thong? | About 1 hour by speedboat and about 1.5 hours by big boat, each way, depending on the sea. With hotel pickup and drop-off the full day is about 8 hours by speedboat and 10–11 hours by big boat.
Can you swim in the Emerald Lake at Ang Thong? | No. Swimming in the Emerald Lake (Thale Nai) on Koh Mae Ko is not allowed. You walk 15–20 minutes up wooden stairs to a viewing platform above the lake, then swim at the beach or on the snorkelling stop instead.
How hard is the Ang Thong viewpoint climb on Koh Wua Talab? | It is steep: about 500 m of rough steps and rock, with ropes near the top, taking 25–30 minutes up. Wear shoes with grip and carry water. It is optional, and you can stay on the beach instead.
Can you stay overnight in Ang Thong Marine Park? | Yes, on Koh Wua Talab, the headquarters island, in a park bungalow or a tent. Our overnight trips cost 3,600 THB (tent) or 4,200 THB (bungalow) per adult and include the boat, the day tour, day 1 lunch and the accommodation, which we book for you.
Is Ang Thong suitable for children? | Yes. Infants aged 0–2 travel free, children aged 3–11 pay the child price, and children's life jackets are always provided and worn on the boat. The Emerald Lake stairs suit most children; the Wua Talab viewpoint suits those who can climb unaided for 30 minutes.
What happens if my Ang Thong trip is cancelled for bad weather? | If we or the park cancel because the sea is unsafe, you choose a full refund or a new date. If you cancel yourself, Ang Thong tours are fully refundable 48 hours or more before departure, with no refund under 48 hours or for a no-show.
What is the best month to visit Ang Thong? | February is usually the calmest month, and March and April have the clearest water for snorkelling. May to September is good on most days. October is mixed, and the park is closed from 1 November to 15 December.
