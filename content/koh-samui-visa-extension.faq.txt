Can I extend my 30-day visa exemption on Koh Samui? | Yes, once, by up to 30 days, at Koh Samui Immigration in Maenam. You need form TM.7, your passport with signed copies, a 4×6 cm photo, proof of address and 1,900 THB. The officer decides, so bring complete paperwork.
How much does a visa extension cost in Thailand? | The government fee is 1,900 THB per person, paid in cash at the immigration counter. The same fee applies to tourist visa and DTV extensions. Agents charge their own service fee on top.
How early can I apply for an extension? | You can apply at any point before your stamp expires, and the extra days are added to the end of your current permission. Most people go in the last 7–10 days. Don't wait until the final day in case the office is closed for a weekend or holiday.
What documents do I need for a TM.7 extension? | Passport, signed copies of the photo page, entry stamp and TDAC, a completed TM.7, one 4×6 cm photo taken within 6 months, 1,900 THB cash, proof of your Samui address (hotel booking or rental contract with TM.30 receipt) and ideally an onward flight.
~Where is Koh Samui immigration? | At 333 Maenam Road, Soi 1, Moo 1, Maenam 84330 on the north coast, phone +66 77 423 440. It moved from Nathon in 2016, so ignore old Nathon map pins.
Is Koh Samui immigration open on Saturdays? | No. The office usually opens Monday to Friday, 08:30–16:30, and is closed on Saturdays, Sundays and Thai public holidays. If your stamp ends on a weekend, apply during the week before.
Can I extend more than once? | Not on a visa exemption or tourist visa: you get one extension of up to 30 days. After that you must leave Thailand. DTV holders can extend each 180-day entry once by 180 days.
What happens if I overstay by a day? | You pay a 500 THB fine at the airport or immigration when you leave, and the overstay is recorded. Fines rise by 500 THB a day up to 20,000 THB, and overstays of more than 90 days bring a re-entry ban.
