Is October a good time to visit Koh Samui? | It depends on your plans. October is when the north-east monsoon arrives, with about 295 mm of rain over 16 rain days and the first red flags on Chaweng and Lamai. It is good for low prices, green scenery, Pig Island and a last Ang Thong trip before the park closes on 1 November.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in October? | About 295 mm on 1991–2020 averages, over about 16 days with 1 mm or more. That is two and a half times September's rain, and only November is wetter. The rain often comes in heavy bursts rather than short showers, but about half the days stay dry.
Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in October? | Yes, until 31 October, the last day of the season. The park closes every year from 1 November to 15 December and reopens on 16 December. Book for early in the month and early in your stay, because October is one of the riskiest months for weather cancellations on the crossing.
What happens if my Ang Thong trip is cancelled in late October? | If we cancel for weather or sea safety, you choose a full refund or a new date, and late in October a new date means 16 December or later, after the closure. If you cancel yourself, Ang Thong tours are refunded in full 48 hours or more before departure and not at all under 48 hours.
Is the sea rough around Koh Samui in October? | Sometimes. Typical daily wave highs average only about 0.4 m, but thunderstorms can bring 30-knot gusts for half an hour, and the first north-east swell brings red flags to Chaweng and Lamai. The north and west coasts stay mostly calm, and Pig Island boats from Thong Krut cross sheltered water.
When is the Full Moon Party in October 2027? | Saturday 16 October 2027. The full moon falls on Ok Phansa (about Friday 15 October), an alcohol-sales ban day, so the party moves a night later. Many websites still list 15 October. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person; entry, about 200 THB, is paid in cash at the beach.
Is Koh Samui cheap in October? | For rooms, yes. October and November are the cheapest months of the year for hotels, because they are the wettest, and 2–4 weeks ahead is usually enough for flights. Daily costs stay the same: street dishes 50–120 THB and a one-hour Thai massage 300–500 THB. Some cafés and restaurants shorten their hours.
What festivals are on Koh Samui in October 2027? | Ok Phansa, the end of Buddhist Lent, on about 15 October, followed by the moved Full Moon Party on 16 October. The Chak Phra temple-float procession and fair in Nathon falls around the end of Lent, in mid or late October or in November, and the annual Vegetarian Festival brings "jay" food to the Chinese shrines.
Is October or November better for Koh Samui? | October. It averages about 295 mm of rain against about 445 mm in November, and Ang Thong is still open until 31 October; in November the park is closed. Both months have the lowest room rates of the year. In November, Pig Island tours run on any date, weather permitting.
