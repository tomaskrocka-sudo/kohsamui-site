Where is Plai Laem? | Plai Laem is the north-east tip of Koh Samui, the headland between Big Buddha (Bang Rak) and Choeng Mon on the Route 4171 loop, in Bo Phut subdistrict. Wat Plai Laem, at 9.5713, 100.0673, is its best-known landmark.
Can you swim at Plai Laem? | Only in places. The shore is mostly resort frontage with shallow water that varies from one hotel to the next, so many hotels here rely on their pools. For a proper swim, Choeng Mon's calm bay is 5 minutes away by car.
Is Plai Laem close to the airport? | Yes. Samui Airport is about 3–4 km away, roughly 5 minutes by car, which makes Plai Laem one of the most convenient bases for a first or last night. Some rooms get aircraft noise, so ask whether yours is under the flight path.
What is there to see in Plai Laem? | Wat Plai Laem, a lakeside Thai-Chinese temple with an 18-armed Guanyin and a laughing Buddha, and Big Buddha on the Koh Fan islet 1 km to the west. Both are free; allow about 2 hours for the pair, ideally before 09:30.
Is Plai Laem a good base for the Koh Phangan ferries? | Yes. The Big Buddha pier, where the Haad Rin Queen leaves for Haad Rin at 10:30 and 17:00, and the Bang Rak piers used by Lomprayah and Seatran are a few minutes' drive away. Check which "Bangrak pier" your ticket names.
Are there beach clubs in Plai Laem? | Yes. The Bang Rak and Plai Laem shore has several sunset beach clubs, including Chi Samui, Tembo Beach Club, Malika and 79 Beach Club, with DJ sets, day beds and fire shows. Expect minimum spends on day beds at weekends.
Do your tours pick up from Plai Laem? | Yes. Hotel pickup is included from most areas of Koh Samui, including Plai Laem; Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Bang Kao and Thong Krut (and other remote areas) add 400 THB per person, return.
Is Plai Laem good for families? | For a short stay, yes: it is close to the airport, the temples are easy with children and resort pools make up for the shallow shore. For daily beach time with small children, Choeng Mon next door is the better base, with the calmest water on the island.
