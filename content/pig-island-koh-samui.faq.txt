Where is Pig Island on Koh Samui? | Pig Island is Koh Madsum, a small island off Koh Samui's south-west coast, next to Koh Tan. Boats leave from Thong Krut pier and take 10–15 minutes by speedboat or 20–30 minutes by longtail.
How do you get to Pig Island from Koh Samui? | Join a tour from Thong Krut pier, which includes a return hotel transfer from most areas of Koh Samui, or charter a private longtail or speedboat. There is no public ferry to Koh Madsum.
How much does a Pig Island tour cost? | Our shared speedboat tour costs 1,450 THB per adult and 1,250 THB per child aged 3–11. The small-group longtail is 1,750 THB, the sunset tour 1,550 THB, and private boats start at 5,950 THB per boat for up to 8 guests.
Is there an entrance fee for Koh Madsum? | Yes, about 50 THB per person, paid in cash on arrival. It is included on our small-group longtail and sunset tours; on the speedboat tour and the private trips you pay it on the island.
Can you swim with the pigs on Koh Madsum? | You can stand in the shallows while the pigs wade nearby, and they sometimes swim to cool off. Don't chase, lift or ride them, give piglets space and wear water shoes, because their hooves are sharp.
Is Pig Island open in November? | Yes. Pig Island trips run every day of the year, weather permitting, including November and December while Ang Thong is closed. Thong Krut is on the sheltered south-west coast, away from the north-east monsoon swell.
Is it ethical to visit Pig Island? | It can be, if visitors treat the pigs as animals rather than props. Our crews stop guests from chasing, lifting or riding the pigs and allow feeding only with food the guide approves; morning and late-afternoon visits avoid the midday heat.
Speedboat or longtail: which Pig Island tour is better? | The speedboat (1,450 THB, 4–5 hours) is faster and drier. The small-group longtail (1,750 THB, about 6 hours, max 12 guests) is slower and more traditional, includes the island fee and lunch on Koh Tan, but has more spray and less shade.
Can we book a private Pig Island trip? | Yes. A private longtail costs 5,950 THB per boat for up to 8 guests, a private speedboat 11,950 THB for up to 10, and the three-island longtail with Koh Rap 8,450 THB for up to 8, all with a private hotel transfer.
Is the Pig Island tour good for kids? | Yes, it is our most family-friendly trip: a short crossing, shallow water and easy snorkelling at Koh Tan. Children's life jackets are always provided and worn, infants aged 0–2 travel free, and landings are a short wade onto sand.
