Why go: a big, comfortable boat for a big group

Most private yachts on Koh Samui take 10–15 guests. Serenity takes 25 for day charters at one price, with a covered lounge, a sun deck, a slide and four air-conditioned cabins. That makes it a strong choice for birthdays, weddings, family reunions and company outings, where the cost per guest drops quickly.

What you'll do

You choose the length and we agree the route. A 2-hour sunset sails the north coast past the Big Buddha. A half day reaches Koh Som or the south-east coast of Koh Phangan. A full day can cross to Phangan's north-east bays and snorkel at Koh Ma, head south to Koh Tan and Koh Madsum, or from 16 December visit Ang Thong Marine Park. The overnight anchors in a quiet bay with the cabins for up to 10 guests.

Who it suits

  • Groups of 15–25 who want to stay together on one boat.
  • Events with catering: there is space for a buffet and a BBQ on board.
  • Families with grandparents or small children who need shade and a cabin.

Who should choose something else

  • Couples and small groups: a smaller yacht such as Nina or Kindred Spirit costs far less.
  • Anyone wanting to sail: Serenity is a motor catamaran.

Good to know

  • Season: February to September has the calmest sea; October to December is windier and routes may change.
  • Ang Thong: the park is closed from 1 November to 15 December.
  • Planning: for groups over 15, book at least a week ahead in high season (mid-December to April).

Before you book

Send us your date, group size, the length of charter and any catering ideas. We check the boat's calendar, since it also runs scheduled shared trips on Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, and confirm the route with the captain before you pay.
