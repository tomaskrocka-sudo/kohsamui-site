Is the sea rough on Koh Samui today? | Check the live panel at the top of this page: it shows today's wave height, swell and wind with a Calm, Choppy or Rough verdict. In the north-east monsoon (October to January) the east coast is often rough while the west and south-west coasts stay calmer.
When is the sea calmest around Koh Samui? | February to May, and March and April in particular, when typical daily wave highs are only 0.3–0.5 m. June to September is also good on most days, with some afternoon chop on the west coast.
What wave height cancels boat trips on Koh Samui? | There is no single number: the captain decides each morning from the TMD forecast, any warning in force and the actual sea on the route. As a guide, trips run in waves below about 0.5 m, get bumpy up to about 1.25 m, and exposed routes are cancelled above that or under a marine warning.
Do ferries to Koh Phangan run in the monsoon? | Yes, on most days. The Samui–Phangan crossing takes 20–50 minutes and ferries run in most weather, but storms cause delays and occasional cancellations, mostly from November to January. Leave a spare day before an international flight.
Why is Ang Thong closed from November to mid-December? | The Department of National Parks closes Ang Thong from 1 November to 15 December every year for visitor safety in the north-east monsoon and to rest the reefs. The park reopens on 16 December and can also close for a day or two when storms are forecast.
Which side of Koh Samui is calmer in the monsoon? | The west and south-west, in the lee of the island: Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam and Thong Krut. That is why Pig Island trips from Thong Krut run on most monsoon days while Chaweng and Lamai fly red flags.
~Is the speedboat or big boat better if I get seasick? | The big boat. It takes longer but rolls more gently, while a speedboat slams over swell. On the Ang Thong run the big boat takes about 1.5 hours each way against the speedboat's hour; take a tablet 30–60 minutes before boarding either way.
What happens if my tour is cancelled for weather? | If we cancel because the sea is unsafe, you choose a full refund or a new date, and we tell you by WhatsApp or email as early as possible. Rain alone doesn't cancel a trip; only unsafe sea or an official warning does.
