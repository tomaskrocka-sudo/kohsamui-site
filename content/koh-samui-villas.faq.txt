How much does a private pool villa on Koh Samui cost? | In low season most 2–3 bedroom pool villas in our listings start at 8,000–20,000 THB per night for the whole villa; 4–5 bedrooms 15,000–40,000 THB. Peak season (Christmas to mid-January, July–August) costs more and needs longer minimum stays.
Are the villa prices and availability up to date? | Yes. Rates, minimum stays, house rules and availability for the next 12 weeks are refreshed every hour, and the dates you pick on a villa page are priced live.
Do I pay when I send a booking request? | No. We first confirm availability and the final price with the villa, then send a secure payment link in THB. The deposit and balance follow the villa's payment terms shown on its page.
Which area is best for a villa on Koh Samui? | Chaweng Noi and Choeng Mon for calm bays near the airport and restaurants, Bophut for Fisherman's Village, Maenam for value and quiet, and Taling Ngam or Lipa Noi for sunsets and the most privacy.
Can I book fewer bedrooms in a large villa? | Often yes. Villas listed as, for example, "3–5 bedrooms" can be booked with fewer rooms at a lower rate; unused bedrooms are locked and the villa stays private.
Is electricity included in the villa price? | It depends on the villa. Some include electricity, others charge per unit used. Each villa page lists what is included and which services cost extra.
Can you arrange airport transfers, a chef or boat trips for our villa? | Yes. Add it to your request or message us on WhatsApp; we arrange transfers, drivers, private chefs and our own boat trips to Ang Thong and Pig Island.
How far ahead should I book a villa for Christmas and New Year? | Three to six months ahead for the best choice. Minimum stays over the festive period are usually 4–7 nights.
