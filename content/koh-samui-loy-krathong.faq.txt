When is Loy Krathong 2026? | Loy Krathong 2026 is on Tuesday 24 November, the full moon of the twelfth Thai lunar month. Some English-language sites say 25 November, but Thai calendars and the moon itself put it on the 24th. It is also the night of the Full Moon Party on Koh Phangan. In 2027 it is expected on Saturday 13 November.
Where is the best place for Loy Krathong on Koh Samui? | Chaweng Lake, opposite Central Samui, is the main public spot, with stalls and crowds from about 19:00 to 21:00. The Big Buddha temple near Bang Rak is quieter and more spiritual. Calm north-coast beaches such as Bophut, Maenam and Bang Rak also work, and many resorts hold their own evening.
Can I release a sky lantern on Koh Samui? | No. Sky lanterns (khom loi) are banned on Koh Samui because of the fire risk, so don't buy or release one even if someone offers. Float a krathong on water instead, ideally made from bread or banana trunk and leaves. The famous lantern releases are in northern Thailand, under strict permits.
Where can I buy a krathong on Samui? | Stalls sell krathongs from the afternoon of 24 November around Chaweng Lake, at fresh and night markets and at roadside stands. Many hotels sell them or run krathong-making workshops. Choose a bread or banana-leaf krathong fixed with toothpicks, and avoid foam ones completely.
Is Loy Krathong a public holiday? | No. Loy Krathong is not a public holiday in Thailand, so shops, banks and offices stay open, tours run as normal and there is no alcohol-sales ban. Celebrations take place in the evening after dark, so you can do a day trip and still float a krathong at night.
Can I do Loy Krathong and the Full Moon Party on the same night? | Yes. Float a krathong at Chaweng Lake or your hotel between about 18:30 and 20:00, then take a later boat to Haad Rin. Our shared Full Moon speedboat departs in an 18:00–23:00 window and costs 1,400 THB return with hotel pickup from most areas. Party entry, about 200 THB, is paid at the beach.
What happens if it rains on Loy Krathong? | Celebrations go ahead in light rain, with people floating krathongs under umbrellas; heavy rain thins the crowds and moves hotel events indoors. November is Samui's wettest month, with about 445 mm of rain on average, so bring a rain jacket. Full Moon boats only cross when the sea is safe.
