Where can I rent a kayak on Koh Samui? | At Coral Cove on the east coast for about 300 THB an hour, from beach vendors in Chaweng and Choeng Mon, and at many beachfront hotels, which often lend kayaks free to guests. For the best paddling, kayaks are included on every Ang Thong tour we sell.
How much is kayak or SUP rental on Samui? | A kayak at Coral Cove costs about 300 THB an hour in 2026. Stand-up paddleboards are mostly rented by delivery to your hotel or beach, with daily and weekly rates quoted on request.
Is kayaking included on the Ang Thong tour? | Yes, on all our Ang Thong tours: speedboat (1,800 THB), big boat (1,500 THB), VIP, overnight and private. The park fee of 300 THB per adult is paid in cash, and the park is closed from 1 November to 15 December 2026.
When is the sea calm enough to paddle on Koh Samui? | February to April has the flattest water, and January to September is good on most mornings. From October to December the east coast is rough with red flags; the north and west coasts are calmer but watch for offshore wind.
Can children kayak on Koh Samui? | Yes, from about 4 or 5 in the middle seat of a double kayak with an adult and a fitted child life jacket. Choeng Mon on a calm morning and the mangrove kayak on our jungle safari are the gentlest places to start.
Can I paddle to Koh Fan Noi from Choeng Mon? | Yes, on a calm morning: it is a short paddle of a few hundred metres from the north end of the beach. At low tide a sandbar joins the island to the beach; check the tide and avoid it in the October–December monsoon.
Is SUP good on Koh Samui? | Yes, on flat early mornings in the calm north and north-east bays such as Choeng Mon, Maenam and Bophut. The breeze builds by early afternoon, and the east coast is too rough from October to December.
Where can I kayak when Ang Thong is closed? | Around Koh Tan and Koh Rap from a private boat out of Thong Krut, in the mangroves on our jeep safari with kayak, or at Choeng Mon on calm mornings. Ang Thong reopens on 16 December.
