Why go: a comfortable sailing catamaran for sleeping on board

Kindred Spirit is a Fountaine Pajot Belize 43, a 14 m cruising catamaran with air-conditioned cabins. On day sails it is one of the better-value private sailing boats on Koh Samui for up to 15 guests; its real strength is the overnights, which take small groups to anchor in Ang Thong Marine Park and wake up among the islands after the day boats have left.

What you'll do

Day sails go to Koh Phangan or south to Koh Tan and Koh Madsum, with snorkelling, swimming and fishing. The 2-day trip sails about four hours to Ang Thong, spends the afternoon and night there, then returns via Koh Phangan. Longer trips add Koh Tan, Koh Phangan or Koh Tao and the Koh Nang Yuan viewpoint.

Who it suits

  • Couples and families of up to 4–6 who want a first sailing holiday.
  • Groups of up to 15 who want a full day under sail at a moderate price.

Who should choose something else

  • Travellers who want everything catered: the Independence sailing yacht includes drinks and a hostess.
  • Anyone wanting Ang Thong in a day: the Ang Thong private speedboat is quicker.

Good to know

  • Ang Thong: the park is closed from 1 November to 15 December, so overnight dates start on 16 December.
  • Packing: soft bags store better than suitcases in a catamaran cabin.
  • Sea: overnight anchorages are chosen for shelter from the wind; the skipper may change them on the day.

Before you book

For overnights, tell us how many guests, your dietary needs and whether you want us to arrange provisions or a chef. For day sails, tell us your hotel so we can confirm whether the van price applies to your area.
