Why go: skip the ferry queue and the late-night crossing

Most travellers reach Samui from the mainland on a coach-and-ferry combination. It is cheap, but it is slow, has fixed times and can leave you stuck in Surat Thani after a late flight. A private speedboat leaves when your group is ready, from whichever of the two mainland piers suits you, and lands on Koh Samui or Koh Phangan in about 1.5–2 hours. For a group of 8–10 with flights into Surat Thani, the boat turns a half-day journey into a short transfer at a time you choose.

Tha Pee is the quick option from Surat Thani airport, at 35–45 minutes by road. Donsak is the main car-ferry port, further away but cheaper by boat. Both run either direction, so this works just as well for leaving the islands to catch a mainland flight or train.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: groups of 6–10, families with a lot of luggage, and travellers landing at Surat Thani late in the day.
  • Choose something else if: you're 1–4 people. The scheduled ferries cost a small fraction of this, and flying direct to Samui airport often costs less than a share of the boat. Compare every route in our Surat Thani to Koh Samui guide.
  • Prone to seasickness: a small boat on open water for 2 hours is hard going in the monsoon. Consider the car ferry from October to December.

Good to know

  • Flight delays: send us your flight number so the boat can be timed, and allow a buffer. Arrivals from 17:30 carry the late fee because the crossing ends in darkness.
  • Peak season: a high-season fee applies between 20 December 2026 and 20 January 2027. Book early for those dates.
  • Luggage: pack valuables and electronics in a dry bag. Spray reaches the deck.
