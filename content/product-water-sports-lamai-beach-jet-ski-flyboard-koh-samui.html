Why go: adrenaline in short, easy doses

Lamai beach on Koh Samui's east coast is one of the island's main spots for motorised water sports. You can book a short jet ski ride, try a flyboard (standing on a board lifted by water jets, after a few minutes of training), ride a banana boat with friends, try wakeboarding, or go parasailing over the bay. Packages combine several activities in one session. An instructor, life jackets and accident insurance are included.

The partner's published prices are inconsistent between activities, so we confirm the price of exactly what you want before you pay.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: groups of friends, teenagers with a parent, and anyone staying in Lamai who wants a quick thrill.
  • Choose something else if you want a longer ride to another island: the jet ski safari to Pig Island is guided and more of an adventure.
  • Prefer quieter water sports? See kayaking and SUP.

Good to know

  • Jet ski disputes: beach jet ski rental on Samui has a record of damage claims. Photograph the machine, get the terms in writing and never hand over your passport.
  • Swimmers: stay outside the buoyed swimming zone and well clear of people in the water.
  • Season: the east coast is calmest from February to September; from October to December swell and red flags often stop activities.
  • Alcohol: don't ride after drinking; insurance does not pay out.

Choosing an activity

First-timers usually enjoy the flyboard most: after a short briefing many people are standing within a few minutes. Parasailing needs steady wind and calm water, so it often stops in the afternoon. Packages are better value if you want to try three or more activities in one session.
