Do taxis on Koh Samui use meters? | No; although many have a meter sign on the roof, Samui taxis charge fixed rank prices or a fare you agree before getting in, so always settle the price first.
How much is a taxi from Samui airport to Chaweng? | Roughly 400–600 THB per car at the official airport kiosk, about 10–15 minutes; a shared minibus costs about 100–250 THB per person.
Is there Grab on Koh Samui? | Yes, Grab works best in Chaweng, Bophut and Lamai, with short hops of about 80–120 THB, but coverage is thin on the west coast and pickups at the airport and piers are often resisted by local taxis.
Does Bolt work on Koh Samui? | Bolt operates in Thailand and has a promotion at Central Samui, but it has far fewer drivers on Samui than Grab, so do not rely on it outside Chaweng.
How much is a songthaew on Koh Samui? | Locals pay 30–80 THB along the ring road, for example 30–40 THB from Chaweng to Lamai; visitors are often charged 50–150 THB, paid in cash when you get off.
Do songthaews run at night? | Only rarely; they thin out after about 20:00 and largely stop by 22:00, after which you need a taxi, Grab or a night songthaew charging taxi-style prices.
Do I need a licence to ride a scooter on Koh Samui? | Yes, you need your home licence plus an International Driving Permit with the motorcycle category; without it you can be fined and your travel insurance may not pay out.
Can I get around Koh Samui without renting anything? | Yes, if you stay in Chaweng, Lamai or Fisherman's Village you can walk locally, use songthaews by day and Grab or taxis at night, and book tours with hotel pickup.
How much is a driver for a full day? | A taxi or private driver for a full day costs about 2,000–3,000 THB per car, enough for a round-island tour with stops.
Do your tours include hotel pickup? | Yes, hotel pickup is included from most areas of Koh Samui; remote areas such as Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Bang Kao and Thong Krut add 400 THB per person return on Chaweng-side departures.
