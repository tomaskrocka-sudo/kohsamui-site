Why go: a true luxury motor yacht, fast

A Sunseeker Manhattan 60 is a British-built flybridge motor yacht designed for exactly this kind of day: fast runs between islands, a big sun deck, an air-conditioned saloon and three cabins below. Minor Affair cruises at about 23 knots, so Koh Tao, the furthest day destination, is about 1.5 hours from Samui. A captain, mate and hostess look after up to 6 guests at the listed price, with lunch served on board.

The sunset charter is a short, private evening on the water. Day charters go to Koh Phangan's coast, Ang Thong (from 16 December) or Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan. The overnight charter combines Koh Tao, a sunset beach in Ang Thong and a BBQ dinner, with the night spent at anchor and the return the following afternoon.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: honeymooners, milestone celebrations, villa groups who want a high-end private day, and corporate hosting.
  • Choose something else if: you want luxury without the price. A large sailing catamaran such as Coco Paradise takes far more guests for much less. For a sunset on a budget, see our sunset cruise guide.

Good to know

  • Price: listed prices are before 10% service charge and 7% VAT, so budget about 18% on top. We quote the exact total before you pay.
  • Season: February to September is best for Koh Tao and overnight trips. From October to December, plan the sunset or a Koh Phangan day.
  • Guests: the yacht holds up to 12. Ask us for pricing above 6.
  • On deck: go barefoot or wear soft-soled shoes. Hard soles and heels mark the teak.
  • Transfers: the return transfer is included, so you only need to be ready at your hotel or villa.
