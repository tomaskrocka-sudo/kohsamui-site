What is the weather like on Koh Samui today? | The live widget at the top of this page shows current temperature, rain and wind for the island centre, refreshed every 30 minutes from Open-Meteo. As a rule, expect 29–33 °C by day and about 24–26 °C at night all year, with a sea temperature of 28–30 °C.
What is the best month to visit Koh Samui? | February. It is the driest month (about 64 mm of rain over 4 rain days) with the calmest sea, which makes it the best month for Ang Thong and island hopping. March and April are almost as good, and April is the hottest.
When is the rainy season on Koh Samui? | October to December, with the north-east monsoon. October averages about 295 mm of rain, November about 445 mm and December about 263 mm. Samui is largely sheltered from the May–October monsoon that soaks Phuket and Krabi.
Does it rain all day in November? | Usually not. November has about 16 rain days (1 mm or more), so roughly half the month stays dry, but the rain tends to come in long, heavy bursts and some days are wet from morning to night. Plan indoor backups and check the hourly forecast rather than the daily icon.
Is Koh Samui good in July and August? | Yes. While the Andaman coast is in monsoon, Samui has mostly sunny days, short showers (about 100–120 mm a month) and calm sea for boat trips. It is busy and hotel prices rise with European school holidays, so book early.
Is December a good time to visit Koh Samui? | It improves as the month goes on: rain falls from about 445 mm in November to about 263 mm in December and usually eases through the month. Ang Thong reopens on 16 December, and Christmas and New Year bring peak prices.
Does Koh Samui get typhoons? | Full typhoons are rare in the southern Gulf of Thailand, but tropical storms can pass between late October and January. Tropical Storm Pabuk in January 2019 suspended ferries and closed Ang Thong for several days. Follow Thai Meteorological Department warnings if a storm is forecast.
When is Ang Thong Marine Park closed? | Every year from 1 November to 15 December, for the monsoon and reef recovery, and occasionally at short notice during storms. It reopens on 16 December; our Ang Thong tours can be booked for any date from then.
How warm is the sea around Koh Samui? | Between about 28 °C (December to February) and 30 °C (April to July) all year, so you never need a wetsuit for swimming or snorkelling. Divers may want a thin 3 mm suit for repeated dives.
What happens to my tour if the weather is bad? | If the captain decides the sea is unsafe, we cancel and contact you by WhatsApp or email, and you choose a full refund or a new date. If the sea is choppy but safe, the trip runs as planned.
