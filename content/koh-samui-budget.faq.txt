Is Koh Samui expensive compared with Phuket? | The biggest difference is getting there: Bangkok Airways runs every domestic flight into Samui (about 2,800–6,800 THB one way from Bangkok), while Phuket is served by many low-cost airlines, so Samui is usually dearer to reach. On the ground, both islands have cheap street food and expensive resorts, so the area and style you choose matter more than the island.
How much money do I need per day on Koh Samui? | Our 2026 estimates, per person sharing a double room and excluding flights: about 1,500–2,500 THB a day on a budget, 4,000–7,000 THB mid-range and 15,000 THB or more for luxury. Add boat days separately, for example 1,450 THB for Pig Island or 1,800 THB plus a 300 THB park fee for Ang Thong.
How much does food cost on Koh Samui? | Street dishes cost 50–120 THB (pad thai 60–80 THB, mango sticky rice 50–80 THB), a market dinner with a drink about 300–500 THB, a western meal in a café 200–500 THB, and a beachfront or resort dinner from about 500 THB per person upwards, often plus 10% service and 7% VAT.
What is the cheapest boat trip from Koh Samui? | Among our shared tours, the Pig Island and Koh Tan speedboat tour at 1,450 THB per adult (1,250 THB per child), 4–5 hours from Thong Krut, plus about 50 THB for Koh Madsum where not included. It runs on any date of the year, weather permitting.
Are there free things to do on Koh Samui? | Yes: Big Buddha (07:00–18:30), Wat Plai Laem, Hin Ta and Hin Yai (parking about 30 THB), Wat Khunaram, the Lad Koh viewpoint, Na Muang Waterfall 1 (parking 20–60 THB), every public beach and the Friday walking street in Fisherman's Village.
Do restaurants add service charge? | Resorts, upmarket restaurants and spas often show prices with "++", which adds a 10% service charge and then 7% VAT, about 17.7% in total. Street stalls and local Thai restaurants usually quote net prices with nothing added.
Should I pay by card or cash on Koh Samui? | Both. Use a card in hotels, malls, 7-Eleven and most restaurants, and carry cash for street food, songthaews, many taxis, massage shops and island fees. Always pay and withdraw in THB, and use AEON ATMs (150 THB fee) to save on the usual 220–250 THB charge.
When is Koh Samui cheapest? | October and November, the rainy season, have the lowest room rates; Ang Thong is closed from 1 November to 15 December, but Pig Island runs all year. May–June and September offer better weather at moderate prices. The dearest period is late December to early January.
