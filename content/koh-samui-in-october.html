October is the month the north-east monsoon arrives on Koh Samui. Rain jumps to about 295 mm over 16 rain days, often in heavy bursts, with highs of 31 °C, lows of 24 °C and a 29 °C sea. It is also the last month to visit Ang Thong Marine Park, which closes every year from 1 November to 15 December. Rooms are at their cheapest of the year, along with November. The Full Moon Party moves to Saturday 16 October 2027 because of Ok Phansa.

Good to know: Ang Thong Marine Park is closed every year from 1 November to 15 December, and 31 October is the last day of the season. If Ang Thong is on your list, book the Ang Thong speedboat tour (1,800 THB plus the 300 THB park fee in cash) for early in the month and early in your stay. The Pig Island speedboat tour (1,450 THB) runs every day, weather permitting.

The verdict

October suits flexible travellers who want low prices and one last chance at Ang Thong. It does not suit a short beach holiday with fixed boat days.

Good for:

  • The lowest room rates of the year, with November
  • Ang Thong before the 1 November closure, best early in the month
  • Pig Island and Koh Tan from sheltered Thong Krut
  • Waterfalls starting to fill, and green hills
  • Ok Phansa, the Chak Phra temple-float procession in Nathon and the Vegetarian Festival
  • The Full Moon Party on Saturday 16 October 2027

Not for:

  • A trip with only one possible boat day
  • Reliable swimming on Chaweng and Lamai, where the first red flags appear
  • Calm crossings to Koh Tao on every day
  • Busy nightlife: early October is one of the quietest times of the year

If you can choose, come in September, with well under half of October's rain. If your dates are fixed, come, but put boat days early and keep two flexible days. See Koh Samui in September and Koh Samui weather by month.

What October rain looks like

October is the turn of the seasons. The wind swings round from the south-west to the north-east and brings the year's rain. It often comes in heavy bursts rather than short tropical showers, but with 16 rain days on average, about half the month stays dry. Waterfalls fill and low-lying roads can flood after the heaviest downpours. Full typhoons are rare, but tropical storms and depressions pass most often between late October and January.

The sea is less predictable than the rain. Model data for 2022–2025 gives typical daily wave highs of only about 0.4 m, no higher than in August, and the wind between the monsoons is often light and variable. The averages hide two things. Thunderstorms can bring 30-knot gusts for half an hour on an otherwise calm day, and the first north-east swell brings red flags to Chaweng and Lamai. When the red flag is up, stay out of the water; swimmers have drowned after ignoring them. The north and west coasts stay mostly calm.

Ang Thong's last month

Ang Thong Marine Park closes every year from 1 November to 15 December, for the monsoon and to rest the reefs. The last operating day is 31 October, and the new season starts on 16 December. October is one of the riskiest months for weather cancellations on the Ang Thong speedboat, and on windy days the speedboat bounces over the swell for most of the hour's crossing.

  • Go early. Book for the first half of October if you can, and for the first days of your stay, so a cancelled trip can move to a later date before the closure.
  • Know the rules. If we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date; late in October, a new date means 16 December or later. If you cancel yourself, Ang Thong tours are refunded in full 48 hours or more before departure, and not at all under 48 hours.
  • Choose the boat for the sea. The big boat (1,500 THB) is slower but rolls more gently in a swell, which makes it the better October choice if you get seasick. The VIP tour (4,500 THB) has only 15 guests on a big speedboat. See speedboat or big boat.
  • Overnight trips in a tent (3,600 THB) or park bungalow (4,200 THB) also run until the closure.

Ok Phansa marks the end of Buddhist Lent and falls on about Friday 15 October 2027 in published Thai calendars. It is an alcohol-sales ban day: 7-Eleven, supermarkets and unlicensed restaurants can't sell alcohol, while licensed hotels and bars in tourist areas may still serve. See drugs and alcohol law.

The Full Moon Party moves off Ok Phansa to Saturday 16 October 2027. Many websites still list 15 October, which is a ban day with no party. The date is provisional until the Haad Rin business association confirms it. Our shared return Full Moon Party speedboat costs 1,400 THB per person, with hotel pickup and drop-off from most areas of Samui; entry (about 200 THB) is paid in cash at the beach. See Full Moon Party dates.

Chak Phra and Thod Pha Pa: around the end of Lent, temple floats from around 16 Samui temples are pulled through Nathon to the pier, with a fair of about nine days. Past editions ran 18–26 October 2022 and 6–14 November 2023, so the 2027 dates may fall in either month. Expect the Nathon ferry piers to be busy on procession day. See Nathon.

Vegetarian Festival: the annual festival falls in October. Look for yellow flags with red Thai lettering, which mark "jay" food: plant-based, without garlic or onion. It is easiest to find around the Chinese shrines in Nathon, Maenam and Hua Thanon.

Looking ahead: the Full Moon Party on Saturday 13 November 2027 is probably also Loy Krathong night; see Loy Krathong on Koh Samui and the party calendar.

Where to stay in October

  • Don't choose a hotel only for its east-coast beach. Chaweng and Lamai see the first red flags of the monsoon. If you stay there, pick a hotel with a good pool.
  • Choeng Mon is a sheltered bay in the north-east, 5–10 minutes from the airport, and often still swimmable when Chaweng is flagged.
  • The west coast (Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam) lies on the far side of the island from the north-east wind and is the calmest coast as the monsoon sets in. It is 40–50 minutes from the airport, and from October the beaches are full by day, because low tides move away from daylight hours.
  • Bophut and Fisherman's Village are good for wet evenings, with covered restaurants close together.
  • Villas: look for a covered terrace and a paved access road, as steep concrete drives are slippery in the rain.

Prices in October

October and November are the cheapest months of the year for rooms, because they are the wettest. Hotels that charge 30–80% more in the peak seasons run their lowest rates now, and 2–4 weeks ahead is usually enough for flights. Daily costs stay the same: street dishes 50–120 THB, a one-hour Thai massage 300–500 THB, a scooter 150–350 THB a day. Some cafés and restaurants shorten their hours or take a break in September and October, so call ahead before driving across the island. See is Koh Samui expensive?

Getting here and around in October

Direct flights from Bangkok Suvarnabhumi take about 1h05–1h15, with roughly 20–25 a day. Ferry operators change their timetables at least twice a year, usually around the October/November and April/May changes, so check times close to your date. Ferries to Koh Phangan and Koh Tao run on choppier water from October to December. Leave a spare day between any island hop and an international flight.

On the island, heavy rain floods low-lying roads, mainly inland and in the south-west, and hill roads turn slippery. Most scooter crashes on Samui happen on wet roads, so wait out downpours in a café. See getting around Koh Samui.

Things to do on a wet October day

  • Na Muang Waterfall as the flow rises; stay out of the water when it is brown and fast. See waterfalls.
  • A Thai cooking class or a long spa session.
  • Muay Thai in Chaweng: both stadiums are covered, so fights go ahead in the rain (tickets from 1,500 THB). See fight nights.
  • Temples between showers: Big Buddha, Wat Plai Laem and Wat Khunaram. See temples.

What to pack

  • A proper rain jacket or poncho and an umbrella.
  • Quick-dry clothes and sandals that grip on wet tiles.
  • A dry bag for your phone and passport on boats and scooters.
  • Insect repellent: mosquitoes are worst after rain, and dengue peaks in rainy periods.
  • Seasickness tablets for boat days.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen: the sun is strong between showers.
