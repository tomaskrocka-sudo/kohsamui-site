Is July a good time to visit Koh Samui? | Yes for the weather, less so for prices. July has highs of 32 °C, about 117 mm of rain over 11 days in brief showers, a calm 30 °C sea and all the main boat trips running. European school holidays begin, so rooms run 30–80% above shoulder rates and boats should be booked a few days ahead.
~Is Koh Samui good in July and August? | Yes. Samui is sheltered from the south-west monsoon that brings rain to Phuket and Krabi from about May to October. July and August see short showers and a calm sea on most days, but they are peak months for crowds and room rates.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in July? | About 117 mm on 1991–2020 averages, over about 11 days with 1 mm or more. Most of it falls in brief showers between sunny spells, against about 445 mm in November.
Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in July? | Yes. Ang Thong closes only from 1 November to 15 December. July is one of the park's busiest months, so book a few days ahead. Our speedboat tour costs 1,800 THB (child 1,400 THB) plus the park fee in cash: 300 THB per adult and 150 THB per child.
When is the Full Moon Party in July 2027? | Tuesday 20 July 2027. The full moon falls on Asahna Bucha (Sunday 18 July), followed by Khao Phansa (Monday 19 July), both alcohol-ban days, so the party moves to the Tuesday. Our shared return speedboat costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup and drop-off from most areas of Samui.
Can I buy alcohol on Koh Samui on 18 and 19 July 2027? | Not in shops. Asahna Bucha and Khao Phansa are alcohol-sales ban days, so 7-Eleven, supermarkets and unlicensed restaurants can't sell alcohol. Since May 2025 licensed hotels and bars in tourist areas may still serve. One calendar puts both holidays a day later, so check closer to the date.
Is the sea rough on Koh Samui in July? | Not usually. Typical daily wave heights are about 0.5 m on the east coast, and boat trips run on most days. Afternoons are often windy, with chop on the west and south-west coasts, so morning departures are smoother.
Is July a good month for families on Koh Samui? | Yes, and it is busy with them. The sea is calm, the east-coast beaches are sheltered, and children aged 3–11 pay a child rate on our boat trips, with infants free and children's life jackets always provided. Book rooms and boats early.
Can you see whale sharks near Koh Samui in July? | Possibly. Whale sharks visit Sail Rock, and in July 2026 the Koh Tao Tourism Association named June to September as the peak for sightings, but they are never guaranteed. Visibility in July is typically 10–25 m.
Is June or July cheaper on Koh Samui? | June. It is shoulder season, while July and August are peak, with room rates 30–80% higher. The weather in the two months is much the same.
