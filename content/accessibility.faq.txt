Are your boat trips wheelchair accessible? | No, none of our shared boat trips is wheelchair accessible. Boarding means stepping down from a pier or across from a floating pontoon onto a moving boat, and some stops land on beaches in shallow water. If you can transfer with help and manage one step down, a private boat from a pier with a fixed walkway is the most realistic option; message us before booking.
Which boat trip from Koh Samui is easiest for limited mobility? | A private boat is easiest, because we choose the pier, the time and the route around your needs. Of the shared trips, the Pig Island tour from Thong Krut is the gentlest: short crossings in sheltered water on the south-west coast, flat beach stops and no climbs. The Pig Island speedboat tour costs 1,450 THB per adult in 2026.
Can I join the Ang Thong tour if I cannot manage the viewpoint climb? | Yes. Both climbs on the Ang Thong tour are optional. The viewpoint trail is 500 m of steep rock steps and roots that takes 25 to 45 minutes up, and the Emerald Lake stairs take 10 to 15 minutes. You can stay at the beach or on the boat instead. The day still lasts about 8 hours by speedboat, so plan for heat and motion.
Is a speedboat trip safe if I am pregnant or have back problems? | We advise against it. Speedboats can bump hard in waves, which is a risk in pregnancy and for back, neck or heart conditions and after recent surgery. Ask your doctor first. The Ang Thong big boat is a steadier ride, and the sea is calmest from February to April, so tell us your situation and we will suggest the gentlest option.
What accessibility standard does this website aim for? | We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA, the standard used by most public bodies. That means readable colour contrast in light and dark mode, full keyboard use with visible focus, labelled forms, real headings, alt text on images and prices in HTML tables rather than images. Maps, payment and WhatsApp are third-party tools we choose for accessibility but do not control.
How do I report an accessibility problem on this site? | Message us on WhatsApp +66 63 704 0888, daily 08:00 to 20:00 Thailand time, or use the email address on our Contact page. Tell us the page link, what you were trying to do and the device, browser or assistive technology you use. If you cannot book online, we take the booking on WhatsApp or email at the same price and terms.
