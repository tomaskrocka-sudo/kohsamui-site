Overlap Stone is a privately run viewpoint on the hill above Maret, about 3 km from Lamai, where two huge boulders balance on a granite outcrop reached by a suspension bridge. Entry is about 300 THB including a soft drink in recent reports (it was 200 THB earlier in 2026), it is open 08:00–18:00, and the walk from the car park is about 500 m. The neighbouring "Overlap Stone 2" has been closed since a forestry raid on 22 July 2026, and we don't recommend the elephant-feeding add-on on the way up.

Map

Overlap Stone is on the hillside inland from the ring road between Lamai and Hua Thanon, about 900 m from the Red Temple (Wat Sila Ngu) and 1.3 km from Hin Ta and Hin Yai as the crow flies.

The walk and the view

  1. 0 min Pay at the entrance by the car park; the fee includes a soft drink.
  2. 0–5 min On the way up you pass an enclosure where young elephants are kept for paid feeding. Walk on (see below).
  3. 5–10 min Follow the path about 500 m across the hillside, with steps and uneven sections.
  4. 10–12 min Cross the suspension bridge to the outcrop. Go one group at a time and hold the rails.
  5. At the stone The upper boulder, roughly 6–9 m across, appears to rest on the lower one at a single point. The view takes in Lamai Bay, the east coast and, on clear days, the open Gulf.

Most visitors spend 15–30 minutes at the stone taking photos, then have their drink at the café terrace. It is a good view and a fun bridge, but a short visit: plan about an hour in total.

The fee, then and now

Overlap Stone is a private business on private land, not a temple or national park. For years it ran on a tip jar or a small fee. In June 2022 provincial officials ordered it closed because it lacked a business licence and its bridge had no construction licence; it has since reopened. Entry was about 200 THB earlier in 2026 and about 300 THB including a soft drink in recent reports. Prices change without notice, so take cash and expect to pay around 300 THB.

Is it worth it? If you want a short, easy walk with a bridge and a photo, yes. If you just want a view over Lamai, Lad Koh and Khao Hua Jook Chedi are free; see the alternatives below.

Overlap Stone 2: closed

Overlap Stone 2, a separate, higher viewpoint on the same hill (previously run as "The Best Samui", about 400 m further north), was raided on 22 July 2026 by forestry officials, district officers and Bo Phut police, who found its buildings on forest land and seized 33 structures. It was still closed in September 2026 with no reopening date. Some maps and tour listings still show it as open, along with its old free shuttle truck from the car park by the Red Temple, 1.5 km below. Don't go: you'll find it shut. This page covers the original Overlap Stone only.

The elephants: why we skip them

On the path up, two young elephants are kept on chains in a small enclosure and offered as a paid feeding add-on. Chained young elephants in a roadside pen are a clear welfare red flag, and paying for feeding keeps the business going. We recommend you walk past.

If you want to see elephants on Samui, choose a sanctuary with no riding, no bathing and space for the animals to roam. Our Koh Samui elephant sanctuary guide explains what to look for.

Road and parking

The access road leaves the ring road in Maret and climbs the hill. It is paved but very steep near the top, with tight bends. Parking at the top is free but small, and it fills on busy mornings.

  • Scooter: manageable for experienced riders in dry weather. Ride up in low gear, ride down slowly and brake before the bends, not in them. Don't attempt it with a passenger if you are new to scooters.
  • Car: fine in a normal car, using first gear on the steepest part.
  • Taxi or Grab: the simplest choice from Lamai or Chaweng; ask the driver to wait or arrange a pickup time, because getting a car back down the hill can take a while.

See Koh Samui roads for more on steep hill roads.

Safety

  • Parts of the route, including around the stones, have no railings. Hold children's hands.
  • Don't climb on the stones themselves or lean over the edge for photos.
  • The bridge sways: cross steadily and don't bounce it.
  • Paths are slippery after rain, especially October–December.
  • Emergency: ambulance 1669, Tourist Police 1155.

Best time to visit

Arrive around 09:00–10:00, when it is cooler and before the jungle safaris and minibuses. The view faces east and north-east over Lamai, so morning light is clearer than late afternoon. February to September gives the clearest skies; in the October–December monsoon, cloud and rain can hide the view and the road is slippery. Today's weather: [kp_weather_now]

A Lamai hill morning

  1. 08:45 Drive or take a taxi up from Lamai; arrive as it opens for the coolest air.
  2. 09:00 Walk to the stone and cross the bridge before the groups arrive.
  3. 09:45 Your included drink on the terrace, looking over Lamai Bay.
  4. 10:15 Down to the Red Temple on the ring road (free, 07:00–17:00).
  5. 10:45 Hin Ta and Hin Yai and a coconut ice cream in the stall lane.
  6. 11:30 Swim at Lamai or snorkel at Silver Beach on a calm day.

Free alternatives

If 300 THB feels steep for a view, try these free viewpoints instead:

  • Lad Koh viewpoint on the ring road between Chaweng Noi and Coral Cove, open 24 hours.
  • Khao Hua Jook Chedi, a golden pagoda on a hill above Chaweng Lake.
  • The Big Buddha terrace, open 07:00–18:30.

Compare them all on Koh Samui hiking and viewpoints. Overlap Stone is also a stop on some jungle safaris; ask us which ones: [kp_wa]

For the best views of all, go to sea: the Pig Island speedboat tour (1,450 THB adult, 1,250 THB child) runs every day of the year. Hotel transfers are included from the main areas, including Lamai.
