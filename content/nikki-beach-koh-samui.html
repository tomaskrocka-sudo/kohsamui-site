Nikki Beach Koh Samui is the island's best-known beach club, part of a 48-suite resort on Lipa Noi beach on the west coast, 28 km and 35–45 minutes from Chaweng. The beach club and restaurant open daily from 11:00 to 19:00. The headline event is the Amazing Sundays brunch, 12:00–16:30. On Fridays there is a BBQ dinner and fire show at its Escape restaurant, 18:00–21:00. Entry is free, but day beds carry a minimum spend, the beach club is for over-16s only, and you should book ahead from December to April.

Nikki Beach sits in the middle of Lipa Noi bay, a few minutes south of the Raja car-ferry pier and 6 km south of Nathon. The ring road runs inland; the resort is down a short lane to the beach.

What it's like

Nikki Beach is the Samui branch of the international Nikki Beach brand, which started in Miami. Expect day beds and loungers around a pool by the sand, a DJ booth and a crowd that dresses up, with the music building from lunchtime towards sunset. White is the house colour, and its best-known one-off event is the White Party (the 2026 edition was on 28 March). It is a polished, upmarket day out. You won't find Chaweng's late-night party here: the beach club closes at 19:00 and the evening moves to Escape for dinner.

The beach in front is Lipa Noi, one of the calmest and shallowest on the island. The resort describes the water as shallow for up to 100 m. At high tide you can swim and paddle; at low tide the sea pulls back over wide sand flats, which look beautiful at sunset but mean a long walk to deep water. Check the tide before you go: [kp_tide_now]

The coast faces west, so the sun sets over the sea all year, which Chaweng and Lamai can't offer. Recent guest reviews are mostly positive about the setting, staff and food, but some complain that the poolside music is loud from noon to 18:00 and that service at the day beds can be slow on busy days.

The resort

Nikki Beach Resort & Spa has 48 suites and villas: pool-access suites (including two- and three-bedroom family suites), ocean-view penthouses, garden villa suites, and beachfront villas with private pools opening onto the sand, up to the Ultimate Beachfront Villa. Stays include breakfast, Wi-Fi, a 24-hour gym, yoga and daily activities, non-motorised watersports equipment, the Little Nikki kids' club (daily) and a scheduled shuttle to Fisherman's Village and Chaweng. The 75 m main pool and the resort beach area are for hotel guests only. Penthouse and Ultimate Beachfront Villa guests staying two nights or more get free private airport transfers. There is also a spa, a boutique and wedding services; see weddings on Koh Samui and honeymoons.

The beach-club menu is international: sushi, fresh seafood, salads and grills, with cocktails and Champagne as the main event. Brunch is a buffet with live cooking stations, sushi and seafood. Escape, open in the evening, serves local and Mediterranean fusion dishes such as snapper crudo and hummus with merguez, plus a seafood display priced at market rate and Australian beef sold by weight. Recent guest reviews describe the restaurant prices as on the high side, which matches what we see across Samui's five-star resorts.

Tips

  • Book ahead. Sunday brunch and front-row day beds fill first, especially from December to April and on festive dates. Bookings are made directly with the resort.
  • Ask about the minimum spend before you pay. It is prepaid and varies by bed and season. Check what it covers and what happens if you leave early or it rains.
  • Bring ID if you live on Samui. Residents get 20 % off day beds and food and drink with a resident card.
  • Time it with the tide. Swim at high tide; at low tide enjoy the pool and the sunset reflections on the flats.
  • Book your ride home before you go. Grab and taxis are scarce on the west coast after dark.

Who it suits, and who should go elsewhere

Good for: couples, groups of friends and celebrations; anyone who wants a long, stylish lunch, a pool and a sunset in one place; and families staying at the resort, who have the kids' club and the main pool.

Go elsewhere if:

  • You want a late party. The beach club closes at 19:00; for pool parties and clubs that run past midnight, go to Chaweng (see Chaweng nightlife and Koh Samui beach clubs).
  • You're on a budget. A sunset drink at a small Lipa Noi beach bar or at Nathon pier costs a fraction of the price; see Koh Samui sunset spots.
  • You have children under 16 and want the beach club itself. Non-guest families are better off at a family brunch in Chaweng or a beach with shallow water and cheap food; see Koh Samui with kids.
  • You're staying in the north-east. The round trip from Bang Rak or Choeng Mon is 1.5 hours; Chi Samui, Tembo and other north-coast clubs are much closer.

Getting there

Nikki Beach is on the far side of the island from most hotels, and transport is the part that most often goes wrong. Grab works in the daytime, but drivers are scarce on the west coast, especially after sunset, and taxis rarely wait.

Nearby

  • The Xperience, 5 minutes away: the island's new art park with a lagoon beach club, pools and a night light and fire show (Day Pass 1,500 THB).
  • Lipa Noi: the beach itself, the Raja ferry pier and quiet villas.
  • Nathon, 6 km north: the island's old town, the pier and a daily night food market.
  • Taling Ngam, about 5 km south: the classic Five Islands sunset; see also the Five Islands longtail sunset dinner.
  • Hin Lad waterfall, inland from Nathon, for a morning walk before an afternoon at the beach club.
  • Laem Yai and Bang Por on the north-west coast for quieter sunsets.
  • More west-coast evenings: sunset spots and sunset cruises.
