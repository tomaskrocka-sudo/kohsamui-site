Is Chaweng or Lamai better for a first visit? | Chaweng, for most people. It has the most restaurants, bars and shops, a gentle swimming beach at the north end and is 10–15 minutes from the airport. Lamai is better if you already know you prefer a quieter, cheaper base.
Which is quieter, Chaweng or Lamai? | Lamai. Its nightlife is pubs, live bands and beach bars, and the beach is less built-up. Central Chaweng is loud until about 02:00, though North Chaweng and Chaweng Noi are quiet at night.
Which beach is better for swimming, Chaweng or Lamai? | For most swimmers, North Chaweng: fine sand and a very gentle slope. Central Lamai is good but shelves steeply, and its north end is shallow and stony. Both get surf, rip currents and red flags from October to December.
Is Lamai cheaper than Chaweng? | Yes. Mid-range rooms cost about 1,500–3,500 THB a night in Lamai against 2,000–4,500 THB in Chaweng, and a Thai massage is about 300–500 THB against 400–600. Market food costs much the same in both.
How far is Lamai from Chaweng? | About 12 km along the coast road, 20–25 minutes by car. A daytime songthaew costs about 30–40 THB for locals and 50–60 THB is a fair tourist price; Grab is about 200–350 THB.
Which is better for families, Chaweng or Lamai? | North Chaweng for small children, thanks to its gentle slope and quiet nights. Lamai suits families with older children who swim well and want lower prices. For toddlers, Choeng Mon beats both.
Is Lamai good for nightlife compared with Chaweng? | For a relaxed night, yes: the daily night market, Shamrock Irish Pub's live band and fire shows at beach bars. For clubs and late parties, Chaweng's Soi Green Mango and ARKbar are 20–25 minutes away.
