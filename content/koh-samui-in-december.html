December on Koh Samui is a month of two halves. It averages about 263 mm of rain over 12 rain days, with highs of 29 °C, lows of 24 °C and a 28 °C sea, and the weather usually improves as the month goes on. Ang Thong Marine Park is closed until 15 December and reopens on Wednesday 16 December 2026. From about 20 December the island fills up for Christmas and New Year, with Full Moon Parties on Thursday 24 and Thursday 31 December.

Good to know: Ang Thong Marine Park is closed every year from 1 November to 15 December. Season reopens 16 December. Book now for later dates. Before the 16th, book the Pig Island speedboat tour (1,450 THB) or the Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan day trip (2,200 THB), sea permitting.

The verdict

December suits a cheaper first half for flexible travellers, or a festive second half for people who book early and accept peak prices. It does not suit a last-minute Christmas trip.

Good for:

  • Christmas and New Year's Eve fireworks and countdowns
  • Ang Thong from 16 December, at the start of the new season
  • Lower hotel rates in the first half of the month
  • The Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve Full Moon Parties
  • Half Moon Festival Magic Forest nights on 3 and 17 December
  • Waterfalls at strong flow, with fewer downpours by late December

Not for:

  • Ang Thong before 16 December
  • Guaranteed calm sea: December still has rough spells on the east coast
  • Cheap or last-minute rooms after about 20 December
  • A Koh Tao day trip squeezed in just before a flight home

If your dates are flexible, the second half of December gives better odds of sun and calm water, and January better still. If you want the driest weather, February to April is the best period; see Koh Samui weather by month.

Early December vs late December

The north-east monsoon is still blowing at the start of December, so the first two weeks can bring long grey spells and heavy bursts of rain. The second half is usually drier and the sea calmer, which is why Ang Thong reopens on the 16th. Tropical storms are rare but possible from late October to January: in December 2025 the Thai Meteorological Department warned of 2–3 m waves in the lower Gulf.

Christmas and New Year's Eve

Christmas is not a Thai public holiday, so markets, shops and tours run as normal, while resorts run Christmas Eve dinners and Christmas Day brunches. Prices marked "++" add 10% service and 7% VAT.

On New Year's Eve, Chaweng has the biggest party, with beach-club countdowns and fireworks along the bay. Central Samui held a free countdown with concerts and fireworks on NYE 2025, and the 2026 programme is usually announced in December. Fisherman's Village is more upmarket; Maenam and Choeng Mon suit families. Resort galas run from about 6,000 THB to 14,500++ THB per person, and many hotels make the gala compulsory for guests staying on 31 December, so check your booking for a gala supplement.

Getting home is the hard part: songthaews mostly stop by about 22:00 and taxis are scarce after midnight. Celebrate within walking distance of your hotel or pre-book a driver. Full details are in Christmas and New Year's Eve on Koh Samui.

Where to stay in December

  • First half of the month: don't choose a hotel only for its east-coast beach. Chaweng and Lamai still get red flags, so pick a hotel with a good pool there.
  • Choeng Mon is a sheltered bay in the north-east, 5–10 minutes from the airport, with some of the calmest water on Samui. It suits families and a quiet New Year's Eve.
  • Bophut and Fisherman's Village are best for dinners and the New Year's Eve fireworks over the bay, but book restaurant tables weeks ahead and swim elsewhere.
  • Chaweng is the place for the big countdown, if you don't mind traffic and noise.
  • The west coast (Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam) lies on the far side of the island from the north-east wind, with quiet sunsets, but it is 40–50 minutes from the airport.
  • Villas: Christmas to New Year has the highest rates and longer minimum stays, often 4–7 nights. Book months ahead.

Prices in December

December has two price levels. Early in the month, rooms are close to their low-season rates. From mid-December, peak-season rates run 30–80% above shoulder-season prices, and the dearest period is from about 20 December to early January, when rooms, flights and transfers all peak. Book flights early: Bangkok–Samui usually costs 2,800–6,800 THB one way. Everyday costs don't change: street dishes 50–120 THB, a one-hour Thai massage 300–500 THB, a scooter 150–350 THB a day. Getting here and around in December

Bangkok Airways flies from Bangkok about 20–25 times a day. Flights out on 1–2 January were full after New Year 2025, so book early or leave on 3 January, and pre-book your airport transfer. Ferries to Koh Phangan and Koh Tao run, but delays are possible in rough spells; leave a spare day between an island hop and an international flight. See how to get to Koh Samui.

On the island, low-lying roads can flood after heavy rain early in the month, and Chaweng Beach Road is jammed on New Year's Eve. See getting around Koh Samui.

What to pack

  • A light rain jacket or poncho, especially for the first half of the month.
  • A dry bag for your phone and passport on boats and scooters.
  • Seasickness tablets for boat days.
  • Insect repellent: mosquitoes are worst after rain, and dengue peaks in rainy periods.
  • Closed shoes for the Full Moon Party (there is broken glass on the sand) and a light layer for the boat back.
  • A thin wetsuit if you plan several dives a day: run-off and wind can cool the surface in December.
