What are the best tours from Koh Samui? | The three best boat days are Ang Thong Marine Park (speedboat 1,800 THB, big boat 1,500 THB, VIP 4,500 THB; open 16 December to 31 October), Pig Island and Koh Tan from Thong Krut (from 1,450 THB, every day of the year) and Koh Tao with Koh Nang Yuan (2,200 THB). On land, the most popular choices are a no-riding elephant sanctuary visit, a 4x4 jungle safari and a Thai cooking class.
How much do Koh Samui tours cost in 2026? | Shared boat trips cost 1,450–2,250 THB for Pig Island, 1,500–4,500 THB for Ang Thong day tours, 3,600–4,200 THB for an Ang Thong overnight and 2,200 THB for Koh Tao. Island activities start at 600 THB (go-karts) and 800 THB (ziplines), and private boats start at 5,950 THB per boat. Park and island fees are extra in cash: 300 THB for Ang Thong, 250 THB for Koh Nang Yuan and about 50 THB for Koh Madsum.
~Do your tours include hotel pickup? | Yes, from the main areas: Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut, Maenam, Choeng Mon, Bang Rak and Plai Laem. Remote areas, including Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Bang Kao, Thong Krut and Laem Yai, cost 400 THB per person return. Private boats include transfers only where the product says so, and you can always meet the boat at the pier.
Which tours run in November and early December? | Ang Thong Marine Park is closed from 1 November to 15 December 2026 and reopens on 16 December. In that window Pig Island and Koh Tan trips run every day from Thong Krut, weather permitting, Koh Tao runs with more weather cancellations, and land activities such as cooking classes, elephant sanctuaries, ziplines and Muay Thai run as normal.
Ang Thong speedboat or big boat: which is better? | The speedboat (1,800 THB) is faster, about 1 hour each way, and gets you back to the pier by 16:00–16:30, so most first-time visitors prefer it. The big boat (1,500 THB) takes about 1.5 hours each way and returns around 18:00, but it is cheaper, has a shaded deck and rolls less, which suits people who get seasick.
Are the boat trips suitable for children and toddlers? | Yes. Children aged 3–11 pay the child price, infants 0–2 travel free and children's life jackets are on every boat. For toddlers the Pig Island speedboat tour is the easiest choice: 4–5 hours with a 15–30 minute crossing. Ang Thong involves a long day, a stairway to the Emerald Lake and a steep viewpoint climb.
Can I join a snorkelling tour if I can't swim? | Yes. Every tour supplies life jackets and you can snorkel while wearing one, with the crew keeping watch. Tell us when you book. The main sights of Ang Thong, the Emerald Lake and the Wua Talab viewpoint, are reached on foot, and the Koh Tan reef is shallow.
What happens if the sea is too rough on my tour day? | We check the forecast every day and cancel when the sea isn't safe. If we cancel, you choose a full refund or a new date. A bad forecast alone is not a cancellation by us, so if you cancel yourself the normal refund rules apply.
How do I cancel and how much do I get back? | Message us on WhatsApp or by email with your booking reference. Our own day tours are refunded in full 48 hours or more before departure, 50% at 24–48 hours and not at all under 24 hours. Ang Thong tours are refunded in full 48 hours or more before departure and not after that. Island activities, charters and yachts are refunded in full minus the bank fee 4 days or more before, 50% at 1–3 days and not under 24 hours.
Can I book a tour for tomorrow? | Yes, online until midnight Thailand time tonight. There is no same-day online booking, so for a seat today message us on WhatsApp on +66 63 704 0888 (08:00–20:00) and we check what still has space.
Do you offer private tours for families and groups? | Yes. Private boats are priced per boat: Pig Island by longtail 5,950 THB (up to 8) or speedboat 11,950 THB (up to 10), Ang Thong by private speedboat 33,000 THB, and catamarans, junks and motor yachts from 7,500 THB. Private vans with a driver cost 2,500 THB for 4 hours for up to 10 people.
What cash do I need to bring on the day? | Bring Thai baht for fees that are never in the tour price: 300 THB per adult and 150 THB per child for the Ang Thong park fee, 250 THB per adult for Koh Nang Yuan, about 50 THB per person for Koh Madsum where not included, and about 200 THB for Full Moon Party entry. Add a little for drinks and tips.
