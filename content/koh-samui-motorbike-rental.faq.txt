How much does it cost to rent a scooter on Koh Samui? | A 125 cc automatic scooter costs about 150–350 THB a day, cheapest in Maenam, Nathon and Lipa Noi and dearest in Chaweng and Lamai, and a 150–160 cc model about 250–450 THB a day.
Do I need an international driving permit for a scooter in Thailand? | Yes, tourists need their home licence with the motorcycle category plus an International Driving Permit showing the motorcycle category; only ASEAN licences are accepted without an IDP.
Is my car licence enough to ride a scooter? | No, a car-only licence does not cover any scooter, even a 110 cc one, so you risk a fine and your insurance is unlikely to pay after a crash.
What is the fine for riding without a helmet? | Up to 2,000 THB per person since 1 June 2025, applying to both rider and passenger, so a couple without helmets can be fined up to 4,000 THB.
Should I leave my passport as a deposit? | No, the UK Foreign Office advises against it; offer a cash deposit of about 1,000–3,000 THB for a 125 cc scooter and a passport photocopy instead, and get a written receipt.
Does travel insurance cover scooter accidents in Thailand? | Usually only if you hold a motorcycle licence at home, carry an IDP with the motorcycle category, wear a helmet, stay within the policy's engine limit and have not been drinking.
Can I take a rented scooter to Koh Phangan? | Almost never; Samui rental contracts and insurance usually exclude ferries and other islands, so rent a new scooter on Koh Phangan instead.
Is it safe to ride a scooter on Koh Samui? | It is risky for inexperienced riders because of fast ring-road traffic, steep hill roads, rain, sand and night riding; if you do not ride at home, use Grab, taxis or tours instead.
How much is a monthly scooter rental? | About 2,000–4,500 THB a month for a 125 cc scooter, with the best deals in Maenam, Nathon and Lipa Noi, plus a cash deposit.
