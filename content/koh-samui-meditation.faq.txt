~Can tourists meditate at temples on Koh Samui? | You can sit quietly in the hall of any temple, but most don't run drop-in classes. For guided practice, join a course at Dipabhavan in the hills above Lamai or a monk-led retreat at the Samui International Meditation Center in Bophut.
Are there silent retreats on Koh Samui? | Yes. Dipabhavan, a branch of the Suan Mokkh forest monastery, runs silent residential courses on a donation basis, with English-language retreats scheduled through the year, and Vikasa runs a set-date silent retreat. The best-known 10-day silent retreat is at Suan Mokkh on the mainland, from the 1st to the 11th of each month.
How much does a meditation retreat cost? | Temple retreats are donation-based (Dipabhavan) or very cheap: Suan Mokkh's 10-day course costs 2,000 THB including food and a room. A 7-night monk-led retreat in Bophut costs about US$405, and resort programmes with meditation start around US$1,000 a week.
Do I need experience to join a meditation retreat? | No. Beginners are welcome at Dipabhavan, the Bophut centre and Suan Mokkh, and instruction starts from the basics of breathing and posture. What matters more is being ready for silence, early starts and simple food, and being in good physical and mental health.
What should I wear to a temple retreat? | Loose, modest clothes that cover shoulders and knees (upper arms too at Suan Mokkh), in light colours and not see-through. Bring slippers for taking off at halls, a shawl for cool mornings and insect repellent.
Can I combine meditation with a holiday? | Yes. Many visitors do a short retreat, then a few beach days: a Thai massage, the Pig Island tour, which runs every day of the year, or a day at Ang Thong from 16 December. Book your retreat dates first and fit tours around them.
How do I register for the Suan Mokkh retreat? | In person only, at the hermitage near Chaiya on the last day of the month before the retreat, between 07:00 and 15:00, first come, first served. You can't book by email or phone, and you can't join late or leave early.
Can I keep my phone during a meditation retreat? | Not on strict retreats: Suan Mokkh collects phones, tablets and notebooks for the 10 days, and Dipabhavan courses ask you to set devices aside. Sort out visas, flights and money before you start, and give family the centre's contact for emergencies.
