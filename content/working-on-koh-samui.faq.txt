Can I work on Koh Samui as a foreigner? | Yes, legally, if a Thai employer sponsors you for a non-immigrant visa (usually the Non-B) and a work permit, and the job isn't reserved for Thai nationals. Remote work for foreign employers or clients is possible on a Destination Thailand Visa.
Can I work on a visa exemption or tourist visa? | No. Any work for a business in Thailand, paid or unpaid, is illegal on a visa exemption or tourist visa, and the penalty is a fine of 5,000–50,000 THB and deportation.
How do I get a work permit in Thailand? | Your employer applies for it after you arrive on a Non-Immigrant B visa, mostly through the online e-WorkPermit system. The employer normally needs 2 million THB of paid-up capital and 4 Thai employees per foreign worker, and processing typically takes 3–7 working days.
Can I be a dive instructor on Koh Samui? | Yes, if a registered dive centre employs you and gets you a Non-B visa and a work permit for that job. Teaching or guiding dives without a permit, including as an unpaid intern, is illegal.
Which jobs are foreigners not allowed to do in Thailand? | 27 occupations are completely reserved for Thais, including tour guiding, Thai massage, hairdressing, driving vehicles, street vending, agent and broker services, legal services, secretarial work and many traditional crafts. A further 13 are open only under conditions.
Can I volunteer without a work permit? | Usually not. Thai law counts work whether or not you are paid, so volunteering, work exchanges for accommodation and helping at a friend's business can all need a permit. Registered volunteer programmes can arrange the right visa and permit.
Can I work remotely on a DTV? | Yes, for employers and clients outside Thailand. A DTV doesn't allow a Thai work permit, a job with a Thai company or freelance work for Thai clients.
What happens to an employer who hires foreigners illegally? | A fine of 10,000–100,000 THB per worker, and for repeat offences up to 1 year in prison and/or 50,000–200,000 THB per worker plus a 3-year ban on hiring foreign workers.
Can I open a business on Koh Samui? | Yes, but most service businesses fall under the Foreign Business Act, which limits foreign shareholding unless you have a Foreign Business Licence, BOI promotion or a treaty exemption. Nominee shareholders are illegal; take advice from a licensed lawyer first.
