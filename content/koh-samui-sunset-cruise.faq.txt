What time is sunset on Koh Samui? | Between about 17:55 in November and 18:45 in July. In January it is around 18:15, from February to May around 18:30, and in October around 18:05. The best light is the 30 minutes before.
Which side of Koh Samui has the best sunset? | The west and south-west: Taling Ngam, where the sun sets behind the Five Islands, plus Lipa Noi, Nathon pier and the sea off Thong Krut. The east coast (Chaweng, Lamai) faces the sunrise, and the north coast gets evening colour over the water towards Koh Phangan.
How much is a sunset cruise on Koh Samui? | Our shared Pig Island Sunset Tour costs 1,550 THB per adult and 1,350 THB per child with dinner. Private sunset sails start at 20,000 THB per boat on the catamaran Victoria (up to 10 guests), 36,000 THB plus VAT on Kia Ora (up to 15) and 41,000 THB for the Red Baron sunset dinner (up to 20).
Is dinner included on a Koh Samui sunset cruise? | Yes on the Pig Island Sunset Tour and the Red Baron sunset dinner cruise. On the private catamarans, drinks and fruit are included and food is extra; on Victoria you may bring your own food and drinks free of charge.
Can we book a private sunset boat for two on Koh Samui? | Yes. The sailing catamaran Victoria runs a 2.5-hour sunset sail from 16:00 to 18:30 from 20,000 THB for the boat, and the motor yacht Casablanca can be quoted for a couple who want cabins and privacy.
Can I propose on a sunset cruise on Koh Samui? | Yes, and a private boat is the best way to do it. Tell us it is a proposal when you enquire, and we quote flowers, a cake, decorations or a photographer as extras with a few days' notice.
Do sunset cruises on Koh Samui run in the rainy season? | Yes, whenever the sea is safe. From October to December there is more cloud and rain, and the south-west route from Thong Krut is the most sheltered. If we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.
What should I wear on a sunset cruise? | Swimwear under light clothes for the swim stop, a light long-sleeved layer for the breeze after dark, and flat shoes or bare feet on deck. Bring a waterproof bag for your phone and some cash for the island fee on the Pig Island tour.
