Where is the best sunset on Koh Samui? | Taling Ngam on the south-west coast: a quiet village beach facing west, with the Five Islands silhouetted offshore and the sun setting over open sea all year. Lipa Noi and Nathon pier, a little further north, are close seconds.
Can you see the sunset from Chaweng? | No. Chaweng faces east, so it gets the sunrise. The nearest real sunset from Chaweng is at Bang Rak and the Big Buddha terrace, 15–20 minutes' drive, or Fisherman's Village in Bophut, 20–25 minutes.
What time is sunset on Koh Samui? | Between about 17:56 in mid-November and 18:45 in mid-July. In December–February it is roughly 18:00–18:30 and in April roughly 18:30. Arrive 45 minutes early for golden hour.
Is a sunset cruise worth it? | On a clear February–April evening, yes: you see the whole horizon with no buildings and get a swim first. Shared catamaran sunsets cost about 1,500–2,500 THB; our Pig Island Sunset Tour is 1,550 THB with snorkelling and dinner. In October–December cloud often hides the sun.
Which beach has both a sunset and good swimming? | Lipa Noi, at high tide: the water is very shallow and calm and the beach faces west. Maenam is calm for swimming in the dry season with a sunset along the bay from April to September; Taling Ngam is swimmable at high tide.
How do I get back from the west coast after sunset? | Book a driver for a fixed return time before you go, because taxis are scarce, Grab is weak on the west coast and songthaews mostly stop between 20:00 and 22:00. If you ride a scooter, don't drink and take the steep roads slowly.
What is the best month for sunsets on Samui? | February to April, with only 4–6 rain days a month and the calmest sea. October to December is the monsoon, when many evenings cloud over; November is the wettest month.
Can I watch the sunset at the Big Buddha? | Often, yes. The terrace is open 07:00–18:30 and looks west across Bang Rak bay; from November to January sunset falls between 17:56 and 18:18, before closing. In mid-year the sun sets after 18:30, so watch from Bang Rak beach instead.
