Is June a good time to visit Koh Samui? | Yes. June is hot and mostly sunny, with highs of 33 °C, about 134 mm of rain over 10 days in mostly short showers, a calm 30 °C sea and shoulder-season prices. Ang Thong is open and quieter than in high season.
Is June rainy season on Koh Samui? | Not in the way it is in Phuket. Samui's main rains come with the north-east monsoon from October to December. June averages about 134 mm over 10 rain days, mostly short showers followed by sunshine, because the island is sheltered from the south-west monsoon.
Is Koh Samui better than Phuket in June? | For weather, yes. The Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi, Khao Lak) is in the south-west monsoon from about May to October, while Samui is sheltered and its boats run normally. Bangkok Airways flies Phuket–Samui about 7–8 times a day.
Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in June? | Yes. Ang Thong closes only from 1 November to 15 December. June brings good conditions with some westerly chop, and fewer visitors than July and August. Our speedboat tour costs 1,800 THB plus the 300 THB park fee in cash.
When is the Full Moon Party in June 2027? | Saturday 19 June 2027, on Haad Rin, Koh Phangan. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup and drop-off from most areas; entry (about 200 THB) is paid in cash at the beach.
Which side of Koh Samui is calmest in June? | The east coast. In June the wind blows from the south-west and west, so Chaweng, Lamai and Choeng Mon are sheltered and calm, while the west and south-west coasts get some chop in the afternoon.
Why is the sea so far out on Koh Samui in June? | June brings the lowest water of the year, with low tide in the morning around 10:00. The north and west coast beaches (Maenam, Bang Por, Lipa Noi) sit on wide, flat sand and mud, so the water retreats a long way. Swim there in the late afternoon or evening.
Is June good for families on Koh Samui? | Yes. The sea is calm on most days, the east-coast beaches are sheltered, and rooms cost less than in July and August. Children aged 3–11 pay a child rate on our boat trips, infants travel free, and children's life jackets are always provided.
Can you kitesurf on Koh Samui in June? | Only on some days. The summer south-west wind is lighter on Samui than on Koh Phangan; lessons run at Nathon on the west coast, best from about 09:00 to 12:00. For a kite-focused summer trip, Phangan has stronger wind.
Is June or July better for Koh Samui? | The weather is much the same: June averages about 134 mm over 10 days and July about 117 mm over 11 days, both with a calm sea. June is quieter and cheaper; July is busier, with European school holidays and room rates 30–80% above shoulder prices.
