Why go: real off-road driving in the Samui hills

Most of Koh Samui's interior is steep, forested and hard to reach by car. On this safari you drive your own Polaris 4WD ATV up dirt tracks into the central hills, after a briefing and a few laps of a practice circuit. The short options are a taste of the machines and the trail; the longer ones climb to a 360-degree viewpoint and stop at the Magic Garden (the Secret Buddha Garden), a hillside sculpture garden built by a local durian farmer, and the small Tan Nim waterfall next to it. The 2.5 hour ride adds a Thai lunch with a view.

The appeal is the mix: an adrenaline ride on clay and rock tracks, followed by one of the island's most unusual sights, without having to ride a rented scooter up roads that defeat many tourists.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: couples and friends sharing an ATV, and anyone who wants an active morning or afternoon.
  • Choose something else if you are pregnant, have back or neck problems, or dislike mud and noise. The jeep safaris reach the same hills with a driver.
  • Short of time: the 30 minute ride fits around a beach day, but it does not reach the garden.

Good to know

  • Safety: keep a good gap behind the ATV in front, follow the guide's line and take steep descents slowly.
  • Weather: October to December is the rainy season. Tracks get slippery and the operator may shorten the route. Mornings are usually drier.
  • Clothes: you will be splashed with red clay. Leave white clothes at the hotel.
  • Insurance: accident insurance is included, but check that your travel insurance covers off-road vehicles before you ride.
  • Getting to the base: on the short options you need your own transport. Ask us for a taxi if you don't want to ride a scooter up the hill.

Good to know: damage rules are not published on the partner page. Ask us before you ride, and never hand over your passport as a deposit.
