The Full Moon Party is on Haad Rin Sunrise Beach on Koh Phangan, 25–50 minutes by boat from Koh Samui. The easiest option is a shared return speedboat with hotel pickup from most areas of Samui, 1,400 THB per person with us; the cheapest is the public Haad Rin Queen from Big Buddha pier at about 200 THB, but it has no late-night boat back. The next parties are Tuesday 24 November 2026 (also Loy Krathong), Thursday 24 December (Christmas Eve) and Thursday 31 December (New Year's Eve).

We are a local Australian-Thai team that has run boats from Samui since 2006. We sell the return boat to the party, not the party entry: you pay about 200 THB in cash at the beach.

Shared return speedboat (our pick)

For most people staying on Samui, a shared return speedboat is the simplest way to do the party and sleep in their own bed. Ours costs 1,400 THB per person in 2026, roughly half the price of the minivan-and-boat packages commonly sold on the island.

  • Hotel pickup and drop-off from most areas of Koh Samui
  • Return speedboat crossing to Haad Rin and back
  • Life jackets for every passenger
  • Party entry, about 200 THB cash at the beach
  • Drinks and food
  • Remote-area transfers: Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Bang Kao and Thong Krut (and other remote areas) add 400 THB per person, return

Departures run in an 18:00–23:00 window. After payment we confirm your booking by email or WhatsApp and send your pickup time the evening before. Online booking for a party night closes at midnight Thailand time the night before; for same-day requests, message us on WhatsApp.

Thailand's legal drinking age is 20, and the party is an adult event; carry a copy of your passport in case of an ID check. Prices are charged in THB only.

Private speedboat for your group

A private speedboat takes up to 10 guests, picks the whole group up from your hotel or villa, and leaves and returns when you choose. It makes sense for groups of six or more, for anyone who wants to arrive early and leave at 02:00, and for celebrations. It is also the most comfortable option on the busiest nights (24 December and 31 December), when public piers are crowded. Groups over 10 should message us on WhatsApp.

Public ferries and speedboats

Haad Rin Queen from Big Buddha pier

The Haad Rin Queen is the only public boat that sails directly from Samui to Haad Rin pier, a short walk from the party beach. The slow boat takes about 50 minutes and costs about 200 THB, with departures at 10:30 and 17:00. A faster boat on the same route costs about 500 THB and takes 25–30 minutes, with an afternoon departure at 15:00. Some timetables also list a 18:30 speedboat. [check]

The catch is the return: there is no public boat back to Samui in the middle of the night. The first public returns leave the next morning (the slow boat at 09:30). Unless you have a room on Phangan, you would wait on the beach until morning. Big Buddha pier is at Bang Rak on Samui's north-east coast: directions to Big Buddha pier.

Ferries to Thong Sala and Baan Tai

Around 40 scheduled sailings a day link Samui and Phangan, but most land at Thong Sala, on the opposite side of Phangan from Haad Rin. Lomprayah, Songserm, Seatran and Boonsiri charge about 300–350 THB for the 20–45-minute crossing from Bang Rak, Maenam or Nathon, with the last daytime sailings around 17:00–17:30. Echo speedboats from Petcherat Marina go to Baan Tai, closer to Haad Rin, for about 490 THB; late departures on party nights cost more. From Thong Sala or Baan Tai you then need a songthaew or taxi over the hill to Haad Rin, typically 150–200 THB per person on party nights. For the full timetable, see Koh Samui to Koh Phangan.

Good to know: touts sell cheap "Full Moon boat" tickets at bars and on Chaweng Beach Road. Some are for unlicensed boats or don't include a return. Board only licensed boats with life jackets, and only the boat on your ticket.

Stay safe

The UK Foreign Office warns of assaults, including sexual assaults, drink spiking and unlicensed transport around Full Moon events. Take these seriously:

  • Watch your drink being made, never leave it, and don't accept drinks from strangers.
  • Stay with your group and agree a meeting time and place for the boat home.
  • Never swim at night, and never after drinking. Currents off Phangan are strong in November and December.
  • Glass and burnt rope litter the sand: wear closed shoes and stay away from fire-skipping ropes.
  • Drugs carry heavy penalties, police are present, and vapes are illegal in Thailand. See drugs and alcohol law.
  • Take only what you need: cash, a passport copy and your phone.
  • Tourist Police 1155 · Ambulance 1669 · Police 191.

Weather and sea

November and December fall in Samui's north-east monsoon. November is the wettest month (about 445 mm of rain over 16 days) and December averages 263 mm. The crossing to Haad Rin is exposed to north-east swell, so boats run only when it is safe. If we cancel for weather or sea safety, you choose a full refund or a new date. Day-tour cancellation terms: full refund 48 hours or more before departure, 50 % at 24–48 hours, no refund under 24 hours or for a no-show.

Five mistakes Samui visitors make

  1. Booking the raw lunar date. Four 2027 parties move a day because of Buddhist holidays (22 February, 21 May, 20 July and 16 October). Many websites still show the original dates, which are alcohol-ban days with no party. Check our dates table.
  2. Going to the wrong "Bang Rak pier". Lomprayah, Seatran and Petcherat Marina are all called Bang Rak pier, and Big Buddha pier is next door. Ferry tickets name the pier; read yours. With a door-to-door boat, the driver handles this.
  3. Leaving the return to chance. There are no public boats back to Samui in the middle of the night, and late private boats on the beach charge what they like at 04:00. Book a return before you go.
  4. Carrying a passport and all your cards. Lost passports are the most common problem at the party. Bring a copy, the cash you need and one card at most.
  5. Booking a dawn tour for the next morning. Pickups for full-day boat trips start around 07:00. Plan an afternoon trip, a rest day or a massage instead, and do your big island day two days later.

The day after

Most people sleep until midday after the party. If you want something gentle in the afternoon, the trips from Thong Krut in the south of Samui run all year and don't start at dawn:

  • Pig Island sunset tour: 1,550 THB adult, 1,350 THB child, ending with the sunset.
  • Pig Island speedboat tour: 1,450 THB adult, 1,250 THB child.
  • Pink Dolphins and Pig Island: 2,250 THB.

Ang Thong Marine Park is closed from 1 November to 15 December every year; from 16 December the Ang Thong speedboat tour (1,800 THB) is a good choice two days after the party, not the morning after.
