How far is the Full Moon Party from Koh Samui? | Haad Rin, the Full Moon Party beach on Koh Phangan, is about 15–20 km across the sea from Samui's north-east coast. A speedboat crossing takes about 25–40 minutes and the Haad Rin Queen slow boat about 50 minutes. Add hotel pickup time on Samui: from Chaweng or Bophut that is usually 15–30 minutes to the pier.
What is the cheapest way to get to the Full Moon Party from Samui? | The Haad Rin Queen slow boat from Big Buddha pier is cheapest, at about 200 THB each way, but it sails only at 10:30 and 17:00 and the first boat back is at 09:30 the next morning. Unless you book a room on Koh Phangan, you would stay on the beach all night. Our shared return speedboat is 1,400 THB.
What is the easiest way to get to the Full Moon Party and back? | A shared return speedboat with hotel pickup is the easiest. Ours costs 1,400 THB per person in 2026, with pickup from most areas of Koh Samui and drop-off at your hotel after the party. Remote areas such as Nathon and Lipa Noi add 400 THB return. Groups of up to 10 can book a private speedboat instead.
What time do boats return to Samui after the Full Moon Party? | Our shared boats return to Samui in set slots during the night, and your return time is confirmed when you book. Private boats return whenever your group chooses. Public boats do not run in the middle of the night: the first Haad Rin Queen back to Big Buddha pier leaves at about 09:30 the next morning.
Is it safe to take a boat at night to the Full Moon Party? | It is safe on a licensed boat with life jackets and a sober crew, in a sea that has been checked. Fatal boat accidents in Thailand are linked to overloading and rough seas, which is why we cancel when it isn't safe. Avoid cheap tout tickets for unknown boats, and never board a boat other than the one on your ticket.
Do I need to buy a Full Moon Party ticket? | You don't need to buy anything in advance. Entry is about 200 THB per person, paid in cash at the beach for a wristband. There is no official online entry ticket, and we don't sell entry. What you should book in advance is the boat, because seats from Samui sell out on Loy Krathong, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.
Can I book a Full Moon Party boat on the day? | Online booking for our Full Moon boat closes at midnight Thailand time the night before the party, so book by then. For same-day requests, message us on WhatsApp at +66 63 704 0888; we will tell you honestly whether seats remain. On Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, seats are usually gone well before the day.
Is it better to stay on Samui or Koh Phangan for the Full Moon Party? | Stay on Samui if you want better hotels, a calm next day and other trips during your holiday; a return boat solves the transport. Stay on Phangan if the party is the main reason for your trip and you want to come and go as you like, but expect inflated Full Moon week room rates and minimum stays at Haad Rin.
What happens if the sea is rough on Full Moon Party night? | We only cross when the sea is safe. If we cancel because of weather or sea conditions, you choose a full refund or a new party date. November and December are the riskiest months because of the north-east monsoon; February to April has the calmest crossings. Public ferries can also cancel at short notice.
Is the Full Moon Party on Loy Krathong this year? | Yes. In 2026, Loy Krathong and the Full Moon Party are both on Tuesday 24 November. You can float a krathong at Chaweng Lake or on a Samui beach in the early evening and still catch a later boat to Haad Rin. In 2027 the two are expected to coincide again on Saturday 13 November.
