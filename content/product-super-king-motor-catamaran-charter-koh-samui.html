Why go: a roomy catamaran close to the southern islands

Super King is a motor catamaran based on Samui's south coast, a short hop from Koh Tan and Koh Madsum. That location makes it one of the few big, comfortable boats you can charter for just a half day to the southern islands, and its three cabins open up overnight trips to Ang Thong and Koh Tao.

What you'll do

The half day snorkels the reef at Koh Tan and lands at Pig Island. Full days add Koh Rap's long beach, or head north-west to Ang Thong Marine Park from 16 December. Paddle boards, fishing rods and a BBQ grill are on board, and you can order a seafood BBQ for lunch. Overnights anchor in a quiet bay with the cabins for your group.

Who it suits

  • Families with small children, who benefit from the shade, saloon and cabins.
  • Groups of 8–10 wanting more space than a speedboat for a half day.
  • Friends wanting an affordable first overnight on a boat.

Who should choose something else

  • Couples: our private longtail to Pig Island is far cheaper for two.
  • Anyone wanting to sail: choose a sailing catamaran such as Nina.

Good to know

  • Pigs: let them come to you and don't feed them junk food.
  • Sea: the southern islands are sheltered from the north-east winds of October to December, so this route runs on many days when others can't.
  • Ang Thong: closed from 1 November to 15 December.

Before you book

Tell us the number of adults and children and their ages, so we can add any extra-guest charges, and whether you want a BBQ set for lunch. For overnights, we confirm the route and anchorage with the captain before you pay.
