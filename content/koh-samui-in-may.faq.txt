Is May a good time to visit Koh Samui? | Yes, for value and boat trips. May has highs of 33 °C, about 132 mm of rain over 11 days (mostly short afternoon showers), a calm 30 °C sea, Ang Thong open and shoulder-season prices. It is the hottest month, so plan outdoor sightseeing for the morning.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in May? | About 132 mm on 1991–2020 averages, over about 11 days with 1 mm or more. Most of it comes as short afternoon showers or thunderstorms followed by sunshine, not the long grey spells of the November monsoon.
Is May the hottest month on Koh Samui? | Yes. May has the highest average daytime temperature of the year, 33 °C, with nights around 26 °C and high humidity. June also averages 33 °C.
When is the Full Moon Party in May 2027? | Friday 21 May 2027. The full moon falls on Visakha Bucha (Thursday 20 May), an alcohol-ban day, so the party moves to the following night. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup and drop-off from most areas.
Are bars open on Visakha Bucha? | Licensed hotels and licensed bars in tourist areas may serve alcohol on Buddhist holy days since May 2025. 7-Eleven, supermarkets and unlicensed restaurants can't sell alcohol on Thursday 20 May 2027.
When is the Samui Regatta in 2027? | The 24th Samui Regatta runs from Saturday 22 to Saturday 29 May 2027, with keelboat racing off Chaweng Beach, beach parties and a closing gala. Watching from the beach is free.
Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in May? | Yes. Ang Thong closes only from 1 November to 15 December. In May the sea is mostly calm, with occasional afternoon storms, and the park is noticeably quieter than in high season. Our speedboat tour costs 1,800 THB plus the 300 THB park fee in cash.
Do boat trips run in May? | Yes. Ang Thong, Pig Island and Koh Tao trips all run. Afternoon thunderstorms can bring short, strong gusts, so morning departures are the safer bet; the captain makes the final call on the day, and if we cancel for sea safety you choose a full refund or a new date.
Is May good for diving near Koh Samui? | Yes. Visibility around Koh Tao and Sail Rock is typically 15–30 m and the sea is flat in the mornings. Whale sharks are possible in May but never guaranteed.
Is Koh Samui cheaper in May? | Yes, compared with the peaks. May is shoulder season, with room rates well below mid-December to February and July to August, when rates run 30–80% higher. Food, massages and scooters cost the same all year.
