ARKbar Beach Club sits on the sand in central Chaweng and is open every day from 10:00 to 02:00. Entry is free: the pool party runs from 14:00 to 19:00, a free fire show starts at 20:00 with sets every hour until midnight, and the beach party with DJs goes on until 02:00. Children are welcome until 21:00, when the venue becomes 18+. A walk-in beachfront sunbed has a 300 THB minimum spend, a beer costs 120–150 THB and a cocktail 220–320 THB.

Map and location

ARKbar runs from Chaweng Beach Road down to the sand in the middle of the beach, with its 187-room resort, two pools and restaurants on one plot. Soi Green Mango is a short walk away.

What it is like

ARKbar is the biggest and busiest beach club on Samui, and it is built for volume rather than polish. By day it is a resort beach club: loungers along the sand, an adults' pool with a swim-up bar, a separate children's pool with a slide, and a pool party that builds through the afternoon. Expect a young international crowd, lots of groups and birthdays, and loud music.

After dark the sand in front of the main stage becomes the dance floor. The fire shows are the best free entertainment in Chaweng: fire batons and fire breathing, with a new set at the top of each hour from 20:00. The peak is between 22:00 and midnight. It is crowded and hot, and on Saturdays in high season expect to wait at the bar.

Headliner nights bring visiting international DJs; Blasterjaxx and Tujamo have both played here. These nights extend the schedule and may carry a ticket or a higher table minimum.

Food and drink prices

There are two kitchens: the beach-club restaurant, open from 10:00 with an all-day menu, and HIP Restaurant, open from 07:00 for breakfast and through to late. Food is resort-beach-club standard: Thai dishes, stone-baked pizza and burgers. Sunbeds, pool access and tables

  • Walk-in beachfront sunbed: 300 THB minimum spend, no booking. On busy days the front row is gone by early afternoon.
  • Pool use for non-hotel guests: 500 THB minimum spend per person, children included. Towels cost 100 THB per person plus a 500 THB deposit.
  • Pool daybeds: larger and more private, with a higher minimum spend.
  • Booked tables: from a 2,000 THB minimum spend, varying by table and season, booked online. Online day bookings close at 09:00 and night bookings at 15:00; after that, tables are walk-in only.
  • Beach tables at night: first come, first served, no minimum spend.

Good to know: the minimum spend is credited against food and drink, not an extra charge. Ask what it covers before you sit down, and pay in baht if a card machine offers a currency conversion.

New Year's Eve 2026: the Countdown Beach Festival

On Thursday 31 December 2026 ARKbar holds its Countdown Beach Festival. This year it is ticketed from the door: in 2025 entry was free before 21:00 and 500 THB after, but for 2026 everyone needs a ticket.

  • Doors: 17:00; fire show at 20:00, an extended fire show before midnight, countdown and fireworks over Chaweng Bay at 00:00. Last general entry at midnight.
  • Standing ticket: 500 THB, sold in advance for the whole night. Advance sales close on 25 December 2026; a limited number are sold at the door. Tickets are non-refundable.
  • Adults only (18+): bring valid photo ID.
  • Table packages: 15,000–50,000 THB for 4–6 guests, with bottles and entry included, paid in full in advance. One guest must check in by 21:00 or the table can be released. Beach tables for standing-ticket holders cost 8,000 THB for 4–6 people on the night, including a bottle with mixers or 20 beers.
  • Hotel guests get free general entry.
  • No parking on the night: come by taxi, songthaew or on foot.

The Full Moon Party on Koh Phangan falls on the same night. For every option, see Christmas and New Year on Koh Samui and the party calendar 2026–2027.

Tips

Best time to go

  • For the pool: arrive by 14:00 on Wednesday or Friday for a sunbed and the live performers.
  • For the fire show: the 20:00 set is the one for families, who must leave by 21:00; later sets are adults only and more crowded.
  • For the party: 22:00 to midnight, then on to Soi Green Mango if you want to keep going until 04:00.
  • Season: December to April and July–August are busiest. From late October to December the north-east monsoon brings rain and big waves to Chaweng; fire shows depend on the weather and beach parties are cut short on wet nights.

What to bring

  • Swimwear, sun cream and sunglasses by day; a light cover-up for the evening.
  • Photo ID (a passport photo on your phone usually works; for 18+ events a passport or a photocopy is safer).
  • Cash in small notes for taxis and tips, plus a card for the bar.

Safety

  • Don't swim in the sea after drinking, and never at night. Chaweng has red-flag days and rip currents from October to December; see beach safety.
  • Watch your drink and keep your phone in a front pocket. Thai drug laws are severe: read drugs and alcohol law and scams.

Getting home

If you are staying in central Chaweng, walk. Otherwise use Grab, which works well in Chaweng but surges after 02:00 and in rain, or a red-and-yellow taxi from Beach Road, agreeing the fare first (about 300–500 THB to Bophut or Lamai, more after midnight). Songthaews mostly stop by 20:00–22:00. Never ride a scooter after drinking: police checkpoints are common on the ring road at night and insurance won't pay. More in getting around Koh Samui.

Who it suits, and who should go elsewhere

Go if you want a big, affordable beach party with free entry, groups and birthdays, backpackers and first-timers in Chaweng, or a family afternoon by the pool with a fire show at 20:00.

Go elsewhere if you want a calm sunset or a smart crowd: SEEN Beach Club at Avani Chaweng or the north-coast clubs in Bang Rak are quieter and more polished, and Coco Tam's in Fisherman's Village has fire shows in a more relaxed setting. Compare them all in our Koh Samui beach clubs guide. Light sleepers should not book a room next door: the music runs until 02:00.
