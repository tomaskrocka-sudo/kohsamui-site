Why go: big-group comfort on a fast, new boat

This charter uses one of three identical 39 ft speedboats, built in 2025, with twin 600 hp engines, a toilet and a freshwater shower. Those last two matter on a long day with children or a big group. The price bands are clear: one price for up to 10 guests and a second for 11–20. Shared between 20 people, a full private day costs little more per head than many shared tours.

The morning half day goes to Koh Phangan's bays. The afternoon trip finishes at sunset. Full days cover Samui's southern islands (Koh Tan, Pig Island and Koh Rap), Koh Phangan with the Koh Ma sandbar, Ang Thong from 16 December, or Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan. The honeymoon trip is built for two: snorkelling at Koh Ma, Secret Beach on Koh Phangan, then dinner on a cliff-top terrace as the sun goes down before a night crossing home.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: groups of 10–20, birthday and wedding parties, families who want a toilet on board, and couples on the honeymoon route.
  • Choose something else if: you're 4 or fewer on a budget. The Little Voyager costs less than half. If you want to sail rather than motor, see our catamaran charter guide.

Good to know

  • Fees: entrance and landing fees aren't included. Budget for the Ang Thong park fee and the island fees in cash.
  • Night return: the honeymoon trip comes back after dark. The crossing is short, but bring a light layer.
  • Season: February to September is calmest. In the October–December monsoon, choose the southern islands.
