Yoga on Koh Samui is led by resorts and retreat centres rather than a village of studios like Koh Phangan's Sri Thanu. The best-known names are Vikasa on the cliffs between Chaweng Noi and Coral Cove, Absolute Sanctuary near Choeng Mon, Kamalaya at Laem Set and Samahita on Laem Sor beach in the south, all running daily classes and multi-day programmes. In 2026, all-inclusive yoga retreats start at about US$650 per person for 3 nights, and 200-hour teacher trainings at about US$3,480. Studio drop-in prices change often, so confirm them directly before you go.

Why yoga on Samui is different from Koh Phangan

Koh Phangan, 20–50 minutes away by boat, has the Gulf's densest yoga scene: dozens of studios around Sri Thanu on the west coast, cheap drop-in classes, long teacher trainings and a large community of long-stayers. Samui works differently. Its yoga is concentrated in a handful of full-service retreats and wellness resorts with their own accommodation, chefs and spas, plus small studios and hotel classes. That suits travellers who want comfort, fixed programmes and an airport 5–45 minutes away rather than a backpacker-style community. If you want to hop between many studios, spend part of your stay on Phangan; our sister site has a full Koh Phangan yoga guide.

Drop-in classes, retreats and teacher trainings

Drop-in classes

Single classes are the cheapest way to practise on Samui. Vikasa's ocean-front sala is the best-known drop-in venue; drop-in classes there have been priced at about 500 THB [check]. Small studios in Fisherman's Village and Chaweng run their own schedules, and many resorts offer a free or low-cost morning class for guests. Arrive 10–15 minutes early, as popular sunrise and sunset classes fill up in high season.

Retreats

Retreats bundle accommodation, daily yoga, meals and usually some spa or wellness treatments into a package of 3–14 nights. On Samui they range from simple and sporty (Samahita, Vikasa) to full medical-style wellness programmes (Kamalaya, Absolute Sanctuary). Most accept beginners; the difference is how many classes a day you'll do and how strict the food and alcohol rules are.

Teacher trainings

Yoga teacher trainings (YTT) are usually 100, 200 or 300 hours. A 200-hour course runs about three weeks, full-time, six days a week, and is the standard first qualification; 300-hour trainings follow for experienced teachers. On Samui, Vikasa is the main training school with scheduled dates through 2027 (see below). Check whether a course is registered with Yoga Alliance if you plan to teach abroad.

Vikasa

A cliffside retreat on the ring road between Chaweng Noi and Coral Cove, with an ocean-front yoga sala, a café, a pool and beachfront rooms. It runs daily classes for all levels, set-date signature retreats (including a silent retreat), flexible stays with unlimited classes and meals, and Samui's main teacher-training calendar. Published dates for 2026–2027: 300-hour advanced training 27 October–27 November 2026; 100-hour AXIS training 17–28 December 2026 (from US$2,240); 200-hour foundation training 3–24 January 2027 and 28 March–18 April 2027 (from US$3,480); 300-hour training 29 April–27 May 2027 (from US$5,510). Classes on a typical retreat day run from about 09:30 to 11:00, with more sessions later in the day.

Samahita Retreat

A beachfront yoga and fitness retreat on Laem Sor in the quiet south, about 45 minutes from the airport. Programmes of 3, 5 and 7 days include accommodation, a yoga and fitness schedule, buffet meals, a saltwater pool, float therapy and evening herbal steam (18:00–20:00), with prices from about US$654 per person sharing for 3 nights including service and VAT. The setting is remote, which is the point.

Absolute Sanctuary

A wellness resort near Choeng Mon, about 5 minutes from the airport, with three yoga spaces and a studio. Yoga sits inside wider programmes: Be Fit (daily yoga and Pilates, 6 nights from about US$2,152), Yoga Immersion, Pilates Reformer Bootcamp and detox packages. Most programmes have a 6-night minimum.

Kamalaya

Samui's best-known wellness resort, on the hillside at Laem Set in the south-east, about 45 minutes from the airport. Its Personal Yoga Synergy programme runs 3–7 nights (from about US$1,905 for 3 nights, including service charge and VAT), alongside detox, sleep and fitness programmes. First-time guests staying fewer than 7 nights must book at least a 3-night wellness programme. In 2026 Kamalaya also sold day passes with a holistic class from 1,800 THB++.

Other options

Small studios open and close often on Samui, so treat any list older than a season with caution. Shahda Yoga in Fisherman's Village runs small-group classes combining yoga, breathwork and meditation. Many large resorts run morning classes for their own guests. For a studio near your hotel, ask us on WhatsApp and we'll check what's running that week.

How to choose a class or retreat

  • Level: look for "all levels" or "beginners" in the description, and tell the teacher about injuries before class.
  • Intensity: count the classes per day. Two a day plus fitness sessions is a lot in 30 °C heat.
  • Food and alcohol rules: some retreats are vegetarian and alcohol-free; others leave it to you.
  • Location: the south-coast retreats are remote, so you'll need taxis for anything outside; Absolute Sanctuary and Vikasa are close to Chaweng.
  • What's included: airport transfers, treatments, laundry and service charges vary. Ask for an itemised quote.
  • Cancellation terms: retreats set their own deposit and refund rules; read them before paying.

Safety and etiquette in class

  • Practise in the morning or late afternoon; midday heat makes dehydration and dizziness more likely.
  • Drink water before class, and add electrolytes after a sweaty session.
  • Sea-view shalas are open-air: bring insect repellent for sunrise and sunset classes.
  • Take shoes off before entering the shala, switch phones to silent, and arrive before the start.
  • Skip inversions and deep twists in pregnancy unless the teacher is trained in prenatal yoga.
  • Don't practise straight after a big meal, a boat day in the sun or alcohol.

Where to stay for yoga

  • At the retreat: the simplest choice for a programme of 3 nights or more.
  • Chaweng Noi and Coral Cove: walking or a short taxi ride to Vikasa, with Chaweng's restaurants 10 minutes away.
  • Choeng Mon: near Absolute Sanctuary and the airport, with a calm swimming beach.
  • Bophut: for small studios and the best restaurants.
  • Bang Kao and the south: near Samahita and Kamalaya, quiet and rural.

Combine yoga with spa, detox and meditation

Many visitors pair morning yoga with an afternoon massage: a one-hour Thai massage costs about 300–500 THB at a good shop. Tamarind Springs' herbal steam caves (1,500 THB++) are a good recovery afternoon. For structured programmes, compare the detox and wellness resorts; for silent practice, see meditation on Koh Samui; for massage prices, the spa guide. All options are on the retreats hub.

Combine yoga with island tours

Most retreats leave a free afternoon or day, and a boat trip is the best way to use it.

Ang Thong Marine Park

A full day among 42 limestone islands with kayaking, the Emerald Lake and a viewpoint climb, on the speedboat tour (1,800 THB) or the calmer VIP tour with only 15 guests on a big speedboat (4,500 THB). The park is closed from 1 November to 15 December and reopens on 16 December; the cash park fee is 300 THB per adult.

Pig Island

The Pig Island and Koh Tan speedboat is a half day (4–5 hours, 1,450 THB) with snorkelling and a meal, so it fits a retreat afternoon. It runs every day of the year, including the Ang Thong closure.

Koh Tao

A long day (about 9 hours) for the clear water around Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan, 2,200 THB. Choose a rest day from practice.

Hotel pickup is included from most areas of Koh Samui. Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Bang Kao and Thong Krut (and other remote areas) add 400 THB per person, return; pickup zones vary by pier, so check the product page, especially if you are staying at a south-coast retreat.

Visas for long trainings

Most visitors get 30 days visa-free on arrival since 15 September 2026, extendable once by 30 days at Samui immigration in Maenam (1,900 THB). A 200-hour training plus travel time fits inside that, but a 300-hour course or back-to-back trainings may not. Thailand's Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) has a "soft power" track for courses such as Muay Thai and Thai cooking; whether a particular yoga training qualifies depends on the school's paperwork [check]. Full rules are in our Thailand visa guide.
