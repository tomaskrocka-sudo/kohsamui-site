What is the best beach on Koh Samui? | For most visitors it is Chaweng North: fine white sand, a gentle slope and restaurants nearby, 10–15 minutes from the airport. For small children choose Choeng Mon, for sunsets Taling Ngam or Lipa Noi, and for a cheap quiet stay Maenam.
Which Koh Samui beach is best for swimming? | Chaweng North, central Lamai, Choeng Mon and Silver Beach, from January to September. Central Lamai drops off quickly, so it suits confident swimmers. From October to December check for red flags on Chaweng and Lamai and use sheltered Choeng Mon instead.
Which beach is best for families with small children? | Choeng Mon, a sheltered bay with a gentle slope 5–10 minutes from the airport, and Lipa Noi on the west coast, which is very shallow with no coral and best at high tide. Avoid central Lamai with toddlers because it shelves steeply.
Can you swim on Koh Samui in November? | Sometimes. November is the wettest month (about 445 mm of rain) and Chaweng and Lamai often fly red flags with rip currents. On calm days Choeng Mon and the west coast are usually swimmable, but never swim when a red flag is up.
Why is the sea so shallow at Maenam and Lipa Noi? | The north and west coasts sit on a wide, shallow shelf, so the water retreats a long way at low tide. Lipa Noi turns into flats; Maenam narrows at high tide and then deepens to chest height about 5 m out. Plan swims around high tide and check our live tide card.
Is Bophut beach good for swimming? | We don't recommend it. The water in front of Fisherman's Village is often cloudy, an outflow is reported near the east end and jellyfish are reported. Swim at Choeng Mon or Chaweng and come to Bophut for dinner and the view to Koh Phangan.
Are there box jellyfish on Koh Samui? | Yes, rarely. Nine of Thailand's 10 recorded box jellyfish deaths since 2002 were off Samui and Koh Phangan. Avoid swimming at dusk and at night, use netted areas where they exist, and treat a sting with vinegar for at least 30 seconds without rubbing or fresh water, then call 1669.
Where can I watch the sunset from a beach on Koh Samui? | On the west and north-west coasts: Taling Ngam (with the Five Islands offshore), Lipa Noi, Bang Por and Laem Yai, or the free Nathon pier. Chaweng and Lamai face east, so they get sunrise, not sunset.
Are Koh Samui beaches free to use? | Yes. All Thai beaches are public up to the high-water line, so you can walk onto any of them. Sunbeds and umbrellas belong to resorts and restaurants, which may rent them to non-guests (about 200 THB at Choeng Mon) or let you use them if you order food and drinks.
Is smoking allowed on Chaweng Beach? | Chaweng was not on the 2018 national list of 24 no-smoking beaches, but Bophut was, with fines up to 100,000 THB. Many Chaweng resorts ban smoking on their frontage, vapes are illegal everywhere in Thailand and smoking cannabis in public carries fines up to 25,000 THB.
