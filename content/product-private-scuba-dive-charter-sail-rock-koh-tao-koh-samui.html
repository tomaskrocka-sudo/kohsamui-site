Why go: the Gulf's best dive sites on your schedule

Most divers on Samui join a shared boat with 10–16 other people. This charter gives you the boat, the instructor and the site choice for a group of up to 4, which matters most at sites where timing counts. Sail Rock is a granite pinnacle halfway to Koh Phangan, with a vertical swim-through called the Chimney and frequent schools of barracuda and trevally. Koh Tao has the most varied sites in the region. Koh Ma, off the north tip of Koh Phangan, has a shallow reef that suits nervous divers and beginners. The northern Ang Thong sites are rarely visited and reopen on 16 December.

Private also means mixed groups work. A certified diver and a first-timer can share the boat, each with the right instructor ratio, and a non-diving partner can snorkel at the same stop.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: couples and small groups of mixed ability, photographers who want longer dives, and families with a child aged 10 or over trying diving for the first time.
  • Choose something else if: you dive alone on a budget. The shared dive and snorkel speedboat costs a fraction per person. If you only want to snorkel, the private snorkelling charter is cheaper.
  • Want certification? Open Water and Advanced courses are available with the same team on request; see our diving guide.

Good to know

  • Season: visibility is usually best from March to September, and whale sharks are seen most often between March and May. October to December brings rougher seas and more cancellations.
  • Flying: leave at least 18 hours after diving before you fly, and 24 hours after several days of diving.
  • Insurance: accident insurance is included, but check that your travel policy covers scuba diving to the depth you plan.
  • Medical: everyone completes the PADI medical questionnaire. Answer it honestly.
