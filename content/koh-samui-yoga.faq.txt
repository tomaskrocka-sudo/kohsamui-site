Where is the best area for yoga on Koh Samui? | The cliffs between Chaweng Noi and Coral Cove for Vikasa and its drop-in classes, the north-east near Choeng Mon for Absolute Sanctuary, and the quiet south coast (Laem Set and Laem Sor) for the Kamalaya and Samahita retreats. Bophut has small studios close to good restaurants.
Can beginners do yoga on Koh Samui? | Yes. Most classes and retreats on Samui are labelled all levels, and the retreats start with gentle hatha or yin sessions. Tell the teacher about injuries, practise in the cooler morning or late afternoon, and drink plenty of water in the heat.
Do I need to book drop-in classes in advance? | In high season (December–April) it's wise to book sunrise and sunset classes a day ahead or arrive 15 minutes early, because popular shalas fill up. In the low season you can usually walk in. Schedules change often, so check the day's timetable first.
How long is a yoga teacher training? | A 200-hour training on Samui takes about three weeks full-time; Vikasa's 2027 dates are 3–24 January and 28 March–18 April. 100-hour courses take about 12 days, and 300-hour advanced trainings about a month.
What is the best time of year for a yoga retreat on Samui? | January to April for dry, sunny days and calm sea, or May to September for lower prices with short showers. October to December is the rainy season, but covered shalas keep retreats running and prices are at their lowest.
Is Koh Samui or Koh Phangan better for yoga? | Phangan for a large, affordable studio scene around Sri Thanu with many drop-ins and trainings; Samui for comfortable retreats and wellness resorts with private rooms, spas and an airport on the island. Many people do a retreat on Samui and a week of drop-ins on Phangan.
What should I bring to a yoga class on Samui? | A light, breathable outfit, a towel (you will sweat), a refillable water bottle and insect repellent for open-air sunrise and sunset classes. Most studios and retreats provide mats, blocks and straps.
Can I combine yoga with island tours? | Yes. The Pig Island speedboat takes 4–5 hours and runs every day, so it fits a free afternoon; Ang Thong is a full day from 16 December to 31 October; and Koh Tao is a long day best kept for a rest day from practice.
How much does a yoga retreat on Koh Samui cost? | In 2026, simple all-inclusive retreats start at about US$650 per person for 3 nights sharing, a week at Vikasa from about US$1,030, and wellness-resort yoga programmes from about US$1,900 for 3 nights or US$2,150 for 6 nights.
