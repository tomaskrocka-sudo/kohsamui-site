Why go: a jungle party without the ferry hassle

The OXA jungle party is one of Koh Phangan's regular night-time events: three stages of electronic music in a jungle setting inland from Ban Tai, running most Fridays through the high season. Getting there from Koh Samui on your own means a late ferry, a taxi on Koh Phangan and no way home until the morning boats. This package handles all of it: a pickup at your Samui hotel, a speedboat across, a minivan to the venue, entry, and the 05:00 boat home with a drop-off at your door.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: adults staying on Koh Samui who want a big night of electronic music on Koh Phangan.
  • Choose something else if you want the original beach party: the Full Moon Party speedboat goes to Haad Rin on full-moon nights.
  • Want to leave early? The shared boat waits until 05:00; a private boat sets your own times.

Good to know

  • Drugs: Thai drug penalties are severe, and police run checks around Koh Phangan party nights. Read our drugs and alcohol law guide before you go.
  • Drinks: buy sealed drinks from the bar and keep them with you.
  • Boat safety: wear the life jacket on the crossing and don't board drunk enough to fall; the crew can refuse anyone unsafe.
  • Weather: from October to December the crossing can be rough. If the boat is cancelled, choose another date or a full refund.

Planning your night

Eat a proper dinner before the 22:00 pickup; food at the venue is limited and the night is long. Agree a meeting point with your group in case phones die, and keep the driver's contact from your confirmation. If you need to be back before dawn, book a private boat with your own return time instead of the shared package.
