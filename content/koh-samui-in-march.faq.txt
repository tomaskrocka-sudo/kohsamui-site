Is March a good time to visit Koh Samui? | Yes. March has only about 5 rain days, calm sea, highs of 30 °C and a 29 °C sea, and every boat trip runs. Hotel prices start to ease after the February peak, which makes March one of the best months for good weather at lower rates. The main downside is the building heat.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in March? | About 116 mm on 1991–2020 averages, but over only about 5 days with 1 mm or more. That total is close to July's, yet July spreads it over about 11 days. Most March days are dry and sunny, and the rain does little to disrupt boat trips.
Is March a good month for boat trips from Koh Samui? | Yes, one of the best. February to May has the calmest sea, and March's typical daily highest waves are about 0.5 m on the east coast and 0.4 m on the west and south. Ang Thong (1,800 THB plus the 300 THB park fee), Koh Tao (2,200 THB) and Pig Island (1,450 THB) all run in season.
Is March good for diving and snorkelling near Koh Samui? | Yes. March and April usually give the clearest water of the year in the Gulf, and Sail Rock's visibility is usually best then. For snorkelling, the good reefs are offshore: Koh Tan on the Pig Island trip, Ang Thong and Koh Nang Yuan by Koh Tao, where a 250 THB landing fee is paid in cash.
When is the Full Moon Party in March 2027? | Monday 22 March 2027, on Haad Rin, Koh Phangan. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup from most areas, and runs subject to safe seas. Entry, about 200 THB, is paid in cash at the beach. We re-check every party date about 30 days ahead.
Is March cheaper than February on Koh Samui? | Yes, for rooms. Peak rates, 30–80% above shoulder-season prices, run from mid-December to February, and hotel prices start to ease in March. Mid-range doubles cost about 1,500–3,500 THB in Lamai and 2,000–4,500 THB in Chaweng in the shoulder season. Boat trips, food and massages cost the same all year.
Is it too hot on Koh Samui in March? | For most people, no. Highs are about 30 °C, against 32–33 °C in April and May, but nights stay warm and humidity is high. Plan sightseeing for the morning, spend 12:00–15:00 in the shade or the sea, and drink more water than you think you need.
Are there festivals on Koh Samui in March 2027? | Few. Beyond the Full Moon Party on 22 March and the weekly markets and Muay Thai nights, the Samui Open Beach Volleyball usually runs in late March at Central Samui (26–29 March in 2026; the 2027 date had not been announced by November 2026). Songkran follows on 13–15 April 2027.
Should I come in March or April for Koh Samui? | March for lower heat and room rates, April for Songkran and the clearest water. March averages 30 °C highs and a 29 °C sea; April 32 °C and 30 °C. Both are calm, shoulder-season months, though rooms around Songkran (13–15 April 2027) book up early.
