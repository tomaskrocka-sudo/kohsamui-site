Is September a good time to visit Koh Samui? | Yes, especially for value. September averages about 118 mm of rain over 11 rain days, the sea is good for boat trips on most days and Ang Thong is open. The school-holiday crowds have gone and room rates fall to shoulder-season levels. It is the last reliable month before the October monsoon.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in September? | About 118 mm on 1991–2020 averages, over about 11 days with 1 mm or more. That is close to July and well under half of October's 295 mm. Expect mixed sun and showers, with most showers passing within an hour or two.
Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in September? | Yes. The park is open from 16 December to 31 October, and September is good for the crossing on most days. The big boat tour costs 1,500 THB and the speedboat tour 1,800 THB, both plus the 300 THB park fee in cash. September is also quieter on the boats than July and August.
Can I do the Koh Tao day trip from Samui in September? | Yes, and it is a good month for it. February to September is the best season for the long open-water crossing; from October to January it is the trip most often moved for weather. Our day trip to Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan costs 2,200 THB plus a 250 THB Nang Yuan fee in cash.
When is the Full Moon Party in September 2027? | Wednesday 15 September 2027, the night before a morning full moon, on Haad Rin Sunrise Beach, Koh Phangan. The date is provisional until the Haad Rin association confirms it. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person; entry, about 200 THB, is paid in cash at the beach.
Is Koh Samui quiet in September? | Yes. September and early October are the quietest months for nightlife, and some bars, cafés and restaurants cut their hours or take a break, so call ahead before a long drive. Ang Thong tours and the Friday Fisherman's Village market are noticeably less crowded than in July and August.
Is September a good month for diving near Koh Samui? | Yes. The Gulf diving season runs from February to October, and in July 2026 the Koh Tao Tourism Association named June to September as the peak for whale shark sightings at sites such as Sail Rock. The Andaman coast is at its wettest in September, so the Gulf is the better choice.
What is Sart Duean Sip on Koh Samui? | The southern Thai festival of merit for the dead, held at every temple on Samui with krayasat rice sweets. In 2027 it falls on about Thursday 30 September; the date is calculated from the lunar calendar, so confirm it nearer the time. Cover shoulders and knees if you visit a temple.
Is September or October better for Koh Samui? | September, for weather. It averages about 118 mm of rain against about 295 mm in October, when the north-east monsoon arrives with heavy bursts of rain and the first red flags on Chaweng and Lamai. October is cheaper and is the last month for Ang Thong before its 1 November closure.
