Why go: a big, fast boat for a small group

Phuchareen is a 40 ft speedboat with three outboard engines and a crew of three, which makes it one of the more powerful boats you can charter from Koh Samui for a group of eight. That power matters on the longer routes: the run to Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan is quicker and you get more time snorkelling. Closer to home, it is a comfortable way to spend a day around Koh Phangan's beaches or the small islands off Samui's south coast.

Because the boat is yours, the route and timing are agreed with the captain on the day. Lunch is at a beach restaurant of your choice along the way.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: groups of six to eight, families with older children, and anyone who wants Koh Tao without the crowds of a shared boat.
  • Choose something else if you are two or four people going only to Pig Island: our Pig Island private speedboat is far cheaper.
  • Bigger groups: the boat carries up to 35 with a small per-head charge.

Good to know

  • Ang Thong: the marine park is closed from 1 November to 15 December every year.
  • Comfort: speedboats are bumpy in swell. The back of the boat is the smoothest place to sit.
  • Cash: island and park fees are paid in cash. Koh Nang Yuan does not allow plastic bottles or fins on the island.
  • Weather: the captain decides on safety. If the trip is cancelled for weather, you choose a new date or a full refund.
