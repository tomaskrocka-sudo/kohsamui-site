How much does a private speedboat cost?

Three things set the price: the boat, the route and the number of guests. A couple or a family of four on the PP 30 ft pays from 19,000 THB for the day; a group of six on the NP pays from 28,000 THB; a group of 12 on the AP pays from 30,000 THB, which works out at 2,500 THB per person. On the boats we price by route, the southern islands are the cheapest and Koh Tao the most expensive, because of fuel and time.

National park and island fees are not included and are paid in cash on the day: Ang Thong 300 THB per foreign adult and 150 THB per child; Koh Nang Yuan 250 THB per adult; Koh Madsum (Pig Island) about 50 THB; Koh Rap about 100 THB. Lunch is extra on most boats.

If your plan is one of our fixed routes, a fixed-price private trip is often cheaper than a fleet charter and can be booked online straight away: the Ang Thong private speedboat costs 33,000 THB per boat for about 8 hours, and the Pig Island private speedboat costs 11,950 THB for up to 10 guests from Thong Krut.

Where to go: charter itineraries

Ang Thong Marine Park

The classic full day: the Emerald Lake on Koh Mae Ko, the viewpoint climb on Koh Wua Talab, snorkelling and kayaking among 42 limestone islands about 30 km west of Samui, roughly an hour each way by speedboat. On a private boat you can reach the viewpoints before or after the shared tours. The park is closed from 1 November to 15 December every year and reopens on 16 December. Read the Ang Thong guide before you go.

Pig Island, Koh Tan and Koh Rap

The year-round route and the cheapest on every boat: the beach pigs on Koh Madsum, the shallow reef at Koh Tan and the quieter beach on Koh Rap. These islands are off the south-west coast and stay calmer than the east side in the monsoon months, so this is our standard November route.

Koh Som and Tongson Bay

Short hops off the north-east coast for a half day with children: clear, shallow water from December to April and little time in transit.

Koh Phangan beaches

A private boat is the easy way to Phangan's beaches without roads: Haad Yuan and the Haad Rin peninsula, Than Sadet and Thong Nai Pan on the east coast, and Bottle Beach in the north. Phangan's south coast is 20–30 minutes from Samui's north coast.

Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan

The longest day, about 1.5 hours each way: snorkelling around Koh Tao and the sandbar viewpoint on Koh Nang Yuan (250 THB fee per adult, no plastic bottles, fins or drones). Choose a twin-engine boat for the crossing.

Samui coastal sunset

Run along the west or south-west coast in the late afternoon and watch the sun drop behind the Five Islands; sunset is between about 17:55 in November and 18:45 in July. See sunset cruises.

Full Moon Party night

For the party, the fixed-price Full Moon Party private speedboat takes up to 10 guests from Samui to Haad Rin and back at times you choose, with hotel pickup and drop-off on Samui. Entry is about 200 THB per person at the gate.

Half day or full day?

Most of our speedboat programmes are 8-hour days, which is what Ang Thong, Koh Tao or a proper Koh Phangan beach day needs. Coco Cay also runs 4-hour programmes to Koh Tan and Koh Madsum or to Koh Phangan, and half days on other boats are quoted on request.

Choosing the right boat

Match the boat to your group first, then to the route. For two to four guests, the PP 30 ft is the obvious choice unless you want more comfort, in which case Coco Cay (44 ft, sun pads and a restroom) is the premium option. For five to eight, the NP or KT give more room for little extra. For groups of 10 to 14, the AP's price already includes 12 guests. Above 20, choose the MN (up to 37), the FS or PP 35 ft (up to 28) or the AP (up to 30).

For long crossings (Koh Tao, Ang Thong in a breeze), a bigger, twin or triple-engine boat rides more smoothly. Small speedboats have no toilet, so plan a stop on longer routes. If anyone gets seasick, is pregnant or has back problems, a catamaran is gentler; for a luxury day, look at our motor yachts, and for big groups at the full charter fleet.

When to charter

February to April is the calmest and clearest time for any route, including Koh Tao. May to September is good on most days, because Samui is sheltered from the south-west monsoon that hits Phuket. From October to December the north-east monsoon brings rain and swell to the east and north-east coasts, peaking in November (about 445 mm of rain); then we route south and west, and Ang Thong is closed from 1 November to 15 December. Christmas and New Year book out weeks ahead.

Piers and meeting points

Most of our speedboats leave from piers on Samui's north coast; the fixed-price Pig Island private boats leave from Thong Krut in the south-west. The exact pier depends on the boat and is sent with your confirmation as a map pin. Where hotel transfer is included, we collect you; otherwise you are welcome to come to the pier yourself. Hillside villas on steep roads may need a meeting point at the bottom of the hill. Ferry piers are listed on our places map.

Safety

Every boat carries life jackets in adult and children's sizes, first aid and a VHF radio, and our captains hold Thai Marine Department licences. Children must wear a life jacket on board. We never load a boat above its maximum, and the captain has the final say on routes and stops. If the sea is unsafe we move you to a sheltered route, change the date or cancel; if we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date. Check the forecast on our sea conditions page and the tide tables.
