Why go: a classic gulet for your own group

Naga is a 30 m two-masted wooden gulet built in 2005 with six air-conditioned en-suite cabins, a covered aft deck for dining, a saloon and a sun deck with 20 loungers. Chartered privately, it is one of the few boats on Koh Samui that can host a group of 20 to 50 for a day, or 10 for a night on board with a chef.

Meals, transfers and crew are included, so a full day costs less per head for a big group than many smaller yachts.

Which charter to choose

The sunset cruise from 17:00 to 20:00 suits parties and dinners, with a welcome drink and finger food. The half day runs in the morning or afternoon along the north coast to Koh Som with breakfast or lunch. The full days cover Koh Phangan or the Samui bays, or Ang Thong from 16 December, with breakfast, lunch, kayaks and snorkel gear. The overnight includes all meals by a private chef, bed linen and toiletries, and anchors for the night before returning around midday. Every option includes return hotel transfer on Koh Samui for the whole group.

Who it suits

  • Groups of 15–50: birthdays, reunions and company days.
  • Couples or families booking an overnight with cabins and a chef.
  • Anyone who wants Ang Thong in comfort on their own schedule.

Who should choose something else

  • Couples on a day trip: a small catamaran is far cheaper.
  • Shared-trip budgets: join the Naga yachting day per person.

Good to know

  • Under sail: sails go up when the wind suits; the yacht also motors to keep time.
  • Ang Thong season: the park reopens on 16 December.
  • Booking: online booking for tomorrow closes at midnight Thai time.
