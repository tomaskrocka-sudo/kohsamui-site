Go between February and April for the calmest sea and the least rain on Koh Samui. Stay in Choeng Mon or Chaweng Noi for quiet, swimmable bays close to the airport, or on the west coast for sunsets over the sea: Taling Ngam faces the Five Islands. Book one private day on the water: a private speedboat from 19,000 THB per boat, the Ang Thong private speedboat at 33,000 THB, a longtail sunset with dinner on the beach from 7,500 THB for two, or the shared boutique yachting day to Ang Thong at 4,500 THB per person.

Is Koh Samui good for a honeymoon?

Yes, with two caveats. Samui has what most couples want: warm sea all year (28–30 °C), a long list of resorts with private pool villas, good restaurants, couples' spas and easy day trips by boat to islands with no roads. Direct flights from Bangkok take about 1 hour 10 minutes, and the airport is 5–15 minutes from the north-east beaches, so you lose little of your first and last days. The caveats: the rainy season falls from October to December, which catches couples who plan around the European autumn, and flights to Samui cost more than flights to Phuket because one airline runs most of them. Chaweng is busy and loud at night; the rest of the island is much quieter.

Where to stay on your honeymoon

Decide between sunrise bays on the east and north-east, which have the best swimming, and the sunset coast in the west, which is quieter but further from everything. Mid-range rooms cost about 2,000–5,000 THB a night in the shoulder season; luxury resorts start around 10,000 THB and add 30–80% from mid-December to February.

Resorts couples ask about

We don't sell rooms, but these names come up most often, so here is where they are. On the north-west tip at Laem Yai is the Four Seasons, where much of The White Lotus season 3 was filmed. Above Taling Ngam in the south-west are the Conrad, the InterContinental and Nivata, all with cliff-edge pool villas facing the sunset. A three-day romantic plan

  1. Day 1, morning Late breakfast, then a couples' massage. Pick a resort spa for the setting or a good beach shop for value (about 300–600 THB an hour).
  2. Day 1, evening Fisherman's Village in Bophut for dinner along the old wooden shophouses; on Fridays the walking street runs from about 17:00 to 23:00.
  3. Day 2, all day Your private boat: Pig Island and the reef at Koh Tan in the morning, a quiet beach for lunch and a sunset run home. From 16 December, consider Ang Thong instead.
  4. Day 3, morning Big Buddha and Wat Plai Laem before 09:00, when it's cool and empty, then a swim at Choeng Mon or Silver Beach.
  5. Day 3, afternoon Drive to Taling Ngam on the west coast. Early dinner at Baan Suan Lung Khai, then sunset over the Five Islands from a clifftop bar or the beach.

Sunset spots and dinners

The sun sets over the sea only on the west and south-west coasts. Taling Ngam faces the Five Islands and has the best sunsets on Samui; Lipa Noi, Nathon pier and Bang Por on the north-west are close behind. The north-coast bays (Bophut, Maenam, Bang Rak) don't get the sun dropping into the water, but the sky glows over Koh Phangan.

More romantic things to do

  • Sunset horse ride at Taling Ngam: a guided 60-minute ride on coastal trails and beach, with a 17:00 sunset slot, from 1,700 THB per rider; a private return van (3,000 THB per group) is compulsory. See the sunset ride.
  • Cook together: a garden cooking class for up to 4 guests costs 2,000 THB per person, with transfer from the main areas.
  • Koh Phangan by private boat: a 6-hour afternoon honeymoon trip to Koh Phangan on a 13 m speedboat runs 12:30–18:30 from 35,000 THB per boat.
  • Waterfall swim: Na Muang 1 has a pool 5–10 minutes from the car park, free and fullest October to January.
  • Sunrise at Lad Koh viewpoint: the rocks between Chaweng Noi and Coral Cove face east; stay well back from the edge, as waves have swept people off the rocks.
  • Friday night market: the Fisherman's Village Walking Street in Bophut, about 17:00–23:00.

Honeymoon in the rainy season

If your dates fall in October, November or early December, Samui still works for a honeymoon with the right plan. Choose a resort with a great pool, spa and restaurant, so a wet afternoon is no loss. Base yourself on the west or south-west coast, which is sheltered from the north-east monsoon, and keep your boat day flexible: the Pig Island sunset tour runs every day of the year from Thong Krut, weather permitting, and private boats can move to a calmer day at no cost if we cancel for sea safety. Ang Thong reopens on 16 December. November is the rainiest month, with about 445 mm.

Proposals

A private sunset is the classic Samui proposal: the whole boat is yours, and the crew can stop as the sun touches the horizon. Tell us when you enquire that it's a proposal, and keep the secret on WhatsApp; flowers, a cake, a photographer or a dinner set-up can be quoted as extras with a few days' notice. On land, a reserved sunset sofa at a clifftop bar on the west coast or a quiet beach at Taling Ngam works well. Have a back-up evening in case of rain.

Spas and wellness for couples

A one-hour Thai massage costs about 300–500 THB at a good shop (400–600 in Chaweng), and resort spas charge about 1,800–3,500 THB for an hour, more at the top resorts. Tamarind Springs in the hills behind Lamai is a forest spa with herbal steam caves and plunge pools among granite boulders; its Steam and Dream session costs 1,500 THB++ per person, and longer packages with massage run from 5,500 THB++. Couples who want a full reset can book a few nights at a wellness resort such as Kamalaya (programmes from about US$1,836 for 3 nights) or Absolute Sanctuary. The White Lotus coast by private boat

Season 3 of The White Lotus, aired from February to April 2025, was filmed largely at the Four Seasons Resort on Laem Yai, the quiet north-west tip of the island. You can see that coast from a private boat on the way to or from the west-coast islands, and our captains know the stretch. Be realistic: the resort is private, boats keep a respectful distance offshore, and landing at the resort's beach is not part of any trip we sell. Practical tips for honeymooners

  • Entry: most nationalities now get 30 days visa-free (since 15 September 2026), and every arrival must file the free Thailand Digital Arrival Card at tdac.immigration.go.th within 3 days before landing. Details in our Thailand visa guide.
  • Scooters: romantic in films, risky in reality. Samui's roads are steep and accidents are common; you need a licence with the motorcycle category or an International Driving Permit that includes it, or your insurance won't pay.
  • Booking order: for February–April and the Christmas period, book your resort first, then your private boat day as soon as your dates are fixed, because each boat can only take one charter a day.
  • Weather back-up: plan the boat day early in your stay, so a rough-sea day can move to a later date.
