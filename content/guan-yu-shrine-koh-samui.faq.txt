Where is the Guan Yu statue on Koh Samui? | On the hillside above Hua Thanon, on the inland side of the ring road about 4–5 km south of central Lamai; look for the red gate.
How tall is the Guan Yu statue on Koh Samui? | The bronze statue is about 16 m tall, taller than Big Buddha, and is described as the largest bronze Guan Yu statue in Thailand.
Is the Guan Yu Shrine free to visit? | Yes, entry is free; incense and offerings are sold at the shrine if you want to take part.
What happens at the Guan Yu Shrine at Chinese New Year? | Local Chinese-Thai families come to pray and make offerings, with lanterns, incense and celebrations; Chinese New Year 2027 is Saturday 6 February, and the programme is usually published in January.
What should I wear to the Guan Yu Shrine? | Cover shoulders and knees as at any place of worship; shoes can usually stay on outdoors, but follow signs in the shrine hall.
What are the Guan Yu Shrine's opening hours? | It is open daily from about 08:00 to 17:00; some listings give 09:00 or 18:00, and hours can change on public holidays.
Who was Guan Yu? | A general of China's Three Kingdoms period who died in 220 CE and was later deified as a symbol of loyalty, courage and righteousness, worshipped by Taoists, Buddhists and Confucians.
How do I offer incense at the Guan Yu Shrine? | Buy a bundle at the shrine, light it from the flame provided, hold it with both hands, bow three times and place it in the urn; never blow incense out.
How long do I need at the Guan Yu Shrine? | About 30 minutes; combine it with the Hua Thanon fish market, 300 m away, and the Red Temple, about 1 km north.
Can I get to the Guan Yu Shrine by songthaew? | Yes, ring-road songthaews pass the gate during the day; locals pay about 30–80 THB and services mostly stop by 20:00–22:00.
