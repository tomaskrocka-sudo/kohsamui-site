Is April a good time to visit Koh Samui? | Yes, if you can handle heat. April has highs of 32 °C, about 83 mm of rain over 6 rain days, a 30 °C sea and some of the calmest water of the year, so it is one of the best months for beaches, boat trips and diving. It is poor for waterfalls and for long midday walks.
How hot is Koh Samui in April? | Daytime highs average 32 °C and nights stay around 26 °C, with high humidity. April and May are the hardest months for walking and sightseeing, so plan outdoor visits for the early morning and spend 12:00–15:00 in the shade or the sea.
Does it rain on Koh Samui in April? | A little. April averages about 83 mm over 6 days with 1 mm or more, against about 445 mm in November. Most of the month is dry.
Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in April? | Yes. Ang Thong is open from 16 December to 31 October and closes only from 1 November to 15 December. April has calm crossings and some of the clearest water of the year. Our speedboat tour costs 1,800 THB plus the 300 THB park fee in cash.
~When is Songkran 2027 on Koh Samui? | Songkran is a public holiday from Tuesday 13 to Thursday 15 April 2027, and on Samui the street water fights usually start on Monday 12 April. Chaweng Beach Road is the main water fight, with smaller celebrations in Lamai and a more traditional one in Nathon.
When is the Full Moon Party in April 2027? | Wednesday 21 April 2027, the week after Songkran, on Haad Rin, Koh Phangan. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup and drop-off from most areas; entry (about 200 THB) is paid in cash at the beach.
Is April good for diving and snorkelling? | Yes. The Gulf diving season runs from February to October, and visibility is often the year's best in March and April. Ang Thong, Koh Tan and Koh Nang Yuan are all calm and clear, and whale sharks are possible but never guaranteed.
Are hotels more expensive in April? | Mostly not. April is shoulder season, with rooms well below the mid-December to February peak. Songkran week (12–15 April) is the exception: it is one of the busiest weeks of the year and sells out early.
Where should I stay on Koh Samui for Songkran? | Stay in Chaweng to be in the middle of the water fight, or in Bophut and Fisherman's Village for a calmer base a short Grab ride away. Maenam, Choeng Mon and the west coast are quiet enough to avoid most of it.
Is April or May better for Koh Samui? | April is drier (about 83 mm over 6 days against about 132 mm over 11 days in May) and has Songkran. May is a degree hotter, with short afternoon showers, fewer visitors and the Samui Regatta from 22 to 29 May 2027. The sea is calm in both.
