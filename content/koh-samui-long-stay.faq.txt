How much is a monthly villa rental on Koh Samui? | A modest private pool villa starts at around 40,000 THB a month on a long contract, and large sea-view villas can cost well over 100,000 THB in high season. Studios and one-bedroom condos run from about 12,000 THB in Maenam to 30,000 THB in Chaweng.
What is the minimum rental period for long-term villas? | Our long-term villas need a minimum of 6 months, with rent on request. Across the island, 6 and 12 months are the standard contract lengths; monthly stays are possible but cost more per month.
How is electricity charged in Koh Samui rentals? | Either you pay the official Provincial Electricity Authority bill, or the landlord charges their own rate per unit, which is often well above the state tariff. Ask for the rate in writing, read the meter on move-in and ask to see recent bills, because air-conditioning drives the total.
How much deposit do landlords ask for? | Commonly one to two months' rent as a security deposit plus the first month in advance. Get the amount and the refund terms in the contract, and photograph the inventory and meter readings on the day you move in.
Which area is best for a long stay on Koh Samui? | Maenam for quiet and value, Bophut for cafés and restaurants with a calm beach, Lamai for gyms and a small-town feel, and the hills above Chaweng Noi for sea-view villas. Base yourself where you will shop and work, not just where the beach is nicest.
Who files the TM30 for my rental? | The landlord, house owner or condo manager, within 24 hours of you moving in. Ask for a photo of the receipt: immigration in Maenam checks it when you apply for extensions or file 90-day reports.
Can I rent a scooter monthly on Koh Samui? | Yes. A 125 cc scooter costs about 2,000–4,500 THB a month, cheapest in Maenam, Nathon and Lipa Noi. You still need a motorcycle licence and an International Driving Permit, and you should never leave your passport as a deposit.
When is it hardest to find a long-term rental? | From December to April and in July and August, when holiday demand peaks and some owners switch to short holiday lets. May–June and September–November are the easiest months to find a place and negotiate.
Should I sign a long-term lease before I arrive? | It is safer not to. Book a holiday villa or hotel for the first 2–4 weeks, then view properties in person, test the internet and drive the access road before paying any deposit.
