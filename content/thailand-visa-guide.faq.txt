Do I need a visa for Thailand in 2026? | Not if you hold one of the 60 visa-exempt passports (including the UK, US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and all EU states): you get 30 days for tourism on arrival. Mauritius and Seychelles get 15 days, some countries have bilateral deals of 14, 30 or 90 days, and Azerbaijan, Belarus and Serbia get a visa on arrival. Everyone else applies online at thaievisa.go.th before travel.
Is the 60-day visa exemption still available? | No. The 60-day exemption for 93 countries was repealed by Royal Gazette notices dated 31 August 2026 and ended on 15 September 2026. It was replaced by a 30-day exemption for 60 countries. Only a 30-day extension at immigration gets you back to about 60 days in total.
I entered before 15 September 2026. How long can I stay? | You keep the stay stamped in your passport on entry, including a full 60-day stamp, if you entered on or before 14 September 2026. Your next entry after leaving Thailand falls under the new 30-day rule.
~Can I extend my stay on Koh Samui? | Yes. Koh Samui Immigration in Maenam can extend a 30-day visa exemption once by up to 30 days on form TM.7 for 1,900 THB, at the officer's discretion. Tourist visas (60 days) can also be extended once by 30 days.
~Where is the immigration office on Koh Samui? | At 333 Maenam Road, Soi 1, Moo 1, Maenam 84330, on the north coast, phone +66 77 423 440. It moved from Nathon in 2016 and usually opens Monday to Friday 08:30–16:30, closed on weekends and public holidays.
What is the TDAC and is it free? | The Thailand Digital Arrival Card is the online arrival form every foreign national must file within 3 days before arriving by air, land or sea. It is free on the official site tdac.immigration.go.th. Any site that charges a fee is a look-alike.
Is there a tourist entry fee for Thailand? | Not yet. A flat 450 THB fee per foreign arrival has been proposed and went to public consultation in October 2026, but it still needs Cabinet approval and a Royal Gazette notice. Nothing is collected as of November 2026.
How many times can I enter Thailand overland visa-free? | Twice per calendar year through land-border checkpoints on the 30-day or 15-day exemption. Nationals of Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia and Singapore are exempt from this cap. Air arrivals have no fixed cap, but frequent entries can be questioned or refused.
What is the fine for overstaying in Thailand? | 500 THB per day, up to a maximum of 20,000 THB. Overstays of more than 90 days also bring a re-entry ban of 1 to 10 years if you surrender, and 5 or 10 years if you are arrested.
Can I work on Koh Samui on a visa exemption or DTV? | No work for Thai employers or Thai clients is allowed on either. The visa exemption is for tourism only. The DTV allows remote work for a foreign employer or your own foreign clients. Local jobs need a Non-Immigrant B visa and a work permit.
