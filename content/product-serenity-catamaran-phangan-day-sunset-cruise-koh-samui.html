Why go: a big, steady catamaran on a set schedule

Serenity is a 22 m motor catamaran with air-conditioned cabins, shade, a sun deck and a slide, which makes it one of the most comfortable shared boats on Koh Samui. Two scheduled trips use it: a midweek day out to Koh Phangan's east and north coasts and a Saturday sunset dinner along Samui's north shore. Both include hotel transfers and food, so there is little to pay on the day.

What you'll do

The full day crosses to Koh Phangan for a swim at Haad Yuan, lunch and free time at Thong Nai Pan, one of Phangan's prettiest bays, and snorkelling at Koh Ma, where a sandbar joins the island to the coast. Paddle boards and fishing lines are on board. The sunset cruise is shorter: out of Bang Rak, past the Big Buddha, and at anchor off Fisherman's Village for a BBQ dinner as the sun goes down.

Who it suits

  • Families: child prices are half the adult price and the boat is stable with cabins for naps.
  • Couples who want a sunset dinner on the water without the cost of a private yacht.
  • Anyone wanting a boat trip in November or early December, when Ang Thong is closed.

Who should choose something else

  • Groups wanting their own boat: see Serenity private charter.
  • Strong snorkellers: Koh Tao has clearer water; see the Koh Tao and Nang Yuan day trip.

Good to know

  • Days: book the right day: Wednesday or Friday for the full day, Saturday for sunset.
  • Sea: October to December is the windiest period on this route.
  • Evenings: it can be breezy on deck after sunset; bring a light layer.
