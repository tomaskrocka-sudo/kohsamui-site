The top things to do on Koh Samui are a boat day to Ang Thong Marine Park (open 16 December to October) or Pig Island (all year), the Big Buddha and Wat Plai Laem, Na Muang Waterfall, Hin Ta and Hin Yai, the Friday walking street in Fisherman's Village and a Muay Thai night in Chaweng. Most sights are free; boat trips start at 1,450 THB. This guide lists 45 ideas we checked in November 2026, with prices, hours, the best season for each and the attractions that have closed.

Boat trips and islands

A day on the water is the highlight of most Samui holidays. Ang Thong Marine Park is closed every year from 1 November to 15 December and reopens on 16 December; Pig Island trips run every day of the year. Hotel pickup is included from most areas of Koh Samui on all our day tours; Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Bang Kao and Thong Krut add 400 THB per person return.

1. Ang Thong National Marine Park

Forty-two limestone islands about 30 km west of Samui, with the Emerald Lake (Thale Nai), a saltwater lagoon inside Koh Mae Ko, and the viewpoint on Koh Wua Talab, a steep climb of about 20–30 minutes with one of the best views in the Gulf of Thailand. Days include snorkelling, kayaking and a beach lunch. Our speedboat day tour costs 1,800 THB (child 1,400), the big boat 1,500 THB (child 1,100), and the VIP tour 4,500 THB, with only 15 guests on a big speedboat. You can also stay the night (tent 3,600 THB, bungalow 4,200 THB) or charter a private speedboat for 33,000 THB per boat. The national park fee is 300 THB per foreign adult and 150 THB per child, paid in cash at the park. Trying to choose? Read speedboat vs big boat.

2. Pig Island (Koh Madsum) and Koh Tan

The pigs that wander the beach on Koh Madsum are Samui's most photographed animals, and Koh Tan next door has the best shallow-reef snorkelling close to the island. Boats leave from Thong Krut on the south-west coast, about 30 minutes away. Our shared speedboat is 1,450 THB (child 1,250); there is also a longtail trip (1,750 THB), a sunset tour (1,550 THB) and Pink Dolphins and Pig Island (2,250 THB). The Koh Madsum island fee is about 50 THB in cash. Please don't chase the pigs or feed them junk food. Pig Island is the best boat day in November and early December, when Ang Thong is closed and the south-west coast is more sheltered. Guide: Pig Island Koh Samui.

3. Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan

A long day north to the dive island of Koh Tao and the three islets of Koh Nang Yuan, joined by a sandbar, with a short, steep climb to the famous viewpoint. Our speedboat day trip costs 2,200 THB (child 1,600); the Nang Yuan landing fee is 250 THB per adult in cash, and plastic bottles are not allowed on the island. It is open water, so it is the trip most affected by swell: February to September is best.

4. Private boats and sunset cruises

For families, groups and couples who want their own schedule, private speedboats start at 19,000 THB per boat and sailing catamarans at 20,000 THB per boat; shared sunset cruises on the market run about 1,500–3,500 THB per person. The private Pig Island longtail is our best value at 5,950 THB per boat. See private speedboats, catamaran charter and sunset cruises, and ask for a quote on WhatsApp.

Beaches worth the trip

The east coast is for swimming and sunrises, the north is calm, shallow and tidal with the best dining, the west has the sunsets and tidal flats, and the south is quiet and local. The sea is calm from January to September (calmest February–April); October to December brings surf, rip currents and red flags to Chaweng and Lamai. Full comparison: Koh Samui beaches.

5. Choeng Mon

A sheltered north-east bay with soft white sand and one of the calmest seas on the island; at low tide a sandbar leads to the islet of Koh Fan Noi. Ideal for families with small children and 5–10 minutes from the airport. Choeng Mon guide.

6. Chaweng North

The quietest stretch of Samui's busiest beach, with fine white sand on a gentle slope and the best swimming of the big beaches, yet a short walk to Chaweng's restaurants. Red flags are common from October to December. Chaweng guide.

7. Silver Beach (Crystal Bay)

A small cove framed by granite boulders between Chaweng Noi and Lamai, with pale sand and clear water for a swim and a snorkel. It fills by about 10:00, so come early. Silver Beach guide.

8. Lamai

About 4 km of sand with a slower pace than Chaweng, a nightly market and good-value stays. The centre is best for swimming but shelves steeply; the north end is shallow and stony, so wear reef shoes. Lamai guide.

9. Lipa Noi

A quiet west-coast beach with very shallow, calm water and no coral: the best choice on the island for toddlers, at high tide. At low tide the sand flats stretch far out. Sunsets are excellent. Lipa Noi guide.

10. Taling Ngam

A long village beach in the south-west with fishing boats, cliff-top resorts and the island's best sunset over the Five Islands. It is tidal, so swim near high tide. Nearby Baan Suan Lung Khai, a southern seafood restaurant in the MICHELIN Guide Bib Gourmand list for 2026, needs a reservation. Taling Ngam guide.

Temples and culture

All the main temples are free. Cover shoulders and knees, carry a sarong and take your shoes off inside halls. Our Koh Samui temples guide has the full etiquette and two half-day routes.

11. Big Buddha (Wat Phra Yai)

The golden seated Buddha on Koh Fan islet, reached by a causeway on the north-east coast, about 3 km from the airport. Free, open 07:00–18:30 [kp_open spec="Mo-Su 07:00-18:30"]; go before 09:30 or in the late afternoon. Big Buddha guide.

12. Wat Plai Laem

A colourful lakeside temple about 1 km east of Big Buddha, built 2004–09, with an 18-armed white Guanyin and a laughing Buddha. Free, about 06:00–18:00 [kp_open spec="Mo-Su 06:00-18:00"]; children love feeding the fish. Wat Plai Laem guide.

13. Wat Khunaram and the mummified monk

The body of the meditation master Luang Pho Daeng, who died in 1973 while meditating, sits upright in a glass case on the ring road in Na Mueang. Free, daylight hours; dress modestly and photograph respectfully. Wat Khunaram guide.

14. Laem Sor Pagoda

A gold-tiled chedi on the shore at the end of Bang Kao beach on the quiet south coast, with a white chedi on the hill behind. Free, 07:00–18:00 [kp_open spec="Mo-Su 07:00-18:00"]; best in late-afternoon light and close to the Pig Island pier. Laem Sor guide.

15. The Red Temple (Wat Ratchathammaram)

A terracotta-red hall covered in carved reliefs on the ring road south of Lamai. Free, 07:00–17:00 [kp_open spec="Mo-Su 07:00-17:00"]; go early for the light. Red Temple guide.

16. Guan Yu Shrine and Hua Thanon

A 16 m bronze statue of the Chinese general Guan Yu above Hua Thanon, a Muslim fishing village with a morning fish market and the island's central mosque. Free, about 08:00–17:00. Guan Yu guide · Hua Thanon guide.

Waterfalls and viewpoints

Samui's waterfalls are best from October to January, when they flow strongly but paths are slippery; from February to April they thin to a trickle. Never jump from the rocks, and leave quickly if the water rises after heavy rain. Full guides: Koh Samui waterfalls and hiking.

17. Na Muang Waterfall 1

The island's easiest waterfall, a short walk from the car park to a cascade with a pool you can swim in. Free (parking 20 THB scooter, 60 THB car), open about 09:00–17:00. Use the waterfall car park, not the first lot by the elephant camp.

18. Na Muang Waterfall 2

Higher up the same road, a steep 20–40 minute climb with ropes on some sections leads to a taller fall. Free, about 09:00–18:00 [kp_open spec="Mo-Su 09:00-18:00"]; busy by about 10:30, so start early and wear shoes with grip.

19. Hin Lad Waterfall

A jungle waterfall about 2 km south of Nathon, reached past the forest temple Wat Namtok Hin Lad. The trail between tiers is steep and uneven; allow about two hours for the round trip. Hin Lad guide.

20. Lad Koh Viewpoint

A free roadside lookout on the ring road between Chaweng Noi and Coral Cove, worth a 10-minute stop at sunrise. Stay on the platform: a tourist was swept off the rocks here by a wave in November 2024. Lad Koh guide.

21. Overlap Stone

A balanced-rock viewpoint on the hill above Maret, about 3 km from Lamai, reached by a steep road and a walk of about 500 m with a suspension bridge. It is open 08:00–18:00 [kp_open spec="Mo-Su 08:00-18:00"]. Entry is about 300 THB including a soft drink. [check] "Overlap Stone 2" has been reported closed since a forestry raid in July 2026. We don't recommend the elephant-feeding add-on there. Overlap Stone guide.

22. Secret Buddha Garden (Tarnim Magic Garden)

A hilltop garden of sculptures in the central highlands, begun in 1976 and opened to visitors after its creator died in 1990. Entry is 80–100 THB (sources differ) and it is open 09:00–18:00 [kp_open spec="Mo-Su 09:00-18:00"]. The road is very steep: go by 4x4 or on a tour, not on a rental scooter. Secret Buddha Garden guide.

23. Hin Ta and Hin Yai

The "Grandfather and Grandmother Rocks", two naturally shaped rock formations at the south end of Lamai, reached through a lane of snack and souvenir stalls. Free (parking about 30 THB); open about 08:30–18:30 (Saturday 08:00–19:00). Stay off the wet rocks when waves are up, especially from October to December.

Adventure on land

The island's forested interior rises to Khao Pom (635 m), Samui's highest point. Adventure activities are sold through partners; we take enquiries on WhatsApp and confirm prices before you pay.

24. 4x4 jungle safari

The classic island tour: about 7 hours (roughly 10:00–17:00) by open 4x4 truck to Hin Ta and Hin Yai, a swim at Na Muang waterfall, Wat Khunaram, the Secret Buddha Garden, a hilltop Thai lunch and Big Buddha. The market price is about 1,700 THB including lunch and entry fees. Enquire about the jungle safari.

25. Zipline

The Tree Bridge zipline in the Maenam hills has 8 platforms and about 2 km of line, takes about an hour, and costs about 1,590 THB with 4x4 pickup from Chaweng to Maenam; sessions at 09:30, 12:30 and 14:30. Other parks operate near Lipa Noi and in the hills. Koh Samui zipline.

26. ATV and quad bikes

Guided ATV rides through jungle tracks start at about 1,490 THB for an hour, with transfers and helmet. Ride only with a guided operator, check the brakes and keep your insurance in mind: many travel policies exclude quad bikes. ATV and quad bikes.

27. Hiking

Marked trails are few and the interior is steep, hot and confusing, so hike with a local guide or stick to known routes such as the Na Muang 2 climb and the Hin Lad trail. Start early, carry 2 litres of water and tell someone your plan. Koh Samui hiking.

Animals: ethical choices only

28. An ethical elephant visit (no riding)

Choose a venue where elephants are not ridden and visitors don't bathe with them. The strongest welfare credentials on Samui belong to Samui Elephant Sanctuary (Bophut and Chaweng Noi), which is listed by World Animal Protection, has no riding and bans visitor bathing; a half day costs about 3,000 THB (child 1,500 THB) with transfers and a vegetarian meal. Even here visits include feeding, so no Samui venue meets the very strictest standard. Some online sellers still advertise "elephant trekking" on Samui: avoid any venue that offers rides, bathing, shows or chained animals. Read our guide to ethical elephant visits before you book anything.

Other wildlife: dolphin-spotting is part of our Pink Dolphins and Pig Island trip (sightings are never guaranteed), and the pigs on Koh Madsum live freely on the beach. More: Koh Samui wildlife.

Water sports

29. Scuba diving

The best dive site from Samui is Sail Rock, a pinnacle between Samui and Koh Phangan; two-dive day trips start at about 4,650 THB with gear, lunch and transfers. Many people learn to dive on Koh Tao instead. Around Ang Thong, the diving season is February to October, clearest in March and April. Koh Samui diving.

30. Snorkelling

The best snorkelling is off the islands: Koh Tan (on Pig Island trips), Ang Thong and Koh Nang Yuan. From the shore, try Silver Beach and Coral Cove on calm mornings. Wear water shoes on coarse sand and never stand on coral. Koh Samui snorkelling.

31. Kayak and stand-up paddle

Kayaks rent for about 300 THB an hour at Coral Cove, and paddleboards are available on the calmer beaches. Paddle in the morning before the wind rises, and stay inside the bay. In Ang Thong, kayaking is included on most tours. Kayak and SUP.

32. Jet ski, the safe way

Beach jet-ski rental has a long record of scams: renters are blamed for old damage and asked for large sums. If you rent, photograph and video every panel before you ride, never hand over your passport, and agree the price in writing. The safer choice is a guided ride: our Jet Ski Safari to Pig Island costs 9,950 THB per jet ski. Koh Samui jet ski.

33. Fishing trips

Shared fishing days and night squid-fishing trips run from Samui, with the catch cooked for you; shared day trips cost about 1,500 THB per person on the market, and private charters are priced per boat. Koh Samui fishing.

Food, markets and cooking

34. Fisherman's Village Walking Street, Bophut

Every Friday from about 17:00 to 23:00 [kp_open spec="Mo 17:00-23:00; We 17:00-23:00; Fr 17:00-23:00"], the old wooden shophouse street in Bophut fills with food stalls, crafts and music; smaller markets run on Monday and Wednesday. The road closes to traffic from about 16:30 on Fridays and illegally parked scooters are towed (release from 500 THB), so use the paid parking. Typical prices: pad thai 60–80 THB, mango sticky rice 50–80 THB, a full market dinner 300–500 THB per person. Fisherman's Village guide.

35. Chaweng Walking Street

From Thursday to Saturday evenings (about 16:00/17:00 to 22:00/23:00) a market runs by Chaweng Lake, opposite Central Samui, with street food and clothes. Koh Samui walking streets.

36. Lamai and Nathon night markets

Lamai Night Market, by the bridge on the beach road, runs every evening from about 17:00 with cheap food and live music; go between 18:00 and 20:00. Nathon's waterfront night market runs daily from about 16:30 to 22:00 and is busiest on Tuesdays. Bring cash, and expect outdoor markets to be rained off on some evenings in November and December. Koh Samui markets.

37. Hua Thanon morning fish market

Come early to see the catch landed and sold in the Muslim fishing village of Hua Thanon, south of Lamai. It is a working market: dress modestly and ask before photographing people.

38. Thai cooking class

Half-day classes teach four or five dishes, often starting with a market tour. Prices vary widely by school and by whether transfers are included; well-known schools charge from about 4,200 THB through booking sites with private transfers. A good rainy-day choice. Cooking classes.

39. Southern Thai food and wai khua

Try Samui's own dish, wai khua (octopus in coconut milk), plus southern sour curry (gaeng som), khua kling dry curry and khanom jeen noodles with southern curry. Seafood is best in Bang Por, Bang Rak and Hua Thanon. Restaurants · seafood.

Nightlife and shows

40. A Muay Thai fight night

Two stadiums in Chaweng hold fight nights: Phetchbuncha (Monday, Wednesday and Saturday) and Samui International Muay Thai Stadium (Tuesday, Friday and Sunday). Fights start at about 21:00 and run for about three hours; there are no fights on Thursdays. Tickets cost 1,500 THB standard, 2,000 THB ringside and 2,500 THB VIP; buy at the box office, not from touts. Chaweng Stadium is closed. Fight nights · Muay Thai training.

41. Fire shows and beach clubs

ARKbar on Chaweng Beach has fire shows every night, and Coco Tam's in Fisherman's Village runs shows at about 19:15 and 21:00 (no ticket; cocktails about 250–350 THB). Chaweng is the late-night centre; Lamai is quieter. Koh Samui nightlife · beach clubs.

42. Full Moon Party trip from Samui

The Full Moon Party is on Haad Rin beach on Koh Phangan. The next dates are Tuesday 24 November 2026 (also Loy Krathong), Thursday 24 December and Thursday 31 December 2026. Our Full Moon Party speedboat costs 1,400 THB return with hotel pickup from most areas; party entry (about 200 THB) is paid at the gate, and we don't sell entry tickets. All Full Moon Party dates.

Wellness

43. Thai massage

A one-hour Thai massage costs 300–500 THB in most of the island (400–600 THB in Chaweng); oil massages run 400–700 THB, and resort spas 1,800–3,500 THB or more. Koh Samui spas.

44. Yoga, meditation and retreats

Samui is one of Thailand's wellness centres, with drop-in yoga classes, meditation centres and residential retreats such as Kamalaya (from about US$1,836 for 3 nights) and Absolute Sanctuary. Yoga · retreats · meditation.

45. Golf

Santiburi Samui Country Club in Maenam is the island's 18-hole course (par 72); a round costs about 5,600 THB including caddie and cart. Book ahead in high season. Koh Samui golf.

Things to do on Koh Samui with kids

  • Calm beaches: Choeng Mon for swimming and the sandbar; Lipa Noi at high tide for toddlers.
  • Pig Island: short crossings and animals on the beach; children's life jackets are provided on all our boats and must be worn. Child prices apply from 3 to 11 years; infants up to 2 travel free.
  • Na Muang 1: a pool you can swim in, a short walk from the car park.
  • Temples: feeding the fish at Wat Plai Laem and climbing the steps at Big Buddha.
  • Hin Ta and Hin Yai: rock-scrambling on calm days with an adult close by.
  • Markets: the Friday walking street in Fisherman's Village for snacks and street performers.

New in 2026: The Xperience art park

The Xperience in Lipa Noi is a full afternoon and evening out: 12 zones of giant sculptures and light art, a lagoon beach club and pools, and projection mapping and fire shows after dark. Open Wednesday to Sunday, Day Pass 1,500 THB. Book tickets.

Rainy-day and November ideas

November is Samui's wettest month and Ang Thong is closed from 1 November to 15 December. Rain usually comes in heavy bursts rather than all day, so plan flexible days:

  • A Thai cooking class or a spa afternoon.
  • Muay Thai training: drop-in classes at gyms cost from about 500 THB.
  • Temples: Wat Khunaram, Big Buddha and Wat Plai Laem are short visits you can fit between showers.
  • Central Samui in Chaweng for shops, cinemas and food courts. Shopping malls.
  • Pig Island when the sea allows: the south-west is more sheltered from the north-east monsoon, and we reschedule or refund if it isn't safe.
  • Waterfalls at full flow, with care: paths are slippery and water rises fast after heavy rain.
  • Night markets: weather permitting.

How to fit it in: a sample 5-day plan

Most visitors try to do too much. Samui's roads are slow in Chaweng traffic and the sun is strong, so plan one big thing a day and leave the afternoons loose. This plan works from a base in Chaweng, Bophut or Lamai; swap days around to match the sea forecast, since boat days should go on the calmest day.

  1. Day 1 Arrive, swim at your nearest good beach, then dinner at a night market (Lamai daily, Chaweng Thursday–Saturday, Fisherman's Village on Friday).
  2. Day 2 Boat day: Ang Thong from 16 December to October, or Pig Island and Koh Tan any day of the year. Early night; pickups start in the morning.
  3. Day 3 North-east temples at 07:30 (Big Buddha, Wat Plai Laem), a swim at Choeng Mon, massage in the afternoon, Muay Thai at 21:00 (not Thursday).
  4. Day 4 South loop by scooter, car or 4x4 safari: Hin Ta and Hin Yai, the Red Temple, Guan Yu, Wat Khunaram, Na Muang 1 for a swim, and sunset at Laem Sor or Taling Ngam.
  5. Day 5 Rest day, or a second boat day: Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan in calm months, or a private longtail to Pig Island at your own pace.

With seven days, add a cooking class, the zipline or a day at Silver Beach and Coral Cove, and keep one day spare for weather. With three days, do days 2, 3 and 4. Our Koh Samui itinerary has detailed plans for 3, 5, 7 and 10 days, and Koh Samui on a budget shows what a day costs.

What to skip, and what has closed

  • Elephant riding, bathing and shows: we don't list or sell them. Skip the chained-elephant feeding at Overlap Stone.
  • Samui Aquarium and Tiger Zoo: status unclear in 2026, with no confirmed opening and serious welfare concerns raised about captive tigers in the past. Not listed.
  • Samui Butterfly Garden, Coco Splash waterpark and Samui Snake Farm: reported closed or unconfirmed in 2026. We'll list them again only after a phone check.
  • Namuang Safari Park: its old listings advertised elephant rides; the same address now trades as an elephant venue we are still checking. Not listed until we can confirm no riding.
  • Overlap Stone 2: reported closed since a forestry raid in July 2026.
  • Chaweng Stadium (Muay Thai) and Starz Cabaret (closed since 2021).
  • Khao Sok as a day trip: it is 4–6 hours each way from Samui. Do it as a 2-day trip or not at all.
  • Beach jet-ski rental without photos and a written price: see number 32.
  • Sky lanterns at Loy Krathong: they are banned on Samui.
