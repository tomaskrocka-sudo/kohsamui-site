Why go: a traditional junk for your own group

A teak junk with a wide wooden deck is one of the most photogenic ways to celebrate on the water. Chartering Chantara privately means your group sets the mood, the menu and the music, and the price stays fixed up to 10 guests before a per-guest charge for larger parties, up to 36.

What you'll do

The 3-hour sunset charters leave Bang Rak Bay at 16:00 and cruise the north coast past the Big Buddha towards Koh Som and the Koh Phangan channel, with appetisers or a Thai dinner served as the light fades. The full day follows the junk's classic route to Koh Ma and Thong Nai Pan on Koh Phangan, with lunch, snorkelling, beach time and a sunset sail home.

Who it suits

  • Birthdays, anniversaries, hen and stag groups and wedding guests.
  • Families with grandparents: the junk is stable and has shaded seating.
  • Groups of 15–36, where the cost per person is reasonable for a private boat.

Who should choose something else

  • Couples: the shared Chantara day cruise costs a fraction of the charter.
  • Groups who want speed and more islands: a private speedboat.

Good to know

  • Sunset times: on Samui the sun sets between about 18:00 and 18:40 through the year.
  • Drinks: bring your own with corkage, or ask us for a drinks package.
  • Booking ahead: weekends and the Christmas to New Year period fill early.

Before you book

Tell us the date, the number of adults and children, whether you want appetisers or dinner on a sunset charter, and any celebration plans. For groups above 10 we add the extra-guest charge and confirm the total before you pay.
