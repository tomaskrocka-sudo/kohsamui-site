How long is the boat from Koh Samui to Koh Tao? | Fast boats take about 1.5–2 hours, usually with a stop at Thong Sala on Koh Phangan, while slow ferries take about 2.5–3.25 hours.
Is there a direct boat from Samui to Koh Tao? | Yes, the 15:30 Lomlahkkhirin speedboat from Bangrak runs non-stop in about 1.5 hours, and Lomprayah's 08:00 catamaran from Pralarn goes straight to Koh Nang Yuan.
How much is the ferry from Samui to Koh Tao? | About 700 THB on Lomprayah, Lomlahkkhirin and Boonsiri, about 800 THB on the Songserm speedboat, 550 THB on Lomprayah's slow ferry and about 450 THB on the Songserm high-speed ferry from Nathon.
Can I visit Koh Tao in a day from Koh Samui? | Yes, either on our Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan speedboat day trip (2,200 THB adult plus the 250 THB island fee) or by taking an 08:00 ferry out and a 15:00–15:30 boat back.
What does Koh Nang Yuan cost to enter? | The entrance fee is 250 THB per adult and 120 THB per child, paid in cash on arrival and not included in tour prices; day visitors may stay 10:00–15:00, and plastic bottles, cans, fins and drones are not allowed.
What time is the last boat back from Koh Tao to Samui? | The last scheduled boats leave Mae Haad between about 15:00 and 16:30 depending on the operator and day, so check the board at the pier in the morning.
Is the sea rough between Samui and Koh Tao? | It can be from October to December, when north-east swell makes the 60–70 km crossing bumpy and some speedboats are cancelled; February to April is the calmest period.
Can I go diving at Koh Tao from Samui? | Yes, dive day trips from Samui visit Sail Rock and Koh Tao's sites from about 4,650 THB, or you can stay on Koh Tao, where Open Water courses are cheaper.
