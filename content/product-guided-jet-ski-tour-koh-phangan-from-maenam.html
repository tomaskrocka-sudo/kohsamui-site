Why go: your own jet ski, across to another island

Most jet ski rentals on Koh Samui are 15 to 30 minutes of loops off a busy beach. This guided tour from Maenam on the north coast is a proper outing: you ride a recent Sea-Doo behind a guide across the open water to Koh Phangan, then stop at three or four of its beaches to swim and snorkel. The short Discovery and sunset rides stay near Koh Som, a small island off the north coast, and are a good first taste.

Starting from Maenam keeps the crossing short. The north coast faces Koh Phangan directly, so you spend more time at the beaches and less time bouncing over swell.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: confident adults and couples sharing a jet ski who want an active morning.
  • Choose something else if you are pregnant, have back problems or are nervous in open water. A private speedboat reaches the same Koh Phangan beaches in comfort.
  • Families with young children: the Pig Island speedboat tour is gentler.

Good to know

  • Weather: from October to December the north-east monsoon makes the crossing rough, and many days are shortened or cancelled. February to September is usually calmer.
  • Damage terms: beach jet ski rentals on Samui are known for inflated damage claims. Ask us for the written terms before you ride, photograph the machine, and never leave a passport as a deposit.
  • Fitness: holding on through chop for three or four hours is hard on arms and back, for passengers too.
  • Snorkel stops: keep well clear of swimmers and moored longtails; the guide shows where to beach the jet skis.
