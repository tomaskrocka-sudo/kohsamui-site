Why go: a speedboat with some comfort for a small group

Most private speedboats are open boats with bench seats. Rubi Grace is a 38 ft sports cruiser built for leisure: a shaded cockpit, a sun pad on the bow, an enclosed toilet, a freshwater shower and a changing area. Twin 300 hp engines cruise at about 30 knots, so the long routes to Ang Thong and Koh Tao leave plenty of time at the stops.

There are four full-day routes. Koh Phangan runs up the coast to Bottle Beach, Thong Nai Pan and Than Sadet. The around-Samui day links Koh Tan, Pig Island and the Five Islands. Ang Thong and Koh Tao are the long days.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: couples and small groups of up to six who want privacy and a toilet on board.
  • Choose something else if your group is larger than eight: the per-head charge adds up, and a bigger boat such as Sao costs less.
  • Want to relax rather than race? A sailing catamaran is slower but much smoother.

Good to know

  • Ang Thong: closed from 1 November to 15 December. The Emerald Lake can be viewed but not swum in.
  • Viewpoint hikes: the climb on Koh Wua Talab is steep, with ropes; wear shoes with grip.
  • Lunch: eaten at a beach restaurant on the route. Bring cash.
  • Weather: the captain decides whether a route is safe. If it is cancelled for weather, you choose a new date or a full refund.

Choosing a route

Koh Phangan's east coast is the best snorkelling-and-beaches day when the sea is calm, with stops at Bottle Beach, Thong Nai Pan and Than Sadet. The around-Samui day is the safe choice for families and on breezy days. Koh Tao gives the clearest water but the longest time at speed.
