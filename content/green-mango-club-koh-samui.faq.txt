What time does the Green Mango Club open and close? | It is listed as open every night from about 21:00 to 04:00 in 2026, although some listings give 03:00 and the closing time depends on its licence and the season. Before midnight it is nearly empty; the dance floors fill from about 00:30 and peak between 01:00 and 03:00.
Is there an entry fee at the Green Mango Club? | Entry is usually free on normal nights; you pay for drinks instead. Special events and holiday parties can charge at the door, so check the live listing on this page for dated events.
How old do you have to be to get into the Green Mango Club? | You must be 20 or over, Thailand's legal drinking age, and the door can ask for ID. Carry a copy of your passport photo page and leave the original in your hotel safe.
What music does the Green Mango Club play? | Mainstream club music: hip-hop, R&B and chart hits in the area by the entrance, and EDM, house and singalong remixes in the main room. For techno and underground electronic nights, Sound Club Samui is about 60 m up the same lane.
Which night is best at the Green Mango Club? | Saturday is the busiest, followed by Friday, and high season (mid-December to February) is busier than the rest of the year. Monday and Tuesday are the quietest nights.
How much are drinks at the Green Mango Club? | Cocktails cost roughly 200–350 THB in 2026, about two to three times shop prices. Buckets are the cheapest way for a group to drink but are strong. Bring cash and check the menu board before you order.
Is there a dress code at the Green Mango Club? | It is casual, but wear a shirt and shoes. Bare chests and swimwear are likely to be turned away.
How do I get home from the Green Mango Club at 03:00? | Walk if you are staying in central Chaweng. Otherwise use Grab (about 200–400 THB to Lamai or Bophut late at night) or agree a fixed fare with a taxi on Chaweng Beach Road before you get in. We can book a pre-agreed pickup on WhatsApp. Never ride a scooter home after drinking.
Is the Green Mango Club safe? | It is a busy mainstream club, and the main risks are the usual late-night ones: spiked drinks, pickpockets in the crowded soi, padded bills and drink-riding. Keep your drink in sight, zip your pockets, pay per round and arrange your ride home in advance.
Is the Green Mango Club still open in 2026? | Yes. Guides updated in February, July and October 2026 list it as open nightly, and it has been part of Chaweng's nightlife for more than 20 years.
