We collect only what we need to run your booking (your name, email, WhatsApp number, hotel, travel date and guest numbers) and, with your consent, anonymous statistics about how the site is used. Card payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see your full card number. We don't sell your data. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time using the email address on our Contact page or on WhatsApp +66 63 704 0888. This policy applies from 1 November 2026 and is written to meet Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA) and, for visitors in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The data controller is the business that runs this website: an Australian-Thai tour team on Koh Samui and Koh Phangan since 2006, licensed by the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT licence 43/01467), with its Koh Samui office on Chaweng Beach Road, Bo Phut 84320, Thailand. For any privacy question, use the email address on our Contact page or WhatsApp +66 63 704 0888.

5. International transfers

We are based in Thailand, so if you book from the EU or elsewhere, your data is transferred to Thailand. Some providers, such as Stripe, Google and Meta, process data in other countries, including the United States. Where data leaves Thailand or the EU, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions, and on the fact that the transfer is needed to perform your booking.

7. Cookies

When you first visit, a banner asks for your cookie choice. Analytics cookies stay off until you accept them, and choosing "Reject all" is honoured. We run Google Consent Mode, which keeps Google tags in a privacy-preserving state until you consent. We use no advertising cookies by default.

TypeExamplesNeeds consent?
NecessaryCart and checkout, security, your cookie choiceNo
PreferencesLight or dark theme (stored in your browser), display currency, languageNo; stored only on your device
AnalyticsGoogle Analytics: pages viewed, time on site, device typeYes

You can change your choice at any time with the cookie settings button at the bottom of every page, or by clearing cookies in your browser. Blocking necessary cookies may stop the cart and checkout from working. The full, current list of cookies is shown in the cookie settings panel.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA and, if you are in the EU, the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, where we don't need to keep it by law;
  • object to or restrict our use of your data, including for direct marketing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier use.

To use any of these rights, contact us at the email address on our Contact page or on WhatsApp +66 63 704 0888. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we reply within 30 days. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) or, if you live in the EU, to the data protection authority in your country.

9. Children

Bookings must be made by an adult aged 18 or over. We collect children's ages only to price the booking and plan life jackets and seating. We don't knowingly collect data directly from children.

10. Security

  • The whole site runs over HTTPS.
  • Card payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor.
  • Booking data is accessible only to logged-in staff with booking roles, protected by strong passwords.
  • The site is backed up daily.

If we learn of a data breach that puts your rights at risk, we tell the authorities and you as the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top shows the current version. For booking terms, see our terms and conditions.
