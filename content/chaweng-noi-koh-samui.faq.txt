Is Chaweng Noi better than Chaweng? | It is quieter, with no bar strip and few jet skis, and has a little snorkelling off the rocks. Chaweng has finer sand, a longer swimming beach and hundreds of restaurants on foot. Choose Chaweng Noi for calm days and Chaweng for convenience; they are only 5–10 minutes apart.
Can you swim at Chaweng Noi? | Yes, from January to September the water is clear and the centre of the bay slopes gently. From October to December the north-east monsoon brings surf and red flags, and you should stay out of the water whenever a red flag is flying.
How do I get onto Chaweng Noi beach if I'm not staying there? | Thai beaches are public up to the high-water line. Use the lanes beside the beach restaurants off the ring road, or go through a resort restaurant and buy lunch or a drink. Access points change with building work, so ask locally on the day.
Is there snorkelling at Chaweng Noi? | A little, around the rocks at both ends of the bay: small fish, urchins and some coral, best on a flat morning. Bring your own mask and water shoes. Coral Cove and Silver Beach, a few minutes south, are better, and the best reefs are offshore at Koh Tan.
What are sea lice? | They are tiny jellyfish and anemone larvae that get trapped under swimwear and cause an itchy red rash for a day or two. Shower and take off wet swimwear straight after swimming, wash it before reuse, and use an antihistamine or hydrocortisone cream from a pharmacy.
Is Chaweng Noi good for families? | It works for families with older children who swim well and want a quiet beach. With toddlers, Choeng Mon is easier: calmer water, a gentler slope, simpler beach access and more restaurants on the sand. The hillside villas above Chaweng Noi are on very steep roads.
