Where is the immigration office on Koh Samui? | Koh Samui Immigration is at 333 Maenam Road, Soi 1, Moo 1, Maenam 84330, on the north coast, about 15 km from Chaweng. Phone +66 77 423 440. Search "Koh Samui Immigration Office Maenam" on Google Maps.
What are Koh Samui immigration's opening hours? | It usually opens Monday to Friday, 08:30–16:30, and is closed on Saturdays, Sundays and Thai public holidays. Hours for the Samui unit aren't officially published, so call +66 77 423 440 before a long trip.
Can I do my 90-day report online? | Usually yes, after your first report, which must be made in person in Maenam. Later reports can be filed at tm47.immigration.go.th within 15 days before and 7 days after the due date. The TM.30 address on file must match.
What is TM30 and who files it? | TM.30 is the notification that a foreigner is staying at an address. The owner, landlord or manager must file it within 24 hours of arrival. Hotels do it automatically. Tenants need the receipt for extensions and 90-day reports.
How much is a re-entry permit? | A single re-entry permit costs 1,000 THB and a multiple re-entry permit costs 3,800 THB, using form TM.8. You need one to keep an extension of stay valid when you leave Thailand.
Is Koh Samui immigration still in Nathon? | No. The office moved from Nathon to Soi 1, Maenam, in 2016. Old maps and blogs that show Nathon are out of date.
Do I need an appointment? | Walk-ins have long been accepted, but arrive before 08:30 because queue numbers can run out. Some agents report an online appointment calendar for the Samui unit, so ask when you call.
Is there a dress code at immigration? | Yes, in practice. Wear long trousers or a knee-length skirt, cover your shoulders and avoid beachwear. Officers can ask people in shorts or vests to come back properly dressed.
