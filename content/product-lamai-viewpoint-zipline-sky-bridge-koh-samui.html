Why go: a short, sharp hit of adrenaline above Lamai

Lamai Viewpoint sits on the hill behind Lamai beach, with views across the bay and the jungle towards the south coast. The zip lines run about 80 m above the ground, and the longest is around 440 m. That is enough for real speed, and the whole thing fits into a morning. You can walk up the hill or take the small cable car. After a safety briefing you clip in and ride. Packages differ in the number of rides, the Sky Adventure course on the sky bridge and a drone video of your ride.

The drone video is the reason the packages are priced as they are. If you want to share it, choose E, D, B or A. If you only want the ride, H is the cheapest way up, and C adds a third ride and the cable car without filming.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: teenagers and adults after a quick thrill, groups staying in Lamai or Chaweng, and anyone who wants a big view without a long hike.
  • Choose something else if: you are over 100 kg, pregnant or have heart problems. Those are firm limits. For views without the drop, the Lad Koh viewpoint and Hin Ta Hin Yai rocks are nearby and free or almost free.
  • Walk-up packages: the climb is short but steep and hot. If you're not used to the heat, pay for the cable car.

Good to know

  • Safety: check that the guide clips both lanyards before every ride. Keep hands off the cable and follow braking signals at the landing.
  • Weather: rides pause in lightning and strong gusts. In the October–December rains, mornings are usually drier than afternoons.
  • What to wear: closed shoes and shorts or trousers. Tie back long hair, and leave loose sunglasses and hats in a locker or with a friend.
  • Getting there: the access road is steep. Only ride up by scooter if you are confident on hills.
