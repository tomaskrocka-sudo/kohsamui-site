Lamai or Chaweng: which is better for a first visit? | Chaweng is better if you want the most restaurants, clubs and a 10–15 minute airport run; Lamai is better if you want the same quality of sand, mid-range rooms about 20–30% cheaper (1,500–3,500 THB) and quieter nights, 25–30 minutes from the airport.
Can you swim at Lamai Beach? | Yes, from January to September the central section is the best place to swim, but it drops steeply and you are out of your depth a few metres out; the north end is shallow and stony, and red flags are common from October to December.
When is the Lamai night market? | The Lamai Night Market runs every evening from about 17:00 on the beach road just before the river bridge, with the most stalls and live music between 18:00 and 20:00; bring cash.
Is there still a Lamai Sunday walking street? | Sources disagree in 2026: some listings still show a Sunday market from about 17:00 to 22:00 in central Lamai, while others say it ended after the pandemic, so treat it as unconfirmed and use the daily night market instead.
How far is Lamai from Samui Airport? | About 15 km by road, 25–30 minutes in normal traffic; an airport taxi costs approx. 400–700 THB and Grab about 200–300 THB from a meeting point outside the terminal.
What is there to do in Lamai at night? | Eat at the night market, then choose between live-band pubs such as the Shamrock Irish Pub, reggae and beach bars on the beach road, or a 15–20 minute Grab to Chaweng for clubs that stay open later.
Is Lamai good for families with children? | Yes for older children and good swimmers, and for budget-conscious families; for toddlers the steep shelf in central Lamai is a concern, so choose a resort with a pool or spend beach time at calmer Choeng Mon or Lipa Noi.
Where are Hin Ta and Hin Yai? | At the south tip of Lamai beach, about 2 km from the centre (GPS 9.4522, 100.0395); entry is free, parking is about 30 THB, and the lane is open Sunday–Friday 08:30–18:30 and Saturday 08:00–19:00.
Is Lamai safe to swim in the rainy season? | Often not: from October to December the north-east monsoon drives waves and rip currents straight onto Lamai, red flags go up on many days, and November is the wettest month with about 445 mm of rain.
Do your tours pick up from Lamai? | Yes, hotel pickup is included from Lamai on our shared tours, and Lamai is only about 20–25 minutes from Thong Krut pier, where our Pig Island boats leave.
Is Beach Republic in Lamai still open? | The pool bar and most of the resort were reported closed in 2025–2026 with only a beach café remaining, so don't plan a brunch or party there until it reopens.
Is there snorkelling at Lamai? | Not much: Lamai's seabed is sand and stones with little to see, so drive 5–10 minutes north to Silver Beach or Coral Cove, or take a boat to Koh Tan, the best shallow reef near Samui.
