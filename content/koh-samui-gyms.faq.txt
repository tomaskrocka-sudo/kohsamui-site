Where can I train Muay Thai on Koh Samui? | At camps in Chaweng, Bophut and Lamai, including Superpro Samui in the Bophut–Chaweng area (drop-in 500 THB) and Lamai Muay Thai Camp (Monday to Saturday, 07:00–19:00). Most camps run two group sessions a day and welcome beginners.
How much is a gym day pass on Samui? | About 400 THB at the fight-camp gyms in 2026; Superpro's indoor gym is 400 THB a day, 1,000 THB a week or 2,000 THB a month. Resort gyms are usually for in-house guests only, and wellness-resort day passes start around 1,800 THB++.
Are there CrossFit boxes on Koh Samui? | We could not confirm a CrossFit-affiliated box operating on Samui in late 2026. The fight camps run functional-fitness and HYROX-style sessions that are the closest equivalent; check the official affiliate map before you travel if CrossFit itself matters.
When is the Samui Half Marathon? | The 2026 Bangkok Airways Samui Half Marathon was on Sunday 21 June, with a Kids Run on 20 June. The 2027 date had not been announced in November 2026; it is usually held in June.
Is it too hot to run on Samui? | Not if you pick your time: run at dawn from about 06:00 or after 17:30, carry water and add electrolytes. Middays are 29–33 °C and humid all year, and the ring road is dangerous for runners, so use beaches at low tide or quiet lanes.
Can I get a visa to train Muay Thai? | Yes. The Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) has a "soft power" track that covers Muay Thai courses, with stays of up to 180 days per entry, a 10,000 THB fee and proof of 500,000 THB in funds. From 31 August 2026 you also need a police clearance and must apply where you live.
How much does a month of Muay Thai training cost on Samui? | About 7,500–10,000 THB a month for unlimited group classes in 2026, or 2,500–3,000 THB a week. All-in packages with a room, meals and private sessions cost about 25,000 THB a week or 85,000 THB for four weeks.
~Can children train Muay Thai or BJJ on Koh Samui? | Some camps run kids' classes; Superpro, for example, offers a monthly kids' BJJ membership at 2,500 THB. Ask the camp about age groups and class times before you commit.
