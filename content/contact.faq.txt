How quickly do you reply? | We answer WhatsApp every day from 08:00 to 20:00 Thailand time (UTC+7), and that is the fastest way to reach us. Messages sent after 20:00 are answered from 08:00 the next morning. Email is answered during the same hours. If your trip is tomorrow or your pickup is late, use WhatsApp on +66 63 704 0888 rather than email.
Can I book by WhatsApp? | Yes. Send your trip, date, number of adults, children and infants, and your hotel on WhatsApp +66 63 704 0888, and we reply with availability and a secure payment link charged in THB. Shared tours are usually quicker to book online with Book now, but same-day requests, groups of 10 or more and private boats always go through WhatsApp.
Can I visit your office? | Yes, by arrangement. Our Koh Samui office is on Chaweng Beach Road in Bo Phut, but it is a working booking desk rather than a walk-in shop, so message us on WhatsApp first and we will tell you when someone is there. You never need to visit to book, pay or collect tickets: everything is confirmed by email or WhatsApp.
How do I change or cancel a booking? | Message us on WhatsApp or email with your booking reference and what you want to change. Cancellations follow our terms: day trips are fully refundable 48 hours or more before departure and 50% refundable 24 to 48 hours before; Ang Thong tours are fully refundable 48 hours or more before and not after. Date changes depend on space on the new date.
What if I can't find my pickup driver? | Wait in your hotel lobby or at the main entrance, then message us on WhatsApp +66 63 704 0888 if the driver has not arrived 10 minutes after your pickup time. Do not take a taxi to the pier on your own, because pickups run in a set order and we can call the driver directly. A missed pickup that was our fault is never treated as a no-show.
Can I phone you instead of sending a message? | Yes. +66 63 704 0888 is our phone and WhatsApp number, staffed from 08:00 to 20:00 Thailand time every day. We still recommend a WhatsApp message for bookings, because your date, guest numbers, hotel and pickup time are then in writing for both of us, and we can send your payment link and confirmation in the same chat.
