How much is car rental on Koh Samui? | A compact car costs approx. 800–1,200 THB a day and an SUV 1,500–2,500 THB, with lower daily rates for weekly and monthly rentals and higher prices in December–January.
Do I need an international driving permit to rent a car? | Yes, carry your home licence plus an International Driving Permit; some agencies rent without one, but police and insurers can treat you as unlicensed.
Can I rent a car at Samui airport? | Yes, international brands including Hertz and Budget have desks at or near the airport, and many local agencies deliver cars to the airport or your hotel.
Is it easy to drive on Koh Samui? | The 51 km ring road is straightforward and traffic drives on the left, but scooters filter on both sides, hill roads are steep and Chaweng is congested in the evening.
Do I need a 4x4 on Koh Samui? | Not for the ring road or most hotels; a 4x4 helps only on unpaved hill tracks such as the road to the Secret Buddha Garden, and a small SUV is enough for most steep villa roads.
Can I take a rental car to the mainland? | Only if your contract allows it in writing; many Samui rental agreements forbid ferry crossings or void insurance on the Donsak car ferries.
What insurance should I take? | Choose Class 1 (first-class) insurance with the lowest excess you can get, and check that it covers single-vehicle accidents, tyres and glass.
