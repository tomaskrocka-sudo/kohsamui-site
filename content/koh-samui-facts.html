Koh Samui is Thailand's second-largest island (228.7 km²), in the Gulf of Thailand about 35 km off the Surat Thani coast, with a 51 km ring road, an airport owned and run by Bangkok Airways, and ferries to the mainland, Koh Phangan and Koh Tao. About 70,000 people are registered as residents, the highest point is Khao Pom (635 m), and the sea stays at 28–30 °C all year.

Every figure below was checked against official or primary sources and current operator data. Prices are 2026 prices in Thai baht (THB) and are approximate where they vary by season or seller. For context and advice, see the full Koh Samui travel guide.
