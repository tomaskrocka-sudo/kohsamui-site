Why go: a full private day around Koh Rap

Koh Rap is the quiet island of Samui's southern group. It lies south-west of Koh Tan, a short boat ride from Thong Krut, and has a long white beach with shade, calm water, a small bar selling drinks and basic toilets. Most visitors only see it for an hour on a three-island trip. This private boat gives you the whole day: a long swim, snorkelling off the beach on calm days, a slow lunch and an afternoon on the sand while the shared boats crowd Pig Island.

The boat and captain are yours for the day for 9,950 THB per boat. A typical day starts around 09:00 from Thong Krut, but the time is yours to choose. You can stop at Koh Madsum (Pig Island) on the way out, when the pigs are active and before the beach gets busy, and at Koh Tan for its shallow reef or a beach-restaurant lunch. Then spend the rest of the day at Koh Rap. If you want to stay later for the evening light, tell us when you book and we plan the timing with the captain.

How is this different from the three-islands private boat? That trip (8,450 THB, 5–6 hours) gives each island equal time in a half-to-most-of-a-day. The full day costs 1,500 THB more, runs longer and is built around Koh Rap, with Pig Island and Koh Tan as optional extras. Choose it if your idea of a boat day is a long stretch on one quiet beach rather than hopping between stops.

A sample day

  • 09:00 Board at Thong Krut pier.
  • Morning Optional stop at Koh Madsum for the pigs, or Koh Tan for a snorkel.
  • Late morning to afternoon Koh Rap: swimming, snorkelling on calm days, shade and a long lunch break (bring a picnic or eat on Koh Tan on the way).
  • Late afternoon Return to Thong Krut, or stay out for the evening light if agreed.

Who this tour is for

  • Couples and families who want a beach day away from other tour groups.
  • Groups who have already done Pig Island and want a slower, emptier day.
  • Readers, swimmers and snorkellers who would rather stay put than hop between islands.
  • Photographers who want the islands in morning and late-afternoon light.

Who should choose another tour

  • Want all three islands with equal time in a shorter day: the three-islands private boat.
  • Want a fast half day with more stops: the Pig Island private speedboat.
  • Only want the pigs: the shared speedboat tour or the private longtail.
  • Want limestone cliffs, viewpoints and lagoons: the Ang Thong private speedboat (from 16 December).
  • Need restaurants and facilities all day: Koh Rap's bar is basic; a beach day at Chaweng or Lamai suits you better.

What to bring

  • Food and plenty of water for the boat. Koh Rap's bar is small and outside drinks can't be taken onto the island.
  • Cash in small notes for island fees (about 50 THB per person on Koh Madsum and on Koh Rap) and drinks.
  • A beach umbrella or sun shelter if you have one, plus reef-safe sunscreen, hats and sunglasses.
  • Swimwear, towels, a change of clothes and water shoes.
  • Snorkel fins if you want them, and a dry bag for phones.
  • A book: this is a slow day.

Good to know

  • Sea by month: February to September is best for calm water and clear snorkelling, and February to April is calmest. October to December is the rainy season: light rain doesn't stop the boat, but strong wind can, and the captain may stay closer to Thong Krut. If we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.
  • Sun: a full day on a beach and a boat means hours of exposure. Shade on Koh Rap is under the trees; reapply sunscreen after every swim.
  • Children: adult and child life jackets are on board and children wear them at all times on the boat. Bring shade and more water than you think you need.
  • Mobility: boarding is from Thong Krut pier, but at the islands you step into shallow water from the boat.
  • Snorkelling: there is reef off the beach at Koh Rap on calm days; Koh Tan has the most reliable easy snorkelling. Don't stand on coral.
  • Rubbish: take every bottle and wrapper back to the boat and dispose of it on Samui.
  • Drones: registered drones only (CAAT and NBTC); ask before flying near other visitors or the pigs.

Pickup and meeting point

The boat leaves from Thong Krut pier on the south-west coast, and we send the exact location and meeting time on WhatsApp. Hotel transfers can be arranged on request from the main areas of Koh Samui (Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut, Maenam, Choeng Mon, Bang Rak and Plai Laem). Thong Krut is about 45–60 minutes by road from Chaweng and 30–40 minutes from Lamai. Tell us your group size when you book and we confirm the right boat.
