Why go: see Samui at your own pace

A private vehicle is the easiest way to see the island's main sights without renting a scooter. You set the order, stay longer where you like and skip what doesn't interest you. The 4-hour minibus covers the north and east highlights; the 8-hour 4x4 adds the south-west coast and the steep hill road to the Secret Buddha Garden that standard cars and most scooters can't manage.

Because you pay per vehicle, it costs about the same as a shared tour for a group of five or more.

Minibus or 4x4

The minibus works for the sights on the ring road: Big Buddha, Wat Plai Laem, Hin Lad waterfall, the Guan Yu shrine at Hua Thanon, Wat Khunaram and Hin Ta and Hin Yai. The 4x4 adds the climb to the Secret Buddha Garden and the jungle road to Na Muang 2, which a normal car can't handle after rain. If you have children, older guests or a lot of photography planned, the full day in the 4x4 avoids rushing; for a quick overview before a beach afternoon, the 4-hour minibus is enough.

Who it suits

  • Families and groups of 4–10 who want their own space and timing.
  • Guests with mobility needs who prefer short walks and an air-conditioned car between stops.
  • Photographers who want early light at the temples or late light at the rocks.

Who should choose something else

  • Solo travellers or couples on a budget: the shared island sightseeing tour costs less.
  • Anyone wanting an off-road adventure rather than sightseeing: try a jungle safari.

Good to know

  • Temples: cover shoulders and knees and remove shoes where signs ask. Big Buddha is free.
  • Plan ahead: tell us your must-see stops when you book so the route works.
  • Booking: online booking for tomorrow closes at midnight Thai time.
