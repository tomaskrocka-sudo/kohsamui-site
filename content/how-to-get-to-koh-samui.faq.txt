What is the fastest way to get to Koh Samui? | A direct Bangkok Airways flight from Bangkok Suvarnabhumi to Samui airport (USM) takes about 1h05–1h15, with roughly 20–25 flights a day, and Chaweng is only 10–15 minutes from the airport.
What is the cheapest way to get to Koh Samui? | A government coach plus Seatran ferry from Bangkok costs about 620–960 THB, and a low-cost flight to Surat Thani plus a 330–520 THB coach-and-ferry combo is the cheapest way to arrive the same day.
Does Koh Samui have an airport? | Yes. Samui International Airport (USM) is in the north-east of the island near Bangrak, privately owned by Bangkok Airways since 1989, with flights from Bangkok, Phuket, Krabi, Chiang Mai, U-Tapao, Singapore and Hong Kong.
Why are flights to Koh Samui more expensive than to Surat Thani? | Bangkok Airways owns Samui airport and flies every route except Scoot's Singapore service, so there is little competition, while three low-cost airlines compete on flights to Surat Thani.
Which pier do ferries from the mainland arrive at? | Seatran car ferries and most Lomprayah and Boonsiri boats from Donsak arrive at Nathon on the west coast, while Raja Ferry car ferries arrive at Lipa Noi, a few kilometres further south.
Do I need to book ferry tickets in advance? | Usually not; you can buy at the pier or office on the day, but book ahead on Full Moon Party dates and between 20 December and 5 January.
Are ferries cancelled in bad weather? | Speedboats and catamarans to Koh Phangan and Koh Tao can be cancelled in strong north-east wind from October to December, while the big Donsak car ferries usually keep running except in tropical storms.
Can I take a car to Koh Samui? | Yes, Raja Ferry and Seatran carry cars from Donsak to Lipa Noi and Nathon in about 1.5 hours, with a car on Seatran costing roughly 550–670 THB in 2026, but check your rental contract allows ferry crossings.
How do I get from Koh Phangan to Koh Samui? | About 40 boats a day cross in 20–50 minutes for 200–500 THB, mostly from Thong Sala to Bangrak, Maenam or Nathon, plus the Haad Rin Queen from Haad Rin to Big Buddha pier.
Can I land at Surat Thani and reach Samui the same day? | Yes, if you land before about 15:30, because the last coach-and-ferry combos leave Surat Thani airport around 16:00 and reach Samui about 3–4 hours later.
