Is Lipa Noi good for swimming? | Yes at high tide, when the water is shallow, calm and sandy-bottomed; at low tide the sea retreats over wide flats and there is nowhere deep enough to swim.
Is Lipa Noi good for small children? | It is one of the best beaches on Samui for babies and toddlers: at high tide the water is shallow, warm and wave-free on most days, but there are few restaurants or shops nearby.
Where does the Raja ferry arrive on Koh Samui? | The Raja car ferry from Donsak lands at its own port at Lipa Noi on the west coast; the crossing takes about 1.5 hours and a foot passenger ticket costs 220 THB.
Is Lipa Noi too remote? | For some people: it is 40–50 minutes from the airport and 35–45 minutes from Chaweng, Grab is unreliable on the west coast, and you need a car or scooter for most meals.
Can I watch the sunset at Lipa Noi? | Yes, the beach faces west and the sun sets over the sea between about 17:55 in November and 18:45 in July; low tide gives mirror-like reflections on the wet sand.
Do your tours pick up from Lipa Noi? | Yes, hotel pickup is available from Lipa Noi for an extra 400 THB per person return.
Is Lipa Noi calm in the rainy season? | Usually yes: the west coast is sheltered from the October–December north-east monsoon waves, so it is often flat when Chaweng and Lamai have red flags, though it rains just as much.
Is Nikki Beach in Lipa Noi still open? | Nikki Beach Koh Samui was operating in 2026, with a Saturday party, a Sunday brunch and occasional big events such as a White Party in March.
How far is Lipa Noi from Nathon? | About 6 km by road, 10–15 minutes; Nathon has the ferries, the night food market (16:30–22:00) and the government hospital.
Can I take a rental scooter from Samui on the Raja ferry? | Physically yes, but most Samui rental contracts don't cover the bike off the island, so check your agreement and insurance before you travel.
