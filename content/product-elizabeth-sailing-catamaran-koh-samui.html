Why go: a comfortable, classic sailing catamaran

Elizabeth is an 11 m sailing catamaran built in 2009. The two air-conditioned cabins set it apart from other boats at this price. They are a cool place to escape the midday sun, change or put a child down for a nap. A crew of two sails the boat, and the BBQ on board means lunch can be cooked at anchor.

The half days stay close to Samui. In the morning you sail along the coast, anchor in a sheltered bay to snorkel, paddle board and swim, and come back by lunchtime. The afternoon sail ends with the sunset. The full day crosses to Koh Phangan for longer stops in its quieter bays.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: couples, families with young children who need shade and a cabin, and small groups of friends who want to sail rather than motor.
  • Choose something else if: you're more than 15 people. The Madam C takes 30. For the lowest price on a simpler boat, see the Aloha.

Good to know

  • Sailing vs motoring: sails go up when there is wind. Calm days mean motoring, which is smoother.
  • Season: February to September is calmest. From October to December, half days near the coast are more reliable than the Koh Phangan crossing.
  • Lunch: order the BBQ when you book. That gives the crew time to prepare it.
  • Bigger groups: the boat takes up to 15 people. Above 8, an extra-guest fee applies per person, so message us before you book.
  • Getting there: book the return van with the charter. One van carries the whole group, up to 10 people.
