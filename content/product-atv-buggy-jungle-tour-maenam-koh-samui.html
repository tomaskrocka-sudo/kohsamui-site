Why go: jungle tracks and a waterfall in two hours

The hills behind Maenam are some of the greenest on Koh Samui, with plantation tracks that climb quickly above the north coast. A guided ATV ride gets you there without riding a scooter on the main road, and the 2-hour route is short enough to fit into a morning.

The 3-hour route is the better choice if you want views: it adds a hillside temple and several viewpoints over the Gulf. Buggies suit couples who'd rather sit side by side than ride pillion.

ATV, shared ATV or buggy

Your own ATV gives full control and is the most fun if you are confident. Sharing an ATV halves the cost but the passenger feels every bump. A buggy has two seats side by side, a steering wheel and a roll cage, so it suits couples and anyone who would rather drive a small car than a quad. All three follow the same guide on the same tracks. If you want something calmer afterwards, the beaches at Maenam are 10 minutes from the base.

Who it suits

  • First-time quad riders with a guide in front.
  • Couples and friends who want a morning adventure.
  • Guests staying in Maenam, Bophut or Chaweng.

Who should choose something else

  • Pregnant guests or anyone with back problems: the tracks are rough.
  • Experienced riders who want proper off-road: try the enduro dirt bike tour.
  • Families with young children: the jungle zipline suits ages 7+.

Good to know

  • Safety: keep your distance from the vehicle in front and ride at the guide's pace.
  • Insurance: check that your travel insurance covers ATV riding; damage you cause is your cost.
  • Start times: the operator runs several daily departures; we confirm your exact pickup after booking.
  • Booking: online booking for tomorrow closes at midnight Thai time.
