The sun sets over the sea on Koh Samui's west and south-west coasts, so the best sunset boats watch it from that side, behind the Five Islands off Taling Ngam. Our shared option is the Pig Island Sunset Tour from Thong Krut with snorkelling and dinner (1,550 THB adult, 1,350 THB child, 5–6 hours); for couples and groups we charter private boats, from a 2.5-hour sunset sail on a sailing catamaran (from 20,000 THB per boat for up to 10) to a 3-hour sunset dinner on the Red Baron junk for up to 70. Sunset is between about 17:55 (November) and 18:45 (July).

Pig Island Sunset Tour (shared)

The afternoon version of our most popular trip. A minibus collects you from your hotel in the early afternoon (pickup from most areas of Koh Samui) and drives you to Thong Krut pier in the south-west. A shared speedboat takes you to Koh Madsum to meet the pigs in the softer afternoon light, then to Koh Tan to snorkel on the reef, and dinner is served as the sun drops behind the Five Islands. You are back at your hotel after dark, 5–6 hours after pickup. Snorkel gear, life jackets and dinner are included; the Koh Madsum island fee (about 50 THB) is paid in cash where it is not included. It runs every day of the year, weather permitting, and can be booked online until midnight Thai time the day before. See the Pig Island Sunset Tour.

Private sailing catamaran: Victoria

Victoria's sunset programme runs 2.5 hours from 16:00 to 18:30, from 20,000 THB for up to 10 guests (maximum 13). On board are soft drinks and ice, seasonal fruit, a paddle board, kayak, snorkel gear and a Bluetooth speaker, and you may bring your own food and drinks free of charge; champagne glasses are provided. It is the best-value private sunset for a couple, a family or a small group of friends.

Large catamaran: Kia Ora

Kia Ora's sunset programme also runs from 16:00 to 18:30, to Koh Som and Tongson Bay off the north-east coast, from 36,000 THB plus 7% VAT for up to 15 guests (maximum 45). Water, soft drinks, towels and snorkel masks are included; food and hotel transfers are extra. A good choice for a big birthday with room to move.

Junk sunset dinner: Red Baron

The traditional 98 ft wooden junk runs a 3-hour sunset dinner cruise from 41,000 THB for up to 20 guests, with up to 70 on board for a supplement per extra guest. Meals and soft drinks are included. This is the boat for a wedding party, a company dinner or a family reunion.

Private speedboat or motor yacht

Any of our nine speedboats or two motor yachts can be booked for a sunset run along the west coast; send us your date and group size for a price. A speedboat is quicker but bumpier and has no dining space; the motor yacht Casablanca (41 ft, two cabins, two bathrooms) suits a couple or small group who want privacy and comfort.

Where the sun sets

Koh Samui's east coast (Chaweng, Lamai, Choeng Mon) faces the sunrise, not the sunset. For the sun going down over the sea, go west or south-west: Taling Ngam has the island's best sunset behind the Five Islands, Lipa Noi and Nathon pier face straight out to sea, and Thong Krut looks across to Koh Tan and Koh Madsum. On the north coast, Bophut, Bang Rak and Maenam look out towards Koh Phangan and get pink evening skies over the water, with the sun setting to the west along the coast. From November to January the sun sets further south, which favours the south-west coast; from May to August it sets further north, which favours Bang Por and Laem Yai. Proposals and celebrations

A private sunset is the classic proposal on Samui: the whole boat is yours, and the crew can time a stop for the moment the sun touches the horizon. Tell us when you enquire if it is a proposal, an anniversary or a birthday, and we will tell you what the boat can arrange: flowers, a cake, decorations, a photographer or catering are quoted as extras and need a few days' notice. For a larger celebration, the Red Baron sunset dinner takes up to 70. See also our yacht charter page.

Season

February to April gives the clearest sunsets and calmest sea. May to September is good, with the latest sunsets of the year (up to about 18:45 in July). October to December is the north-east monsoon: more cloud and rain, with November the wettest month (about 445 mm on 16 days). Boats run when it is safe, and the south-west route from Thong Krut is the most sheltered at that time of year. A cloudy evening can still bring a good sky. If we cancel because the sea is unsafe, you choose a full refund or a new date. Check the evening forecast on Windy and our Koh Samui weather by month.
