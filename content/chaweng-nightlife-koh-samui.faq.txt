What time does Green Mango open and close? | The Green Mango Club on Soi Green Mango in Chaweng opens nightly at about 21:00 and closes at about 04:00, depending on its licence. It is nearly empty before 23:00 and fills from about 00:30, with Saturday the busiest night. Entry is usually free; you must be 20 or over, so carry ID.
Is ARKbar free to enter? | Yes. Walking in to ARKbar on Chaweng Beach is free, and the fire show and DJs on the sand from about 20:00 to 24:00 cost nothing. You pay for drinks (about 150–300 THB), and sunbeds carry a minimum spend of about 300 THB. After about 21:00 the beach party is adults only.
Which night is best in Chaweng? | Saturday is the peak night: the clubs are fullest, Elephant Beach Club runs DJs until about 03:00, CLUB SEEN has its weekend party and Phetchbuncha Stadium has a Muay Thai card. Friday is a close second, with Chaweng Walking Street, a fight at Samui International and an afternoon pool party listed at ARKbar.
Where can I watch Muay Thai in Chaweng? | At two stadiums about 400 m apart: Phetchbuncha Stadium on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday, and Samui International Muay Thai Stadium on Tuesday, Friday and Sunday, both from about 21:00 to midnight. There are no fights on Thursday. Tickets cost 1,500–3,000 THB; buy at the box office or from us, not from touts.
Is there a cabaret show in Chaweng? | Yes. Paris Follies Cabaret, under the Eiffel Tower sign on Chaweng Beach Road, is open daily from about 19:00 to midnight with shows of about an hour, and you enter by buying a drink. Starz Cabaret closed in November 2021, although some old guides still list it.
Where can I eat late in Chaweng? | Si Khao Night Market runs daily until 23:00 and Food Village, across from Chaweng Lake, until about midnight. From Thursday to Saturday Chaweng Walking Street by the lake has stalls until 22:00–23:00. After midnight it is 7-Eleven toasties and noodle carts on the beach road.
How do I get back to my hotel after midnight in Chaweng? | Walk if you can, or use Grab, which works well in Chaweng but surges after 02:00 and in rain. Taxis on the beach road don't use meters, so agree the fare first and expect 20–30 % more at night. Songthaews stop by about 22:00. Never ride a scooter after drinking.
Is Chaweng nightlife safe? | Generally yes on the busy main streets, with care. Watch your drink being made, check bar bills, buy fight and boat tickets from the venue or a licensed seller, don't swim at night and never ride after drinking. Save the Tourist Police number, 1155.
How much does a night out in Chaweng cost? | Expect about 1,500–2,000 THB per person for a big night: a couple of cocktails at a beach bar (200–350 THB each), drinks in the clubs (120–350 THB), street food and a ride home. A Muay Thai ticket adds 1,500–3,000 THB. Prices rise 10–20 % from mid-December to February.
