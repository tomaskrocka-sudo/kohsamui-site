Is there an ethical elephant sanctuary on Koh Samui? | Yes, with caveats. One venue, with sites in the Bophut hills and above Chaweng Noi, is listed as high-welfare by World Animal Protection, and several others offer no-riding, no-bathing visits. No Samui venue meets the strictest standard, because all include some hand-feeding.
Can you bathe with elephants on Koh Samui? | Some venues still offer feed-and-shower or bathing programmes, but welfare organisations advise against them and we don't sell them. The better sanctuaries let you watch the elephants bathe and mud-wallow on their own from a platform or the bank.
How much does an elephant sanctuary visit cost on Koh Samui? | A 3-hour half day with transfer and a meal costs about 3,000 THB per adult and 1,500 THB per child in 2026. Short 1–1.5 hour visits cost 1,000–2,000 THB, and our visits range from 1,000 to 3,500 THB per adult.
Is an elephant sanctuary visit suitable for young children? | Yes. Most venues let children under 4 in free and charge child prices from age 4 or 5 to 11. The 1.5-hour visit and the observation-only skywalk visit are the easiest with under-6s; keep small children close and follow the keepers.
Why don't you sell elephant riding? | Elephants only accept riders after being trained to submit, a process that traditionally involves separating calves from their mothers and breaking their will, and riding seats strain their backs. We sell only no-riding, no-bathing visits.
What should I wear to an elephant sanctuary on Samui? | Clothes you don't mind getting muddy, closed shoes or strapped sandals, a hat, sunscreen and insect repellent. Bring a change of clothes for the drive back and some cash for tips.
Are hotel transfers included on Samui elephant visits? | On most of our visits, yes, from the main beach areas such as Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut, Maenam, Choeng Mon and Bang Rak. Steep hillside villas get a nearby meeting point or a small surcharge, and the 1-hour feed-and-walk visit has no transfer.
How long is an elephant sanctuary visit? | Half-day visits last 3 hours on site, usually 09:00–12:00 or 14:00–17:00, plus 30–45 minutes' drive each way. Short visits last 1–1.5 hours, and the museum programmes run 3¾–5¾ hours door to door.
Which Samui elephant visit has the least contact with the animals? | The skywalk visit: you watch from a 400 m elevated walkway and a viewing area, with no riding, bathing or hands-on contact, for 3,000 THB per adult and 1,700 THB per child.
Is the elephant camp near Na Muang Waterfall ethical? | We don't recommend the elephant attractions on the Na Muang waterfall road, where older listings still describe riding and shows. If you visit the waterfall, park in the waterfall's own car park and book elephant time at a no-riding sanctuary instead.
