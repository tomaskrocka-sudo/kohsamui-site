What's on in Koh Samui this weekend? | Every Friday the Fisherman's Village Walking Street runs in Bophut from about 17:00 to 23:00, Chaweng Walking Street runs Thursday to Saturday by Chaweng Lake, Muay Thai fights are on in Chaweng on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, and beach clubs hold pool parties on Saturday and Sunday. When is the next Full Moon Party and how do I get there from Samui? | The next Full Moon Parties are Tuesday 24 November, Thursday 24 December and New Year's Eve, Thursday 31 December 2026, on Haad Rin, Koh Phangan. Our shared speedboat costs 1,400 THB return with hotel pickup from most areas of Samui and departures between 18:00 and 23:00; entry of about 200 THB is paid at the gate.
Which night is the Fisherman's Village Walking Street? | Friday is the main night, from about 17:00 to 23:00, when the Bophut beach road closes to traffic from about 16:30. Smaller market nights run on Monday and Wednesday at The Wharf end of the village.
Which nights are Muay Thai fights on Koh Samui? | Six nights a week in Chaweng: Samui International Muay Thai Stadium on Tuesday, Friday and Sunday, and Phetchbuncha Stadium on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday, both from about 21:00. There are no fights on Thursdays. Tickets cost 1,500 THB, 2,000 THB ringside and 2,500 THB VIP.
Are there parties on Koh Samui itself? | Yes, but no Full Moon-style beach rave. Chaweng has the clubs on Soi Green Mango (open until about 04:00), free fire shows on the beach every night from 20:00, and beach-club pool parties, mainly on Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. The big moon parties are a short boat ride away on Koh Phangan.
Is alcohol sold on Buddhist holidays on Koh Samui? | Shops and 7-Eleven cannot sell alcohol on holy days, but hotels, licensed bars and entertainment venues in tourist areas may still serve. The 2027 ban days are 21 February, 20 May, 18 and 19 July and 15 October; there are none in November or December 2026.
Can I release a sky lantern at Loy Krathong on Samui? | No. Launching sky lanterns near airports is illegal in Thailand, Samui's airport is between Chaweng and Bophut, and the island treats lanterns as banned. Float a bread or banana-leaf krathong on Chaweng Lake or the sea instead.
Do I need to buy party tickets in advance? | Full Moon Party entry (about 200 THB) is paid at the gate, so you only need to book the boat, and Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve boats sell out first. Half Moon Festival tickets are sold online in advance, and our Half Moon package from 2,700 THB includes the ticket and the boat.
Are Koh Samui's markets on in the rainy season? | Yes, the markets run all year, but in November and December heavy showers can thin out or close an outdoor walking street early. Fridays in Fisherman's Village usually go ahead; check the forecast and go early in the evening when rain is expected.
