Where is the best viewpoint on Koh Samui? | For a free stop, Lad Koh on the ring road between Chaweng Noi and Coral Cove, best at sunrise. For a panorama, Khao Hua Jook Chedi above Chaweng Lake or the paid Overlap Stone above Lamai (about 300 THB including a drink).
Are there marked hiking trails on Koh Samui? | Very few. The established walks are the paths to Na Muang 1 and 2 and Hin Lad waterfalls, the 500 m Overlap Stone walk and the paths inside the Secret Buddha Garden. Most of the interior is farmland on private tracks without signs.
Can you hike to Khao Pom, the highest point? | We know of no public, marked trail to the 635 m summit of Khao Pom, so we don't publish a route. Don't try it on unmarked farm tracks without a local guide; the Secret Buddha Garden on its slopes is the practical alternative.
Is the Lad Koh viewpoint safe? | The platform is safe; the rocks below are not. A 24-year-old visitor was swept off them by 2–3 m waves in November 2024, after which the municipality closed access to the rocks. Stay on the platform, especially October–December.
How do I get to Khao Hua Jook Chedi? | Turn uphill off Chaweng Lake Road north of the lake and walk or ride slowly up the short, steep road to the golden chedi at 9.5371, 100.0589. Entry is free; cover shoulders and knees and go at sunrise or after dark when it is lit.
Is Overlap Stone worth the entrance fee? | It is worth it if you want a short walk, a suspension bridge and a wide view over Lamai, but at about 300 THB it is the priciest viewpoint on Samui. Free alternatives are Lad Koh and Khao Hua Jook Chedi; skip the chained-elephant feeding on the way up.
What is the best time of day to hike on Samui? | From sunrise (about 06:00–06:40 depending on the month) to 10:00, before the heat and the tour groups; or after 16:00 for viewpoints. Highs reach 29–33 °C year-round, so avoid walking in the middle of the day.
Do I need a guide to hike on Koh Samui? | Not for the waterfall paths or viewpoints in this guide, which are short and easy to follow. You do need a guide or a 4x4 tour for anything on the central ridge beyond the Secret Buddha Garden, because tracks are unmarked and on private land.
Can I walk from Choeng Mon to Koh Fan Noi? | Yes, at low tide a sandbar links Choeng Mon beach to Koh Fan Noi. Cross only on a falling tide and return well before the water rises, or you can be cut off; check the tide table first.
