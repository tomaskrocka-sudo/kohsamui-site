The next Full Moon Party is on Tuesday 24 November 2026, the same night as Loy Krathong, followed by Thursday 24 December (Christmas Eve) and Thursday 31 December (the New Year's Eve countdown). In 2027 there are 13 more parties, and four of them move off Buddhist holy days: Monday 22 February, Friday 21 May, Tuesday 20 July and Saturday 16 October. The party takes place on Haad Rin Sunrise Beach (Haad Rin Nok) on Koh Phangan, 25–50 minutes by boat from Koh Samui. Next party on Haad Rin

[kp_next_party]

Every party follows the same basic pattern. Bars along Haad Rin Sunrise Beach start their sound systems in the evening, the beach fills from about 21:00, the peak is between midnight and 03:00, and the last stages play past sunrise. Entry is a wristband bought for about 200 THB in cash at the beach entrances. There are no official online entry tickets for the Full Moon Party, so treat any website selling "Full Moon tickets" with caution. We sell the boat from Samui, not the entry.

Why some Full Moon Party dates move

Thailand bans alcohol sales on five Buddhist holy days a year. When the full moon lands on one of them, the Haad Rin association moves the party by a day, usually to the following night. In 2027 that happens four times:

  • Makha Bucha is Sunday 21 February 2027, so the party moves to Monday 22 February.
  • Visakha Bucha is Thursday 20 May 2027, so the party moves to Friday 21 May.
  • Asahna Bucha and Khao Phansa (the start of Buddhist Lent) fall on 18 and 19 July 2027 in most calendars, so the party moves to Tuesday 20 July. One calendar puts the holidays a day later; the party date itself is the same in both schedules.
  • Ok Phansa (the end of Lent) is expected on about Friday 15 October 2027, so the party moves to Saturday 16 October.

Two other 2027 dates sit the night before the astronomical full moon: 15 September (full moon on the morning of 16 September) and 13 November (full moon on the morning of 14 November). That is normal, because the party is a night event.

Many websites still publish the raw lunar dates (21 February, 20 May, 18 July, 15 October 2027). If you book a boat or a room for one of those nights, you will arrive on an alcohol-ban day with no party. Check the date against this table before you book flights. There are no ban days in November or December 2026.

How the night works

  1. 18:00–23:00 Pickup from your hotel on Samui and transfer to the pier (our shared boats depart within this window).
  2. +25–40 min Speedboat crossing to Haad Rin; you land a few minutes' walk from the beach.
  3. 21:00 Pay the entry at the beach and get your wristband. The bars start the fire shows.
  4. 00:00–03:00 The busiest part of the night. Every bar plays a different style.
  5. Your return slot Meet the crew at the agreed point for the boat back to Samui; your return time is confirmed when you book.
  6. Drop-off Transfer from the Samui pier to your hotel.

Music on the beach

Sunrise Beach is lined with bars from end to end, each with its own sound system facing the sand. Expect separate zones for EDM and big-room house, drum and bass, psytrance and trance, hip hop and R&B, and chart music. The line-up changes by bar and by season, so walk the length of the beach before settling. Fire shows and fire-skipping ropes run on the sand from the early evening; the burns from skipping ropes are a real and common injury, so watch rather than join in after a few drinks. If you need a break, the side streets behind the beach have food stalls and quieter bars.

Getting there from Koh Samui

There are three practical ways to do the party from Samui and sleep back in your own hotel:

  • Our shared return speedboat: 1,400 THB per person in 2026, with hotel pickup from most areas of Koh Samui. Nathon, Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Bang Kao and Thong Krut (and other remote areas) add 400 THB per person, return. Book your seat.
  • A private speedboat: your own boat for up to 10 guests, with your own departure and return times. See the private Full Moon boat.
  • The public Haad Rin Queen from Big Buddha pier: about 200 THB on the slow boat, but daytime and early-evening departures only and no late-night return, so you would stay until the morning boats.

Special nights

Loy Krathong and the Full Moon Party (24 November 2026)

Loy Krathong, when Thais float candle-lit krathongs on water, falls on Tuesday 24 November 2026, the same night as the party. You can float a krathong at Chaweng Lake or a Samui beach at about 19:00 and still make a later boat to Haad Rin. In 2027 Loy Krathong is expected on Saturday 13 November, again the party night, but Thai calendars for 2027 are not yet published. See Loy Krathong on Koh Samui.

Christmas Eve Full Moon Party (24 December 2026)

The full moon falls on Thursday 24 December 2026, so the party is on Christmas Eve. It is one of the busiest nights of the year, together with New Year's Eve. Ang Thong Marine Park reopens on 16 December, so you can combine a park trip and the party in the same week.

New Year's Eve countdown party (31 December)

The 31 December party is not lunar: it runs every year on New Year's Eve, regardless of the moon. In 2026 it is a Thursday, and in 2027 a Friday. Boats and rooms on Haad Rin sell out first for this date. See Christmas and New Year's Eve on Koh Samui for the Samui fireworks if you would rather stay on the island.

What to bring

  • Enough cash for entry and drinks, in small notes; leave cards in the hotel safe if you can.
  • A photocopy or phone photo of your passport; leave the original in the safe.
  • Closed shoes or sturdy sandals that won't come off in the sand.
  • A waterproof phone pouch, a charged phone and our WhatsApp number saved: +66 63 704 0888.
  • Your hotel's name and address written down, for the drop-off.
  • A light layer for the boat ride back; spray off the sea can be cool at 04:00.

Stay safe on party night

The UK Foreign Office warns of assaults, including sexual assaults, drink spiking and unlicensed transport around Full Moon events. Practical rules:

  • Watch your drink being made and never leave it; buy sealed drinks if in doubt.
  • Stay with your group and agree a meeting point and time for the boat.
  • Don't swim after drinking. The sea at night is dark, and currents are strong in November and December.
  • Board only licensed boats with life jackets, and only the boat you booked.
  • Drugs carry heavy penalties in Thailand, and police checks around the party are common. Vapes are illegal too. See drugs and alcohol law.
  • Tourist Police: 1155. Ambulance: 1669.

How we check these dates

We follow three rules. First, a date goes into the table only when two independent party schedules agree on it. Second, we cross-check it against the Thai Buddhist holiday calendar and the astronomical full moon, which is how we catch the four moved 2027 dates. Third, we re-check each party about 30 days ahead, and again in the week of the party, and update the "Last checked" column. If a date changes after you have booked our boat, we contact you by email or WhatsApp straight away to rearrange your booking.
