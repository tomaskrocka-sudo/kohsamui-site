What is the best detox resort on Koh Samui? | Kamalaya at Laem Set is the best known, with detox programmes from an intro of 3 nights (from about US$2,284) to a full-board comprehensive detox of 7–14 nights (from about US$4,867). Absolute Sanctuary near Choeng Mon suits people who want detox combined with fitness and Pilates, close to the airport.
How much does a wellness retreat on Samui cost? | In 2026, from about US$654 per person sharing for 3 nights at Samahita, about US$1,836 for 3 nights at Kamalaya and about US$2,152 for 6 nights at Absolute Sanctuary, rising to US$4,000–5,000 for a 7–8-night detox or weight programme. Check whether prices include the 10% service charge and 7% VAT.
Is there a minimum stay at Koh Samui wellness resorts? | Usually. Kamalaya requires first-time guests staying fewer than 7 nights to book at least a 3-night programme, and its full detox and balance programmes start at 7 nights. Most Absolute Sanctuary programmes start at 6 nights; Samahita starts at 3.
Are detox programmes safe? | For healthy adults supervised by trained staff, generally yes, but fasting, juice diets, colon treatments and heat can be risky with diabetes, heart or kidney conditions, pregnancy, eating disorders or regular medication. See your own doctor before booking and tell the resort everything at the first consultation.
When is the best time for a wellness retreat on Koh Samui? | February to April for dry, calm weather and boat days, or May to September for lower prices. October to December is the rainy season, but indoor programmes continue and rates are at their lowest.
Can I visit a wellness resort spa without staying? | Yes at some. In 2026 Kamalaya sold day passes open to all travellers from 1,800 THB++ (with pools, wellness facilities and a holistic activity) to 5,300 THB++ with a spa treatment, and Tamarind Springs near Lamai is a day spa with herbal steam caves from 1,500 THB++.
Detox resort or yoga retreat: which suits me? | Choose a detox or wellness resort for a structured programme with consultations, treatments and tracked results, especially for burnout, sleep or weight goals. Choose a yoga retreat such as Vikasa or Samahita for daily practice, healthy food and a lower price, with more free time.
How far are the wellness resorts from the airport? | Absolute Sanctuary is about 5 minutes away, Vikasa about 20–25 minutes, and Kamalaya and Samahita in the south about 45 minutes. Most resorts arrange transfers on request, often at extra cost.
