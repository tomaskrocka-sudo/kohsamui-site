Why go: the long day out, by open jeep

If you want to see as much of Koh Samui as possible in one day, this is the safari to book. It starts in the south, climbs into the hills on 4x4 tracks normal cars can't manage, and finishes on the north coast at the Big Buddha. The open ex-army jeeps are part of the fun, especially for children and groups of friends.

What you'll do

The morning covers the south: the Hin Ta and Hin Yai rocks, the mummified monk at Wat Khunaram and a swim at Na Muang waterfall. Then the jeep climbs to the island's high viewpoints before a Thai lunch. In the afternoon you visit the Secret Buddha Garden, cool down with a water-pistol fight, stop at the Big Buddha and watch a coconut-cracking demonstration before the drive home.

Who it suits

  • Families with children from about 5, and groups who like a bit of noise and mud.
  • Visitors with one free day who want the whole island in one go.
  • German speakers: German-speaking guides are available.

Who should choose something else

  • Anyone pregnant, with back problems or who tires in the heat: this is a long, bumpy day.
  • If meeting elephants matters, the 4x4 safari with elephant feeding includes a non-riding sanctuary.
  • For a shorter morning, the half-day highlights tour uses an air-conditioned van.

Good to know

  • Temples: carry a sarong or scarf; shoulders and knees should be covered at Wat Khunaram and the Big Buddha.
  • Sun: the jeeps are open, so wear a hat and reapply sunscreen after the waterfall.
  • Rainy season: October to December brings heavy showers; the tracks stay passable but get muddy.
