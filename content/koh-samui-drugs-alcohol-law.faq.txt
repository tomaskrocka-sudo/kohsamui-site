Is cannabis legal on Koh Samui in 2026? | Only on prescription. Since 25 June 2025, cannabis flower can only be bought with a prescription from a licensed practitioner, limited to a 30-day supply, and smoking it in public can bring a fine of up to 25,000 THB. A stricter Cannabis Control Act is before parliament.
Can I bring a vape to Thailand? | No. Importing, owning or using vapes, e-cigarettes, pods, e-liquids and heat-not-burn devices is illegal, with no exception for tourists. Devices are confiscated and typical fines are 5,000–30,000 THB, with possible detention.
What time can you buy alcohol in Thailand? | From 11:00 to 24:00 every day since late May 2026, when the old 14:00–17:00 afternoon ban ended. Licensed entertainment venues, hotels and international airport terminals are exempt from these hours.
Are bars open on Buddhist holidays? | Licensed entertainment venues and hotels may still sell alcohol on the 5 Buddhist holy days under an exemption in force since May 2025. Ordinary restaurants, shops, supermarkets and 7-Eleven must stop selling for the whole day.
What is the legal drinking age in Thailand? | 20. Shops and bars may ask for ID, so carry a copy of your passport. Selling alcohol to under-20s is an offence for the seller.
Can I smoke on the beach in Koh Samui? | Not on Bophut beach, one of 24 Thai beaches where smoking has been banned since 2018 (fines up to 100,000 THB), nor in national parks such as Ang Thong. Elsewhere, use designated areas and take your butts with you. Cannabis and vapes are illegal on every beach.
What happens if I'm caught with drugs in Thailand? | Expect arrest, heavy fines or prison; possession of Class A drugs can carry the death penalty, and positive urine tests count as use. Ask for your embassy and an interpreter, and don't sign anything you can't read.
Are there alcohol bans in December 2026? | No Buddhist holy-day bans fall in November or December 2026, so Loy Krathong, Christmas and New Year's Eve are unaffected. The next holy-day ban is Makha Bucha on Sunday 21 February 2027.
