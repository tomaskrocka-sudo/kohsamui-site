Can a foreigner buy land on Koh Samui? | No. Foreign individuals and foreign-controlled companies can't own land in Thailand. On Samui foreigners usually register a lease of up to 30 years, own the villa itself separately from the land, or buy a condominium unit within the 49% foreign quota.
Is a 30+30+30-year lease valid? | Only the first 30 years is secure. A lease can't exceed 30 years under section 540 of the Civil and Commercial Code, and in Supreme Court case 4655/2566 the court held renewal clauses void because they got round that limit. Treat renewal options as a personal promise, not a guarantee.
Does Thailand have 99-year leases now? | No. A 99-year lease for foreigners has been proposed repeatedly since 2024, but as of November 2026 no law allowing it has been passed, and the Finance Ministry has said no progress has been made at policy level. The maximum registered lease is still 30 years.
What is a Chanote? | A Chanote (Nor Sor 4 Jor) is Thailand's full land title deed, surveyed and fixed with numbered boundary markers. It is the safest title to buy or lease; a surveyed Nor Sor 3 Gor is the next best, while Sor Kor 1 and tax receipts are not titles at all.
Can foreigners buy a condo on Koh Samui? | Yes, freehold, as long as foreign owners together hold no more than 49% of the building's floor area and you bring the purchase money into Thailand from abroad in foreign currency. Check that the building is registered under the Condominium Act; some Samui "condos" are not.
How much are property transfer fees in Thailand? | The transfer fee is 2% of the appraised value, plus either 3.3% specific business tax (if the seller has owned it for under 5 years) or 0.5% stamp duty, and withholding tax for the seller. Registering a lease costs 1% of the total rent plus 0.1% stamp duty.
Can I build on a hillside plot on Koh Samui? | Only within strict limits. Above 80 m elevation, and on slopes of 35% or more, the 2025 environmental rules allow only single detached houses up to 6 m high with capped footprints and large open areas. Have the slope and elevation surveyed and get written confirmation from the municipality first.
Is buying through a Thai company legal? | Only if the company is genuinely Thai-controlled and its Thai shareholders invest their own money. Using Thai nominees to hold land for a foreigner is illegal and can lead to criminal charges and an order to sell the land.
Where is the Land Office on Koh Samui? | The Surat Thani Land Office has a Koh Samui branch that handles title searches, transfers and lease registrations. Confirm its current address and hours by phone before you go, and take your lawyer.
Do foreigners pay property tax in Thailand? | Yes. Owners of land, houses and condos pay the annual Land and Building Tax, collected on Samui by the city municipality. Second homes are taxed from 0.02% of the appraised value, and villas let to holidaymakers are usually taxed at commercial rates from 0.3%.
