Is February the best month to visit Koh Samui? | For most travellers, yes. February is the driest month, with about 64 mm of rain over 4 days, and has the calmest sea of the year, so Ang Thong, Koh Tao and Pig Island all run on most days. The downsides are peak-season prices and busier boats, so book rooms and trips early.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in February? | About 64 mm on 1991–2020 averages, over about 4 days with 1 mm or more, the least of any month. That is roughly a seventh of November's rain. Showers are usually short, but cold surges from the north can still bring a windy, wet day or three.
Is the sea calm around Koh Samui in February? | Usually, yes: February has the calmest sea of the year and ferries run normally. It is not guaranteed. On 19 February 2026 a cold surge brought 1–2 m waves, red flags and a swimming ban to Chaweng and Lamai. These surges usually last one to three days, so keep a spare day for boat trips.
When is the Full Moon Party in February 2027? | Monday 22 February 2027. The full moon falls on Sunday 21 February, which is Makha Bucha, an alcohol-ban day, so the party moves a night later. Many websites still list 21 February. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup from most areas; entry, about 200 THB, is paid at the beach.
Can you buy alcohol on Koh Samui on Makha Bucha 2027? | Not in shops. On Sunday 21 February 2027, 7-Eleven, supermarkets and ordinary shops can't sell alcohol, and nor can unlicensed restaurants and street stalls. Hotels and licensed bars in tourist areas may still serve, so Chaweng's bars usually open as normal. Monday 22 February is the substitute public holiday.
Where should I stay on Koh Samui for Chinese New Year 2027? | Maenam or Bophut. Maenam's lion and dragon dances on Saturday 6 February are on your doorstep, and Fisherman's Village is a short ride away for dinner. Nathon has the most traditional rites but few places to stay, so visit for the evening. Book early: Chinese New Year adds visitors from across Asia to an already busy month.
Is February expensive on Koh Samui? | Yes, it is peak season. Room rates run 30–80% above shoulder-season prices from mid-December to February, and Chinese New Year adds demand in early February. Everyday costs don't change: street dishes 50–120 THB and a one-hour Thai massage 300–500 THB. For similar weather at lower rates, come in March.
Are the waterfalls worth visiting in February? | Not really. Na Muang and Hin Lad thin to a trickle from February to April, after flowing fullest from October to January. February is better spent on the water or on viewpoints: the trails are dry and the humidity lower than in April and May.
Is February or March better on Koh Samui? | February has less rain (about 64 mm against 116 mm) and the calmest sea; March has only one more rain day, a slightly warmer sea, better visibility for diving and lower room rates. Choose February for Chinese New Year and the most settled weather, March for value and diving.
