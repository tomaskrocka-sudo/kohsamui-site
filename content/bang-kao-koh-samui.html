Samui's south coast (Bang Kao, Laem Sor and Laem Set, between Thong Krut in the west and Hua Thanon in the east) is the island's quietest and most local stretch, 9 km and 15–20 minutes from Lamai. Bang Kao beach is shallow, with coral rubble and patchy sand, so it only works for swimming at high tide and with water shoes. The highlights are the gold-tiled Laem Sor Pagoda on the shore (free, 07:00–18:00) and the wellness resorts of Laem Set. Our tours pick up here for an extra 400 THB per person return.

Map of the south coast

The south coast runs for about 12 km. Driving east to west you pass Laem Set (the rocky headland below Hua Thanon), Na Tien, Bang Kao, Laem Sor and finally Thong Krut. The ring road runs inland here, and side roads drop to the sea through coconut plantations, so signposting is confusing; use a map app.

Bang Kao beach

Bang Kao is a long, quiet village beach with fishing boats, coconut palms and very few facilities. The sand is patchy, grey-white in places and mixed with broken coral, and visitors have reported litter washed up after storms, which nobody clears quickly. At low tide the sea retreats over wide, shallow flats. It is a place for a walk, a book in the shade and seeing village life, not a classic swimming beach. A few small resorts and villas front parts of the beach.

Swimming and safety

  • Swim at high tide only. At low tide the water is ankle- to knee-deep for a long way out. Check the tide before you go.
  • Wear water shoes: coral pieces, shells and the odd sea urchin on the bottom.
  • Monsoon: the south coast faces away from the north-east monsoon, so waves are usually smaller than on Chaweng and Lamai in October–December, but rain is just as heavy. November is the wettest month island-wide, at about 445 mm.
  • Box jellyfish: present in Samui waters, with risk higher in the monsoon months and in calm, hot weather after rain. Don't swim at dusk; vinegar for at least 30 seconds on a sting, no rubbing, call 1669.
  • No lifeguards and few people around: don't swim alone.
  • Medical help is far: the main hospitals are in the Chaweng area, 30–40 minutes away; ambulance 1669.

Laem Sor Pagoda and Khao Chedi

Laem Sor Pagoda (Wat Phra Chedi Laem Sor) is a chedi covered in yellow-gold tiles, standing right on the shore at the western end of Bang Kao bay. It is open 07:00–18:00 and free. It is especially photogenic in the late afternoon, when the low sun lights the tiles and the sea is calm. On the hill to the west stands Khao Chedi, a white chedi. Dress respectfully: cover shoulders and knees and remove shoes where asked. Full details on our Laem Sor Pagoda page and on Koh Samui temples.

Laem Set and the wellness resorts

Laem Set is the rocky headland at the eastern end of the south coast, below Hua Thanon. The shore here is boulders and small coves rather than a swimming beach. It is best known for wellness resorts, above all Kamalaya, at 102/9 Moo 3, Laem Set Road, about 45 minutes from the airport. Kamalaya's residential programmes start at around US$1,836 for three nights (Relax and Renew) and rise to about US$4,954 for a seven-night comprehensive programme, with a minimum stay of three nights. Other retreats, such as Samahita, sit on the coast near Laem Sor. Comparison on Koh Samui wellness resorts and retreats.

Hua Thanon and Thong Krut

The south coast is bookended by two working villages that are worth a short stop:

  • Hua Thanon, east: the Muslim fishing village with a morning fish market (go 07:00–09:00), the Central Mosque and the 16 m Guan Yu statue above it.
  • Thong Krut, about 5 km west: the pier for Koh Madsum (Pig Island), Koh Tan and Koh Rap, and a few seafood restaurants.

Food and nightlife

Bang Kao has a few local food stalls and small restaurants attached to resorts, but we could not confirm a specific seafood restaurant on Bang Kao beach for 2026. Better options nearby: seafood by the kilo in Thong Krut, halal stalls in Hua Thanon in the morning, and the MICHELIN Bib Gourmand 2026 seafood restaurant Baan Suan Lung Khai in Taling Ngam (11:00–14:00 and 16:30–19:00, reservation required). There is no nightlife on the south coast; Lamai is 15–20 minutes away. See Koh Samui seafood.

Where to stay in the south

The south coast suits wellness stays, long stays and people who want real quiet. Accommodation is a mix of wellness resorts, small beach resorts and private villas; there is little budget accommodation and almost no walk-in choice of restaurants. You need your own transport: Chaweng and the airport are 30–45 minutes away, and Grab drivers are scarce here, especially at night. If you want the south coast's calm with easier access to food, stay in south Lamai and visit for the day. See where to stay on Koh Samui and long stays on Koh Samui.

Nearby sights inland

  • Wat Khunaram: the mummified monk, on the ring road about 15 minutes north-east.
  • Na Muang Waterfall 1: 09:00–17:00, free, parking 20 THB scooter / 60 THB car; thin February–April.
  • Secret Buddha Garden: hillside sculpture garden on a very steep road; best on a tour.
  • Guan Yu Shrine: 09:00–17:00 above Hua Thanon.
