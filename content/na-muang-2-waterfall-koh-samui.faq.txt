Is Na Muang 2 waterfall free? | Yes, the left-hand path signed "Free Jungle Walk" costs nothing, and parking at the top car parks is often free. Some visitors report a charge of about 50 THB on the private right-hand route through a café and garden.
How do I find the right path at Na Muang 2? | From the top car parks take the left fork downhill about 100 m past a small shop and cross a wooden bridge over the stream. If you have not crossed a wooden bridge within a few minutes, you are on the wrong path.
Are there ropes on the Na Muang 2 trail? | Yes, fixed ropes help on the steepest boulder sections. Test each rope before you load it, keep three points of contact and go one person at a time.
Is Na Muang 2 dangerous? | It is the most dangerous site on Samui for falls: at least four visitors have died there since 2019, most recently on 5 January 2026, all after slipping on wet rock on the upper tiers. Stay off the edges, don't climb after heavy rain and wear grippy shoes.
Can children climb to Na Muang 2? | Not young children. Fit teenagers with proper shoes and close supervision can manage it in dry weather; families with small children should swim at Na Muang 1 instead.
Can you swim at the top of Na Muang 2? | Only in the small, shallow plunge pools on the left-hand route, well back from the edge of each drop. Swimming is not allowed at the top of the private right-hand route, and jumping is unsafe anywhere on Na Muang 2.
Can I ride a scooter up to Na Muang 2? | Yes, a 125 cc scooter can make the hill road with two people, but it is steep. Ride in low gear, brake before the bends rather than in them, and take the descent slowly.
How tall is Na Muang 2 waterfall? | About 80 m in total, over at least five tiers, which makes it the tallest waterfall on Koh Samui. Na Muang 1 below it is about 18 m.
