~How long does it take to drive around Koh Samui? | About 90 minutes non-stop on the 51 km ring road (Route 4169). With a swim, lunch and three or four sights such as the Big Buddha, Na Muang Waterfall and Hin Ta and Hin Yai, plan a full day.
Which side of the road do they drive on in Koh Samui? | On the left, as in the UK and Australia. Give way to traffic already on the ring road when you pull out of a side road, and expect scooters to overtake on both sides.
Are Koh Samui roads dangerous? | They are riskier than most visitors are used to. Thailand has one of the world's worst records for motorcycle deaths, and on Samui most crashes involve hired scooters on wet hills or at night. Wear a helmet, ride sober and avoid the hill roads in the rain.
Where are the police checkpoints on Koh Samui? | Most often near Chaweng Lake Road, near the airport and at points along the ring road, Route 4169. Officers check helmets, licences and International Driving Permits and sometimes test for alcohol at night.
What is the fine for not wearing a helmet? | Up to 2,000 THB per person since 1 June 2025, for the rider and each passenger. Older figures of 500 THB are out of date.
Do roads flood in the rainy season? | Yes, in places. From October to December, and especially in November, low points on inland roads and the south-west back roads can flood in heavy rain, and sand and potholes appear on bends. If you can't see the road surface, turn back.
What should I do after a scooter accident? | Call 1669 for an ambulance and 191 or 1155 for the police, photograph the scene and number plates, call your rental shop and your insurer's emergency line, and get a police report for the claim. Don't sign anything you can't read and never hand over your passport.
Which roads on Koh Samui are the steepest? | Route 4170 through Lipa Noi, Thong Krut and Taling Ngam, the roads to the Secret Buddha Garden, Overlap Stone and Na Muang, and many hillside villa driveways in Chaweng Noi and Bophut. Use a low gear and engine braking on the way down.
What is the drink-drive limit in Thailand? | 0.05% blood alcohol for most drivers, and 0.02% for drivers under 20, provisional licence holders and anyone without a valid licence. On Samui, the safest rule is not to ride at all after drinking.
Can I park at Fisherman's Village on a Friday? | Not inside the walking-street area: the road closes from about 16:30 and scooters left there can be towed, with a release fee of 500 THB or more. Park along the main road and walk in.
