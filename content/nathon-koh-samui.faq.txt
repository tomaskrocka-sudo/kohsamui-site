Is Nathon worth visiting? | Yes for 2–3 hours: walk the old shophouse streets, watch the sunset from the pier and eat at the daily night food market (about 16:30–22:00), but it is a port town, not a beach destination.
Is there a beach in Nathon? | No, the town faces the sea along a sea wall with piers and boats; the nearest swimming beach is Lipa Noi, 6 km south, which is very shallow and best at high tide.
When is the Nathon night market? | Every day from about 16:30 to 22:00 near the pier and waterfront, with Tuesday the busiest night; arrive around 17:00–18:00 before popular stalls sell out, and bring cash.
Which ferries leave from Nathon? | Seatran car ferries sail roughly hourly to Donsak on the mainland (about 1.5 hours), Lomprayah runs catamarans to Donsak with bus connections, and Songserm, Lomprayah and Boonsiri boats go to Thong Sala on Koh Phangan, with connections to Koh Tao.
How much is the Seatran ferry from Nathon to Donsak? | A foot passenger ticket costs about 170–180 THB at the pier in 2026 (more through online resellers), and a car with driver about 550 THB; the crossing takes about 1.5 hours.
Is the immigration office in Nathon? | No, the Koh Samui Immigration Office moved to Maenam in 2016: 333 Maenam Road, Soi 1, Monday–Friday 08:30–16:30, phone +66 77 423 440.
Where is the government hospital on Koh Samui? | Koh Samui Hospital is at 61 Moo 1, Ang Thong, on the edge of Nathon; it is open 24 hours, phone +66 77 913 200.
Is there still a walking street in Nathon on Saturdays? | No, the Saturday walking street has been discontinued and we found no evidence of it in 2025 or 2026; the daily night food market is what runs now.
How far is Nathon from Chaweng? | About 24 km by road, 30–40 minutes; a taxi costs roughly 500–750 THB and a songthaew about 60–80 THB at the local fare in daylight.
What time is the last boat from Nathon to Koh Phangan? | In the autumn 2026 schedule the last departure was Songserm Express at 17:15, with the last Lomprayah at 16:00; times change seasonally, so confirm when booking.
Do your tours pick up from Nathon? | Yes, hotel pickup is available from Nathon for an extra 400 THB per person return.
What is the Chak Phra festival in Nathon? | At the end of Buddhist Lent (October or November), decorated temple floats from about 16 Samui temples are pulled through Nathon to the pier, with a fair of about nine days; dates are set locally each year.
