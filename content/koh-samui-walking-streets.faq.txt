Which nights is there a market in Fisherman's Village? | Friday is the main night, from about 17:00 to 23:00 along the beach road in Bophut. Smaller, more local markets set up on the same road on Monday and Wednesday evenings.
What time does the Fisherman's Village walking street start and finish? | The road closes to traffic from about 16:30, stalls are open by 17:00 and the market runs until about 23:00. The busiest time is about 19:00–20:30, so arrive at 17:30–18:00 to park and eat in comfort.
Where do I park for the Fisherman's Village walking street? | In The Wharf car park at the eastern end, which fills by about 18:00, or in the paid lots in the lanes between the ring road and the beach road. Coming by Grab or taxi avoids the problem altogether. Never leave a scooter on the closed road: they are towed or clamped.
Is there a walking street in Chaweng? | Yes. Chaweng Walking Street runs Thursday to Saturday beside Chaweng Lake, opposite Central Samui, from about 16:00–17:00 to 22:00–23:00. It is mostly food, with some clothes and souvenirs, and the mall's car park and toilets are across the road.
Is the Maenam walking street still on? | Uncertain. It used to run on Thursday evenings in Maenam's Chinatown, but a local source updated in late 2025 says it has disappeared since the pandemic, while some guides still list it. J&J Street Food opposite the Chinese gate is open daily as a reliable alternative.
Is the Lamai Sunday walking street still on? | Uncertain. Some 2026 guides still list it, but a careful local source says it has gone. The daily Lamai Night Market before the river bridge, from about 17:00, and the Lamai Seafood Market, until about midnight, are reliable every evening.
Is there a walking street in Nathon? | Not any more. Nathon's Saturday walking street has ended. The daily Nathon Night Market on the waterfront by the pier runs from about 16:00 to 22:00, is busiest on Tuesday and is the cheapest dinner on the west coast.
Which walking street is best for families on Koh Samui? | Chaweng Walking Street by Chaweng Lake, from Thursday to Saturday: it has wider paths, shared tables and Central Samui's toilets and air-conditioning across the road. Fisherman's Village on Friday is more atmospheric but very crowded from 19:00, so take children early, at about 17:30.
