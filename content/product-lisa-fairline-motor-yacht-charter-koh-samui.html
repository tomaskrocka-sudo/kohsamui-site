Why go: an easy luxury half day to the southern islands

Lisa is a British-built Fairline motor yacht that suits the short hop from Samui's south coast to Koh Tan and Koh Madsum: you can be snorkelling within 45 minutes of leaving, then take the dinghy ashore to Pig Island while the crew stays with the boat. Paddleboards, fishing gear, beer or wine and hotel transfer are all included.

The afternoon half day is timed to finish with sunset on the way back, and the full days add Koh Rap, Koh Phangan, Ang Thong (from 16 December) or a morning looking for pink dolphins.

Half day or full day

The morning half day leaves at 09:00, with snorkelling and paddleboarding at Koh Tan before the dinghy takes you to Koh Madsum, and you are back by 13:00. The afternoon half day leaves at 14:30 and returns with the sunset. Full days start at 10:00 or 10:30 and include lunch on board: add Koh Rap's long beach, spend the morning looking for dolphins, or cross to Koh Phangan's quieter coves. From 16 December the yacht can also reach Ang Thong.

Who it suits

  • Couples and families who want a luxury half day without a long crossing.
  • Groups of 6–10 celebrating a special occasion.
  • Guests staying in the south: Lamai, Hua Thanon, Bang Kao and Thong Krut.

Who should choose something else

  • Anyone on a budget: our private speedboat to Pig Island covers the same islands for a fraction of the price.
  • Sailing fans: try a catamaran.

Good to know

  • The pigs: let them come to you; don't chase or lift them.
  • Dolphins: the crew keeps a distance and doesn't chase them.
  • Booking: online booking for tomorrow closes at midnight Thai time.
