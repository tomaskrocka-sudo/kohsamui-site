Season 3 of The White Lotus (aired February–April 2025) was filmed largely at the Four Seasons Resort Koh Samui, on the Laem Yai headland in the island's north-west. The resort is private, with rooms reported at about US$1,900 a night and villas up to about US$14,000. HBO's official list adds other Samui locations you can visit freely, including Fisherman's Village in Bophut and Choeng Mon Beach. The easiest way to see the resort's coast is from the water, on a private boat or a sunset cruise along the north-west and west coasts.

Since the series aired, "White Lotus Koh Samui" has become one of the most searched phrases about the island. This page sticks to what is on the record: the locations named in HBO's own release of 11 February 2025, published prices, and what visitors can actually do. We don't use stills from the show, and we don't send people to private property.

The Four Seasons Koh Samui

The Four Seasons Resort Koh Samui stands on a steep hillside on a former coconut plantation at Laem Yai, the north-western tip of the island, about 5 km north of Nathon and 22 km (30–35 minutes) from Chaweng. Its villas step down the slope to a private-feeling bay facing west for sunsets. In the series it plays the White Lotus resort, with the Ratliff family villa, the pool, the beach and the restaurants all filmed on site; some interiors were built in a Bangkok studio.

Can you visit without staying?

The resort is private property with a gated entrance, and turning up to look around isn't possible. The resort has several restaurants and bars, and luxury resorts on Samui sometimes accept outside guests for dinner or a spa treatment by advance reservation, but we could not confirm the Four Seasons' current policy, so contact the resort directly before planning around it. The themed "White Lotus Bar" pop-up at its CoCoRum lounge ran from 17 February to 16 May 2025 and has ended.

Thailand treats the beach below the high-water line as public, but the shore at Laem Yai is mostly rock and pebble, reachable mainly from the sea, and walking across resort land to reach it is trespassing. [check] The honest answer is that the best view most of us will get is from a boat offshore, which is also the most beautiful way to see it.

The arrival scenes: Ang Thong Marine Park

The guests in the series arrive by boat past limestone islands, and HBO lists Ang Thong National Marine Park, 42 islands west of Samui, as one of the arrival locations. The park closes every year from 1 November to 15 December and reopens on 16 December. Our Ang Thong speedboat tour costs 1,800 THB adult, and the boutique yachting day on a 30 m wooden gulet costs 4,500 THB adult, park fee and hotel transfer included. See Ang Thong Marine Park.

Other filming locations in Thailand

  • Bangkok: the Mandarin Oriental lobby and its Bamboo Bar, Wat Pho pier, Royal Osha restaurant, Siri Sala Private Thai Villa (the Hollinger house) and Sing Sing Theater; some hotel interiors were built at Acts Studio.
  • Phuket: Anantara Mai Khao (the wellness sanctuary and spa), Ta Khai restaurant at Rosewood Phuket (dinner scenes), Bangla Boxing (the Muay Thai stadium), Café Del Mar (the beach club), Cape Pawna Hotel, Villa Amaravida, Phuket Yacht Haven Marina and Phang Nga Bay, Ko Lawa Yai and Ya Nui Beach.
  • Koh Phangan: Haad Rin Beach, the real Full Moon Party beach, for episode 5. From Samui it is 30–50 minutes by speedboat; see Full Moon Party from Koh Samui.

Want Muay Thai like the Phuket scene? Samui has fights six nights a week in Chaweng: Muay Thai fight nights.

The "White Lotus effect" on Samui

  • Online searches for the Four Seasons Koh Samui were reported up about 40 % in January 2025, before the first episode aired.
  • Foreign bookings for Samui over Songkran 2025 (10–17 April) were up 65 % year on year, according to SiteMinder hotel data reported by the Bangkok Post.
  • Minor Hotels reported direct bookings at its Anantara resorts on Samui up 41 %.

The effect has been strongest at the top end. For most visitors it simply means the island's luxury resorts and the west coast are better known, and that high-season rooms book up earlier. See where to stay on Koh Samui.

Plan a White Lotus-style week on any budget

  • Wellness: Samui has some of Thailand's best-known wellness resorts, plus drop-in yoga, meditation and Thai massage at a fraction of the price.
  • A temple morning: Wat Pu Khao Thong in the Maenam hills is an active temple; dress with shoulders and knees covered. More: temples on Koh Samui.
  • The town centre scenes: Fisherman's Village in Bophut, best on Friday evening for the walking street. See Fisherman's Village.
  • A beach day: Choeng Mon, the calm bay used for the party scenes, is one of Samui's best family beaches. See Choeng Mon.
  • Sunset: the west coast at Lipa Noi or Taling Ngam. See sunset spots.
  • A boat day: a private boat along the White Lotus coast, or Ang Thong from 16 December.
  • A villa: private pool villas for every budget: Koh Samui villas.

Respectful photography and privacy

  • Resorts are private: don't enter grounds, private beaches or villas to take photos, and don't fly drones over them. Drones in Thailand must be registered, and flying over private property and near the airport is restricted.
  • Photograph from public places: the beach, the road or the sea.
  • Don't photograph guests or staff without permission.
  • At temples, dress modestly, keep your voice down and ask before photographing monks.
  • Choeng Mon and Fisherman's Village are everyday places for locals; filming-location selfies are fine, blocking the street isn't.
