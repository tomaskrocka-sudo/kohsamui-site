Why go: three islands under sail, with the boat to yourselves

This is a full day on a private sailing catamaran around the small islands off Samui's south coast, with no other passengers. You visit Koh Rap for a long beach and paddleboarding, Koh Tan for snorkelling over the reef, and Koh Madsum, better known as Pig Island, where the pigs come down to the shallows. At about 8.5 hours it is longer than most Pig Island trips, so there is time to stay at each stop rather than being rushed back to the boat.

A crew and an English-speaking guide look after the sailing and the stops. Snorkel gear, paddleboards and fishing equipment are on board, with soft drinks, water and fresh watermelon and pineapple.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

It suits families and groups of up to 10 who want a slow, comfortable day at sea, and anyone who gets seasick on small boats: a catamaran's two hulls ride far more steadily than a speedboat or longtail. Split between 8 people, the boat works out at about 3,375 THB each, close to the price of a shared premium tour.

It is not the quickest way to see the pigs. If you have only a morning, the Pig Island speedboat tour or the private speedboat to Pig Island covers the highlights in less time. Groups larger than 10 need a bigger boat; the Super King takes up to 50.

Good to know

  • Lunch is not included. Bring a cool bag, or eat at the small beach restaurants on the islands; carry cash.
  • Koh Madsum charges an island fee of about 50 THB per person in cash.
  • Treat the pigs gently: no chasing or lifting, and feed them only fruit and vegetables.
  • Koh Tan's reef is recovering. Use reef-safe sunscreen and keep off the coral.
  • The south coast is the most sheltered side of Samui in the northeast monsoon, so this trip runs on many days when others are cancelled. November is still the wettest month; if the captain cancels for safety you choose a refund or a new date.
  • A sunset return can be requested at booking, depending on the season.
