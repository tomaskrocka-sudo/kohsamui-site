Is Chaweng the best area to stay on Koh Samui? | For a first visit, usually yes: it has the most restaurants, bars and shops, a good swimming beach and is 10–15 minutes from the airport. It is also the busiest and noisiest area, so couples and families wanting quiet nights often prefer Choeng Mon, Bophut or Maenam.
Which part of Chaweng Beach is best? | North Chaweng for swimming and families: the slope is very gentle and it is quieter. Central Chaweng for nightlife, with ARKbar and the beach clubs. South Chaweng is a quieter middle ground within walking distance of the centre.
Can you swim at Chaweng in November? | Only on calm days. November is the wettest month and the north-east monsoon brings surf, rip currents and red flags to Chaweng. Never swim when a red flag is up; on rough days try sheltered Choeng Mon or the west coast instead.
How far is Chaweng from the airport? | About 5 km, or 10–15 minutes by car outside rush hour. Chaweng Beach Road traffic in the evening can add another 10–15 minutes.
How much is a taxi from the airport to Chaweng? | About 400–600 THB per car at the fixed-price airport taxi counter; Samui taxis do not use meters. Grab costs roughly 300–600 THB, and you meet the driver outside the airport pickup zone.
When is the Chaweng walking street? | Thursday, Friday and Saturday, from about 16:00–17:00 until 22:00–23:00, beside Chaweng Lake opposite Central Samui. Si Khao Night Market (17:00–23:00) and the Central Samui market run daily. Outdoor stalls are often rained off in November and December.
Is Chaweng too noisy for families? | Central Chaweng, around Soi Green Mango and ARKbar, is loud until about 02:00. North Chaweng is quiet at night and has the gentlest water, so families who want Chaweng's convenience usually stay there.
Where is the nearest hospital to Chaweng? | Three private hospitals are in or next to Chaweng: Samui International Hospital at the north end of Beach Road (24-hour emergency room), Thai International Hospital opposite Lotus's and Bangkok Hospital Samui on the ring road near Chaweng Noi (+66 77 429 500). For an ambulance call 1669.
~Chaweng or Lamai: which is better? | Chaweng for a first visit, nightlife and a short airport transfer; Lamai for better value (mid-range 1,500–3,500 THB against 2,000–4,500) and a calmer pace. Both are on the east coast and both get red flags October–December.
