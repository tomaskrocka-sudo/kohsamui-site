What is the wai and should tourists do it? | The wai is the Thai greeting: palms pressed together at chest height with a slight bow. Return a wai from adults who wai you, but you don't need to wai children, vendors or staff serving you; a smile and a nod is polite.
What should I not do in Thailand? | Don't criticise the monarchy, touch anyone's head, point your feet at people or Buddha images, climb on Buddha images, lose your temper in public, wear swimwear away from the beach or bring a vape. Most other mistakes are forgiven with a smile.
What should I wear in Koh Samui? | Swimwear on the beach and by the pool, and light clothes that cover the torso everywhere else. At temples and official offices, cover your shoulders and knees; carry a sarong or light trousers for temple visits.
Is it illegal to criticise the Thai king? | Yes. Section 112 of the Criminal Code makes it a crime to defame, insult or threaten the King, Queen, Heir-apparent or Regent, with 3–15 years in prison per offence, and it is interpreted broadly, including comments online.
Can I touch a monk? | Women must not touch a monk or hand anything directly to one; place it on a cloth or table, or ask a man to pass it on. Men can hand items over respectfully, and everyone should give monks a seat on boats and buses.
Do Thais expect tips? | Not as a rule. Many hotels and restaurants add a 10% service charge; otherwise rounding up or leaving small change is normal, and tips for good service from massage therapists, guides and boat crews are appreciated.
What festivals happen on Koh Samui? | The biggest are Loy Krathong (24 November 2026), Chinese New Year at Nathon and Maenam (6 February 2027), Songkran with water fights in Chaweng, Lamai and Nathon (13–15 April 2027) and the Chak Phra temple-float procession through Nathon around the end of Buddhist Lent in October.
Is vaping illegal in Thailand? | Yes. Importing, possessing and using vapes and e-cigarettes is illegal, with no exceptions for tourists or personal use. Visitors have been fined and detained, so leave them at home.
What is a spirit house? | A small shrine on a post outside homes, hotels and shops, built for the guardian spirits of the land. Owners leave daily offerings of flowers, food, incense and drinks; don't touch them or use the shrine as a photo prop.
Can I buy alcohol on Buddhist holidays in Thailand? | Shops, including 7-Eleven, can't sell alcohol on the main Buddhist holy days such as Makha Bucha and Visakha Bucha, but hotels, licensed bars and entertainment venues may still serve. On other days shop sales run 11:00–24:00.
