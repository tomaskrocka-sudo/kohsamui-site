Why go: shopping with a local who speaks Thai

Koh Samui's shops are spread around the island, and many of the better local ones are not on the main beach roads. This private trip gives you an air-conditioned minibus, a driver and an English-speaking guide for four hours, with the stops chosen by you: Chaweng's malls, tailors, latex mattress and pillow showrooms, jewellery, beauty products, furniture and homeware, or local markets. The guide translates, explains what is fair to pay and can arrange shipping for bulky items.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: villa groups, families and anyone furnishing a home or buying bigger items.
  • Choose something else if you only want souvenirs: the walking street markets are easier and more fun in the evening.
  • Want malls only? See our shopping mall guide; a taxi may be enough.

Good to know

  • Commission shops: some tour drivers in Thailand earn commission from shops. Give the guide a clear list and don't let anyone pressure you. Never buy gems as an "investment".
  • VAT refund: shops displaying the VAT Refund for Tourists sign can issue a form for purchases over 2,000 THB in one shop on one day; keep your passport with you.
  • Payment: markets and small shops prefer cash; bigger stores take cards.
  • Shipping: get a written quote and receipt for anything shipped home.

Planning your trip

Send us your shopping list when you enquire, so the guide can plan an efficient route and avoid crossing the island twice. Mornings are quieter in the malls; tailors usually need two or three days for a fitting, so visit them early in your holiday. For furniture and large items, ask for a shipping quote in writing before you pay.
