Why go: reach the Gulf's best sites in comfort

From Samui, the best diving is at Koh Tao, Sail Rock and Chumphon Pinnacle, all 1–1.5 hours away by fast boat. This shared trip uses a 47 ft triple-engine speedboat, so you spend more time in the water and less time crossing. Groups are capped at 16 guests, with no more than 4 divers per instructor. Snorkellers ride the same boat and are taken to shallower reef at each stop.

Each site has its own character. Sail Rock is a pinnacle rising from about 40 m, famous for its vertical Chimney and big schools of fish. It is for certified divers only. Chumphon Pinnacle is deeper still and is where whale sharks turn up most often. Koh Tao has the widest choice of easy sites for beginners and snorkellers. Ang Thong returns to the rotation on 16 December.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: solo divers and couples, first-timers who want to try scuba, and mixed groups where some dive and some snorkel.
  • Choose something else if: you only want to snorkel and see Koh Nang Yuan. Our Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan day trip is cheaper and includes the viewpoint. For a group of 3–4 divers who want to pick the site, compare the private dive charter.
  • Prone to seasickness: this is open water. Take tablets before breakfast.

Good to know

  • Season: March to September usually has the best visibility. October to December has the most cancellations and rough crossings.
  • Flying: wait at least 18 hours after diving before you fly.
  • Kit: bring your own mask if you have one that fits well. Everything else is supplied.
