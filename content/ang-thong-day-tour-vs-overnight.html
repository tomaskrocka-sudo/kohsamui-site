Book a day tour (speedboat 1,800 THB, big boat 1,500 THB) if you have one free day and want the highlights: the Emerald Lake, the Wua Talab viewpoint, kayaking and snorkelling. Stay overnight (tent 3,600 THB, bungalow 4,200 THB) to have Koh Wua Talab almost to yourself after the day boats leave at about 15:30, with sunset and sunrise from the viewpoint; we book the park accommodation and take care of everything. All Ang Thong bookings get a full refund 48 hours or more before departure and none under 48 hours, and the park is closed from 1 November to 15 December.

The verdict

  • Day tour if you have one day, travel with young children, need air-conditioning to sleep, or just want the classic photos. Choose the speedboat for a shorter day or the big boat for a steadier ride.
  • Overnight if you are a couple, a photographer, a keen walker or a family with older children, and you would trade a hot shower and air-conditioning for an empty beach at dusk and the viewpoint at dawn.
  • Bungalow over tent if you want a proper bed and a fan; tent if you like camping and want to save 600 THB per adult.

What the overnight includes

You stay on Koh Wua Talab, the park's headquarters island, which has the ranger station, the main beach, the viewpoint trail, the park restaurant, the bungalows and the campsite. It is the only island in the park where visitors can sleep.

  • Hotel transfers from most areas of Koh Samui on both days
  • Shared speedboat out on day 1 and back on day 2
  • The full day tour on day 1 with an English and Thai-speaking guide: Emerald Lake, Koh Wua Talab, kayaking and snorkelling
  • Thai buffet lunch on day 1, drinking water and soft drinks on the boat
  • Mask and snorkel, and kayaks on day 1 when the sea allows
  • One night in a tent (with sleeping pad, sleeping bag, blanket, pillow, mosquito net and torch) or a park bungalow (beds, fan, bedding and towels)
  • Booking the park accommodation for you
  • Life jackets and basic accident insurance (ages 3 months to 75)
  • National park fee: 300 THB per foreign adult, 150 THB per child aged 3–14, cash
  • Day 1 dinner, day 2 breakfast and day 2 lunch at the park restaurant
  • Remote-area transfers (Lipa Noi, Nathon, Taling Ngam, Bang Por, Bang Kao, Thong Krut and similar): 400 THB per person return

Why not book the park yourself? The park's own rates are low (600 THB for a two-person bungalow, 80 THB per person to camp), but the national parks' online booking system was down for long stretches in 2026, phone booking needs Thai, bungalows are limited and allocated by the park, and you still need a boat there and back on two different days. Our overnight trips solve all of that in one booking.

Two-day timeline

Times are approximate and depend on the sea; your pickup time arrives by email and WhatsApp the evening before.

Day 1

  1. 07:30–08:30 Hotel pickup.
  2. 09:00 Speedboat departs; about 1 hour to the park.
  3. ~10:00 Park entry; pay the park fee in cash.
  4. 10:15–11:15 Emerald Lake on Koh Mae Ko.
  5. 11:30–15:00 Day tour on Koh Wua Talab with Thai buffet lunch, kayaking and snorkelling.
  6. 15:30 The day boats leave; you stay. Check in to your tent or bungalow.
  7. 17:00–18:00 Sunset from the beach or the viewpoint.
  8. 18:00–19:00 Dinner at the park restaurant (it closes at 19:00).
  9. 21:00 Electricity off; torches out.

Day 2

  1. 05:30–06:00 Optional sunrise climb to the viewpoint with a torch.
  2. 07:00–08:30 Breakfast at the park restaurant.
  3. 08:30–10:30 Kayaking and snorkelling before the day boats arrive, weather permitting.
  4. 10:30–12:00 Beach time or a forest walk.
  5. 12:00–13:00 Lunch at the park restaurant.
  6. 13:00–15:00 Free time; pack up and meet the guide on the beach.
  7. ~15:00 Boat back from Koh Wua Talab.
  8. 16:00–16:30 Back on Koh Samui, then hotel drop-off.

Neither option has air-conditioning or hot water, and power runs only until 21:00, so charge your phone and power bank in the afternoon. Nights are warm: average lows on Samui stay around 24–26 °C all year, but a light layer helps on a windy December or January night.

Who the overnight suits

The overnight is the best way to see Ang Thong if you value the place over comfort. The viewpoint at sunset and sunrise, without the day-tour crowds, is the main reason to stay, and morning kayaking on a still sea before 10:00 is the second. Photographers get two golden hours. Couples get a quiet beach and a night sky away from Samui's lights.

It is not for everyone. Skip it if you need air-conditioning to sleep, want nightlife, or are travelling with toddlers who need a routine. Don't count on buying anything besides meals and drinks at the park restaurant; no alcohol is sold in the park, and fires are not allowed. Children can stay overnight; children's life jackets are always provided and worn on the boat. We suggest it for children who can manage the viewpoint stairs and sleep without air-conditioning.

What to pack for the night

  • Cash: park fee plus about three meals at the park restaurant
  • Torch or headlamp (one torch comes with the tent kit) for the sunrise climb
  • Insect repellent and long sleeves for the evening
  • Power bank: electricity stops at 21:00
  • Shoes with grip for the viewpoint, and sandals for the beach
  • Swimwear, rash vest and reef-safe sunscreen without oxybenzone, octinoxate, 4-MBC or butylparaben
  • A small towel (bungalow guests get towels) and toiletries
  • A light layer for the night and a dry bag for the boat
  • Any medication you need: there is no pharmacy on the island

Weather and the closure

February to April gives the calmest crossings, the clearest snorkelling and dry nights, and is the best time to stay overnight. May to September is good on most days, with occasional afternoon storms. The park closes every year from 1 November to 15 December and reopens on 16 December; December to February is peak season, when park quotas apply and bungalows go first, so book early. If we cancel because the sea is unsafe on either day, you choose a full refund or a new date. Read more in the Koh Samui weather guide.
