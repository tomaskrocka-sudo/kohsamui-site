Is January a good time to visit Koh Samui? | Yes. January averages about 127 mm of rain over 8 days, with highs of 29 °C and a 28 °C sea, and Ang Thong is open. The catches are peak-season prices and a few windy days, mostly early in the month, when the east coast gets swell and some boat trips are moved.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in January? | About 127 mm on 1991–2020 averages, over about 8 days with 1 mm or more. That is roughly double February's rain but less than half of December's. Most January days are dry and sunny; the rain tends to come with short windy spells at the tail of the north-east monsoon.
Is the sea rough around Koh Samui in January? | Mostly not, but it can be for a day or three. Typical daily highest waves on the east coast are about 0.8 m, with some rough days, and on 8 January 2026 a strong wind warning closed Ang Thong for a day. The west and south coasts stay more sheltered, and Pig Island trips from Thong Krut rarely lose a day.
Should I book Ang Thong in advance for January? | Yes. December to February is Ang Thong's peak season and park quotas limit visitor numbers, so book early, especially for the limited park bungalows on the overnight trip. Plan the trip early in your stay so a windy day can be moved. The speedboat day tour costs 1,800 THB plus the 300 THB park fee in cash.
When is the Full Moon Party in January 2027? | Friday 22 January 2027, on Haad Rin, Koh Phangan. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup from most areas, and runs subject to safe seas. Entry, about 200 THB, is paid in cash at the beach. We re-check every party date about 30 days ahead.
Can you swim on Chaweng and Lamai in January? | Usually, yes. January to September is the best season for swimming on the east coast, as the monsoon surf eases. Windy spells can still bring swell and red flags, especially early in the month, so check the flag before you go in and never swim when it is red.
Is January high season on Koh Samui? | Yes. Peak rates, 30–80% above shoulder-season prices, run from mid-December to February, and 20 December to 5 January is the busiest fortnight of the year. Flights off the island on 1–2 January fill up first. Book rooms, flights and Ang Thong dates early.
Is January or February better for Koh Samui? | February, by a small margin. It has about half January's rain (64 mm over 4 days against 127 mm over 8) and the calmest sea of the year, at the same peak prices. January has fuller waterfalls and the tail-end windy spells; February has Chinese New Year on 6 February 2027.
Does the Koh Tao day trip run in January? | Yes, when the sea allows. The Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan speedboat trip (2,200 THB plus a 250 THB Nang Yuan fee) crosses the most open water of any of our trips, and from November to January it is the one most likely to be moved for wind. Book it early in your stay.
