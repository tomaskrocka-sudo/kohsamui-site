What time is the Nikki Beach Koh Samui Sunday brunch? | Amazing Sundays brunch runs every Sunday from 12:00 to 16:30 at the Beach Club, with live cooking stations and resident DJs. The resort's restaurant page gives 12:00–16:00, so arrive by 12:30 and book ahead from December to April.
How much is the Nikki Beach Samui brunch? | The resort doesn't publish the 2026 price online. The last published rate we found was 1,990++ THB per adult, 990++ THB for children aged 5–12 and free for under-4s (2024); "++" adds 10 % service and 7 % VAT, so confirm the current price when you book.
Do you have to pay to get into Nikki Beach Koh Samui? | There is no entry fee. Day beds and loungers carry a minimum spend that is prepaid when you book and redeemable on food and drink; one guest reported 7,000 THB in April 2026. Hotel guests pay half the standard minimum, and Samui residents get 20 % off with a resident ID.
Can children go to Nikki Beach Koh Samui? | The Beach Club itself is adults only (16+). Children are welcome in the restaurant, at Escape and, for hotel guests, at the 75 m main pool and the Little Nikki kids' club, which runs daily.
What are Nikki Beach Koh Samui's opening hours? | The Restaurant and Beach Club open daily from 11:00 to 19:00, and the Escape restaurant serves dinner daily from 18:00 to 23:00. It is not a late-night venue: for parties after dark, Chaweng is 35–45 minutes away.
What happens at Nikki Beach on a Friday night? | Escape hosts a BBQ dinner and fire show every Friday from 18:00 to 21:00. Book a table, and arrange your ride home in advance because taxis are scarce on the west coast at night.
Does Nikki Beach Koh Samui still have a Saturday party? | Older guides list a Saturday "Sounds of Sunshine" party, but it isn't on the resort's own 2026 programme, which lists only the Sunday brunch and Friday BBQ as weekly events. Check with the resort before planning a Saturday visit.
What is on at Nikki Beach for Christmas and New Year 2026? | Christmas Eve dinner on 24 December (18:00–22:30), Christmas Day brunch on 25 December from 12:00, Bazaar by the Beach on 26 December (12:00–22:00), a New Year's Eve gala dinner on 31 December (18:00–01:00) and New Year's Day brunch on 1 January 2027 from 12:00. Prices had not been published in early November 2026.
How do I get to Nikki Beach from Chaweng? | It is about 28 km, or 35–45 minutes by car, via the ring road and Nathon. Grab is unreliable on the west coast after sunset, so most visitors book a private driver for the return; we arrange drivers on WhatsApp at +66 63 704 0888.
Can you swim at Nikki Beach Koh Samui? | Yes, at high tide. Lipa Noi is calm and very shallow, with water shallow for up to 100 m, and at low tide the sea retreats over wide sand flats. The beach club also has a pool for over-16s.
