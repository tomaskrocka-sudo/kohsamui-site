What is the best night market on Koh Samui? | Fisherman's Village Walking Street in Bophut, every Friday from about 17:00 to 23:00, is the biggest and best for food, crafts and atmosphere. For cheap local food every night, the Lamai Night Market and the Nathon Night Market are better value. Chaweng Walking Street by Chaweng Lake runs Thursday to Saturday.
~What day is the Fisherman's Village market? | The main Fisherman's Village Walking Street is on Friday evening, from about 17:00 to 23:00, along Bophut's beach road. Smaller markets run there on Monday and Wednesday evenings, and the Free Wave Market by the village sign is open daily from about 16:00 to midnight.
Is there a night market every day on Koh Samui? | Yes. The Lamai Night Market, Lamai Seafood Market, Nathon Night Market, Si Khao and Central Samui in Chaweng, the Free Wave Market in Bophut, Plaza The Green Market in Bang Rak and J&J Street Food in Maenam all open daily, usually from about 16:00 or 17:00. Rain can close outdoor stalls from October to December.
~When is the Chaweng walking street? | Chaweng Walking Street runs on Thursday, Friday and Saturday evenings beside Chaweng Lake, opposite Central Samui, from about 16:00–17:00 until 22:00–23:00. It is mostly food stalls, with some clothes and souvenirs. The Central Samui market next door has stalls every day.
Is the Lamai Sunday walking street still running? | It is uncertain. Some 2026 guides still list a Lamai Sunday market (one says Wednesday and Sunday), while a careful local source reported in late 2025 that it had disappeared after the pandemic. Ask your hotel before you go. The daily Lamai Night Market before the river bridge is a reliable alternative.
How much cash should I bring to a Samui night market? | Bring 300–500 THB per person for dinner, a dessert and a drink at Fisherman's Village, or 100–250 THB at Lamai and Nathon, plus extra for shopping. Almost all stalls are cash only, and small notes help. Foreign-card ATMs charge 220–250 THB per withdrawal.
Is market food safe to eat on Koh Samui? | Generally yes. Choose busy stalls where food is cooked to order and served hot, and where locals are eating. Be careful with seafood that has been sitting out, raw salads in the heat and ice of unknown origin; most markets use factory-made tube ice. Tell vendors about allergies, especially peanuts and shellfish.
Are Koh Samui's markets open when it rains? | Usually, but heavy downpours can close outdoor stalls, especially from October to December during the north-east monsoon. Rain often passes within an hour, so markets can reopen later the same evening. Central Samui in Chaweng, with covered areas and the mall next door, is the safest bet on a wet night.
