Why go: a flexible big-group speedboat

Imagination is a renovated triple-engine speedboat that takes up to 40 guests, with paddle boards on board and a menu of half-day and full-day routes. Its strength is flexibility: as well as the standard island runs, you can book an "around Samui" day and decide the bays and beaches with the captain.

What you'll do

Half days either circle part of Samui's coast for swimming and snorkelling stops or cross to Koh Tan and Koh Madsum. Full days add Koh Phangan's beaches, Ang Thong Marine Park from 16 December, with lunch on Koh Phaluay, or Koh Tao and the Koh Nang Yuan viewpoint.

Who it suits

  • Groups of 15–40: company outings, weddings, school and sports groups.
  • Families who want paddle boards and a relaxed beach day at their own pace.

Who should choose something else

  • Big groups wanting lunch included: Sao is similar in size and includes lunch.
  • Couples and small groups: Marine Apple costs less.

Good to know

  • Cash: bring enough for the whole group's park fees; a group of 30 on the Ang Thong day pays about 9,000 THB in fees alone.
  • Weather: from October to December the captain may switch to sheltered routes.
  • Safety: life jackets are on board for adults and children; speedboats are not advised in pregnancy.

Before you book

Tell us your group size and ages, your preferred route and whether you need transfers on Samui or Koh Phangan. We confirm start times and the pier with the captain, since they vary by route, and add the extra-guest charges for groups above 10. For the Ang Thong day, decide in advance between the chicken and seafood lunch sets on Koh Phaluay, so the restaurant can prepare enough for a large group.
