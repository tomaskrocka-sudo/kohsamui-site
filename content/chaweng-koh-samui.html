Chaweng is Koh Samui's busiest beach town, on the east coast 10–15 minutes from the airport. It has the island's best-known stretch of fine white sand, most of its restaurants, bars and shops (including the Central Samui mall) and the nightlife around Soi Green Mango and ARKbar. North Chaweng is the quietest part and the best for swimming. From October to December the surf builds and red flags go up, so check the flag before you swim.

The beach: north, central and south

Chaweng is one long bay of fine, pale sand facing the open Gulf. Figures of 5–7 km are quoted for its length; you can walk it end to end in about an hour and a half, wading round a few resort sea walls at high tide. The sea is clear and warm (28–30 °C) and in the dry season the water is often flat in the morning, with a light onshore breeze from late morning.

North Chaweng

The north end is the part of Chaweng we send families to. The sand shelves so gently that children can paddle far out on calm days, and the beach is calmer in feel because most of the frontage is resort gardens rather than bars. Samui International Hospital is at the north end of Beach Road, and the airport is a few minutes away.

Central Chaweng

Central Chaweng is the Samui most people picture: sunbeds, beach bars, fire shows every evening and the busiest water-sports operators. ARKbar Beach Club is here, with DJs and free fire shows nightly, and the Soi Green Mango clubs are a short walk inland. It is lively, often loud until late, and the best place to stay if nightlife matters more than sleep.

South Chaweng

South of the centre, the bars thin out and the beach runs down to the rocky point that separates Chaweng from Chaweng Noi. It is a reasonable compromise: a 10–15 minute walk to the centre, less noise at night.

Swimming and safety at Chaweng

From January to September Chaweng is one of the best swimming beaches on Samui. From October to December it is one of the most dangerous. The north-east monsoon drives surf straight onto the beach and creates rip currents, and lifeguards and hotels raise red flags. Drownings here have followed ignored flags. Winter wind surges can bring the same conditions outside the monsoon: on 19 February 2026, waves of 1–2 m closed the water along Chaweng and Lamai and swimming was banned island-wide.

  • Red flag: do not swim. Not even to your waist.
  • Caught in a rip: float, raise an arm, swim parallel to the beach, then come in.
  • Jellyfish: box jellyfish are rare but present in Samui waters. Avoid swimming at dusk and at night. First aid is vinegar for at least 30 seconds, no rubbing, no fresh water; call 1669.
  • Rocks: keep off the rocks at the Lad Koh viewpoint south of Chaweng Noi in big surf; a tourist was swept off them in November 2024.
  • Jet skis: photograph the craft before you ride, agree the price and time in writing, and never leave your passport as a deposit. See Koh Samui jet ski.
  • Belongings: don't leave phones on sunbeds while you swim.

Chaweng Lake, Central Samui and the walking street

Inland from Beach Road, the ring road (Route 4169) runs past Chaweng Lake. This is the everyday side of Chaweng: the Central Samui mall (shops, restaurants and the square where the free New Year's Eve countdown is held), the Lotus's hypermarket, banks and the night markets.

Eating and nightlife

Chaweng has more restaurants than anywhere else on Samui, from 60–80 THB pad thai at the markets to resort fine dining. A market dinner with a drink costs about 300–500 THB a person. Michelin-listed Thai restaurants in Chaweng have included Kapi Sator and Phensiri. [check]

Nightlife

  • Soi Green Mango: the club lane, with The Green Mango Club open nightly about 21:00–04:00 and busiest after midnight.
  • ARKbar Beach Club: on the sand in central Chaweng, open daily about 10:00–02:00, with free fire shows and DJs nightly and pool parties often on Wednesdays and Fridays (14:00–19:00).
  • Bar Solo: live cover bands most nights on Beach Road.
  • SEEN Beach Club (Avani Chaweng): Friday party, Saturday pool party and Sunday brunch.

Starz Cabaret has been closed since 2021 and the old Chaweng Stadium no longer stages Muay Thai. Full venue run-down in Chaweng nightlife and Koh Samui beach clubs. Alcohol may be sold 11:00–24:00 in shops since 29 May 2026; licensed bars keep their own hours.

Muay Thai

Both of Samui's regular fight stadiums are in Chaweng: Samui International Muay Thai Stadium (Tuesday, Friday and Sunday) and Phetchbuncha Stadium (Monday, Wednesday and Saturday). Fights start about 21:00. Tickets cost about 1,500 THB, 2,000 THB ringside and 2,500 THB VIP. There are no fights on Thursdays. See Muay Thai fight nights.

Traffic and getting around

Chaweng Beach Road is one-lane congestion from late afternoon until late at night. Walk where you can, and use the ring road to get across town.

  • Grab: best coverage on the island; a short hop costs about 80–120 THB.
  • Songthaews: to Lamai about 30–40 THB for locals, 50–60 THB is a fair tourist price; to Bophut 40–50 (60–80 tourist). Mostly finished by 20:00–22:00, cash only.
  • Taxis: no meters; agree the fare first. Airport to Chaweng is about 400–600 THB.
  • Scooters: police checkpoints run on Chaweng Lake Road. The helmet fine is up to 2,000 THB per person since 1 June 2025, and you need an International Driving Permit with the motorcycle category for your insurance to pay. See motorbike rental.

Should you stay in Chaweng?

Stay in Chaweng if it is your first visit, if you want to walk to restaurants and bars, or if you have a short stay and want to be close to the airport. Skip it if you want quiet evenings or a village feel; look at Choeng Mon, Bophut or Maenam instead.

  • Budget: guesthouses and hostels from about 500–950 THB, mostly inland of Beach Road.
  • Mid-range: about 2,000–4,500 THB for a double in the shoulder season, more on the sand.
  • Luxury: beachfront resorts from about 10,000 THB a night. Well-known names include Avani Chaweng and SALA Samui Chaweng Beach.
  • Peak season (mid-December to mid-January, July–August) adds 30–80 %.

For families, noise is the main issue: central Chaweng can be loud until 02:00, while North Chaweng is quiet at night. Compare areas in where to stay on Koh Samui and our honest head-to-head Chaweng or Lamai.

Things to do near Chaweng

  • Chaweng Noi and the Lad Koh viewpoint: 3–4 km south on the ring road.
  • Coral Cove and Silver Beach: small coves for snorkelling, 10–15 minutes south. See Coral Cove.
  • Big Buddha and Wat Plai Laem: 15 minutes north. See Big Buddha.
  • Lamai, Hin Ta and Hin Yai: 20–25 minutes south.
  • Elephant sanctuary: Samui Elephant Sanctuary (no riding, no bathing) has a site at Chaweng Noi; see elephant sanctuary.
