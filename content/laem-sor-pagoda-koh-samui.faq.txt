Where is Laem Sor Pagoda? | On Koh Samui's south coast at the end of Bang Kao beach, off Route 4170 between Bang Kao village and Thong Krut; GPS 9.4117, 99.9690.
Is Laem Sor Pagoda free? | Yes, entry to Laem Sor Pagoda and its grounds is free; donation boxes support the temple.
What are Laem Sor Pagoda's opening hours? | The temple is open daily 07:00–18:00; late afternoon gives the best light on the gold tiles.
What should I wear at Laem Sor Pagoda? | Cover shoulders and knees, even though you are on a beach, and take your shoes off in the shrine hall.
Can you walk up to Khao Chedi from Laem Sor? | Yes, the white chedi on the hill a few hundred metres west is reached by a short but steep climb; go early or late, with water and proper shoes.
How old is Laem Sor Pagoda? | The first chedi on the site dates from around 1907; the seaside gold-tiled chedi was built after lightning damaged the hilltop one in 1967.
Is Laem Sor Pagoda real gold? | No, the chedi is covered in yellow-glazed tiles that look gold in sunlight.
Can you swim at Laem Sor? | The shore in front of the chedi is shallow and rocky; swim at Bang Kao at high tide with water shoes, and don't change in the temple grounds.
Is Laem Sor near the Pig Island pier? | Yes, Thong Krut pier, where Pig Island boats leave, is a few kilometres west, so the two fit easily into one day.
How long should I spend at Laem Sor Pagoda? | About 30–45 minutes for the chedi and halls, or 1–1.5 hours if you climb to Khao Chedi.
