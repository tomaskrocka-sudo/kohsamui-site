Do I need any yoga experience to join a retreat? | No. Most Samui yoga retreats are open to all levels and begin with gentle hatha or yin classes, and teachers offer easier variations. Tell the host about injuries in advance, and choose a programme with one or two classes a day rather than three if you are new.
How much does a wellness retreat on Koh Samui cost? | In 2026, from donations at Dipabhavan's silent courses and about US$405 for a 7-night monk-led retreat, to about US$654 for 3 nights at Samahita, about US$1,030 for a week at Vikasa, and US$1,836–5,000 at Kamalaya or Absolute Sanctuary depending on programme and length.
What is the best month for a retreat on Koh Samui? | February to April for dry weather and calm sea, with March the safest bet; May to September is good value. October to December is the rainy season, but covered shalas and indoor programmes keep running and prices are lowest.
Which area of Koh Samui is best for a retreat? | Choeng Mon and Bophut if you want to be near the airport and restaurants, the cliffs near Coral Cove for sea-view yoga, the Lamai hills for forest silence, and Laem Set or Laem Sor in the south for the most secluded setting, about 45 minutes from the airport.
Are meals and transfers included? | Meals are included in most residential retreats (Samahita includes buffet meals; Vikasa's retreats include brunch and dinner; Kamalaya has included breakfast in room rates since April 2026 with fuller boards on some programmes). Airport transfers are often extra, so ask for an itemised quote.
Can I do a detox programme with a medical condition? | Only after talking to your own doctor. Fasting, juice diets, colon treatments and heat therapies can be risky with diabetes, heart, kidney or liver disease, pregnancy, eating disorders or regular medication. Tell the resort's consultant everything at your first appointment.
How do deposits and cancellations work? | Each retreat host sets its own deposit and refund terms, which we send you in writing before you pay. Day tours you book with us refund in full 48 hours or more before departure and 50% at 24–48 hours; Ang Thong tours refund in full 48 hours or more before. If we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.
Can I combine a retreat with tours or a boat day? | Yes. The half-day Pig Island speedboat and the Pig Island sunset tour run every day of the year and fit a free afternoon, and from 16 December a full day at Ang Thong Marine Park is possible. We can arrange transfers from your retreat.
What visa do I need for a month-long retreat or teacher training? | Most nationalities get 30 days visa-free since 15 September 2026, extendable once by 30 days on Samui for 1,900 THB, which covers a 200-hour training. For longer stays, look at the Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) and check with the school whether its course qualifies.
