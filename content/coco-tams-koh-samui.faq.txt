What time is the fire show at Coco Tam's? | There are two shows every night, at about 19:15 and 21:00, on the sand in front of the beanbags. Some 2026 listings give the second show at 21:30, so check on the day if you are coming for the late one.
Do you have to pay to watch the Coco Tam's fire show? | No. There is no ticket or entry fee. You can sit on a beanbag and order drinks, or watch from the beach; only some booked or front tables carry a minimum spend.
What time should I arrive at Coco Tam's to get a beanbag? | About 17:30 for a good spot for the 19:15 show, and earlier on Fridays and in high season. Beanbags are first come, first served, and there are more than 200 of them.
Is there a minimum spend at Coco Tam's? | Not on most beanbags. Some front and booked tables have one: guests reported 1,000 THB per person for a front section and 2,500 THB per person for a booked evening table in 2026. Ask before you sit.
How much are cocktails at Coco Tam's? | About 250–400 THB. That is several times the price at the Friday market's pop-up bars (about 70–99 THB), but you are paying for the seat and the show.
Does Coco Tam's take credit cards? | The bar does, with a 3 % surcharge reported, but there are no card machines on the beach and the beach area has taken baht cash only. Bring cash.
Is Coco Tam's good for children? | Yes, especially the 19:15 show. Keep children seated well back from the performers and away from the front row, which also smells of fire fuel.
What are Coco Tam's opening hours? | Daily from about 12:00 to midnight. Some listings show a 13:00 opening or a 01:00 close, and the fire shows run at about 19:15 and 21:00.
Is Coco Tam's busy on Friday? | Very. The Fisherman's Village Walking Street runs from about 17:00 to 23:00, the beach road closes from about 16:30 and parking runs out by 18:00. Come by Grab or taxi, or choose a Monday to Thursday night.
How far is Coco Tam's from Chaweng? | About 9 km, or 15–20 minutes by car. Grab costs about 150–250 THB; ask to be dropped at The Wharf, which is close to Coco Tam's.
