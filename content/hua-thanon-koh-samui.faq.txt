When is the Hua Thanon fish market open? | It is listed as open daily 07:00–18:00, but the boats' catch is freshest and most plentiful between 07:00 and 09:00 and much of it is sold by midday.
Can tourists visit the Hua Thanon mosque? | The Koh Samui Central Mosque (Masjid Nurul Ihsan, built 1984) is a working mosque: you can admire it from outside, and you may be invited in outside prayer times, but always ask first, remove your shoes and never enter during prayers.
What should I wear in Hua Thanon? | Cover your shoulders and knees anywhere in the village, avoid swimwear away from the beach, and women should carry a scarf to cover their hair if visiting the mosque.
Where is the Guan Yu statue on Koh Samui? | The 16 m bronze Guan Yu statue stands on the hill just above Hua Thanon at GPS 9.4454, 100.0244; it is open 09:00–17:00 and free.
Is there a swimming beach at Hua Thanon? | No, the shore is a working fishing beach with boats and a shallow, muddy-stony bottom; swim at central Lamai, 5–10 minutes north, instead.
How far is Hua Thanon from Lamai? | About 3.6 km by road, 5–10 minutes by scooter or Grab (about 100–150 THB).
Can I take photos at the Hua Thanon market? | Photos of the market and boats are generally fine, but ask before photographing people, especially women and children, and don't fly drones over the village.
Is the food in Hua Thanon halal? | Yes, the village is Thai-Muslim and its stalls serve halal food such as khao yam, roti with curry and southern fish curries, typically 40–70 THB a dish.
Can I buy alcohol in Hua Thanon? | There are no bars in the village and drinking in the street is disrespectful here; buy and drink alcohol in Lamai instead.
Is Hua Thanon worth visiting on a short trip? | Yes, if you like local life: an hour at the market before 09:00, combined with the Guan Yu Shrine and the Red Temple nearby, makes an easy, free morning from Lamai.
What happens in Hua Thanon during Ramadan? | Daytime food stalls are fewer and the evening market livens up around sunset; Ramadan is expected from about 8 February to 9 March 2027, so avoid eating or smoking in the street in daytime.
