Where is the best party on Koh Samui? | Chaweng. ARKbar runs a free beach fire show and DJs every night from about 20:00, the Green Mango Club on Soi Green Mango is open until about 04:00, and CLUB SEEN and Elephant Beach Club have the big weekend parties. For the biggest parties of all, take a boat to the Full Moon or Half Moon on Koh Phangan.
Is there a Full Moon Party on Koh Samui? | No. The Full Moon Party is on Haad Rin Sunrise Beach on Koh Phangan, 30–50 minutes from Samui by speedboat. Most visitors stay on Samui and take a return boat for the night; the next parties are 24 November, 24 December and 31 December 2026.
How do I get from Samui to the Full Moon Party and back? | Book a return boat before you go, because there are no public boats back in the middle of the night. Our shared speedboat costs 1,400 THB per person (1,800 THB on New Year's Eve) with hotel pickup from most areas and return boats through the night; a private speedboat for up to 10 is 35,800 THB return. Entry, about 200 THB, is paid at the gate.
What night is the big party in Chaweng? | Saturday. The clubs on Soi Green Mango are fullest, Elephant Beach Club runs DJs until about 03:00, CLUB SEEN has its weekend party and there is Muay Thai at Phetchbuncha. Friday is a close second, with an ARKbar pool party listed in the afternoon and the Fisherman's Village Walking Street to start the evening.
What time do clubs close on Samui? | Licensed clubs such as the Green Mango Club in Chaweng close at about 02:00–04:00, depending on their licence and zoning. Shops may sell alcohol only from 11:00 to 24:00, and most bars outside Chaweng wind down between midnight and 02:00.
Are there pool parties on Samui? | Yes. ARKbar in Chaweng has pool DJs every afternoon from 14:00 to 19:00, with live performers on Wednesday and Friday, CLUB SEEN at Avani Chaweng holds a weekend pool party, and Nikki Beach on the west coast has a Saturday party. Entry is usually free with a minimum spend on sunbeds, daybeds and cabanas.
~Can you buy alcohol on Buddhist holidays in Koh Samui? | Partly. On Buddhist holy-day bans, shops and 7-Eleven can't sell alcohol, but hotels, licensed bars and entertainment venues in tourist areas may still serve. There are no ban days in November or December 2026; the 2027 bans are 21 February, 20 May, 18–19 July and about 15 October.
Is Samui safe at night for partygoers? | Mostly, with sensible precautions. Watch your drink, check bar bills, use Grab or a pre-agreed taxi home, never ride a scooter after drinking, don't swim at night and book a licensed return boat for Phangan parties. Drug penalties are severe and police are active around party nights. Tourist Police: 1155.
How much does a night out on Koh Samui cost? | About 1,500–2,000 THB per person for a big night in Chaweng (drinks at 100–350 THB, street food and a ride home), and about 3,000–4,000 THB for a Full Moon night from Samui including the 1,400 THB boat, 200 THB entry, drinks and food. Prices rise 10–20 % from mid-December to February.
What's on New Year's Eve on Koh Samui? | A free countdown with concerts and fireworks at Central Samui in Chaweng, a beach party at ARKbar, fireworks along Chaweng, Fisherman's Village, Lamai, Maenam and Choeng Mon, and resort galas such as Anantara Bophut's at 14,500++ THB. Across the water, the NYE Full Moon Party; our shared boat costs 1,800 THB that night.
