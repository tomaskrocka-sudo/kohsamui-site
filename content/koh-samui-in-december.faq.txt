~Is December a good time to visit Koh Samui? | Yes, especially after mid-month. December averages about 263 mm of rain over 12 days, against about 445 mm in November, and the weather usually improves through the month. Ang Thong reopens on 16 December 2026, and Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve are both Full Moon Party nights. Prices peak from about 20 December, so book early.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in December? | About 263 mm on 1991–2020 averages, over about 12 days with 1 mm or more. That is roughly four times February's rain but about 40% less than November's. The first half of the month can still bring long grey spells; the second half is usually drier, though storms remain possible.
Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in December? | Only from 16 December. The park closes every year from 1 November to 15 December and reopens on Wednesday 16 December 2026. Our Ang Thong speedboat day tour costs 1,800 THB plus the 300 THB park fee in cash. Before the 16th, take the Pig Island speedboat tour (1,450 THB) instead.
Is early or late December better on Koh Samui? | Late December has the better weather: the rain eases, the sea calms and Ang Thong is open from the 16th. Early December is cheaper and quieter, with rooms close to low-season rates, but heavy bursts of rain are still likely. Mid-December, before the Christmas rush from about 20 December, is a good compromise.
Is the sea rough around Koh Samui in December? | It can be. December's typical daily highest waves on the east coast are about 0.9 m, with rough spells, and Chaweng and Lamai still get red flags. The sea usually calms later in the month. Pig Island trips from Thong Krut stay sheltered, while the Koh Tao run is the trip most often moved for weather.
When are the Full Moon Parties in December 2026? | Thursday 24 December, Christmas Eve, and Thursday 31 December, the New Year's Eve countdown party. Both are on Haad Rin, Koh Phangan. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person on 24 December and 1,800 THB on 31 December, with hotel pickup from most areas. Entry, about 200 THB, is paid in cash at the beach.
When do hotel prices go up on Koh Samui in December? | From mid-December, when peak rates run 30–80% above shoulder-season prices. The dearest period is from about 20 December to early January, and villas often ask for minimum stays of 4–7 nights over Christmas and New Year. Many hotels also add a compulsory New Year's Eve gala supplement.
What boat trips run in early December on Koh Samui? | Pig Island and Koh Tan from Thong Krut (speedboat 1,450 THB, sunset tour 1,550 THB) on any date, weather permitting; the Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan day trip (2,200 THB) when the sea allows; and the Half Moon Festival package (from 2,700 THB) on 3 December. Ang Thong trips start on 16 December.
Should I book Christmas and New Year on Koh Samui early? | Yes. Galas, NYE dinners and Full Moon boats for 24 and 31 December sell out by mid-December, villas book out months ahead, and flights off the island on 1–2 January were full after New Year 2025. Book Full Moon boats by early December and consider flying home on 3 January.
