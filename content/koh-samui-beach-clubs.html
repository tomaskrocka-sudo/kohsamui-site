Samui's busiest beach clubs are on Chaweng Beach (ARKbar, SEEN at Avani Chaweng and Elephant Beach Club), around Bang Rak and Plai Laem on the north-east coast (Chi Samui, Tembo, Malika and 79 Beach Club), at Coco Tam's in Fisherman's Village, at Cape Away in Maenam for its Sunday party, and at Nikki Beach in Lipa Noi on the west coast. Most charge no entry but set minimum spends on sunbeds, from about 300 THB at ARKbar. Beach Republic in Lamai has been largely closed in 2025–26, so don't plan around it.

Chaweng beach clubs

ARKbar Beach Club

ARKbar is Samui's best-known party beach club, in the middle of Chaweng Beach. The pool party runs every day from 14:00, peaking between 16:00 and 19:00, and on Wednesdays and Fridays a saxophonist, violinist and dancers join the DJ. Entry to the pool party is free. A walk-in beachfront sunbed has a 300 THB minimum spend, and full pool access with a lounger costs 500 THB per person; VIP tables are booked online. From 20:00 there is a free fire show on the beach, and from 21:00 the club is adults only, with the beach party running until about 02:00. Beer starts at about 150 THB. Busiest on Saturday nights and over Songkran and New Year.

SEEN Beach Club, Avani Chaweng

SEEN is the stylish choice in Chaweng: a pool club inside the Avani Chaweng resort with a weekly programme published in 2025 of a Friday "Ignite" party, the "SEENturday" pool party and a Sunday family brunch. Cabanas start at about 5,000 THB per group. SEEN also hosts Samui's biggest one-off events: it held the debut Zamna "On The Beach" festival on 21 February 2026 and Songkran pool parties.

Elephant Beach Club

A beach bar on Chaweng Beach with fire shows most nights and DJ nights on Saturday until about 03:00. Less polished than SEEN, cheaper than both. For the rest of the Chaweng night, see Chaweng nightlife and our Chaweng guide.

North-coast beach clubs

The bays of Bang Rak (Big Buddha Beach) and Plai Laem face north towards Koh Phangan, and the clubs here are built around the sunset. The sea is calm in the dry season but shallow; swimming is better at high tide.

Chi Samui

An upmarket club in Bang Rak with a swim-up pool bar, sunset DJ sets and occasional fire shows, open daily from about 11:00 to midnight. Reserve a sunbed in high season. It held a DJ party over Songkran 2025.

Tembo Beach Club

A laid-back club in Bang Rak with daybeds from about 500 THB and cabanas from about 1,000 THB. Sunset DJ sets are on Monday and Friday, with a tapas night on Tuesday and a cocktail happy hour on Thursday.

Malika Beach Club and 79 Beach Club

Malika is a quieter Bang Rak club for sunset sessions, open Tuesday to Sunday from about 12:00 to 22:00 (closed Monday), with weekend fire shows; expect to spend about 400–600 THB per person. 79 Beach Club opened in 2024 and sells day passes and themed nights, and it ran a free-flow New Year's Eve party in 2025.

Coco Tam's, Fisherman's Village

Coco Tam's is the beanbag beach bar at the end of Fisherman's Village in Bophut, open from about midday to midnight, with fire shows at about 19:15 and 21:00 (some nights about 19:30). Seating is first come, first served, with minimum spends on some of the best spots, and cocktails cost about 250–400 THB. On Fridays it is the natural last stop after the Fisherman's Village Walking Street.

Maenam

Maenam is Samui's quiet north-coast beach, with a long, gently shelving shoreline, and its beach-club scene is small. Cape Away Beach Bar runs a Sunday party with DJs, dancers and fire performers, open from about 10:00 to 22:00, with a more local, relaxed crowd than Chaweng. At the W Koh Samui, Woobar has a resident DJ from about 18:00 to 23:00 and cocktails from about 350 THB, smart-casual after dark. See Maenam.

West coast and sunset bars

The west coast is where the sun sets over the sea, and where the island's most glamorous beach club sits. Nikki Beach Koh Samui, at Lipa Noi, is open in 2026: its beach club runs from about 11:00 to 19:00, with a Saturday party and its well-known Sunday brunch, and it held a White Party on 28 March 2026. Cocktails cost about 300–450 THB. Book the Sunday brunch ahead, especially from December to April.

The big west and north-west coast resorts also have sunset bars open to non-guests, such as Air Bar at the InterContinental and Jahn at the Conrad, but expect resort prices and a dress code after dark. Our sunset spots guide covers free viewpoints and beaches too, and Lipa Noi covers the area.

Brunches

Sunday brunch is a Samui institution in high season. The two best-known beach-club brunches are the family brunch at SEEN Beach Club in Chaweng and the Sunday brunch at Nikki Beach in Lipa Noi. Many five-star resorts also run Sunday brunches with free-flow packages. Expect "++" pricing at resorts, children's rates at family brunches and a booking requirement from December to April. Brunch prices change by season and package, so check the current rate when you book.

Three ways to plan a beach-club day

Chaweng party day

  1. 14:00 Arrive at ARKbar as the pool party starts and claim a sunbed (300 THB minimum spend).
  2. 16:00–19:00 Peak pool-party hours; on Wednesday and Friday the live performers join the DJ.
  3. 20:00 Free fire show on the beach, then dinner on Chaweng Beach Road.
  4. Late Move on to Soi Green Mango, or Elephant Beach Club on a Saturday.

North-coast sunset

  1. 15:00 Daybed at Tembo or Chi Samui in Bang Rak; swim at high tide.
  2. 17:30 Sunset DJ set (Monday and Friday at Tembo).
  3. 19:00 Short ride to Fisherman's Village for dinner, and on a Friday the walking street.
  4. 21:00 Fire show at Coco Tam's.

West-coast Sunday

  1. 12:00 Sunday brunch at Nikki Beach in Lipa Noi, booked ahead.
  2. 15:00 Pool and beach until sunset over the sea.
  3. 18:30 Pre-booked driver home; Grab cover on the west coast is thin.

Pack swimwear, a cover-up for the restaurant areas, sun cream, sunglasses, a waterproof phone pouch, and cash or a card for the minimum spend. In the rainy season from October to December, have a plan B indoors: a sudden squall can clear a beach club in minutes, and many events are moved or cancelled at short notice.

Entry, minimum spends and dress

  • Entry: most Samui beach clubs charge no entry on normal days. Big events (DJ headliners, festivals, NYE) sell tickets or charge after a cut-off time: ARKbar's NYE 2025 countdown was free before 21:00 and 500 THB after.
  • Minimum spends: sunbeds, daybeds and cabanas carry minimum spends, from about 300 THB for a walk-in sunbed at ARKbar to 5,000 THB or more for a cabana at SEEN. The minimum is credited against food and drinks.
  • "++": prices marked "++" add 10 % service charge and 7 % VAT, so 1,000++ THB becomes 1,177 THB.
  • Drink prices: beer about 100–200 THB (from about 150 THB at ARKbar) and cocktails about 250–450 THB at the beach clubs in 2026, compared with about 80–120 THB for a beer in a Lamai bar.
  • Dress: swimwear by day; cover up for the restaurant areas and in the evening. Smart-casual at Nikki Beach, Woobar and the resort sunset bars after dark.
  • Payment: cards are accepted at the larger clubs; carry some cash for smaller bars and tips. Always pay in THB if a card machine offers a currency conversion.

Children at beach clubs

Daytime beach clubs on Samui are usually fine with children, and some actively cater for families: SEEN's Sunday brunch is billed as a family brunch. Party nights are a different matter. ARKbar is adults only from 21:00, and Thailand's legal drinking age is 20. Policies differ by club and event, so check before you go. For family-friendly ideas, see Koh Samui with kids.

Safety

  • Sun: the UV index on Samui is very high from late morning; reapply sun cream after every swim and drink water between cocktails.
  • Alcohol and swimming: don't swim in the sea after drinking, especially at night. Chaweng has rip currents and red-flag days during the north-east monsoon from October to December.
  • Jellyfish: nine of Thailand's ten recorded box-jellyfish deaths since 2002 happened off Samui and Phangan. Many hotels keep vinegar stations; pour it on a sting for at least 30 seconds and don't rub or rinse with fresh water.
  • Drinks: watch your drink and don't leave it unattended, as at any party venue.
  • Getting home: never ride a scooter after drinking. Book a Grab or taxi, and expect 20–30 % higher taxi fares after 22:00.

Arriving by boat

Turning up at a beach club by private boat sounds good, but most Samui clubs have no jetty, the north-coast bays are shallow at low tide, and the east coast has swell from October to December. Whether a boat can drop you off depends on the club, the tide and the sea on the day. If you book a private speedboat or a catamaran charter, ask us about a beach-club stop when you enquire and we will tell you honestly what is possible.
