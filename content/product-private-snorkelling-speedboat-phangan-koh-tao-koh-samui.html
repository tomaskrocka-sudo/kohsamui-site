Why go: your own boat to the best snorkelling around Samui

This charter is run by a dive team, and it shows in the stops. The guide picks reefs for the day's visibility, current and tide, not for where the big tour boats tie up. With no more than 4 guests at the base price, you get long sessions in the water and can move on when you're ready.

The four routes suit different days. Koh Phangan is the shortest crossing. Bays on its west and north coasts have coral close to the beach, so it is a good choice for families. Pig Island and Koh Rap stay in the calmer water south of Samui, with a beach stop and an optional BBQ. Ang Thong adds limestone islands and the Emerald Lake viewpoint, from 16 December. Koh Tao has the clearest water in the Gulf and the most fish, but it is also the longest and roughest crossing.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: couples and small families who want to snorkel properly, and keen snorkellers who want a guide who knows where the reef is in poor visibility.
  • Choose something else if: you are a group of 6 or more. Bigger boats often cost less per head, so compare our private speedboat guide. On a tighter budget, our shared Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan day trip is a fraction of the price.
  • Want to dive as well? Book the private scuba charter with the same team. Non-divers can snorkel from that boat.

Good to know

  • Visibility: usually best from March to September. It drops after storms and in the October–December monsoon.
  • Reef care: don't stand on coral, and use reef-safe sunscreen or a rash vest. National park rangers can fine visitors who damage reefs.
  • Seasickness: take tablets an hour before departure on the Koh Tao route, and sit towards the back of the boat.
