Why go: Sail Rock on your schedule

Sail Rock is the best single dive site within reach of Koh Samui: a granite pinnacle rising from around 40 m to just above the surface, with schooling fish, big groupers and the vertical swim-through known as the Chimney. On the shared dive boats you share it with a crowd. With your own speedboat you choose when to arrive and can time your dives to miss the busiest boats.

A 39 ft twin-engine speedboat is much faster than the big dive boats, so the ride is shorter and the day feels unhurried. A dive professional leads the dives, and lunch is on board between them.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: a group of up to four certified divers, couples where one dives and one snorkels, and photographers who want time at the site.
  • Choose something else if you are diving alone: the shared catamaran trip costs a fraction of the price.
  • Not certified? Start with our diving guide and ask us about trial dives.

Good to know

  • Experience: Sail Rock can have current and depth; dive within your training.
  • Season: visibility is best from March to September. November and December bring swell.
  • Speedboat ride: fast boats bang over chop. Sit towards the back if you have back problems.
  • Flying: leave 18–24 hours before flying after your last dive.

Planning the day

Tell us your certification level, number of logged dives and date of your last dive when you book, so the dive guide can plan profiles and decide whether a refresher is needed. Early departures usually reach Sail Rock before the larger shared boats; agree the plan with the captain the evening before. Underwater photographers should bring spare batteries, as there is no charging on board.
