How far is Samui airport from Chaweng? | About 2 km to the north edge of Chaweng and 10–15 minutes by car to most Chaweng hotels, depending on traffic on the ring road.
How much is a taxi from Samui airport? | Kiosk taxis charge fixed zone prices, roughly 400–700 THB per car for most areas, from about 300–600 THB to Chaweng up to about 600–900 THB to the west coast; a shared minibus costs 100–250 THB per person.
Can I get a Grab from Samui airport? | Grab lists the airport as a pickup point, but local taxi groups resist app pickups, so drivers often meet passengers outside the airport gate; message the driver first and expect a walk.
~Which airlines fly to Koh Samui? | In late 2026 Bangkok Airways flies every domestic route and Hong Kong, and Scoot flies from Singapore, where Bangkok Airways has paused its own flights until 28 March 2027; Thai Airways flight numbers on the Bangkok route are codeshares on Bangkok Airways aircraft.
Is there a lounge for economy passengers at Samui airport? | Yes, Bangkok Airways has long let economy passengers use its Boutique Lounge with free snacks and drinks, and economy passengers can upgrade to the Blue Ribbon Lounge for about 950 THB.
Can I fly to Koh Samui from Singapore? | Yes, Scoot flies Singapore–Samui 1–2 times a day in about 1h55, and you clear immigration at Samui airport; Bangkok Airways' own Singapore flights are paused from 25 October 2026 to 28 March 2027.
Is Samui airport open 24 hours? | No night flights are scheduled; flights run from early morning to evening, and the terminal is not set up for overnight waiting, so plan around your flight time.
Can I go straight from the airport to Koh Phangan? | Yes, the Bangrak piers and Big Buddha pier are 5–10 minutes away by taxi, with boats to Thong Sala and Haad Rin, or you can book a private speedboat at a time you choose.
Why is Samui airport privately owned? | Bangkok Airways built the airport itself and opened it in 1989, before Samui had any airport, and the airline has owned and run it ever since, which is also why it flies almost every route.
