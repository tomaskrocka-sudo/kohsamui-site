Is Koh Samui good for a honeymoon? | Yes: warm sea all year, quiet resort bays, private pool villas, couples' spas and island days by private boat, with the airport 5–15 minutes from the north-east beaches. Avoid October to December, the rainy season, and expect higher flight prices than to Phuket.
What is the best month for a Koh Samui honeymoon? | February to April for the calmest sea and least rain, with March the safest bet. May to September is also good and cheaper. November is the wettest month (about 445 mm) and Ang Thong is closed from 1 November to 15 December.
Where should honeymooners stay on Koh Samui? | Choeng Mon or Chaweng Noi for quiet, swimmable bays near the airport; Taling Ngam or Laem Yai on the west coast for sunsets and seclusion; Bophut and Fisherman's Village if you want restaurants on the doorstep, though the beach there isn't for swimming.
How much is a private boat for two on Koh Samui? | A private longtail around the Five Islands with a seafood dinner on the beach costs 7,500 THB for two. Private speedboats start at 19,000 THB per boat, a 6-hour honeymoon sunset trip on a 39 ft speedboat costs 28,000 THB, and the Ang Thong private speedboat costs 33,000 THB per boat.
Where is the best sunset on Koh Samui? | Taling Ngam on the south-west coast, where the sun drops behind the Five Islands. Lipa Noi, Nathon pier and Bang Por are close behind. In 2026 the sun sets between about 17:56 in November and 18:45 in July.
Can we see the White Lotus hotel by boat? | You can see the Laem Yai coastline, where the Four Seasons Resort used for season 3 sits, from a private boat at a respectful distance offshore. The resort is private, and landing at its beach is not part of any trip we sell.
Is Koh Samui better than Phuket for a honeymoon? | It depends on the month. Samui is quieter and more boutique, with its rain from October to December; Phuket is bigger and livelier, with its rain from May to October and cheaper international flights. From May to September Samui usually has the better weather.
Can we have a couples' massage on Koh Samui? | Yes, almost every spa offers side-by-side couples' rooms. Expect about 300–600 THB per person for an hour at a good shop and about 1,800–3,500 THB per person at a resort spa, with forest-spa packages at Tamarind Springs from 5,500 THB++.
