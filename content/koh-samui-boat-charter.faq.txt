How much is a private boat on Koh Samui? | Our charter boats start at 19,000 THB for a 30 ft speedboat (price for up to 4 guests), 20,000 THB for a sailing catamaran (up to 10), 41,000 THB for the Red Baron junk (up to 20) and 50,000 THB for a motor yacht. Longer routes and extra guests cost more; your written quote gives the exact price.
Is a Koh Samui boat charter priced per person or per boat? | Per boat. Each price covers a stated number of guests (for example 6 on most speedboats, 10 on most catamarans), and each guest above that pays a supplement shown on the quote. Children's life jackets are always provided.
Can we charter a boat to Ang Thong in November? | No. Ang Thong National Marine Park closes every year from 1 November to 15 December and reopens on 16 December. During the closure we run charters to Pig Island, Koh Tan and Koh Rap in the south-west, which are also more sheltered from the monsoon swell.
Which pier do Koh Samui charters leave from? | Most of our charter boats leave from piers on Samui's north coast, and the fixed-price Pig Island private boats leave from Thong Krut in the south-west. We send the exact pier location and meeting time with your confirmation.
Can the charter boat pick us up at our beach? | Ask us with your beach name: it depends on the beach, the tide and the sea, and many Samui beaches are too shallow and tidal for a boat to come in safely, so we normally meet you at a pier. Several of our speedboats include hotel transfers to the pier; ask when you enquire.
Is lunch included on a private charter? | On most boats lunch is extra: the boat stops at a beach restaurant where you order and pay, or we quote a catering package. The Red Baron junk includes meals and soft drinks. Water, soft drinks and fruit are included on most boats.
Can we bring our own drinks on a charter boat? | On several of our catamarans and the motor yacht Casablanca you may bring your own food and drinks free of charge. Rules differ by boat, so tell us what you plan to bring and we confirm it on the quote.
What if the weather is bad on our charter day? | The captain decides on the day. We first offer a sheltered route (usually the southern islands); if the sea is unsafe we cancel, and you choose a full refund or a new date.
Can we charter a private boat for the Full Moon Party? | Yes. Our Full Moon Party private speedboat takes up to 10 guests from Samui to Haad Rin and back at times you choose, with hotel pickup and drop-off on Samui. For larger groups we quote a bigger boat; party entry of about 200 THB per person is paid at the gate.
Do you have charter boats with toilets? | Yes: the speedboat Coco Cay, the catamarans Blue Coco and Kia Ora, and the motor yachts Casablanca and Minor Affair have toilets. Most small speedboats do not, so mention it in your enquiry if it matters.
