Why go: Samui's interior in a couple of hours

Most visitors see only Samui's coast road. A quad ride goes the other way, up dirt tracks through coconut and durian plantations and rainforest, across shallow streams and up to viewpoints over the north and east coasts. The quads are automatic, so you only need the throttle and the brakes. Everyone starts with a briefing and practice laps on a training track before heading out behind a guide.

The 1-hour ride is enough to get muddy and try a few climbs. The 2-hour ride goes further into the hills, stops at a jungle bar for a drink and, when there has been enough rain, at a small waterfall where you can cool off. For no extra cost you can ask for a personal guide instead of joining the group, which is useful for nervous drivers or families who want to go slower.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: couples, groups of friends and families with children aged 5 and over riding as passengers. No experience is needed.
  • Choose something else if: you want a longer day with lunch and Samui's highest viewpoint. The 4-hour ATV and buggy mountain tour is better value for that. For the same scenery without driving, try the jeep safari.
  • Not suitable: pregnant guests and anyone with back, neck or heart problems. The trails are rough and jolting.

Good to know

  • Insurance: many travel policies exclude quad bikes or need a motorbike licence. Check yours before you ride; accident insurance is included only for the tour itself.
  • Damage: ask the base how damage to the quad is handled before you set off, and take photos of your quad first.
  • Heat: the 10:00 start avoids the worst of the midday heat. Drink plenty of water.
  • Waterfall: the waterfall is fullest from about October to January and can be a trickle by late dry season.
