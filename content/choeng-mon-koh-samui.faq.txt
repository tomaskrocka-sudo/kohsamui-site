Is Choeng Mon good for small children? | Yes, it is our first pick for toddlers and young children: a sheltered bay with soft sand, a gentle slope and usually flat water, restaurants and toilets on the beach and the airport 5–10 minutes away. Bring shade, as umbrellas are mainly at the resorts.
Can you walk to Koh Fan Noi? | Yes, at low tide a sandbar links the south-eastern end of Choeng Mon to Koh Fan Noi and Koh Fan Yai. Wear water shoes, start back before the tide turns and stay on the sand, because the islets belong to a private resort.
Is Choeng Mon calm in November? | Often calmer than Chaweng and Lamai, because headlands shelter the bay, but strong north-east winds can still push waves in and red flags do go up. Check the flag and our live sea card before swimming.
How far is Choeng Mon from the airport? | About 5 km, or 5–10 minutes by car. An airport taxi costs roughly 400–700 THB depending on the zone; Grab is usually a little cheaper.
Choeng Mon or Chaweng: which is better? | Choeng Mon for families, honeymooners and calm water; Chaweng for nightlife, shopping and hundreds of restaurants on foot. Choeng Mon is pricier for mid-range rooms (about 3,000–7,000 THB) and quiet at night.
Are there restaurants at Choeng Mon? | Yes. Several beach restaurants and resort dining rooms line the sand, and the lane behind the beach has cheaper Thai restaurants, a 7-Eleven, bars and massage shops. For big choice, Chaweng is 15–20 minutes away.
Is there aircraft noise at Choeng Mon? | Some, because the airport is 5 km away, mostly in the daytime. It is much quieter than Bang Rak (Big Buddha Beach), which lies under the approach path.
