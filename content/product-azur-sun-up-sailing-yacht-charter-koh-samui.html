Why go: proper sailing for a small group

Most charter boats on Koh Samui are speedboats or motor catamarans. Azur Sun Up is a classic monohull sailing yacht, a 38 ft Bavaria Cruiser with a skipper and crew, two cabins and a bathroom with a shower. Under sail it heels and moves with the wind, which is exactly what sailors come for. Guests can help trim the sails or just sit back in the cockpit.

Day sails follow the Samui coast to a quiet bay for a swim, or cross to Koh Phangan for a full day. For up to four, the overnights anchor off Koh Phangan or, on the two-night trip, inside Ang Thong Marine Park.

Who it suits, and who should choose something else

  • Good for: couples and small groups who enjoy sailing, and sailors on holiday who miss being on the water.
  • Choose something else if you want food, drinks and toys included: a catamaran charter is more all-in and stays level.
  • Prone to seasickness? A monohull moves more than a catamaran; choose the sunset sail first.

Good to know

  • What's included: day trips do not include food, drinks or snorkel gear. Pack a cool bag.
  • Space: six guests is the limit on day trips and four on overnights; it is a 12 m yacht, not a party boat.
  • Weather: February to September has steadier, lighter winds; October to December can be gusty and wet.
  • Ang Thong: closed from 1 November to 15 December.

Choosing a trip

First-time sailors should start with the sunset or a half day to see how they feel on a monohull. Experienced sailors get the most from the full day to Koh Phangan, which includes a proper open-water crossing.
