Why go: a guided jet ski safari to Pig Island

Riding your own jet ski across open water to an island is a very different day from sitting on a tour boat. On this guided safari a lead guide rides ahead on his own jet ski and sets the line, the pace and the stops. You follow in convoy around the coast and out to the southern islands, with stops to swim, snorkel and meet the pigs on Koh Madsum (Pig Island), and to snorkel at Koh Tan when conditions allow. The price is 9,950 THB per jet ski, which carries a driver and one passenger, and the outing takes about 4 hours including transfers and the briefing.

The guided format is the point. Jet ski rental on Koh Samui's busy beaches has a long record of problems: no briefing, poorly maintained machines, riders too close to swimmers, and inflated damage claims at the end of the hire. On the safari you get a safety briefing of about 30 minutes with a short practice before you leave, life jackets for both riders, a planned route, a guide who knows the water and decides the safest line around each island, and accident insurance. The jet ski is photographed with you before departure, so its condition is on record.

Routes depend on the day's wind and sea. On calm days the convoy reaches Koh Tan and Koh Madsum; in rougher weather the guide may choose closer islands and sheltered bays. It is not a race. Expect a steady pace that the least confident rider in the group can follow.

Safety checklist

  • Life jackets are worn by driver and passenger for the whole ride.
  • Drivers must be 18 or older; passengers 8 or older.
  • No boat licence is needed for a guided safari, but you must follow the briefing and stay behind the guide.
  • Ask for the damage, deposit and excess terms in writing when you book, and read them before you ride. Never leave your passport as a deposit with any jet ski operator.
  • No alcohol before or during the ride.
  • The guide can end the ride or change the route at any time for safety.

Who this tour is for

  • Confident adults who want an active, adrenaline day on the water.
  • Couples and friends sharing one jet ski (driver plus passenger).
  • First-time riders: the briefing covers the controls, and the guide sets a pace beginners can follow. Tell us if it is your first time.
  • Anyone who wanted to rent a jet ski on the beach but was put off by the stories.

Who should choose another tour

  • Drivers under 18 or children under 8: the Pig Island speedboat tour or a private longtail.
  • Pregnant, or with back or neck problems: jet skis slam over waves; choose the longtail tour and check with your doctor.
  • Nervous swimmers: you may end up in the water; a boat trip is the better choice.
  • A group that wants to stay together on one boat: the Pig Island private speedboat (11,950 THB for up to 10).

What to bring

  • Swimwear with a rash vest or T-shirt over it: spray and sun at speed burn fast.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen (apply before you leave) and sunglasses with a strap. Hats blow off.
  • Cash in small notes for the Koh Madsum island fee (about 50 THB per person).
  • A dry bag for your phone. Leave valuables at your hotel.
  • Water shoes for island landings.
  • Your passport details for the booking and insurance (not the passport itself as a deposit).

Good to know

  • Sea by month: February to September is calmer, and the south and west coasts are usually smoother. October to December brings rain and windier days. Jet skis are more sensitive to swell than boats, so the guide cancels or shortens the route when it is unsafe. If we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.
  • Fitness: holding on through chop for several hours is tiring on the arms and back. Passengers get the bumps too.
  • Other water users: keep well clear of swimmers, snorkellers, longtails and fishing gear. The guide shows the line to follow near the islands.
  • The pigs: beach the jet skis where the guide shows you, well away from the animals, and don't chase, lift or feed them junk food.
  • Photography: use a strapped action camera, not a hand-held phone, while riding. Drones are not practical from a jet ski; don't try.
  • Fuel and extras: fuel for the planned route is included; check the booking confirmation for any surcharge rules.

Pickup and meeting point

We confirm the start point, your pickup time and the meeting instructions by 18:00 the day before. Hotel transfers to the jet ski base can be arranged from the main areas of Koh Samui (Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut, Maenam, Choeng Mon, Bang Rak and Plai Laem). If the road to your villa is too steep or narrow for the vehicle, we send a nearby meeting point with a map pin. Morning and afternoon starts are possible; morning water is usually calmer.
