Ang Thong Marine Park tours

Mu Ko Ang Thong National Park is a group of 42 limestone islands about 30 km west of Koh Samui. A day trip covers the Emerald Lake (Thale Nai), a saltwater lake inside Koh Mae Ko reached by a stairway, the 360° viewpoint on Koh Wua Talab (a steep 25–30 minute climb), kayaking along the cliffs and a snorkelling stop. The park fee is not in any tour price: 300 THB per foreign adult and 150 THB per child (3–14), paid in cash at the park. The park closes every year from 1 November to 15 December, so the first departure of the 2026–27 season is Wednesday 16 December 2026; you can book dates from 16 December now. Read the full Ang Thong Marine Park guide before you choose.

Speedboat day tour (1,800 THB, about 8 hours)

The Ang Thong speedboat day tour is the one most first-time visitors should pick. Pickup is 07:30–08:30, the crossing takes about an hour each way, and you are back at the pier at 16:00–16:30 and at your hotel by 16:30–17:00. Lunch, fruit, water and soft drinks, snorkel mask, kayak, guide, accident insurance and children's life jackets are included. Child price 1,400 THB.

Big boat (1,500 THB, about 10 hours)

The big boat tour visits the same stops on a larger, slower boat with a shaded deck. The crossing takes about 1.5 hours each way, so you reach the park around 10:30 and are back at the pier around 18:00. It rolls less in a chop than a speedboat, which suits people who get seasick, and it is the cheapest way into the park. Child price 1,100 THB.

VIP tour (4,500 THB)

The Ang Thong VIP tour takes only 15 guests on a big speedboat. You get space on board, less waiting at the landings and more time with the guide. Child price 4,200 THB.

Overnight: tent 3,600 THB or bungalow 4,200 THB

You join the day tour, then stay on Koh Wua Talab when the day boats leave at about 15:30 and return on the next afternoon's boat. The camping trip (3,600 THB, child 3,400 THB) and the park bungalow trip (4,200 THB, child 3,900 THB) give you the viewpoint at sunset and sunrise with almost nobody else on the island. We book the park accommodation for you.

Private speedboat (33,000 THB per boat)

The Ang Thong private speedboat includes captain, crew, fuel, snorkel gear, kayaks, water and soft drinks for about 8 hours. You set the start time and the order of the stops, which lets you reach the Emerald Lake before the day-boat rush. Lunch and land transfers are not included: you can meet the boat at the pier, and the boat can stop at a restaurant where you order and pay.

Other ways into the park from 16 December: the Red Baron junk boat Ang Thong day cruise (3,900 THB with the park fee included, Wednesdays and Saturdays) and the Boutique Yachting Day on the sailing yacht Naga (4,500 THB, child 2,900 THB, about 12 hours door to door).

Pig Island (Koh Madsum) and Koh Tan tours

All Pig Island boats leave from Thong Krut pier on Samui's south-west coast; the crossing to Koh Madsum takes 15–30 minutes. You spend time on the beach where a family of domestic pigs lives, then snorkel the shallow reef at Koh Tan. Where the island fee is not included, it is about 50 THB per person in cash. These trips run every day of the year, weather permitting, which makes them the main boat trip in November and early December. Our Pig Island guide and Koh Tan guide have the background.

Shared speedboat (1,450 THB, 4–5 hours)

The Pig Island speedboat tour is the shortest and cheapest boat day from Samui: minibus to Thong Krut, Koh Madsum, snorkelling at Koh Tan and back by the afternoon. Child price 1,250 THB. Your pickup time arrives the evening before.

Sunset tour (1,550 THB, 5–6 hours, with dinner)

The Pig Island sunset tour picks you up in the early afternoon, visits the pigs in softer light, snorkels at Koh Tan, serves dinner on the island and comes back after sunset. Child price 1,350 THB.

Longtail (1,750 THB, about 6 hours, lunch on Koh Tan)

The small-group longtail tour carries a maximum of 12 guests on a traditional wooden boat. Lunch on Koh Tan and the Koh Madsum fee are included. Child price 1,550 THB.

Pink Dolphins & Pig Island (2,250 THB, about 6 hours)

The Pink Dolphins and Pig Island tour first crosses about 45 minutes towards the Khanom coast on the mainland, where the dolphins feed, then visits Koh Tan and Koh Madsum. Sightings are likely but never guaranteed.

Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan day trip

The Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan day trip costs 2,200 THB (child 1,600 THB) by shared speedboat and takes about 9 hours. Pickup is 07:30–08:30; you snorkel at a Koh Tao reef chosen for the day's conditions, land on Koh Nang Yuan for the sandbar and the viewpoint climb, have lunch and make a second snorkel stop before returning in the late afternoon. Koh Nang Yuan charges a landing fee of 250 THB per adult in cash. The island's rules are strict: no plastic bottles or cans (bags are checked at the pier), no fins on the island's reef and no drones. The crossing is the longest of our day trips on open water, so October to January brings more weather cancellations. More on the island in our Koh Samui to Koh Tao guide.

For divers and keen snorkellers there are two shared alternatives: the Sail Rock and Koh Tao fast catamaran day (snorkelling 2,900 THB, two dives 4,950–5,650 THB) and the shared dive and snorkel speedboat day (snorkellers 2,950 THB, certified divers 5,540 THB).

How to choose the right Koh Samui tour

  • Families with toddlers: the Pig Island speedboat tour is 4–5 hours with a 15–30 minute crossing; infants 0–2 travel free and children's life jackets are on board. Ang Thong's Emerald Lake stairway and the steep viewpoint climb are hard with a toddler.
  • Non-swimmers: every tour supplies life jackets, and you can snorkel in one at Koh Tan or Koh Nang Yuan. Ang Thong's highlights (the Emerald Lake and the viewpoint) are on land.
  • Seasickness-prone: choose the Ang Thong big boat or a short Pig Island trip, take a tablet 30–60 minutes before departure and sit at the back of a speedboat.
  • Couples: the Ang Thong VIP tour (only 15 guests), the Pig Island sunset tour or a private longtail to the Five Islands with a seafood dinner (7,500 THB for two).
  • Groups of 6 or more: a private boat often costs about the same per head as shared seats. Ten guests on the Pig Island private speedboat pay 1,195 THB each.
  • Photographers: the Ang Thong overnight gives you the Wua Talab viewpoint at sunset and sunrise; Koh Nang Yuan's viewpoint gives the classic shot of three islands joined by a sandbar.
  • Rainy-season visitors (October–December): book Pig Island on the sheltered south-west coast, keep a day free in case your trip is moved, and fill wet days with a cooking class, Muay Thai or an elephant sanctuary.

Best time of year for boat tours from Koh Samui

February to April has the calmest sea. May to September is good too, because Samui is sheltered from the south-west monsoon that hits Phuket and Krabi. October is mixed, November is the wettest month (about 445 mm of rain) and Ang Thong is closed, and December improves as the month goes on, with Ang Thong reopening on 16 December. Mid-December to February is peak season: the park has daily visitor quotas, so book a few days ahead. See Koh Samui weather by month and today's sea conditions.

Ang Thong closed? What to do 1 November–15 December

From 1 November to 15 December 2026 you can still go to sea from Koh Samui. Pig Island boats leave from Thong Krut on the south-west coast, which is often sheltered from the north-east monsoon; we check the forecast every day and move or refund any trip we cancel. Koh Tao runs too, but the open-water crossing is cancelled more often in this season. Small private boats on the west and south coast (the Dragon King catamaran from Thong Krut, 17,000 THB for a half day, or the Five Islands longtail dinner cruise off Taling Ngam) are good choices on calm afternoons. On wet days, pick a covered activity: the cooking class, an elephant sanctuary visit, Muay Thai or the rum distillery. Our Koh Samui in November guide has more ideas for the season.

Tips for boat tours from Koh Samui

  • Take a seasickness tablet 30–60 minutes before departure, not on the boat.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen and wear a rash vest for snorkelling.
  • Wear water shoes: coral sand and rocks at Koh Tan and Ang Thong are sharp.
  • Pack phones and cash in a dry bag; speedboats throw spray.
  • Bring cash for island fees: 300 THB for Ang Thong, 250 THB for Nang Yuan, about 50 THB for Koh Madsum.
  • Leave the drone at the hotel for Koh Nang Yuan, where drones are banned.
  • Morning departures usually have calmer water than the afternoon.

Responsible tours

We don't sell elephant riding or bathing with elephants on any product; every elephant visit on this site is limited to feeding, watching or walking alongside the herd. On Pig Island, don't chase, lift or ride the pigs, and feed them only if the guide allows it, with food the guide approves: never bread, crisps, sweets, fizzy drinks or alcohol. On the reef, don't touch or stand on coral, don't feed fish and keep your fins up in shallow water. Bring a refillable bottle, which you need anyway for Koh Nang Yuan. Read our snorkelling guide and beach safety guide before you go.
