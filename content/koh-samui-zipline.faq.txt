How much is ziplining on Koh Samui? | From 800 THB for two rides at Lamai Viewpoint to 2,700 THB for the 21-line Golden course. The popular treetop course near Tan Rua costs about 1,590 THB on the market and 1,900 THB with us, transfer from the main beach areas included.
What is the minimum age for the zipline on Koh Samui? | It depends on the course: the treetop course near Tan Rua takes ages 6–70 and 5-year-olds on request, and the jungle park starts at 7. The west-coast course has a 20 kg minimum body weight instead of an age limit.
Is there a weight limit on Samui's zipline courses? | Yes: 120 kg on the treetop course near Tan Rua, 105 kg on the west-coast canopy course and 100 kg at the jungle park and Lamai Viewpoint.
How long does the Koh Samui zipline take? | About 60 minutes on the treetop course and 2.5 hours door to door with transfer and waterfall time. The jungle park takes 1 hour (Silver) or 2 hours (Golden) on the lines, and 5.5–6.5 hours with transfers.
Which areas does the zipline pickup cover? | For the treetop course, Chaweng, Choeng Mon, Bophut, Maenam and Lamai are included; other areas pay 1,800 THB per group and hillside villas 1,000 THB. The west-coast course picks up free from Lipa Noi, Nathon, Taling Ngam and Bang Por.
Does the zipline run when it rains? | Yes, the courses run in light rain all year. Lines may pause for thunderstorms or strong wind, and if a session is cancelled for weather you choose a new date or a refund.
Can I combine the zipline with an ATV ride? | Yes. Book the 09:30 treetop session and the 14:00 quad ride in the same hills; both include transfer from the main beach areas. Message us on WhatsApp to book them together.
What should I wear for ziplining on Koh Samui? | Closed shoes with grip and shorts or light trousers that sit well under a harness. Tie back long hair, leave loose jewellery behind and bring insect repellent.
Is ziplining safe for someone scared of heights? | Most nervous riders are fine after the short practice line. Guides clip you on and off at every platform and you never unclip yourself, so tell the guide at the briefing and they will talk you through each launch.
