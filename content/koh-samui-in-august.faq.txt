Is August a good time to visit Koh Samui? | Yes, for weather. August averages about 102 mm of rain over 10 rain days, with highs of 32 °C and calm sea on most days, so Ang Thong, Pig Island and Koh Tao all run well. The downside is peak-season crowds and room rates 30–80% above the shoulder months, so book early.
How much does it rain on Koh Samui in August? | About 102 mm on 1991–2020 averages, over about 10 days with 1 mm or more. Most rain comes as a short afternoon shower followed by sunshine, because the Thai peninsula shelters Samui from the south-west monsoon that soaks Phuket and Krabi at this time of year.
Is Ang Thong Marine Park open in August? | Yes. The park is open every day from 16 December to 31 October, and August is good for the crossing on most days. Our speedboat tour costs 1,800 THB plus the 300 THB park fee in cash; the big boat (1,500 THB) is slower but gentler if you get seasick. Book a few days ahead in school holidays.
Is the sea calm around Koh Samui in August? | Usually, yes. Typical daily wave highs are about 0.4 m on all coasts, and Chaweng and Lamai are among the island's best swimming beaches from January to September. Afternoons can be windy, so morning boat departures are often smoother. If we cancel a trip for sea safety, you choose a full refund or a new date.
When is the Full Moon Party in August 2027? | Tuesday 17 August 2027, on Haad Rin Sunrise Beach, Koh Phangan. The date is provisional until the Haad Rin association confirms it. Our shared return speedboat from Samui costs 1,400 THB per person with hotel pickup from most areas; party entry, about 200 THB, is paid in cash at the beach.
Is Koh Samui expensive in August? | Rooms are. July and August are peak season, with rates 30–80% above shoulder-season prices, and villas book out months ahead. Daily costs stay the same: street dishes 50–120 THB, a one-hour Thai massage 300–500 THB and a scooter 150–350 THB a day. Book flights 6–10 weeks ahead.
Is Koh Samui better than Phuket in August? | For weather, usually yes. Phuket and Krabi have their south-west monsoon from about May to October, while Samui sits in the lee of the Thai peninsula and has short showers and calm sea on most days. That makes August a good month for boat trips and island hopping from Samui.
Are there box jellyfish on Koh Samui in August? | They are possible. The risk is higher from May to October, after heavy rain, in calm, warm water and in the evening. Swim inside netted areas where they exist and wear a full-length rash vest. For a sting, pour vinegar over it for at least 30 seconds, don't rub it, and call 1669.
Is August or September better for Koh Samui? | The weather is almost the same: about 102 mm of rain in August and 118 mm in September, with highs of 32 °C in both. September is quieter and cheaper once the school holidays end, while August has more going on. Ang Thong is open in both months.
