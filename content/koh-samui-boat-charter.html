Speedboat, catamaran, motor yacht or junk

The choice comes down to how far you want to go, how many of you there are and how much time you want to spend moving rather than swimming. Speedboats are the only sensible way to reach Ang Thong or Koh Tao and back in a relaxed day; catamarans and the junk are slower but give you shade, space and a toilet; the motor yachts are for a luxury day with cabins.

Ang Thong National Marine Park

Forty-two limestone islands about 30 km west of Samui, with the Emerald Lake on Koh Mae Ko and the viewpoint above the park headquarters on Koh Wua Talab. A private boat lets you climb the viewpoints before or after the day-tour crowds and choose your own snorkel stops. The park closes every year from 1 November to 15 December and reopens on 16 December, so in November and early December we route charters south instead. If you only want Ang Thong, our fixed-price Ang Thong private speedboat costs 33,000 THB per boat for about 8 hours, plus the park fee. Background on the islands is in our Ang Thong Marine Park guide.

Pig Island, Koh Tan and Koh Rap

The southern islands are the year-round charter: Koh Madsum with its beach pigs, Koh Tan with the best shallow reef near Samui, and quieter Koh Rap beyond. They sit off the south-west coast, out of the worst of the north-east monsoon swell, which makes them the default route from November to January. Our fixed-price trips from Thong Krut are much cheaper than a north-coast charter for the same islands: a private speedboat to Pig Island costs 11,950 THB for up to 10 guests and a private longtail 5,950 THB for up to 8. See the Pig Island guide.

Koh Som and Tongson Bay

Short, sheltered hops off the north-east coast that suit half days, sunset sails and families with small children: clear, shallow water from December to April and very little time in transit. Several catamaran sunset and half-day programmes use this route.

Koh Phangan beaches

Phangan's best beaches are easiest by private boat because the roads to them are steep or unpaved: Haad Yuan and the Haad Rin peninsula in the south, Than Sadet and Thong Nai Pan on the east coast, Bottle Beach in the north. Lunch is usually at a beach restaurant you choose. Plan around the moon: Haad Rin is packed on Full Moon nights. Our sister site has the full Koh Phangan beaches guide if you want to choose the stops.

Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan

Koh Tao is the longest run (about 1.5 hours each way), so book a full day on a speedboat with two engines. Koh Nang Yuan, three islands joined by sandbars, charges 250 THB per adult in cash and bans plastic bottles, fins and drones. The shared alternative is our Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan day trip at 2,200 THB per adult.

The sunset coast

The sun sets over the sea on the west and south-west coasts, behind the Five Islands off Taling Ngam. Sunset is between about 17:55 (November) and 18:45 (July). Sunset charters run for 2.5–3 hours from late afternoon; see sunset cruises for times by month.

What's included and what's extra

Inclusions differ from boat to boat, and your written quote lists them. As a guide, most of our charters include:

  • The boat with licensed captain and crew for the agreed hours
  • Fuel (or a fuel surcharge already built into the price)
  • Travel or accident insurance
  • Life jackets in adult and children's sizes (children must wear them on board)
  • Snorkel masks or full snorkel gear
  • Drinking water and soft drinks; seasonal fruit on most boats
  • An English-speaking guide on most speedboats and the yachts
  • Hotel transfers on several of our speedboats and on Minor Affair

Usually extra or not included:

  • National park and island fees, in cash: Ang Thong 300 THB adult / 150 THB child, Koh Nang Yuan 250 THB adult, Koh Madsum about 50 THB, Koh Rap about 100 THB
  • Lunch on most boats (the boat stops at a restaurant, or you order a catering package); Red Baron includes lunch
  • Guests above the number included in the price
  • Hotel transfers on some larger boats, and from remote hillside villas
  • Kayaks on some speedboats, diving, alcohol, decorations, a photographer
  • 7% VAT where the price is shown "+ VAT" (Kia Ora)
  • Tips for the crew (optional)

Several of our catamarans and the motor yacht Casablanca let you bring your own food and drinks on board free of charge; ask us when you book, as rules differ by boat. Water toys vary: Victoria Sailing carries paddle boards, kayaks, a dinghy and fishing rods; Blue Coco has a dinghy and a float; Coco Paradise adds a sea pool and water slide for an extra charge (not allowed inside Ang Thong).

Boats with toilets

Small and mid-size speedboats generally have no toilet, so plan a stop on longer routes. Coco Cay (44 ft) has a private restroom; Blue Coco has cabins with their own bathrooms; Kia Ora has a bathroom and showers; Casablanca has two cabins and two bathrooms; Minor Affair is a 61 ft Sunseeker with guest cabins. If a toilet matters, say so in your enquiry and we will only quote boats that have one.

Seasons and prices

Samui's sea follows the north-east monsoon, not the Andaman pattern. February to April is the calmest and clearest; May to September is good on most days; October to December brings rain and swell on the east and north-east coasts, peaking in November (about 445 mm of rain on 16 days). The prices on this page are our published "from" rates. Over Christmas and New Year, demand is highest and boats are booked weeks ahead, so your quote confirms the exact price for your date.

Weather and safety

Every boat we charter carries life jackets (children's sizes included), first aid and a VHF radio, and our captains are licensed by the Thai Marine Department. Fatal boat accidents in Thailand are linked to overloading and rough seas, so we never exceed a boat's maximum, and the captain has the final word on routes and stops on the day. If the sea is unsafe, we move you to a sheltered route, change the date, or cancel: when we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.

[kp_sea_now]

Check the wave forecast yourself on Windy; a big north-east swell usually means a southern or western route. Storms can also hit outside the monsoon: Tropical Storm Pabuk closed ferries and Ang Thong in January 2019. Our Koh Samui sea conditions and tides pages explain what the numbers mean.

Good to know: speedboats slam in a chop. If anyone in your group is pregnant, has a back problem or gets seasick easily, choose a catamaran, the junk or a short sheltered route, and sit at the back of a speedboat where the ride is softest.

Weddings, proposals, birthdays and company groups

Private boats are popular for celebrations because the whole deck is yours. The junk Red Baron takes up to 70 guests for a sunset dinner cruise, wedding party or company event, with meals and soft drinks included. Kia Ora and Coco Paradise take up to 45 on wide, stable decks; Blue Coco and Coco Paradise come with a butler. For a proposal or an anniversary, a sailing catamaran or Casablanca at sunset gives privacy. Tell us what you need when you enquire (catering, a cake, flowers, a DJ or a photographer) and we quote it as an extra; give us at least a few days' notice for catering and decorations, and more in high season.

Cruise-ship passengers: tell us your ship's all-aboard time and where your tender lands, and we will plan a route that brings you back with a clear time buffer. Company groups larger than one boat can split across two boats travelling together.
