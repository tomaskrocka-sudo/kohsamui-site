Why go: an off-road hour in Samui's west-coast hills

The hills behind Lipa Noi are some of the least built-up on Koh Samui: rubber and coconut plantations, red-earth tracks and a short climb to a viewpoint over the Five Islands and the Ang Thong archipelago on the horizon. A guided ATV or buggy ride is the easiest way to see them without hiring a scooter and getting lost on unmarked tracks.

What you'll do

After a briefing and a practice lap at the base you follow the guide in a line along plantation and jungle trails. The short ride is a loop near the base. The longer rides climb to the Lat Wanon waterfall, a small fall best after rain, and the Phu Lipa viewpoint, with time for photos. The pace is set by the slowest driver, so first-timers are fine.

Who it suits

  • Couples and friends who want an hour of fun on the west coast, maybe before sunset at Lipa Noi beach.
  • Families with teenagers: drivers need to be 16, but younger children can ride as passengers. The UTV buggy is the better choice with a child.

Who should choose something else

  • Anyone pregnant, with back or neck problems, or a heart condition: the trails are bumpy.
  • If you want a full day of sightseeing with the off-road part included, a jeep safari covers waterfalls, temples and viewpoints with a driver.

Good to know

  • Safety: follow the guide's line and keep a gap to the vehicle in front. Most injuries on Samui quads happen when riders overtake or speed downhill.
  • Weather: from October to December the tracks can be very muddy; morning slots are usually drier and cooler than the 12:00 and 14:00 rides.
  • Insurance: accident insurance is included, but check that your own travel insurance covers off-road riding.
  • Getting there: the base is on the quiet west side, so book the transfer or plan your own taxi back.
