How long is the ferry from Koh Samui to Koh Phangan? | Speedboats and catamarans take 20–30 minutes, the Seatran Discovery about 45 minutes, and the Haad Rin Queen and Songserm slow ferries about 50–60 minutes.
Which pier on Koh Samui do boats to Koh Phangan leave from? | Most leave from one of three Bangrak piers (Lomprayah pier, Seatran pier or Petcherat Marina), with more from Pralarn pier in Maenam, Nathon, and Big Buddha pier for Haad Rin; always check the operator and exact pier.
How do I get from Samui to Haad Rin? | Take the Haad Rin Queen from Big Buddha pier (ferry 200 THB at 10:30 and 17:00, speedboat 500 THB at 15:00 and 18:30), or take a boat to Thong Sala and a 20-minute taxi to Haad Rin.
How much is the ferry from Samui to Phangan? | Most tickets cost 200–500 THB: 350 THB on Lomprayah, Boonsiri and Songserm speedboats, about 345–430 THB on Seatran, 490 THB on Echo to Baan Tai and 200 THB on the slow ferries.
What time is the last boat from Samui to Phangan? | The last scheduled public boat is usually Echo's 20:30 speedboat from Petcherat Marina to Baan Tai; the last boats to Thong Sala leave around 17:15–17:30 and the last Haad Rin Queen at 18:30.
Can I do a Koh Phangan day trip from Samui? | Yes, take the 10:30 Haad Rin Queen to Haad Rin, move to Thong Sala by taxi, and return on a 16:00–16:30 boat, or hire a private speedboat for the east-coast beaches.
How do I get to the Full Moon Party from Koh Samui? | Our shared Full Moon speedboat costs 1,400 THB return with hotel pickup included from most areas of Koh Samui, private boats are available for groups, and entry of about 200 THB is paid at the gate.
Do ferries run in bad weather? | Large catamarans usually run, but speedboats can be cancelled in strong north-east wind from October to December; check the sea forecast and leave a buffer day before flights.
Can I hire a private speedboat to Koh Phangan? | Yes, a private speedboat goes from your Samui beach or pier to the Phangan beach you choose; send your date and group size on WhatsApp for a quote and pay online to confirm.
