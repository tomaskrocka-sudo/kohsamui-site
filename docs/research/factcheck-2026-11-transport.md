## Key findings

1. **Seatran Donsak fares are much higher than the pages said.** Seatran raised fares five times as diesel prices rose:
   - Foot passenger to Samui: 170 → 180 THB (14 Mar 2026) → 210 (27 Mar) → 220 → 230 (4 Apr) → 250 (8 Apr).
   - Car incl. driver: 580 → 670 (27 Mar) → 730 (4 Apr). No vehicle figure was found after 8 Apr.
   - Motorbike 270, big bike/sidecar 400 (27 Mar). Monks, novices and students in uniform pay half.
   - To Phangan: 250 (14 Mar) → 280 (27 Mar) → 300 → 320 (8 Apr). Car to Phangan 1,040 (27 Mar).
   - The pages had 180–210 on foot and 550–670 per car. No source found reporting a fall since April. visitthailandtoday (20 Oct 2026) still prints 170, which is stale.
   - Sources:
     - https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40063728 (13 Mar 2026: 170→180)
     - https://thethaiger.com/news/samui/koh-samui-and-koh-pha-ngan-ferry-fares-increase-after-fuel-price-jump (27 Mar 2026: 210, motorbike 270, car 670)
     - https://en.zonesamui.com/34220-ferries-to-koh-samui-and-koh-phangan-new-fare-increase-after-diesel-surge (27 Mar 2026: same figures)
     - https://en.thairath.co.th/news/local/2922915 (27 Mar 2026: full Seatran tariff; car 580→670)
     - https://thainewsroom.com/2026/04/03/koh-samui-koh-phangan-ferry-operator-hikes-fares-for-the-4th-time/ (4 Apr 2026: 220→230, vehicle 700→730, Phangan 290→300)
     - https://en.thairath.co.th/news/local/south/2925241 (8 Apr 2026: 230→250, Phangan 300→320)
     - https://www.travelnews.ch/english-corner/31561-the-ferry-crossing-to-koh-samui-is-getting-more-expensive.html (17 Mar 2026)
2. **Raja Ferry is now the cheaper walk-on crossing.** The operator site lists Donsak–Lipa Noi 220 THB and Donsak–Thong Sala 290 THB. Its online timetable lists a 04:00 sailing each way.
   - https://www.rajaferryport.com/
   - https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/surat-thani-to-koh-samui (20 Oct 2026: 220 THB, hourly 05:00–19:00)
3. **Lomprayah kept its fares unchanged** despite oil prices (news item, 26 Apr 2026). It also sells Samui–Phangan from 250 THB (24 Apr 2026). New since 2 Oct 2026: joint van-and-catamaran tickets from Chumphon airport to Koh Tao, Koh Phangan and Samui.
   - https://www.lomprayah.com/news?page=1
   - https://www.lomprayah.com/news/oil-prices-have-gone-up-but-the-lomprayah-service-remains-the-same-en?lang=en
4. **Bangkok Airways is suspending Samui–Singapore for winter 2026/27, 25 Oct 2026 – 28 Mar 2027 (Bangkok–Malé too).**
   - Single source: aerotelegraph, 3 Jun 2026. https://www.aerotelegraph.com/ticker/bangkok-airways-setzt-diese-beiden-routen-im-kommenden-winter-aus/n9x15bg
   - Supporting context: in April 2026 PG had already cut SIN from 17 to 14 a week (21 Apr–24 Oct 2026), replacing a planned Q3 suspension.

     - https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260223-pg3q26
     - https://kucinta-air.blogspot.com/2026/04/bangkok-airways-reduces-flights-to.html
   - No second source confirms the winter suspension. Pages word it as "aviation trade press reported in June 2026" and flag the dates in tables. **Please confirm on the Bangkok Airways booking engine.**

   - aviability: 8 a day. https://aviability.com/en/airport/usm-ko-samui-samui/destinations
   - flightconnections, 16 Oct 2026: 112 flights in the rest of October ≈ 7 a day. https://www.flightconnections.com/flights-to-ko-samui-usm
   - The "as few as 6 from the late-October change" claim was unsupported. The real 2026 cut was 8 → 7 a day, 20 Apr–30 Jun 2026; Krabi ran 2 → 1 a day, 2 May–30 Jun 2026 (aeroroutes 260421-pgns26).

   - Adult 250 THB, child 120 THB, Thai nationals 50 THB.
   - Day visitors 10:00–15:00.
   - No plastic bottles or cans (bags are checked), no fins, no drones.
   - Sources:
     - https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/koh-nang-yuan-guide (28 Oct 2026)
