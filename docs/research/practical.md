**Confidence tags**

1. **Visa-free stay is now 30 days, not 60**, for UK, US, EU (incl. Slovakia), Australia, Canada and others. It took effect **15 Sep 2026**. Anyone who entered before that date keeps the stay stamped on entry. **[verified 2+ sources incl. GOV.UK]**

5. **Cannabis:** since 25 June 2025, buying flower requires a prescription. Public smoking is prohibited. **Vapes/e-cigarettes are illegal.** **[verified 2+ sources]**

## 3. Entry and visas (high stakes: link to official sources on the site)

### 3.1 Visa exemption: 30 days from 15 Sep 2026 **[verified 2+ sources incl. official]**

- **The change:** four Ministry of Interior notices in the Royal Gazette dated **31 Aug 2026**, effective **15 Sep 2026**, repealed the 60-day exemption introduced in July 2024, which covered 93 countries.
- **Who gets what:**
  - **30 days:** 60 countries/territories, including the **UK, US, Canada, Australia, Japan, India and all 27 EU states (incl. Slovakia, Czech Republic, Italy)**.
  - **15 days:** Mauritius and Seychelles.
  - **Visa on Arrival:** Azerbaijan, Belarus and Serbia.
  - Nationals of former 60-day countries not on the new lists need a visa unless a bilateral agreement applies. Bilateral waivers (e.g. China, Russia, some South American states) were not cancelled.
- **Transitional rule:** people who entered before 15 Sep keep the stay stamped on entry.
- **Land borders:** visa-exempt entries are limited to **2 per calendar year**, with exceptions for some ASEAN states. This does not limit air arrivals.
- **Proof at entry (GOV.UK):** you may be asked for **10,000 THB per person / 20,000 THB per family** and an onward ticket. UK nationals have been refused entry without them.
- **Overstay:** 500 THB/day up to 20,000 THB, plus possible detention, deportation, or a re-entry ban of up to 10 years.
- Sources:
  - https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/thailand/entry-requirements (official; updated 12/30 Oct 2026)
  - https://www.khaosodenglish.com/politics/2026/09/01/thailand-reduces-visa-free-stay-from-60-to-30-days/
  - https://freakingnomads.com/news/thailand-officially-cuts-visa-free-stay-to-30-days/
  - Also reported by TTG Asia and ScandAsia: https://www.ttgasia.com/2026/09/08/thailand-cuts-visa-free-stay-to-30-days-for-60-countries/

### 3.2 Extension

- **What:** a visa-exempt 30-day stay can be extended **once by up to 30 days** at a local immigration office. Use form **TM.7**; fee **1,900 THB**; approval is at the officer's discretion. Documents: passport and copies, photo, and possibly an onward ticket. **[verified 2+ sources for the 30-day extension (GOV.UK says "if eligible"; freakingnomads; visasnews); 1,900 THB fee single source (visasnews), consistent with the standard extension fee]**
- Sources:
  - https://visasnews.com/en/thailand-30-day-visa-free-stay-can-be-extended-by-another-30-days/ (17 Sep 2026)
  - https://freakingnomads.com/news/thailand-officially-cuts-visa-free-stay-to-30-days/

### 3.3 TDAC: Thailand Digital Arrival Card **[verified 2+ sources incl. official]**

- **Who:** mandatory since **1 May 2025** for all foreign nationals arriving by air, land or sea.
- **How:** online at **https://tdac.immigration.go.th**, within **3 days before arrival**. Free on the official site; avoid paid look-alike sites.
- **Exempt:** transit passengers who don't pass immigration, and border-pass holders.
- Sources:
  - https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/thailand/entry-requirements
  - https://www.tilleke.com/insights/thailand-launches-mandatory-digital-arrival-card-for-foreign-nationals/3/

### 3.4 Tourist entry fee: NOT in force

- **History:**
  - 300 THB (air) / 150 THB (land/sea) was proposed repeatedly and postponed in July 2025.
  - It was revived in March 2026 under the new government.
  - May 2026 reports called it the "Thailand Tourism Fee" (TTF) of 300 THB, but no start date was given.
- **Latest (24 Aug 2026):**
  - The National Tourism Policy Committee endorsed a **flat 450 THB** for all arrivals.
  - Still needed: a 30-day public hearing, committee review, Cabinet approval and a Royal Gazette notice.
  - Air phase ~180 days after the Gazette notice; land/sea ~1 year later.
  - Q1 2027 is floated as a planning estimate.
  - **Nothing is collected now.**

### 4.4 Sea hazards

**Rip currents and big surf (east coast: Chaweng, Lamai)** **[verified 2+ sources]**
- Most dangerous in the NE monsoon (Oct–Dec), and also during winter cold-air surges.
- Example: 19 Feb 2026, red flags along Chaweng and Lamai with 1–2 m+ waves and swimming banned. A Russian tourist was swept off rocks at Lad Koh viewpoint in Nov 2024; other drownings have followed ignored red flags.
- FCDO: "People have drowned in strong riptides". Follow the red flags.
- Sources:

**Box jellyfish**
- Since 2002, 10 deaths in Thailand, **9 of them in waters off Samui and Phangan**. Hotels were given multilingual signs and vinegar stations (2022).
- First aid: vinegar for 30 s or more; don't rub the sting or apply fresh water.
- Risk is higher in calm seas and after rain.
- FCDO says jellyfish come close to shore **May–October** nationally.

### 4.6 Cannabis **[verified 2+ sources]**

- **Prescription:** since **25 June 2025**, cannabis flower is a "controlled herb". Recreational sale and advertising are banned, and buying requires a **prescription** from a licensed doctor, Thai/Chinese traditional medicine practitioner, pharmacist or dentist.
- **Public use:** smoking or vaping cannabis in public is prohibited, with fines up to **25,000 THB** and/or 3 months' jail.
- **Minimum age:** 20.
- **Extracts:** above 0.2% THC remain Category 5 narcotics. A new ministerial regulation on extracts has been in force since **26 Apr 2026**.
- **Re-listing:** the plant itself has NOT been put back on the narcotics list as of June 2026, though anti-drug groups are pushing for it.
- Sources:
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_in_Thailand
  - https://www.khaosodenglish.com/life/2025/06/27/thailand-starts-banning-the-sale-of-cannabis-without-a-prescription/amp/

- **Site copy:** say "illegal to buy without a prescription; never smoke in public". Avoid detailed legal claims; the rules keep changing.

### 6.2 Monsoon specifics **[verified 2+ sources]**

- Samui sits in the Gulf of Thailand and is largely sheltered from the SW monsoon (May–Oct) that hits Phuket and Krabi.
- Its main rains come with the **NE monsoon, Oct–Dec, peaking in November**. The east coast (Chaweng, Lamai) takes onshore wind and surf, with red flags and rip currents.
- FCDO: "In Koh Samui and south-east Thailand [the rainy season] is usually from November to March". This is broader than climatological data shows.
- Late-December to January storms can happen. Tropical Storm Pabuk (Jan 2019) closed ferries and Ang Thong.
- Sources:
  - visitthailandtoday best-time page
  - GOV.UK safety page
  - https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/822950 (Pabuk 2019)
  - https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/276717

### 6.3 Sea conditions for boat trips **[single source, consistent with climate data]**

- **Best (calmest):** Feb–Apr. February is rated the best month for Ang Thong and island hopping.
- **Generally workable:** May–Sep, with calm seas most days.
- **Rougher:** Oct, and Nov–Dec on some days. Ferries to Phangan/Tao run on choppier water. Sailings can be cancelled during strong NE wind events.
- Source: https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/best-time-to-visit-koh-samui
