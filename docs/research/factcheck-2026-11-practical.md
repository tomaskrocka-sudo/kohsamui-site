## Core facts confirmed unchanged (no edit needed)

- **Visa exemption:** 30 days for 60 countries since 15 Sep 2026 (Royal Gazette notices of 31 Aug 2026). Mauritius and Seychelles get 15 days. Visa on arrival covers only Azerbaijan, Belarus and Serbia. Land entries are capped at 2 per calendar year, with Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia and Singapore exempt. Bilateral deals still apply. The 60-country list on the page matches TAT exactly.
  - https://www.tatnews.org/2026/09/thailand-introduces-new-30-day-and-15-day-visa-exemption-rules-from-15-september/
  - https://eiglaw.com/thailand-introduces-new-30-day-and-15-day-visa-exemption-rules/
  - https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/thailand/entry-requirements (updated 30 Oct 2026)
- **30-day extension:** once, TM.7, 1,900 THB, at the officer's discretion.
  - https://visasnews.com/en/thailand-30-day-visa-free-stay-can-be-extended-by-another-30-days/ (cites the Thai embassies in Washington, Brussels and London)
- **TDAC:** mandatory, free, filed within 3 days before arrival. The March 2026 warning about look-alike sites still stands.
  - GOV.UK entry requirements
  - https://www.khaosodenglish.com/tourism/2026/03/29/10-of-foreign-arrivals-used-fake-tdac-sites-immigration-warns/
- **Proof of funds:** 10,000 THB per person or 20,000 THB per family. Passport valid for 6 months with 1 blank page. Overstay fine 500 THB a day, capped at 20,000 THB.
  - GOV.UK entry requirements
- **Bilateral list** (90 days: Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Peru, South Korea; China and Hong Kong among the 30-day group; Cambodia and Myanmar 14 days) matches the MFA Department of Consular Affairs list dated 12 Dec 2025.
  - https://image.mfa.go.th/mfa/0/zE6021nSnu/บริการประชาชน/Bilat_Ordinary.pdf
