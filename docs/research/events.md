**Confidence tags**
- **[verified 2+ sources]**: two or more independent sources agree.
- **[single source]**: one credible source; not contradicted.

**Note for the build:** the source URLs below are for internal verification only. The site policy is to link out only to own sites, WhatsApp and utility tools, so do not put these URLs on the live site.

## 1. Recurring events: detail

### 1.1 Loy Krathong
- **2026: Tuesday 24 November 2026.** This is the 15th waxing day of the 12th lunar month. Loy Krathong is not a public holiday and has no alcohol ban. **[verified 2+ sources]**
  - Thai sources: thansettakij ("วันอังคารที่ 24 พฤศจิกายน 2569") https://www.thansettakij.com/lifestyle/travel-shopping/672476 ; sanook https://sanook.com/campus/910912/
  - **Conflict:** umetravel (https://www.umetravel.com/loy-krathong-festival/dates.html) and thekohsamuiguide (https://www.thekohsamuiguide.com/post/koh-samui-events-2026) give Wed 25 Nov 2026. **Use 24 Nov**: the Thai-language press is authoritative, and the date matches the Full Moon Party date and the calculated full moon (Tue 24 Nov 2026, 21:53 ICT).
- **2027: probably Saturday 13 November 2027, not officially confirmed.** **[uncertain]**
  - umetravel says Sun 14 Nov 2027. The calculated full moon is Sun 14 Nov 2027 at 10:26 ICT, so the night of the full moon falls on Sat 13 Nov.
  - Both Full Moon Party schedules place the FMP on Sat 13 Nov 2027, and the FMP normally coincides with Loy Krathong (as it does in 2026).
  - Thai Buddhist calendars for BE 2570 had not been published at the time of research. Re-check about 6 months ahead.
- **Where on Samui:**
  - Chaweng Lake and Big Buddha (Wat Phra Yai) are the classic spots. **[single source]**, old: https://www.samui-villa.com/guide/2015/09/cultural-festivals-in-samui/amp
  - Beaches and hotel ponds are also used.
  - **Floating sky lanterns are banned on Koh Samui** because of fire risk: https://www.thekohsamuiguide.com/koh-samui-new-years-eve/ **[single source]**. Advise krathong only, and suggest bread or banana-leaf krathong rather than foam.

### 1.3 Chinese New Year 2027
- **Date:** Saturday 6 February 2027, Year of the (Fire) Goat. https://www.chinatravel.com/chinese-new-year/date **[single source]**; this is the standard published lunar date.
- **Where on Samui:**
  - Maenam's Chinese temple and Chinatown area hosts the main festivities, with a lion and dragon procession. It is the most family-friendly option.
  - Nathon's Hainan temple holds the more traditional celebration; Samui's Chinese families have Hainan roots. It has included face-piercing rituals, which may not suit children.
  - The Guan Yu shrine at Hua Thanon also holds celebrations.
  - Resorts (Conrad, Four Seasons, InterContinental and others) run gala dinners.
  - Source: https://www.thekohsamuiguide.com/chinese-new-year-koh-samui/ **[uncertain]**. It is a 2019 article, so confirm the 2027 programme locally in January 2027.

### 1.6 Buffalo fighting
- **Status: a traditional Samui and southern Thai event, held at small local arenas, mainly on special occasions** (New Year's Day and Songkran in particular). There is **no fixed public schedule**; fights are announced locally a few days ahead, on banners, by loudspeaker trucks and on Thai Facebook groups.
  - https://ferrysamui.com/buffalo-fighting-festival.html: "various stadiums", "likely a festival or two around the New Year", prize money sometimes above 1 million THB. **[uncertain]**: the page header is dated 2024 and gives the event as "TBA".
  - https://www.samui-villa.com/guide/2015/09/cultural-festivals-in-samui/amp: held on New Year's Day and Songkran. **[uncertain]**: 2015 article.
- **Venues historically named (2009):** Bang Makham (near Nathon), Maenam, Lamai, and the Bang Rak/"Ghost Road" crossing. Entry was 360 THB for adults; betting is central to the event. https://samui-weather.blogspot.com/search/label/Buffalo%20fight **[uncertain]**: 17 years old. **I could not find any 2025 or 2026 source confirming venues or dates.**
- **Legal status:** Thailand's Prevention of Animal Cruelty and Provision of Animal Welfare Act B.E. 2557 (2014) exempts local traditional practices (bull, buffalo and cock fighting) from the cruelty offence. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prevention_of_Animal_Cruelty_and_Provision_of_Animal_Welfare_Act **[single source]**. Gambling on the fights is a separate legal grey area.
- **Recommendation:** do not list buffalo fights as a bookable or scheduled event. At most, mention them as a cultural note ("occasional traditional buffalo fights around New Year and Songkran; ask locally"). This is also safer for the brand, given animal-welfare sensitivities among Western guests.

### 1.10 Vegetarian Festival

### 1.11 "Ten Days" / sea festival: no Samui event of that name found
- I found no current Samui event called a "Ten Days sea festival". The closest matches are below.
- **Sart Duean Sip (Tenth Lunar Month Festival) / Sat Thai:** merit-making for the dead at every Samui temple, with krayasat sweets.   - The 2026 edition may be running now (late Oct/early Nov 2026). Check locally.
  - The big water-borne Chak Phra is on the mainland, on the Tapi River in Surat Thani town (8 to 12 Oct 2025). https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5191050/

## 5. News and regulations affecting events (2025 to 2027)

### 5.1 Alcohol sales hours: changed in 2025 to 2026
- **Since 29 May 2026, alcohol may be sold from 11:00 to 24:00 nationwide.**
  - This permanently ends the old **14:00 to 17:00 afternoon ban**, which dated from 1972 and had been lifted on a 180-day trial from December 2025.
  - **[verified 2+ sources]**:
    - https://www.tatnews.org/2025/07/alcohol-sales-and-consumption-rules-updated-in-thailand-what-tourists-need-to-know/ (TAT update dated 29 May 2026)
    - https://www.pattayamail.com/latestnews/news/thailand-updates-alcohol-sales-hours-extends-legal-serving-window-to-midnight-551428
    - https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/visa/story/thailand-officially-revises-alcohol-sales-hours-with-select-exemptions-check-new-timings-533969-2026-05-30
- **Exempt from the hour limits:**
  - hotels licensed under the Hotel Act
  - licensed entertainment venues, which may stay open up to about 02:00 or 04:00 depending on zoning and licence
  - international airport terminals
  - approved event spaces
- **No sales at all** at temples, government offices, petrol stations, public parks or public transport.
- The legal drinking age is **20**.

### 5.2 Buddhist holy days with alcohol sales bans in the window
- **Exemptions since 10 May 2025:**
  - Hotels (Hotel Act), licensed entertainment venues, pubs and bars in tourist areas, international airports and designated special events **may still sell**.
  - Ordinary shops, 7-Eleven and unlicensed restaurants may not.
  - **Practical effect on Samui:** beach clubs and bars in Chaweng are usually open; convenience-store sales stop.
  - **[verified 2+ sources]**: https://www.nationthailand.com/blogs/news/general/40062977 (Makha Bucha 2026) and https://www.thaiholidayguide.com/buddhist-holidays-alcohol-ban-thailand/
  - Penalty for illegal sales: up to 6 months in jail and/or a THB 10,000 fine.
- **There are no ban days in Nov to Dec 2026.** Ok Phansa 2026 was Mon 26 Oct 2026 (https://www.thansettakij.com/lifestyle/travel-shopping/671340).
