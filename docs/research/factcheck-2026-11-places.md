## ang-thong-marine-park (changed)

- **History section, drone sentence.** Old: "Drone flights in national parks need permission from the park. [check]". New: a confirmed sentence. Flying needs the park chief's permission before take-off, plus a valid Thai drone registration, and only in the area the park allows. Commercial filming needs a separate permit from the Department of National Parks (DNP) in Bangkok.
  - Source: https://travel.trueid.net/detail/aVEgZ4pB6kOG (22 Oct 2024; cites the National Park Act 2019, the Air Navigation Act and the 2015 Ministry of Transport drone notice)
- **History section, name.** Old: "The name is usually translated as "golden bowl". [check]". New: "The name Ang Thong (อ่างทอง) translates as "golden bowl"." Basis: the dictionary meaning (อ่าง = basin/bowl, ทอง = gold).
- **Park rules list.**
  - Coral bullet: added "stay at least 2 m from the reef". Source: DNP announcement reported at https://www.khaosodenglish.com/tourism/2025/10/17/tourists-face-3070-penalty-for-banned-sunscreens-in-thai-marine-parks/ (same article confirms the 4 banned sunscreen ingredients and the fine of up to 100,000 THB under sections 20/47 of the National Park Act 2019).
  - New bullet "Drones": park chief's permission only. Source: trueid as above.
- **Confirmed and left unchanged:**
  - Fees: 300 THB foreign adult, 150 THB child 3–14, Thai 40/20 ("as of May 2026").
  - Hours 08:00–17:00.
  - Accommodation: bungalows 600/1,500 THB, camping 80 THB per person, tent rental 450 THB. The online booking system was still down in mid-2026.
  - Viewpoint 500 m, 25–30 min. Thale Nai: a 20-min trail to the viewpoint.
  - Sources: https://www.thainationalparks.com/mu-ko-ang-thong-national-park
- **Annual closure 1 Nov–15 Dec, reopening 16 Dec.**
  - The DNP notice of 21 Oct 2024 closes Ao Kha beach, Koh Wua Talap, Sam Sao beach and Thale Nai from 1 Nov to 15 Dec: https://www.komchadluek.net/kom-lifestyle/travel/589637
  - The park posted a "closed from 1 Nov" notice in Oct 2025: https://gcc.go.th/2025/10/03/ (title only; the page blocks fetching).
  - The DNP's 2026 Royal Gazette closure notice (195 parks, in force from 1 Jan 2026) exists, but its annex with per-park dates could not be read: https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/news-update/1215929
  - thainationalparks.com still says "usually closed 1 November to 15 December".
  - Kept as is. Not marked [check], because two notices from successive years plus the 2026 park guide agree.
- **One-day closure, 8 Jan 2026 (already on the page): confirmed.** Southern Meteorological Centre warning of 2–3 m waves. Source: https://mgronline.com/south/detail/9690000002052
- Checked: 16 Dec 2026 is a Wednesday.
