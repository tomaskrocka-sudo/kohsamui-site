Researched 31 Oct 2026 (web only). Source URLs are **evidence for verification only — not for outbound linking** (several are OTAs/competitors).

Confidence tags:
- **[verified 2+]** two or more independent sources agree
- **[single]** one source only
- **[uncertain]** sources conflict, data is old, or status is unclear

Notes:
- Prices are **retail, per person, in THB** unless stated otherwise. OTA USD prices are converted at about 34 THB/USD, the rate implied by click2gothailand's own THB/USD pairs.
- GPS is decimal lat,lng. Most of it comes from Google Maps links on visitthailandtoday.com (VTT) pages updated Aug–Oct 2026. A few points were decoded from Google Plus Codes; where I could check, the decoded point matches the VTT point.

### 1.1 Ang Thong National Marine Park

**Park entry fee (foreigners):** adult **300 THB**, child **150 THB** (age 3–14 and under 140 cm). Under-3s, monks and people with disabilities enter free. Thai nationals pay 40/20. The fee is cash, paid on site, and often **not** included in tour prices. **[verified 2+]**
